Site 301.15 Criteria Relative to a Finding of Undue Interference

Effective 2015-12-16.

Site 301.15 Criteria Relative to a Finding of Undue Interference. In determining whether a proposed energy facility will unduly interfere with the orderly development of the region, the committee shall consider: (a) The extent to which the siting, construction, and operation of the proposed facility will affect land use, employment, and the economy of the region; (b) The provisions of, and financial assurances for, the proposed decommissioning plan for the proposed facility; and (c) The views of municipal and regional planning commissions and municipal governing bodies regarding the proposed facility. Source. #10994, eff 12-16-15

Env-A 105.47 Waiver

Effective 2020-09-25.

Env-A 105.47 “Waiver” means a grant of permission to deviate from a requirement or a group of requirements, typically given in response to a specific request. Source. #6506-A, eff 5-1-97; renumbered by #8256 (from Env-A 101.299), (see Revision Note #1 at chapter heading); renumbered by #8304 (see Revision Note #2 at chapter heading); renumbered by #9839 (from Env-A 101.212), (see Revision Note #3 at chapter heading); renumbered by #10549, (from Env-A 101.214); ss by #13105, eff 9-25-20 (formerly Env-A 101.215) (see Revision Note #4 at chapter heading for Env-A 100)

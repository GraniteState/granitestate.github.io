Env-A 911.03 Recordkeeping Requirements

Effective 2025-09-20. Expires 2035-09-20.

Env-A 911.03 Recordkeeping Requirements. In the event of a permit deviation, the owner or operator shall: (a) Investigate and take corrective action immediately upon discovery of the permit deviation to restore the affected device, process, or air pollution control equipment to within allowable permit conditions; and (b) Record the following information: (1) A description of the permit deviation, including the applicable permit number and permit condition(s); (2) The probable cause of the permit deviation; (3) The date and time of the discovery of the permit deviation; (4) The actual date(s) and time(s) of the permit deviation; (5) The duration of the permit deviation, including the date and time that the device, process, or air pollution control equipment returned to operation in compliance with an enforceable emission limitation or operating condition; (6) The specific device, process, or air pollution control equipment that contributed to the permit deviation; (7) Any corrective measures taken to address the permit deviation; (8) Preventative measures taken to prevent future permit deviations; (9) The type and amount of any excess emissions that occurred as a result of the permit deviation, if applicable; and (10) If applicable, the calculation or estimation used to quantify the excess emissions.

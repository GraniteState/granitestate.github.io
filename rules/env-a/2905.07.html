Env-A 2905.07 Use of Allowances by Utilities

Effective 2020-02-25.

Env-A 2905.07 Use of Allowances by Utilities. As provided in RSA 125-J:5, XIII, the use of allowances by a utility, as defined in RSA 362:2, shall be subject to such additional conditions as are ordered by the New Hampshire public utilities commission pursuant to its authority. Source. #10002, eff 10-1-11 (from Env-A 2907.09); ss by #12995, eff 2-25-20

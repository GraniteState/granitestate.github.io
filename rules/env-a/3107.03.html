Env-A 3107.03 Protocol Methods

Effective 1997-01-21.

Env-A 3107.03 Protocol Methods. Protocols shall contain methods that are credible, practicable, enforceable, and replicable and shall include each of the following elements: (a) A description of the calculation methods used for determining the reductions achieved by the emissions controls as implemented; (b) Estimates of the accuracy of the appropriate USEPA test method, if available, not to exceed some given value; (c) A description of the recordkeeping program that permits verification of production, materials used, and use of control equipment; (d) The USEPA test methods where available; and (e) A requirement for complete, verifiable records on production, materials used and use of control equipment. (f) Quantification uncertainty adjustment factors applied to the calculated amount of DERs to correct for any inaccuracies estimated in (b), above. Source. #6441, EXEMPT, eff 1-21-97

Env-A 1412.02 Petitions to Modify the RTAP List

Effective 2022-02-03.

Env-A 1412.02 Petitions to Modify the RTAP List. (a) If a person wishes to add a substance or compound to, delete a substance or compound from, or modify a specific parameter on the RTAP list, the person shall petition the commissioner pursuant to RSA 541-A:4 and Env-C 207 by providing the information specified in Env-A 1412.03 in writing to the commissioner. (b) Where data limitations exist which prevent the derivation of an AAL, a person intending to use a substance or compound named on the list shall petition the commissioner by providing the information specified in Env-A 1412.03 in writing to the commissioner.

Env-A 3209.05 Account Certificate of Representation

Effective 2026-06-25. Expires 2036-06-25.

Env-A 3209.05 Account Certificate of Representation. (a) The AAR shall be officially designated upon receipt by the NATS administrator of a form entitled “Account Certificate of Representation”, NHDES-A-03-003, June 2026, completed and submitted to the department by mail or hand delivery to: NHDES Air Resources Division Attn: NOx Budget Trading Program Manager 29 Hazen Drive, PO Box 95 Concord, NH 03302 (b) The account certificate of representation shall be signed by the AAR and the alternate AAR, if applicable. (c) The account certificate of representation for each compliance account shall include the following statement, verbatim: “I certify that I was selected as the AAR or alternate AAR, as applicable, by an agreement binding on the owners and operators of the NOx budget source and each NOx budget unit at the source. I certify that I have all the necessary authority to carry out my duties and responsibilities under the NOx Budget Trading Program on behalf of the owners and operators of the NOx budget source and of each NOx budget unit at the source, and that each such owner and operator shall be fully bound by my representations, actions, inactions, or submissions and by any decision or order issued to me by the department, the administrator, or a court regarding the source or unit. I am authorized to make this submission on behalf of the owners and operators of the NOx Budget sources or NOx Budget units for which the submission is made. I certify under penalty of law that I have personally examined, and am familiar with, the statements and information submitted in this document and all its attachments. Based on my inquiry of those individuals with primary responsibility for obtaining the information, I certify that the statements and information are to the best of my knowledge and belief true, accurate, and complete. I am aware that there are significant penalties for submitting false statements and information or omitting required statements and information, including the possibility of fine or imprisonment.” (d) A new AAR shall be designated by submitting a revised “Account Certificate of Representation”, NHDES-A-03-003, to the NATS administrator, with the following information: (1) All of the information pursuant to (b) and (c) above; and (2) The name of the AAR who is being replaced. (e) The NATS administrator shall confirm the change of AAR once the change is recorded in the NATS. Source. #11001, eff 1-1-16; ss by #14632, eff 6-25-26, EXPIRES: 6-25-36

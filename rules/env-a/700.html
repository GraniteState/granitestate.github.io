Env-A 700 PERMIT FEE SYSTEM

CHAPTER Env-A 700 PERMIT FEE SYSTEM Statutory Authority: RSA 125-C:4 Revision Note: Document #5033, eff 12-27-90. readopted with amendments Chapters Env-A 100 through Env-A 1200. Part Env-A 703 entitled "Devices Subject to This Chapter" was repealed by Document #5033, which also renumbered the remaining parts in Chapter 700. The history of prior filings for former Part Env-A 703, which contained only rule Env-A 703.01, is as follows: #1865, eff 1-25-82; ss by #2232, eff 4-29-83; ss by #2938, eff 12-27-84. Former Part Env-A 704 is now Part Env-A 703. Former Sections Env-A 704.01a and Env-A 704.02 are now Env-A 703.02 and their source notes have been combined. Sections Env-A 703.03 and Env-A 703.06 are new with Document #5033. Former Part Env-A 705 is now Part Env-A 704. Former Part Env-A 706 is now Part Env-A 705.

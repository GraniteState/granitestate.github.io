Env-A 1607.01 Fuel Analysis to Demonstrate Compliance

Effective 2023-11-18.

Env-A 1607.01 Fuel Analysis to Demonstrate Compliance. (a) In order to determine compliance, the department shall sample or require sampling or re-sampling of any fuel. (b) Such sampling, which shall include compositing, testing, and analyzing fuel samples, shall be conducted in accordance with the applicable methodology specified in Env-A 800 or 40 CFR 60. Source. #6519-B, eff 5-29-97 (from Env-A 404.01); ss by #8330, eff 4-23-05; ss by #8878, eff 4-27-07; ss by #10415, eff 9-24-13 (formerly Env-A 1610.01); ss by #13808, eff 11-18-23 Appendix A: Statutes Implemented Appendix B: Statutory References RSA 125-C:10-d: 125-C:10-d Sulfur Limits of Certain Liquid Fuels. I. No person shall import into the state any of the following liquid fuels: (a) No. 2 oil, also referred to as distillate oil, with a sulfur content greater than 0.0015 percent by weight; (b) No. 4 oil with a sulfur content greater than 0.25 percent by weight; or (c) Nos. 5 or 6 oil, also referred to as residual oil, with a sulfur content greater than 0.5 percent by weight. II. Beginning on February 1, 2019 and continuing thereafter, no person shall sell, offer for sale, supply, distribute for sale or use, except for fuel remaining in storage for a device not requiring a permit pursuant to RSA 125-C:11, any of the following liquid fuels: (a) No. 2 oil, also referred to as distillate oil, with a sulfur content greater than 0.0015 percent by weight; (b) No. 4 oil with a sulfur content greater than 0.25 percent by weight; or (c) Nos. 5 or 6 oil, also referred to as residual oil, with a sulfur content greater than 0.5 percent by weight.

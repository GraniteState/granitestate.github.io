Env-A 2806.07 Recordkeeping and Reporting

Effective 2018-12-22.

Env-A 2806.07 Recordkeeping and Reporting. (a) The owner or operator of a non-metallic mineral processing plant shall retain records for a minimum of 5 years on: (1) The total quantity of all materials processed on an annual basis; and (2) The results of all performance testing conducted to determine compliance with the standards specified in Env-A 2803, including 40 CFR 60, subpart OOO. (b) The owner or operator of a non-metallic mineral processing plant shall submit to the department the results of any performance test conducted to determine compliance with the standards set forth in Env-A 2803, including 40 CFR 60, subpart OOO, within 60 days of the test. Source. #9793, eff 10-1-10; ss by #12693, eff 12-22-18 Appendix: State Statute(s) and Federal Regulations Implemented

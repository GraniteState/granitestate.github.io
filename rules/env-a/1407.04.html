Env-A 1407.04 Criteria for Classification of Class III Regulated Toxic Air Pollutants

Effective 2022-02-03.

Env-A 1407.04 Criteria for Classification of Class III Regulated Toxic Air Pollutants. As specified in RSA 125-I: 2, XIV(c), the department shall classify a RTAP as a class III regulated toxic air pollutant if it is any RTAP other than a class I or class II RTAP.

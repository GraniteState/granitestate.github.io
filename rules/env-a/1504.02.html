Env-A 1504.02 Applicability

Effective 2020-01-18.

Env-A 1504.02 Applicability. (a) Pursuant to §176(c)(1) of the Act, this part shall apply to any federal department, agency, or instrumentality that meets the applicability criteria as specified in 40 CFR §93.153. (b) This part shall not apply to any entity subject to a conformity determination for a federal action related to a transportation plan, program, or project developed, funded, or approved under 23 USC §§101 et seq. or the Federal Transit Act, as amended, 49 USC §§1601 et seq.

Env-A 1307.03 Emission Standards for Stationary Internal Combustion Engines Subject to New Source Performance Standards

Effective 2018-08-15.

Env-A 1307.03 Emission Standards for Stationary Internal Combustion Engines Subject to New Source Performance Standards. Stationary internal combustion engines that meet the applicability criteria of 40 CFR §60.4200 or §60.4230, shall be subject to the following NOx RACT emission limits: (a) For stationary compression ignition internal combustion engines, compliance with the NOx emission standards for non-emergency engines in 40 CFR §60.4204 and §60.4206 shall constitute compliance with Env-A 1307; and (b) For stationary spark ignition internal combustion engines, compliance with the NOx emission standards listed for non-emergency engines in 40 CFR §60.4233 through §60.4234 shall constitute compliance with Env-A 1307. Source. #12584, eff 8-15-18

Env-A 4603.02 Submission of CO2 Budget Permit Information

Effective 2020-01-01. Earlier expiration 2009-03-30.

Env-A 4603.02 Submission of CO2 Budget Permit Information. For any existing CO2 budget source, the CO2 authorized account representative (CO2 AAR) shall: (a) Submit to the department complete information requested in the notice of permit reopening for cause issued by the department in accordance with Env-A 609.19; and (b) Submit in a timely manner any supplemental information that the department determines is necessary in order to review the CO2 budget permit and issue or deny a CO2 budget permit. Source. #9283, INTERIM, eff 10-1-08, EXPIRED: 3-30-09 New. #9444, eff 4-3-09; ss by #10468, eff 1-1-14; ss by #12967, eff 1-1-20

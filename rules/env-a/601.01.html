Env-A 601.01 Purpose

Effective 2022-12-17. Earlier expiration 2011-10-24.

Env-A 601.01 Purpose. The purpose of this chapter is to implement the permitting requirements of RSA 125-C and RSA 125-I to regulate the operation and modification of new and existing stationary sources, area sources, and devices in order to achieve and maintain: (a) The ambient air quality standards established pursuant to RSA 125-C and Env-A 300; and (b) The ambient air limits for regulated toxic air pollutants established pursuant to RSA 125-I and Env-A 1400.

Env-A 303.03 Measurement for PM Attainment Determinations

Effective 2022-05-25.

Env-A 303.03 Measurement for PM Attainment Determinations. For the purpose of determining attainment of the primary and secondary ambient air quality standards for PM specified in Env-A 303.01, the following shall apply: (a) PM10 in the ambient air shall be measured by either: (1) A reference method based on 40 CFR 50 Appendix J and designated in accordance with 40 CFR 53; or (2) An equivalent method designated in accordance with 40 CFR 53; and (b) PM2.5 in the ambient air shall be measured by either: (1) A reference method based on 40 CFR 50 Appendix L and designated in accordance with 40 CFR 53; or (2) An equivalent method designated in accordance with 40 CFR 53.

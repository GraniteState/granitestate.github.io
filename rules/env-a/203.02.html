Env-A 203.02 Public Participation in Non-Adjudicative Hearings

Effective 2022-11-19. Earlier expiration 2003-06-30.

Env-A 203.02 Public Participation in Non-Adjudicative Hearings. Any person may participate in a non-adjudicative hearing while the hearing record is open by: (a) Testifying orally; (b) Submitting written testimony pursuant to Env-C 205.06; (c) Submitting written or oral questions through the presiding officer; or (d) Any combination of (a) through (c), above.

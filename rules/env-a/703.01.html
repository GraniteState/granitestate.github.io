Env-A 703.01 Definitions

Effective 2018-10-24.

Env-A 703.01 Definitions. For the purposes of this section, the following definitions shall apply: (a) “Hourly rate” means the annual salary and benefit costs for each department employee who reviews a permit, divided by 1,950; (b) “Individual personnel review fee” means, for each department employee who reviews the permit, the sum of travel costs, public notification costs, and the product of the number of hours or portion thereof which that person spent working on the particular permit, multiplied by the hourly rate of that department employee; and (c) “Working on the particular permit” means reviewing the permit to ensure compliance with the standards integrated in the reviews listed in Env-A 702.02 and processing or amending any permit, necessitated as a result of the review.

Effective 2022-12-17.

Env-A 601.02 Applicability. This chapter shall apply to each stationary source, area source, and device that is required by RSA 125-C:11 to possess a temporary permit or operating permit, whether a permit-by-notification, general permit, or individual operating permit, prior to construction, installation, operation, or modification of such source or device.

Env-A 3304.01 Operating Practices for Large MWC Units

Effective 2018-09-27.

Env-A 3304.01 Operating Practices for Large MWC Units. The operating practices applicable to each large MWC unit shall be as specified in 40 CFR §60.53b(b) and (c). Source. #6518-B, eff 5-29-97; ss by #7704, eff 6-7-02; ss by #9084, eff 2-2-08; ss by #11131, eff 7-18-16; ss by #12628, eff 9-27-18

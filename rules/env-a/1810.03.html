Env-A 1810.03 Asbestos-Containing Cementitious Roofing or Siding Materials

Effective 2024-11-01. Expires 2034-11-01.

Env-A 1810.03 Asbestos-Containing Cementitious Roofing or Siding Materials. The owner or operator who removes cementitious ACM from the side or roof of a building and does not sand, saw, grind, abrade, or otherwise treat the ACM in a manner that might create a fine dust or particles, shall comply with the following: (a) The asbestos abatement entity and workers shall be licensed and certified in accordance with Env-A 1815 and Env-A 1816; (b) The surrounding ground shall first be protected with poly drop cloths such that no ACM can come into contact with the ground; (c) Cementitious ACM shall be removed intact, to the extent possible, by prying out or cutting fasteners; (d) Cementitious ACM shall be carefully lowered to the ground, not dropped, thrown, or slid; (e) All ACM waste shall be removed, packaged, labelled, and disposed of in accordance with Env-A 1812.01(b), Env-A 1812.01(c), Env-A 1812.01(g), Env-A 1812.02 and Env-A 1812.03; and (f) All site preparation requirements specified in Env-A 1808.03(a) shall be completed.

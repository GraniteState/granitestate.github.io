Alc 312.06 Clinical Supervision Training Form

Effective 2026-07-14.

Alc 312.06 Clinical Supervision Training Form. The report form documenting hours of training in clinical supervision shall require: (a) The date of the training; (b) The title of the training; (c) A description of the training course and goals; (d) The sponsor and presenter of the training; (e) The number of contact hours included in the training; (f) From the list outlined in Alc 316.04(a), the topic area addressed by the training; and (g) Attached as extra sheets, copies of certificates and other supporting documentation verifying that such training has been completed. Source. #12001, eff 10-13-16; renumbered by #14584, eff 7-14-26 (formerly Alc 316.06)

Alc 404.01 Required Supervision of CRSWs

Effective 2024-10-07.

Alc 404.01 Required Supervision of CRSWs. A CRSW shall be supervised: (a) By one or more supervisors meeting one of the descriptions set forth in Alc 404.02; and (b) For at least 3 nonconsecutive hours per month while actively practicing. Source. #12548, eff 7-1-18; ss by #14045, eff 10-7-24

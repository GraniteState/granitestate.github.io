Ed 603.02 Candidate Advising

Effective 2023-09-15.

Ed 603.02 Candidate Advising. (a) The program shall be designed to ensure that a PEPP assesses how candidates are advised and counseled throughout the program. (b) The program shall include an advisory process that ensures each candidate: (1) Understands the criteria for successfully meeting the requirements of the licensure program; (2) Receives support in meeting the required criteria; and (3) When necessary, is counseled out of educator preparation and advised into exploration of other career options and programs of study.

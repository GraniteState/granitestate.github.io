Ed 315.01 Statement of Purpose

Effective 2023-09-15. Earlier expiration 2004-10-30.

Ed 315.01 Statement of Purpose. The purpose of Ed 315 is to provide a process for uniform application of the requirements of RSA 193-A. These rules take into account the fact that home education is an alternative to attendance at a public or nonpublic school and is an individualized form of instruction in accordance with chapter 279:2, laws of 1990.

Ed 1129.21 The Joint Rate Setting Process

Effective 2017-03-24.

Ed 1129.21 The Joint Rate Setting Process. (a) The department shall jointly administer the joint rate setting process with the department of health and human services pursuant to the requirements of Ed 1129.21, Ed 1129.22 and Ed 1129.23. (b) When rates are jointly set by the department of health and human services and the department in accordance with RSA 170-G:4, XVII and XVII-a, any conflicts identified by the state agencies existing between rate setting rules of the department of health and human services and the department shall be resolved by both state agencies applying the stricter of the 2 sets of rules.

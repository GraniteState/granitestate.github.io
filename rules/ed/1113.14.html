Ed 1113.14 The Length of the School Year

Effective 2023-10-12.

Ed 1113.14 The Length of the School Year. (a) The length of the school year for preschool children with disabilities shall be determined by the IEP team and included in the IEP or IFSP as required by Ed 1109.01(a). (b) The LEA shall provide a standard school year for children ages 6 – 22 as required by RSA 186-C:15, I, RSA 189:1, RSA 189:24, and Ed 306.18-Ed 306.21. (c) Children with disabilities ages 3 - 22 in need of extended school year services shall receive extended school year services in accordance with Ed 1110. Source.

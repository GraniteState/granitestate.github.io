Ed 1100 STANDARDS FOR THE EDUCATION OF CHILDREN WITH DISABILITIES

CHAPTER Ed 1100 STANDARDS FOR THE EDUCATION OF CHILDREN WITH DISABILITIES Revision Note: Document #9197, effective 6-28-08, readopted with amendments Chapter Ed 1100. Document #9197 made extensive changes to the wording, format, structure, and numbering of rules in Chapter Ed 1100, with the former Part Ed 1101 through Part Ed 1137 renumbered as Part Ed 1101 through Part 1129. Document #9197 replaces all prior filings for rules in Chapter Ed 1100.

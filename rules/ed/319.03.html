Ed 319.03 Open Enrollment School Requirements

Effective 2015-07-01.

Ed 319.03 Open Enrollment School Requirements. The school board or moderator of a school district whose members have voted to designate one or more of its schools as an open enrollment school under RSA 194-D shall notify the state board in writing of the following no later than 60 days prior to the start of each school year: (a) Which schools in the school district have been formally designated as open enrollment schools; (b) How many students each open enrollment school has accepted from within its district and from outside its district; (c) A policy on transportation which is consistent with the provisions of RSA 194-D:2, IX; (d) All policies established under RSA 194-D:4, V; and (e) A statement describing how the school shall meet the requirements of a public school as required by RSA 194-D:2 through RSA 194-D:4, I. Source. (See Revision Note at part heading for Ed 318) #10873, eff 7-1-15

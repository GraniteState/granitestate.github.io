Ed 207.02 Representatives

Effective 2005-04-23. Earlier expiration 2004-10-05.

Ed 207.02 Representatives. (a) Individuals or public or corporate bodies may appoint a representative who is either an attorney-at-law licensed in New Hampshire or is a person knowledgeable about the subject matter of the appeal to represent him or her. (b) In the case of a party being represented by an attorney, all correspondence relative to the case at issue shall be forwarded to the attorney rather than directly to the party the attorney represents. (c) An attorney representing a party shall file an appearance with the hearing officer announcing the fact of representation at the earliest date practical. (d) Parties shall retain counsel at their own expense and requests for appointment of counsel shall not be entertained. Source. #6348, eff 10-5-96, EXPIRED: 10-5-04 New. #8334-A, eff 4-23-05 (from Ed 205.02)

Ed 403.02 Procedures for Evaluation and Determination of the Status of a School for Attendance Purposes

Effective 2024-01-11.

Ed 403.02 Procedures for Evaluation and Determination of the Status of a School for Attendance Purposes. (a) Upon receipt of the required written materials and reports from schools seeking approval, the department shall review the materials received and advise the commissioner whether the schools satisfy the criteria outlined in Ed 403.01. (b) Incomplete applications shall be returned to the nonpublic school for completion. (c) The commissioner shall make a recommendation to the state board based on whether the criteria outlined in Ed 403.01 has been met. (d) The state board, after reviewing the recommendation(s) from the commissioner, shall within 45 business days of receipt of such recommendation(s), take any one of the actions described in Ed 401.02(e). (e) A nonpublic school recommended for denied approval shall have 10 business days to make a request to the commissioner for a grievance hearing before the nonpublic school advisory council grievance committee in accordance with the provisions of Ed 408.07. Source. #13853, eff 1-11-24 (see Revision Note at chapter heading for Ed 400)

Ed 607.02 General Education Content Requirements

Effective 2023-09-16. Earlier expiration 2019-12-17.

Ed 607.02 General Education Content Requirements. (a) Each institution that provides a PEPP shall ensure that each candidate has completed a program of general studies as part of a bachelor’s degree. (b) The general education program shall include the core competencies outlined in Ed 505.02.

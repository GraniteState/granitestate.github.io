Ed 1302.02 Responsibility of School Boards for Receiving Board

Effective 2018-07-14.

Ed 1302.02 Responsibility of School Boards for Receiving Board. (a) The receiving board, in accepting designation as a regional career and technical education center, shall agree to fully cooperate with the education personnel and citizens of the school districts within their region in the planning, designation, and implementation of the career and technical education programs and facilities of the regional career and technical education center. (b) The region as defined in Ed 1301.01(e) shall establish a regional advisory committee according to RSA 188-E:4-a, to advise the receiving board on matters related to career and technical education including the planning, designation, and implementation of the career and technical education programs and facilities of the regional career and technical education center. (c) In addition to (a) and (b) above, the process for constructing a new regional career and technical education center shall follow the requirements in Ed 1303. Source.

Ed 321.14 School Building Aid Application Submittal

Effective 2025-03-14.

Ed 321.14 School Building Aid Application Submittal. (a) To be considered for building aid, completed applications shall be filed with the department no later than April 1, in accordance with RSA 198:15-c, I(b). (b) The application submitted shall include the information identified in Ed 321.15. (c) Hard copies of the application and attachments shall be submitted upon request of the department. (d) The department shall notify the applicant within 30 days of receipt of an application that either: (1) The application is complete and approved for project ranking; or (2) The application is incomplete and is not approved for project ranking.

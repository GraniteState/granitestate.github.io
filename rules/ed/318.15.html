Ed 318.15 Revocation and Withdrawal

Effective 2015-07-01.

Ed 318.15 Revocation and Withdrawal. (a) A charter shall be revoked for any of the reasons listed in RSA 194-B:16, II, or if the board of trustees fails to cooperate in the development of a remedial plan under RSA 194-B:16, III. If the commissioner makes a recommendation to revoke a charter, it shall give the trustees at least 90 days notice of its intent. Revocation shall occur only after notice and opportunity for a hearing as provided under RSA 541-A:31. The hearing shall be conducted in accordance with Ed 200. (b) A charter shall be withdrawn if the board of trustees fails to submit a progress report under RSA 194-B:3, IX. (c) The charter shall be withdrawn if a progress report is filed but fails to: (1) Specify which provisions of RSA 194-B are delaying the opening of the charter school; or (2) Provide a remedial plan for the school to overcome any obstacles. Source. (See Revision Note at part heading for Ed 318) #10873, eff 7-1-15; ss by #13253, eff 8-18-2121 (formerly Ed 318.14)

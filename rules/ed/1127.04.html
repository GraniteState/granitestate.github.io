Ed 1127.04 Criteria for State Payments

Effective 2017-03-24.

Ed 1127.04 Criteria for State Payments. In order for a provider to receive payments from the state, the financially liable school district shall enter cost information into NHSEIS and submit invoices to the department. Source. (see Revision Note at chapter heading for Ed 1100) #9197, eff 6-28-08; ss by #12141, eff 3-24-17

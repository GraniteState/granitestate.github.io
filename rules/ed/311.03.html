Ed 311.03 Physical Examination of Students

Effective 2025-02-21. Earlier expiration 2005-05-17.

Ed 311.03 Physical Examination of Students. (a) A child shall have a complete physical examination prior to or upon school enrollment, pursuant to RSA 200:32. (b) In order for the physical examination to be accepted for school enrollment, the child’s parents or guardian shall provide documentation that shows: (1) The examination has been performed within the past year; or (2) If an examination has not been performed within the past year, the school shall accept documentation of an appointment for a physical examination with a licensed physician, advanced registered nurse practitioner, or physician's assistant within a time determined by the school district for conditional enrollment. (c) School districts shall determine thereafter the frequency of required physical examinations, documentation of exams, or both.

Ed 318.20 Assurances Against Discrimination

Effective 2015-07-01.

Ed 318.20 Assurances Against Discrimination. (a) If any of the persons or entities listed under RSA 194-B:15 and RSA 194-B:16, I, believes a charter school has violated any of the anti-discrimination provisions of the school's own charter, the matter shall, for a charter school approved under the: (1) Local school board process, be directed to the school board for review and disposition; and (2) State board process, be directed to the state board for review and disposition pursuant to Ed 200. (b) A decision of the local school board may be appealed to the state board, in accordance with Ed 200. (c) A decision of the state board may be appealed in accordance with Ed 213. Source. (See Revision Note at part heading for Ed 318) #10873, eff 7-1-15; ss by #13253, eff 8-18-2121 (formerly Ed 318.19)

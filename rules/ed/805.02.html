Ed 805.02 Enrollment

Effective 2022-02-22.

Ed 805.02 Enrollment. (a) The scholarship organization shall approve applications, as described in Ed 805.01, in accordance with RSA 194-F:3, III. (b) The scholarship organization shall accept rolling admissions into the program. (c) Within 30 days of receipt of a completed student application, the scholarship organization shall confirm with the parent or guardian in writing that the application is complete in accordance with the approval criteria set forth in RSA 194-F:3. (d) If the scholarship organization receives an incomplete application, the applicant shall be notified in writing by the scholarship organization within 15 days of receipt which shall include instructions for completing the application.

He-E 700 ADULT PROTECTION SERVICES PROGRAM

CHAPTER He-E 700 ADULT PROTECTION SERVICES PROGRAM Statutory Authority: RSA 161-F:55 REVISION NOTE: Document #10179, effective 9-1-12, readopted with amendments Chapter He-E 700, formerly entitled “Adult Protection Program” and containing Part He-E 701 through Part He-E 709. Document #10179 made extensive changes to the wording, format, structure, and numbering of those provisions, creating a single Part He-E 701 in Chapter He-E 700, now entitled “Adult Protective Services Program.” Document #10179 supersedes all prior filings affecting the provisions in the former Chapter He-E 700. The prior filings affecting the former Chapter He-E 700 include the following documents: #5008, eff 11-27-90, EXPIRED 11-27-96 #6867, eff 10-9-98 #8727, INTERIM, eff 10-9-06, EXPIRED 4-7-07 #8868, eff 4-19-07

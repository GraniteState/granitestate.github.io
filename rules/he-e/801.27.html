He-E 801.27 Respite Care Services

Effective 2022-01-29. Earlier expiration 2020-02-03.

He-E 801.27 Respite Care Services. (a) Respite care services shall be a covered service when provided by or in one of the following settings: (1) A medicaid-enrolled nursing facility, licensed in accordance with RSA 151:2 and He-P 813; (2) A medicaid-enrolled residential care facility licensed in accordance with RSA 151:2 and He-P 804 or He-P 805; or (3) In the participant’s own residence, by: a. Home health care providers licensed in accordance with RSA 151:2 and He-P 809; b. Home care service providers licensed in accordance with RSA 151:2 and He-P 822; or c. Other qualified agencies certified in accordance with RSA 161-I and He-P 601. (b) Respite care services shall be: (1) Provided to the participant on a short-term basis, as described in (2) below, because of the temporary absence or need for relief of those persons normally providing that participant’s care; and (2) Limited to 30 24-hour days of care per state fiscal year.

He-E 805.03 Eligibility

Effective 2017-02-22. Earlier expiration 2017-02-21.

He-E 805.03 Eligibility. (a) Targeted case management services shall be provided to all participants, except those excluded pursuant to the Laws of 2007, Chapter 263:108. (b) Targeted case management services shall be available to participants who reside in hospitals or nursing facilities licensed in accordance with RSA 151, provided that such services: (1) Do not exceed a total of 30 cumulative days of services provided prior to discharge to home from an aforementioned facility or combination of facilities; and (2) Do not duplicate discharge planning services that the facility is normally expected to provide as part of inpatient services. (c) Notwithstanding (a) above, the commissioner of the department shall grant waivers to allow case management services to be provided to the excluded beneficiaries in (a) above as necessary to protect their health and safety.

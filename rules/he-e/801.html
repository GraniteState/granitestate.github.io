He-E 801 CHOICES FOR INDEPENDENCE PROGRAM

PART He-E 801 CHOICES FOR INDEPENDENCE PROGRAM REVISION NOTE: Document #9969, effective 8-8-11, adopted, readopted with amendments and renumbered, and repealed the rules in Part He-E 801, formerly entitled “Home and Community-Based Care for the Elderly and Chronically Ill”, and now entitled “Choices for Independence Program.” Document #9969 replaces all prior filings for rules in the former He-E 801. The filings affecting the former He-E 801 include the following documents: #7488, eff 5-8-01 #7655, eff 2-28-02 #7823, eff 2-8-03 #9326, eff 11-21-08 #9858, INTERIM, eff 2-8-11

Gcd 103.05 Quorum

Effective 2019-09-20.

Gcd 103.05 Quorum. A quorum shall consist of a simple majority of members appointed, including participants by speakerphone or other telecommunication device if such means of attendance is required as an accommodation for a disability. Source. #7634, eff 1-30-02; ss by #10338, eff 5-17-13; ss by #12867, eff 9-20-19

Gcd 403.06 Death or Retirement of the Animal

Effective 2023-11-23.

Gcd 403.06 Death or Retirement of the Animal. Upon the death or retirement of a service animal, the owner or person in possession of the service animal tag shall immediately return the tag to the commission. Source. #10341, eff 5-17-13; ss by #13811, eff 11-23-23 APPENDIX

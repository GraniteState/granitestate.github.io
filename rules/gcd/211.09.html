Gcd 211.09 Filing and Content of Motion for Rehearing

Effective 2013-05-17.

Gcd 211.09 Filing and Content of Motion for Rehearing. (a) A motion for rehearing shall be submitted to the commission within 30 days from the date of the final decision of the commission. (b) A motion for rehearing shall: (1) Identify each error of fact, error of reasoning, or error of law which the moving party wishes to have considered by the commission; and (2) Describe how each identified error caused the commission's decision to be: a. Illegal in respect to jurisdiction, authority of the commission or observance of the law; b. An abuse of discretion; or c. Arbitrary, unreasonable or capricious; (3) State concisely the factual findings, reasoning and legal conclusions proposed by the proponent of the motion; and (4) Include any argument or memorandum of law the proponent of the motion wishes to have considered. Source. #7635-A, eff 1-30-02; ss by #10339, eff 5-17-13

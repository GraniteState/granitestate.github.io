Env-Or 607.03 Groundwater Management Permit Application

Effective 2025-11-04. Expires 2035-11-04.

Env-Or 607.03 Groundwater Management Permit Application. (a) The responsible party, as applicant for a groundwater management permit, shall complete and submit to the department the form entitled “Application for Groundwater Management Permit”, NHDES-S-02-004, 11/2025. (b) The groundwater management permit application shall be dated, signed, and sealed by the professional of record. (c) The applicant shall sign the application form. Such signature shall constitute certification that all information provided on or with the form or that otherwise is submitted by the applicant as part of the application is true, complete, and not misleading to the knowledge and belief of the signer.

Env-Or 615.13 Fines Relating to Site Investigations

Effective 2025-11-04. Expires 2035-11-04.

Env-Or 615.13 Fines Relating to Site Investigations. (a) For failing to perform a site investigation after the department request in accordance with Env-Or 606.01(a), the proposed fine shall be $4,000 per violation. (b) For failing to submit a site investigation report in accordance with Env-Or 606.03(a), the proposed fine shall be $4,000 per violation. Source. #14419, eff 11-4-25, EXPIRES: 11-4-35

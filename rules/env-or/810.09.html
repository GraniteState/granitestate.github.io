Env-Or 810.09 Certificate of No Further Action

Effective 2025-11-04. Expires 2035-11-04.

Env-Or 810.09 Certificate of No Further Action. Upon meeting the criteria for issuing a certificate of no further action specified in Env-Or 609.02, the department shall issue to the program participant a certificate of no further action as described in RSA 147-F:13, V.

Env-Or 603.02 Exemptions to Groundwater Quality Criteria

Effective 2025-11-04. Expires 2035-11-04.

Env-Or 603.02 Exemptions to Groundwater Quality Criteria. Groundwater quality shall be exempt from the groundwater quality criteria of Env-Or 603.01 if: (a) The groundwater is within a groundwater discharge zone permitted in accordance with Env-Wq 402; (b) The groundwater is within a groundwater management zone permitted in accordance with Env-Or 607; (c) The groundwater is contaminated solely with salt and other de-icing chemicals applied for winter road maintenance; (d) The groundwater contamination resulted from backwash from a public water treatment facility that is subject to Env-Wq 402; or (e) The groundwater contamination is not the result of human operations or activities.

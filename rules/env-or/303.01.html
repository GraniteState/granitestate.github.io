Env-Or 303.01 Availability and Applicability of Reference Standards

Effective 2024-10-04.

Env-Or 303.01 Availability and Applicability of Reference Standards. (a) The department shall have at least one copy of each reference standard identified in this part available for inspection by the public at its offices at 29 Hazen Drive, Concord, NH. (b) The reference standards also may be obtained directly from the source, as listed in Env-Or 303.02 through Env-Or 303.06 and Appendix B. (c) In the event that any of the applicable reference standards conflict with these rules or with each other, the most stringent requirement shall apply. Source.

Env-Or 703.09 Action on Applications

Effective 2025-11-04. Expires 2035-11-04.

Env-Or 703.09 Action on Applications. (a) Within 60 days from the receipt of a complete permit application, the department shall determine whether: (1) The requirements specified in Env-Or 703.02 through Env-Or 703.08 have been met; and (2) The proposed release detection monitoring program required by Env-Or 703.05(c) appears adequate to detect any releases. (b) If the requirements identified in (a)(1), above, have been met and the proposed release detection monitoring program required by Env-Or 703.05(c) appears adequate to detect any releases, the department shall issue a permit for a period of 5 years, subject to renewal pursuant to Env-Or 703.11. (c) The department shall notify the applicant of its decision in writing. If the decision is to deny the application, the written notice shall specify the reason(s) for the denial.

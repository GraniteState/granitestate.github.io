Env-Or 402.36 Non-public water system

Effective 2013-09-01.

Env-Or 402.36 “Non-public water system” means a water system that is not a public water system as defined in RSA 485:1-a, XV, reprinted in Appendix C. The term includes all systems designed and intended to provide piped water, regardless of whether the water is used for human consumption, irrigation, or any other purpose. The term does not include a system that provides piped water in a closed loop or that otherwise is configured such that it is impossible for the water to be used on crops or consumed by humans or domestic animals. Source. (See Revision Note #1 at chapter heading for Env-Or 400) #10393, eff 9-1-13

Env-Or 802.05 Covenant not to sue

Effective 2025-11-04. Expires 2035-11-04.

Env-Or 802.05 “Covenant not to sue” means a document issued by the New Hampshire department of justice pursuant to RSA 147-F:6 that restricts the state’s right to sue eligible persons for contamination addressed by an approved remedial action plan. Source. #8809, eff 1-26-07; ss by #10833, eff 6-1-15; ss by #14421, eff 11-4-25, EXPIRES: 11-4-35

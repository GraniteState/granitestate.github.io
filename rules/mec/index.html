Mec Mechanical Licensing Board

182 sections, 47 parts, 8 chapters.

Mechanical Licensing Board (formerly Saf-Mec). (See 2021, 91:82, effective 7-1-21, which amended RSA 153:27-a. The Mechanical Licensing Board was transferred from being a unit within the Division of Fire Safety of the Department of Safety to a unit within the Office of Professional Licensure and Certification. The rules numbered “Saf-Mec” will eventually be renumbered as " Mec "). Med Board of Medicine

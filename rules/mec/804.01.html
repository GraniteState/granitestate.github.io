Mec 804.01 Initial Process for Individuals Seeking Initial Certification as a Water Treatment Technician or Water Treatment Trainee

Effective 2023-07-19.

Mec 804.01 Initial Process for Individuals Seeking Initial Certification as a Water Treatment Technician or Water Treatment Trainee. (a) Each individual seeking certification as a water treatment technician or water treatment trainee shall so do by submitting the following application and supporting documents: (1) A completed “Application for Voluntary Certification of Water Treatment Technician or Trainee” revised 9/2022. (2) The documents specified by Mec 803.03; and (3) The certification fee specified in Mec 806. (b) If the board, after receiving and reviewing the completed application and supporting documents requires further information or documents to determine the applicant’s qualification for certification, the board shall: (1) Notify the applicant in writing within 30 days; and (2) Specify the information or documents it requires. (c) The board shall deny the application if the applicant does not submit the additional information or documents requested under (b) above within 60 days of the request. (d) The board shall approve or deny the application within 60 days of the date that the board’s office receives the completed application, application fee, supporting documents, and any additional information requested. (e) If the board approves the application a certification shall be issued that expires on the last day of the month 2 years from the month of issuance. (f) If the board denies the application, it shall provide the applicant, in writing, the reason for the denial. (g) The board shall deny an application-based misconduct described in Mec 604.01. (h) Any applicant wishing to challenge the board's denial of an application for certification shall: (1) Make a written request for a hearing of the applicant's challenge; and (2) Submit this request to the board: a. Within 60 days of the board’s notification of denial; or b. If the applicant is on active military duty outside the United States, within 60 days of the applicant’s return to the United States or release from duty, whichever occurs later. Source. #13632, eff 7-19-23

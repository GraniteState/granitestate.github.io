Mec 103.02 Meetings of the Board

Effective 2025-10-17.

Mec 103.02 Meetings of the Board. (a) The board shall elect a chair and a vice chair from among its members. (b) The chair shall preside over all meetings of the board. (c) In the absence of the chair at a meeting of the board, the vice chair shall preside. (d) Notice of all meetings shall be given in such a manner as in accordance with New Hampshire RSA 91-A:2 and RSA 310:5. (e) Meetings of the board shall be open to the public to the extent required by RSA 91-A and notice of board meetings shall be posted pursuant to RSA 91-A:2 and RSA 310:5. (f) The annual meeting of the board shall be the first regular meeting held in each state fiscal year. (g) The office of professional licensure and certification shall provide the board on a quarterly basis an accounting from the agency of moneys derived under the provisions of RSA 153:27 through RSA 153:36. Source. #10798, eff 3-18-15; ss by #13483, eff 1-9-23 (formerly Saf-Mec 103.02) (see Revision Note at chapter heading for Mec 100); #14408, eff 10-17-25

Mec 403.02 Required Supporting Materials

Effective 2026-02-13. Expires 2036-02-13.

Mec 403.02 Required Supporting Materials. Individual applicants for renewal licensure shall provide, or arrange for the OPLC to receive, the following documents supporting their applications: (a) Proof of continuing education supporting materials for each continuing education course taken within the licensing period as described in Mec 404; (b) For fuel gas trainees’, proof of enrollment in an approved board sanctioned training program for fuel gas fitting licensure described in Mec 308; and (c) For plumbing apprentice, proof of enrollment as described in Mec 304.04(c), in a United States Department of Labor approved apprenticeship program for plumbing or apprenticeship. Source. #10848, eff 7-1-15; ss by #13771, eff 12-10-23 (formerly Saf-Mec 403.04) (see Revision Note at chapter heading for Mec 400); ss by #14446, eff 2-13-26, EXPIRES: 2-13-36 (formerly Mec 403.03)

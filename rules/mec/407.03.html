Mec 407.03 Application Process for Reinstatement of Lapsed Mechanical Licenses for Business Entities

Effective 2023-12-10.

Mec 407.03 Application Process for Reinstatement of Lapsed Mechanical Licenses for Business Entities. (a) Each corporation, limited liability company, or partnership seeking reinstatement of a lapsed mechanical license to practice fuel gas fitting or plumbing shall do so by submitting the following application packet: (1) A “Universal Application for License Renewal” form provided by the board that meets the requirements of (b) below; and (2) The fee set forth in Plc 1002.25. (b) The business entity shall submit the information described in Plc 308.06 on the “Universal Application for License Renewal”. (c) In addition to the information required by (b) above the business entity shall provide the below requested information on, and documentation with, the “Addendum to the Renewal/Reinstatement Application for Businesses Described in Plc 308.06”: (1) Identification of the business; (2) Identification of the individual who is the corporate officer or responsible managing employee, as applicable, who currently holds a New Hampshire fuel gas fitting license, master plumber license, domestic appliance technician license, or hearth system installer license specific to the discipline for which licensure is sought; (3) A list of all licensed employees of the business entity, including name and license number, who perform fuel gas fitting or plumbing work; (4) A detailed account of the circumstances relating to any court judgments against the business entity if any; and (5) A detailed account of the circumstances relating to any criminal conviction of the business entity if any; (6) Attach to the application proof of current liability insurance; and (7) Attach to the application a letter of good standing from the New Hampshire secretary of state’s office; and (d) The application shall be dated and signed by at least one corporate officer or responsible managing employee, as applicable, who currently holds a New Hampshire fuel gas fitting license, master plumber license, domestic appliance technician license, or hearth system installer license specific to the discipline for which licensure is sought in accordance with Plc 308.08. (e) The application for reinstatement of a business entity license shall be processed in accordance with Plc 308.09 through Plc 308.12.

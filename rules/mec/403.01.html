Mec 403.01 Renewal Application Process for Individuals Seeking Plumbing and Fuel Gas Fitting Licenses and Trainee or Apprentice Licenses

Effective 2026-02-13. Expires 2036-02-13.

Mec 403.01 Renewal Application Process for Individuals Seeking Plumbing and Fuel Gas Fitting Licenses and Trainee or Apprentice Licenses. (a) Each individual seeking a renewal license to practice as a domestic appliance technician, a fuel gas fitter, a master, or journeyman plumber, or seeking to renew as a fuel gas trainee or plumbing apprentice, shall do so by submitting the following: (1) A completed “Universal Application for License Renewal” with the information described in Plc 308.05; (2) A completed “Addendum to the Universal Application for License Renewal for Mechanical Licensing”, revised 12/2025, affirming either of the following: a. If I am applying to renew a plumbing apprentice or fuel gas fitting trainee, I have provided the proof described in Mec 304.04(c) of continued enrollment in an educational program or apprenticeship; or b. If I am a holder of any other type of mechanical license issued pursuant to RSA 153:27-36, I have provided proof of the continuing education required in Mec 403.03 for each licensing endorsement applied for; and (3) The license fee required by Plc 1002.25 which is applicable to the type of license sought. (b) The application for renewal shall be processed in accordance with Plc 308.09 through Plc 308.12. (c) An applicant who wishes to challenge the denial of an application for renewal licensure made pursuant to Plc 308.11 shall follow the process required in Plc 308.12 and file a “Petition for Hearing After Initial Denial of Licensure.” Source. #10848, eff 7-1-15; ss by #13771, eff 12-10-23 (formerly Saf-Mec 403.01) (see Revision Note at chapter heading for Mec 400); ss by #14446, eff 2-13-26, EXPIRES: 2-13-36

Mec 104.01 Office Location; Mailing Address; Telephone, and TTY Numbers

Effective 2025-10-17.

Mec 104.01 Office Location; Mailing Address; Telephone, and TTY Numbers. (a) The board's mailing address is: New Hampshire Mechanical Licensing Board c/o Office of Professional Licensure and Certification 7 Eagle Square Concord, New Hampshire 03301 (b) The board’s office location is: New Hampshire Mechanical Licensing Board Office of Professional Licensure and Certification 7 Eagle Square Concord NH 03301 (c) The board’s telephone number is 603-271-2152. (d) Access for in-state TTY/TDD users shall be through relay New Hampshire by dialing 711 or by dialing 1-800-735-2964. (e) Correspondence, filings, and other communications intended for the board shall be addressed to “New Hampshire Mechanical Licensing Board" at the address stated in (a) above. (f) Information may be obtained from the board by calling the telephone numbers stated in paragraphs (c) or (d) or by written correspondence to the address in (a). Source. #10798, eff 3-18-15; ss by #13483, eff 1-9-23 (formerly Saf-Mec 104.01) (see Revision Note at chapter heading for Mec 100); #14408, eff 10-17-25

Mec 307.02 Required Supporting Materials

Effective 2023-12-10. Earlier expiration 2015-07-20.

Mec 307.02 Required Supporting Materials. Business applicants for licensure shall provide the following documents supporting their applications: (a) Proof of good standing with the secretary of state; (b) Poof of liability insurance; and (c) A List of all current licensed employees that includes: (1) Full name as listed on their license; and (2) N.H. license number.

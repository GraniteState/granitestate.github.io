Mec 102.13 Fuel gas service technician

Effective 2025-10-17.

Mec 102.13 “Fuel gas service technician” means “fuel gas service technician” as defined in RSA 153:27, VII, namely, “any person engaged in the servicing and repair of inside and outside piping from the outlet of the gas meter or first stage regulator or residential and non-residential fuel gas appliances and equipment, including but not limited to heating equipment systems or water heating systems using liquefied propane gas or natural gas.” Source. #10798, eff 3-18-15; ss by #13483, eff 1-9-23 (formerly Saf-Mec 102.08) (see Revision Note at chapter heading for Mec 100); #14408, eff 10-17-25

Authority

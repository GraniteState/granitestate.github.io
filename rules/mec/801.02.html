Mec 801.02 Scope

Effective 2023-07-19.

Mec 801.02 Scope. (a) These rules shall establish voluntary water treatment certification program for the following: (1) Individuals involved in the installation of residential and non-residential water treatment systems; and (2) Individuals involved in the servicing and repair of water treatment systems. (b) No person shall hold themselves out as a certified water treatment technician unless the certification was obtained pursuant to these rules. (c) No person less than 18 years of age shall be issued a certificate pursuant to these rules. Source. #13632, eff 7-19-23

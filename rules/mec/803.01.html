Mec 803.01 Eligibility Requirements for Certification as a Water Treatment Technician

Effective 2023-07-19.

Mec 803.01 Eligibility Requirements for Certification as a Water Treatment Technician. The qualification for certification as a water treatment technician shall be: (a) Successful completion of a water treatment technician educational program described in Mec 805; (b) Successful completion of the examination as described in Mec 805; (c) Good character and professional competence as evidence by the documents required by Mec 804.03; (d) Having: (1) Worked as a water treatment technician trainee for at least one year and have taken and passed the examination described in Mec 805; (2) Having operated as a water treatment technician for at least one year as a certified water treatment technician in another state; or (3) Having operated as a water treatment technician for at least one year prior to January 1, 2024. Source. #13632, eff 7-19-23

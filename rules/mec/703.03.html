Mec 703.03 Required Supporting Material

Effective 2023-07-09.

Mec 703.03 Required Supporting Material. (a) If the applicant is seeking initial certification as a heating installer and heating equipment service personnel, they shall provide evidence of 2000 hours of field experience, by submitting either of the following: (1) A certificate showing the applicant successfully completion the NORA Technician Certification program at the silver or gold level which includes a final exam; or (2) Proof of current licensure in good standing from a state or regulatory board of licensure for oil heating service and oil-fired equipment installation. Source. #13631, eff 7-9-23

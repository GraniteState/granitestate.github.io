Emp 308.04 Timely Reporting

Effective 2016-02-10. Earlier expiration 2015-07-08.

Emp 308.04 Timely Reporting. (a) Reports shall be considered timely filed if: (1) Transmitted electronically not later than midnight of the business day immediately following the due date; (2) Postmarked first class mail not later than midnight of the business day immediately following the due date; (3) Received within 4 business days immediately following the due date if mailed making use of an envelope postmarked by a postage meter; (4) Received within 2 business days immediately following the due date if sent by private delivery service; or (5) Received during business hours on or before the business day immediately following the due date if delivered by the employing unit.

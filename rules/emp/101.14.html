Emp 101.14 Temporary work

Effective 2013-05-07. Earlier expiration 2013-04-01.

Emp 101.14 “Temporary work” means work with no definite end date that is expected to last 26 or fewer weeks from the beginning date of such work, or work with a definite end date that is 26 or fewer weeks from the beginning date of such work. Source. #10193, INTERIM, eff 10-1-12, EXPIRED: 4-1-13 New. #10329-A, eff 5-7-13

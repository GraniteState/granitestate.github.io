Rev 1005.03 Records of Retail Prices for All Tobacco Products

Effective 2023-12-01.

Rev 1005.03 Records of Retail Prices for All Tobacco Products. Upon request from the department, wholesalers shall provide, from their retailer invoices, the prices charged to a retailer for all tobacco products. Source. #10536, eff 3-6-14 (from Rev 1005.02); renumbered by #10766 (from Rev 1006.02); ss by #12389, eff 9-27-17 (formerly Rev 1006.05); ss by #13817, eff 12-1-23 (formerly Rev 1006.03) (see Revision Note #2 at chapter heading for Rev 1000)

Rev 102.01 Commissioner

Effective 2015-10-22.

Rev 102.01 Commissioner. (a) The commissioner is: (1) Appointed by the governor, with the consent of the council, in accordance with RSA 21-J:2; (2) Charged with the duties assigned by RSA 21-G:8-9 and RSA 21-J:3; (3) Responsible for appointing all division directors, in accordance with RSA 21-G:8, II, and all assistant directors, in accordance with RSA 21-J:5, IV; and (4) Responsible for nomination of the assistant commissioner, in accordance with RSA 21-J:4. Source. #6027, eff 4-27-95; ss by #10957, eff 10-22-15

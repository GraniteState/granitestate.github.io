Rev 1905.10 Annual Reports

Effective 2024-03-29.

Rev 1905.10 Annual Reports. (a) Form MS-61, “Tax Collector’s Report”, shall be filed by the tax collector to report to the municipality and the department on all tax collection activities of the municipality. (b) On the MS-61, the municipal tax collector shall provide: (1) Name of municipality and year ending; (2) The following amounts regarding liens: a. At the beginning of the year; b. Executed during the year; c. Redeemed during the year; d. Unredeemed liens that were abated; e. Deeded to the municipality; and f. Balance at the end of the year; (3) Uncollected taxes at beginning of year; (4) Taxes committed; (5) Overpayments; (6) Amounts remitted to treasurer; (7) Abatements; (8) Uncollected taxes; and (9) Dated signature of the tax collector. (c) Form MS-61 shall be filed by: (1) March 1 by municipalities reporting on a fiscal year basis pursuant to RSA 31:94; or (2) September 1 by municipalities reporting on an optional fiscal year basis pursuant to RSA 31:94-a. (d) Form MS-61 shall be filed with: (1) New Hampshire Department of Revenue Administration Municipal and Property Division by uploading it to the Municipal Tax Rate Setting Portal (MTRSP); or by (2) Hand delivering it to the department at: Governor Hugh J. Gallen Office Park South 109 Pleasant Street Medical and Surgical Building Concord, NH. Source. #8599, eff 4-6-06; ss by #10582, eff 5-2-14; ss by #13918, eff 3-29-24 (see Revision Note at chapter heading for Rev 1900)

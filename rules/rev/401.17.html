Rev 401.17 Owner

Effective 2016-10-28.

Rev 401.17 “Owner” means a person who owns residential real estate or other property and: (a) Includes a person who has: (1) Placed property in a grantor/revocable trust; (2) Equitable title in property; (3) A life estate in property; and (b) Does not include a person who has placed property in an irrevocable trust or any other legal entity distinct from the person. Source. #12027, eff 10-28-16; renumbered by #12925 (formerly Rev 401.16)

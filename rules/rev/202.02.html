Rev 202.02 Subscription and Veracity of Documents

Effective 2016-09-20. Earlier expiration 2006-05-19.

Rev 202.02 Subscription and Veracity of Documents. (a) All petitions and motions filed with the department shall be signed by the party, or, if the party appears by a representative, by the representative. (b) The signature on the document shall constitute and certify that: (1) The signor has read the document; (2) The signor is authorized to file it; and (3) To the best knowledge, information, and belief of the signor, there is good ground to support it, and that it is not filed for delay.

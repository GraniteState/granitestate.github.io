Rev 703.10 Room Facilitator

Effective 2024-10-29. Expires 2034-10-29.

Rev 703.10 Room Facilitator. (a) The full retail price charged for occupancy shall be subject to tax, including any fee, service, or other charge required to be paid by the occupant to a room facilitator. (b) Amounts paid by an operator to a travel agent as commission or compensation for the travel agent’s services shall not be subject to tax. Source. #13432, eff 8-12-22; ss by #14105, eff 10-29-24, EXPIRES: 10-29-34

Rev 1702.03 Use of Accrual Basis of Accounting

Effective 2022-11-15.

Rev 1702.03 Use of Accrual Basis of Accounting. (a) Municipalities shall use the accrual basis for: (1) Proprietary funds; (2) Non-expendable trust funds; (3) Pension trust funds; and (4) Deferred compensation plan and other employee benefit trust funds. (b) Under the accrual basis transactions shall be classified by fund and for: (1) Revenues recognized in the accounting period in which they are earned and become measurable. (2) Expenses, rather than expenditures, recognized in the period incurred, if measurable; and (3) Balance sheet by balance sheet account.

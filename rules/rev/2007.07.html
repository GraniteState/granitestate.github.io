Rev 2007.07 Form MS-123, Report of Town/City/Village Officials

Effective 2024-09-10.

Rev 2007.07 Form MS-123, Report of Town/City/Village Officials. (a) The MS-123, “Report of Town/City/Village Officials,” is available at the department’s website at https://www.revenue.nh.gov/sites/g/files/ehbemt736/files/documents/ms-123_pro.pdf. (b) The MS-123 shall be certified and submitted utilizing the MTRSP by the village clerk within 20 days after election or appointment pursuant to RSA 41:19.

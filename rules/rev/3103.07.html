Rev 3103.07 Payments of Liabilities

Effective 2010-04-02.

Rev 3103.07 Payments of Liabilities. (a) When a taxpayer has a payment due with any document, and a payer has not withheld the tax, or has not withheld a sufficient amount to fully satisfy the tax liability due the State of New Hampshire, such payment shall be filed with the document unless the tax liability is under one dollar. (b) Taxpayers may make payments using any of the following methods: (1) Check or money order made payable to the state of New Hampshire; or (2) Cash. Source. #9689-B, eff 4-2-10

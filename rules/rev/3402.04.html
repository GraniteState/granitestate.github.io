Rev 3402.04 Municipal Assessing Officials Procedure for Distribution of Form PA-7, “Notice of Intent to Cut Wood or Timber”

Effective 2024-07-16.

Rev 3402.04 Municipal Assessing Officials Procedure for Distribution of Form PA-7, “Notice of Intent to Cut Wood or Timber”. After signing and assigning an operation number to a Form PA-7, the municipal assessing officials shall: (a) Within 5 business days, send a copy of the Form PA-7 to the DRA by: (1) Mail to: New Hampshire Department of Revenue Administration P.O. Box 487 Concord, NH 03302-0487; (2) E-mail at timber@dra.nh.gov; or (3) Fax at (603) 230-5947; and (b) Provide a copy of the signed Form PA-7 to the owner, and upon request to: (1) The logger; (2) The forester; or (3) The person responsible for the cut. Source. #10664, eff 9-4-14; ss by #14026, eff 7-16-24

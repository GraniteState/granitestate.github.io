Rev 1109.03 Organization Code 20: Middle School

Effective 2022-12-09. Earlier expiration 1997-02-21.

Rev 1109.03 Organization Code 20: Middle School. The organization code 20, middle school classification, shall be for a school organization between an elementary school and a high school under rules adopted by the commissioner of education under Ed 306, minimum standards for public school approval.

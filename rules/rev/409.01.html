Rev 409.01 Elderly Exemption

Effective 2024-04-27.

Rev 409.01 Elderly Exemption. (a) An applicant shall not be entitled to an elderly exemption pursuant to RSA 72:39-b unless the applicant meets all the eligibility requirements of this part. (b) The municipal assessing officials shall grant an elderly exemption to an entitled applicant in an amount adopted by the municipality in accordance with RSA 72:39-b and prorated, if applicable in accordance with RSA 72:41. (c) The elderly exemption shall be deducted from the assessed value of an entitled applicant’s residential real estate. Source.

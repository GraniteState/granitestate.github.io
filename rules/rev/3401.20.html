Rev 3401.20 Yield tax

Effective 2024-07-16.

Rev 3401.20 “Yield tax” means the 10% tax assessed upon the stumpage value of wood or timber cut during a tax year pursuant to RSA 79:3. The term includes “timber tax”. Source. #10664, eff 9-4-14; ss by #14026, eff 7-16-24

Rev 803.03 Stating Exemption Claimed on the Deed

Effective 2024-09-10.

Rev 803.03 Stating Exemption Claimed on the Deed. Any instrument to be recorded transferring real estate, or an interest in real estate, for which an exemption, pursuant to RSA 78-B, is claimed shall state the basis for the exemption. Source. #8579, eff 3-08-06; ss by #10665, eff 9-9-14; ss by 14071, eff 9-10-24

Authority

  • RSA 78-B:2, V, VIII, IX, XXI appendix

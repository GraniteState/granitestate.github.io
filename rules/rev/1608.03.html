Rev 1608.03 Amended Tax Returns

Effective 2023-02-07.

Rev 1608.03 Amended Tax Returns. (a) Retailers amending an original communications services tax return shall file a Form DP-135, communications services tax return, and place an “X” in the box next to the word “Amended Return” on the return. (b) Amended communications services tax returns not requesting a refund or credit shall be filed within 3 years of the filing date for the original return. (c) Amended communications services tax returns requesting a refund or credit shall be filed by whichever date is later: (1) Within 3 years of the original due date for the tax; or (2) Within 2 years from the date the tax was paid. (d) The 2-year period from the date the tax was actually paid shall not be applicable to an amended return requesting a refund or credit where the payment of tax, interest, or penalty that was paid was a result of an assessment or a demand for payment.

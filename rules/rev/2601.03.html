Rev 2601.03 Governmental unit

Effective 2017-03-22.

Rev 2601.03 “Governmental unit” means any: (a) State legislative body; (b) State or municipal department; (c) Commission; (d) Board; (e) Institution; (f) Bureau; (g) Agency; (h) Instrumentality; (i) State universities created by statute; (j) County; (k) City; (l) Town; (m) School district; (n) School administrative unit; (o) Village district; or (p) Unincorporated place. Source. #7479, eff 4-24-01; ss by #9443, eff 4-1-09; ss by #12138, eff 3-22-17

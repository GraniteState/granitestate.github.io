Rev 3200 EDUCATION TAX CREDIT PROGRAM

CHAPTER Rev 3200 EDUCATION TAX CREDIT PROGRAM REVISION NOTE: Document #13462, effective 10-13-22, adopted, repealed, readopted, readopted and renumbered, or readopted with amendments all of the rules in Chapter Rev 3200 on the education tax credit program. This required some renumbering of existing rules in Part Rev 3201 as noted in the source notes. Document #13462 adopted Part Rev 3207 titled “Administration” and repealed Rev 3204.02 titled “Form ED-04. Education Tax Credit Scholarship Receipt.” Document #13462 replaces all prior filings affecting Chapter Rev 3200. The only prior filing affecting the repealed Rev 3204.02 was Document #10236, effective 11-28-12.

Rev 401.04 Armed forces

Effective 2024-04-27.

Rev 401.04 “Armed forces” means “armed forces” as defined in RSA 21:50, II, and includes the armed forces of any of the governments associated with the United States in the wars, conflicts or armed conflicts, or combat zones in RSA 72:28.

Effective 2016-10-28.

Rev 420.04 Form PA-35, “Municipal Assessing Officials’ Response to Tax Credits/Exemptions or Tax Deferral Application.” The municipal assessing officials shall: (a) Send Form PA-35 to the applicant on or before July 1: (1) Prior to the date of notice of tax if the applicant timely completed and filed Form PA-29; or (2) Following the date of notice of tax if the applicant timely completed and filed Form PA-30; and (b) On Form PA-35: (1) Notify the applicant of the municipal assessing officials’ decision; and (2) Notify the applicant of the applicant’s appeal rights. Source. #12027, eff 10-28-16; renumbered by #12925 (formerly Rev 419.04)

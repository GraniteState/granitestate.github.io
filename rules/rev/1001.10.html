Rev 1001.10 Reporting period

Effective 2023-12-01.

Rev 1001.10 “Reporting period” means one calendar month, unless otherwise authorized by the department. Source. #4707, eff 11-21-89; ss by #5301, eff 12-26-91; ss by #6662, eff 12-25-97; ss by #8526, eff 12-21-05 (formerly Rev 1001.10); ss by #10536, eff 3-6-14 (from Rev 1001.05); renumbered by #12639 (formerly Rev 1001.07); ss by #13817, eff 12-1-23 (formerly Rev 1001.08) (see Revision Note #2 at chapter heading for Rev 1000)

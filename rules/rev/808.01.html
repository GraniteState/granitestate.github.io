Rev 808.01 Taxpayer Records and Information

Effective 2024-09-10. Earlier expiration 2006-02-21.

Rev 808.01 Taxpayer Records and Information. Every transferor and transferee shall maintain all appraisals, accounting, financial, or general information relied upon to establish the value of the real estate transferred or any other information required to be shown on any document or attachment required pursuant to RSA 78-B, Rev 802, Rev 805, Rev 806, Rev 807, Rev 809, and Rev 810. Source. #6691, eff 2-21-98, EXPIRED: 2-21-06 New. #8579, eff 3-8-06; ss by #10665, eff 9-9-14; ss by 14071, eff 9-10-24

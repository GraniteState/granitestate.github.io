Rev 1406.03 Form PA-20, “Utility Property Tax Information Update.”

Effective 2019-01-08.

Rev 1406.03 Form PA-20, “Utility Property Tax Information Update.” (a) Pursuant to RSA 83-F:5, V, the utility shall annually, on or before May 1, complete and file Form PA-20, “Utility Property Tax Information Update,” including any accompanying documents as required by (b) and (c) below, for the prior taxable period. (b) Form PA-20 shall be accompanied by the following documents: (1) An operating expense summary; (2) A maintenance expense summary; (3) A taxes, other than income, summary; (4) Form PA-20-E, “Utility Property Tax List of Pollution Control Exemptions,” including the exemption determination letter from New Hampshire department of environmental services, if applicable; (5) If a utility regulated by the New Hampshire public utilities commission or the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, a copy of the utility’s most recent regulatory report; and (6) If a non-regulated utility: a. A detailed balance sheet; and b. A detailed income statement. (c) If one or both of the documents required by (b)(6) above are not available, the utility shall provide the following documents: (1) Federal income tax returns; and (2) State business tax returns. (d) Any information received by the department shall be subject to confidentiality pursuant to Rev 2903.02. Source.

Rev 1501.17 Taxpayer identification number

Effective 2024-05-23.

Rev 1501.17 “Taxpayer identification number” means: (a) Social Security number; (b) Federal employer identification number; (c) Individual taxpayer identification number; (d) Preparer tax identification number; or (e) Department identification number. Source. #10662, eff 8-27-14; ss by #13977, eff 5-23-24

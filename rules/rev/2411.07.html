Rev 2411.07 Estimated Tax Forms

Effective 2026-05-02. Expires 2036-05-02.

Rev 2411.07 Estimated Tax Forms. A business enterprise having an annual projected tax liability in excess of $260 shall complete and file the applicable estimated tax form: (a) Form NH-1040-ES, “Estimated Proprietorship Business Tax”; (b) Form NH-1041-ES “Estimated Fiduciary Business Tax”; (c) Form NH-1065-ES, “Estimated Partnership Business Tax”; or (d) Form NH-1120-ES, “Estimated Corporation Business Tax.” Source. #6130, eff 11-23-95; ss and moved by #7178, eff 12-23-99 (from Rev 2411.08), EXPIRED: 12-23-07 New. #9065, eff 1-10-08; ss by #10332, eff 5-9-13; ss by #11032, eff 1-29-16 (formerly Rev 2411.09); ss by #14573, eff 5-2-26, EXPIRES: 5-2-36

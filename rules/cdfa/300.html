Cdfa 300 COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT BLOCK GRANT RULES

CHAPTER Cdfa 300 COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT BLOCK GRANT RULES REVISION NOTE #1: Effective 7-1-03, Chapter 319:14 of the Laws of 2003 transferred the rules Pln 300 governing the Community Development Block Grant (CDBG) program from the Office of State Planning to the Community Development Finance Authority (CDFA). Chapter 319:13 of the Laws of 2003, effective 7-1-03, inserted RSA 162-L:11-19 relative to the CDBG program, including rulemaking authority under RSA 162-L:16, subsequently amended by Chapter 104:1 of the Laws of 2004, effective 7-16-04. The CDFA adopted and filed amendments to Pln 300, as an interim rule under Document #8254, effective 1-12-05. The CDFA subsequently adopted and filed Cdfa 300 in Document #8387, effective 6-29-05, which was a readoption with amendments and renumbering of Chapter Pln 300, and which also made extensive changes to the wording and format. Document #8387 superseded all prior filings for Chapter Pln 300. The prior filings affecting rules in Chapter Pln 300 included the following: #2537, eff 11-10-83 | #5388, eff 5-4-92 #2550, eff 12-6-83 | #6065, eff 7-25-95 #3156, eff 12-10-85 | #6736, eff 4-24-98 #4355, eff 1-20-88 | #6970, eff 3-30-99 #4734, eff 1-22-90 | #7498, eff 5-25-01 #5050, eff 1-21-91 | #8254, eff 1-12-05 REVISION NOTE #2: After Document #8387, effective 6-29-05, the following filings were effective and affected Chapter Cdfa 300: #8515, effective 1-1-06 #10244, effective 12-19-12 #12663, INTERIM, effective 11-15-18, EXPIRED 5-14-19 NOTICE OF EXEMPTION FROM RSA 541-A The Community Development Finance Authority became exempt from RSA 541-A pursuant to 2019, 299:2 and 299:3, effective 7-29-19, which inserted RSA 541-A:21, I(kk) and adopted RSA 162-L:4-a, respectively. Pursuant to RSA 162-L:4-a, the Authority “may adopt rules in accordance with its own procedures to facilitate, implement, and carry out the powers, duties, and purposes of the authority enumerated in this chapter and additional powers and purposes as shall be conferred upon it.” Cdfa 300, which expired 5-14-19, remains out of effect unless or until adopted again by the Authority under its own procedures, and the Authority does not have to file the rules with the Administrative Rules office of the Office of Legislative Services. Questions for the latest version of the rules on the Community Development Block Grant should therefore be directed to the Authority.

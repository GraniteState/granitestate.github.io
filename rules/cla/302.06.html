Cla 302.06 Claimant’s Right to an Adjudicative Hearing

Effective 2005-12-09. Expires 2013-12-09.

Cla 302.06 Claimant’s Right to an Adjudicative Hearing. A claimant shall be entitled to a hearing under the following circumstances: (a) When the claim has not been administratively approved; (b) When the claim has been administratively approved in an amount different from the amount claimed; or (c) For the purpose of determining the board's jurisdiction when the claimant wishes to challenge dismissal of the claim pursuant to Cla 302.05(d).

Nuh 200 RULES OF PRACTICE AND PROCEDURE

CHAPTER Nuh 200 RULES OF PRACTICE AND PROCEDURE REVISION NOTE: Document #13934, effective 6-22-24, repealed Part Nuh 201 through Part Nuh 217 in Chapter Nuh 200, titled “Practice and Procedure”, and renamed the chapter as “Rules of Practice and Procedure”. Document #13934 also adopted a new Part Nuh 201 titled “Applicability and Waiver of Substantive Rules”, containing Nuh 201.01 titled “Rules of Practice and Procedure” and Nuh 201.02 titled “Waiver of Administrative Rules.” Document #13934 replaces all prior filings affecting the rules in the former Chapter Nuh 200. The prior filings included the following documents. Italics indicate the rules were subject to expiration only pursuant to RSA 541-A:17, II: #1393, eff 7-1-79 #2224, eff 1-1-83 #2922, eff 12-10-84, EXPIRED: 12-10-90 #5097, eff 3-18-91 #5326, eff 2-13-92, EXPIRED: 2-13-98 #5524, eff 12-8-92, EXPIRED: 12-8-98 #7719-A, eff 6-25-02 (Nuh 201-Nuh 216) #7719-B, eff 6-25-02, EXPIRED: 6-25-10 (Nuh 217) #9830-A, eff 12-10-10 (Nuh 217) #12435, eff 12-16-17 (Nuh 214.04(b)) #12623, eff 9-25-18 (Nuh 217) As practice and procedure rules, the rules in Document #13934 will not expire except pursuant to RSA 541-A:17, II.

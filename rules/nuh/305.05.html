Nuh 305.05 Supervision

Effective 2025-02-08. Earlier expiration 2018-12-10.

Nuh 305.05 Supervision. (a) The acting administrator shall be supervised by a licensed administrator who is in good standing with the board and has at least 5 years’ experience operating a licensed nursing home. (b) Any licensed administrator about to become a supervisor of an acting administrator shall notify the OPLC of their intent. (c) No licensed administrator shall be permitted to supervise more than one acting administrator at a time. (d) The licensed administrator supervising an acting administrator shall provide at least 8 hours per week of direct onsite supervision of the acting administrator. (e) The licensed administrator supervising the acting administrator shall be responsible for all aspects of the facility’s operation.

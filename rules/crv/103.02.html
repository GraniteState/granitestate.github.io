Crv 103.02 Responsibilities of the Commission

Effective 2001-09-28.

Crv 103.02 Responsibilities of the Commission. The commission’s responsibilities include: (a) Assembling and publishing a comprehensive inventory of natural resources of the Connecticut River valley; (b) Coordinating a regional promotional program with the department of resources and economic development; (c) Providing for interstate cooperation with the state of Vermont; (d) Cooperating with local communities and regional planning commissions; (e) Reviewing and evaluating other river and watershed protection endeavors; (f) Seeking and expending additional public and private funds; (g) Encouraging the protection of agricultural, historical, and natural resources, and the provision of additional public access to recreational sites; and (h) Providing grants to further the purposes of the commission. Source. #7567, eff 9-28-01

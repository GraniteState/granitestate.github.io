Saf-C 1408.01 Registration Form

Effective 2024-11-28. Expires 2034-11-28.

Saf-C 1408.01 Registration Form. (a) All persons required to register a carnival or amusement ride pursuant to RSA 321-A:5, shall make application for said registration to the division by completing and submitting the form DSCO 62 “Carnival-Amusement Device Registration Form,” (rev. 11/24). (b) The applicant shall complete the form DSCO 122 “Amusement/Recreational Ride or Device Operator’s Annual Inspection Report,” (rev. 11/24) and submit it with the DSCO 62 form. (c) By signing the DSCO 122 form, the person responsible for the operation of the rides and devices listed thereon shall certify that: (1) Such person has inspected those rides and devices on a specified date and that, to the best of their knowledge, those rides and devices comply with Saf-C 1400 and RSA 321-A and that they are safe to operate for the public; and (2) Non-destructive testing (NDT) requirements, as set forth by the manufacturer of the ride, by the United States Consumer Product Safety Commission, or both, have been completed. (d) The applicant shall include with the application the required fee per carnival or amusement ride, pursuant to Saf-C 1408.03.

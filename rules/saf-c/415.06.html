Saf-C 415.06 Revocation of Rental Agent Safety Certificate

Effective 2016-12-19.

Saf-C 415.06 Revocation of Rental Agent Safety Certificate. (a) The commissioner shall revoke a rental agent safety certificate, after notice and an opportunity for a hearing, upon a finding that a rental agent has: (1) Rented or leased a motorboat to any other person without complying with the requirements of RSA 270-D:2-c; (2) Rented or leased a motorboat to any person who has not provided proof to the rental agent of having met the requirements of RSA 270-D:10; or (3) Rented or leased a motorboat that is unsafe to any other person. (b) For the purposes of (a)(3) above, there shall be a rebuttable presumption that a rented or leased motorboat is unsafe if it fails to comply with applicable laws or rules, including part Saf-C 403 of this chapter. (c) All hearings shall be held pursuant to the procedures set forth in Saf-C 202. Source.

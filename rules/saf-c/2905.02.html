Saf-C 2905.02 Law Enforcement Substance Abuse Reduction Initiative Application, Data Required

Effective 2021-07-09. Earlier expiration 2020-03-31.

Saf-C 2905.02 Law Enforcement Substance Abuse Reduction Initiative Application, Data Required. (a) Applicants for grant funding shall complete and submit the “Law Enforcement Substance Abuse Reduction Initiative Application”, DSAD 67, (rev. 05/20). (b) The authorizing official of the applicant shall identify and provide a justification for any submitted material in the application that the authorizing official believes should not be released pursuant to a request under RSA 91-A. (c) The authorizing official shall sign and submit the DSAD 67, (rev. 9/19), by the date specified in Saf-C 2905.01(d). (d) The authorizing official, via his or her signature, shall certify that he or she understands that: (1) Any funds awarded be used to supplement existing funds for program activities, and will not supplant other local or funding sources for routine law enforcement activities; (2) This program is a reimbursement-based program; (3) Municipal invoices, payroll validation and payment vouchers or cancelled checks shall be submitted to receive grant fund reimbursement; and (e) To the extent that local data is used in support of an application, the authorizing official shall certify that the data is correct to the best of the official’s knowledge.

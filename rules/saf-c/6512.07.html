Saf-C 6512.07 Denial of Certificate of Removal Hearing

Effective 2022-09-20.

Saf-C 6512.07 Denial of Certificate of Removal Hearing. (a) A user who is denied a certificate of removal may request a hearing, pursuant to Saf-C 203. (b) The scope of the hearing shall be to determine if, by a preponderance of the evidence, whether: (1) The user has committed an interlock violation, as defined by RSA 265-A:38-a, I (a)-(i), within the preceding 120 days; (2) The expiration date of the ignition interlock order, including any extensions of time, has elapsed; (3) The user does not have any court or administrative hearings pending regarding an allegation of having committed one or more ignition interlock device violations; and (4) The user does not have any alleged ignition interlock program violations for which a hearing has yet to be scheduled.

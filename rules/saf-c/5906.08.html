Saf-C 5906.08 Vehicle Equipment and Supplies

Effective 2025-07-23. Expires 2035-07-23.

Saf-C 5906.08 Vehicle Equipment and Supplies. (a) A unit provider shall equip each land or water vehicle operating at the EMT or EMT-B level, AEMT level, or paramedic level with the required equipment specified in Table 5906.1. (b) A unit provider shall equip each air medical transport vehicle with the required equipment specified in Table 5906.2.

Saf-C 7008.02 Quorum

Effective 2016-06-29.

Saf-C 7008.02 Quorum. A quorum of the commission shall consist of not less than 9 members and a majority vote by the members present shall be necessary to pass a motion unless otherwise specified by law. In the absence of the chair or vice-chair, a quorum of the commission shall designate a pro tempore officer for the officer or officers absent. Source. #9091, eff 2-21-08 (See Revision Note at Chapter Heading for Saf-C 7000); ss by #11128, eff 6-29-16 (formerly Saf-C 7009.02)

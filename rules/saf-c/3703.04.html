Saf-C 3703.04 Availability of Funds

Effective 2017-02-23. Earlier expiration 2017-03-14.

Saf-C 3703.04 Availability of Funds. If at any time the fund does not have money available to reimburse the application, the department shall: (a) Notify the applicant that the default bench warrant fund has insufficient funds for reimbursement; (b) Retain a chronological list of unreimbursed requests; and (c) As funds become available reimburse the applicant’s beginning with the oldest date on the list.

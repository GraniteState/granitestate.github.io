Saf-C 5204.01 Basis for Cost Recovery

Effective 2018-08-23.

Saf-C 5204.01 Basis for Cost Recovery. (a) Costs shall be calculated for each hazardous material or hazardous waste incident, as specified in Table 5204-1, Table 5204-2, or Table 5204-3, whichever is greater, determined as follows: (1) Personnel costs shall be based on a rate not to exceed $135 per hour however, actual documented local governing body approved hourly rate or overtime rate per responder, as applicable, will apply based on the actual cost as invoiced or paid, including FICA, retirement, and Medicare tax; (2) Equipment and apparatus costs are based on the cost per hour and included a fully equipped apparatus and fuel; (3) Cost of level B PPE to include all respiratory protection, tape and level B suit is $100 per person, per day, and, if damaged, replacement cost; (4) Cost of level A PPE to include all respiratory protection, tape and level A suit is $150 per person per day, and if damaged replacement cost; (5) Costs for expendable materials shall be reimbursed based on the current retail value or restocking price of the item(s); (6) Costs shall not include costs incurred for a response to a fire or other emergencies where the discharge of hazardous materials or waste is incidental to the fire or other emergency and does not require the use of specialized hazardous materials or waste response equipment or specially trained personnel; (7) Miscellaneous items, which are not mentioned above, that are used and expended as part of a hazardous material and/or hazardous waste incident response shall be reimbursed when accompanied by proof of payment in the form of an invoice or receipt; (8) An administrative processing fee shall be charged in the amount of 20% of the total costs of (1)-(4) above; (9) An initial response charge of $400 per incident shall be charged to the responsible party; (10) The billing agency, at the time of billing, shall notify the party billed that they may appeal to the commissioner within 30 days of receipt of the bill; (11) Responding agencies shall base the cost on a match to the item listed in the table; (12) In cases where the item exists in multiple tables, the responding agency shall use the higher of the costs listed; (13) In cases where the item does not exist in the table, it should be matched as closely as possible to the item used.

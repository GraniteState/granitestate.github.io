Saf-C 3702.04 Submission of Form

Effective 2017-02-23. Earlier expiration 2017-03-14.

Saf-C 3702.04 Submission of Form. (a) The following shall be provided on the form by the applicant: (1) Docket number of the offender’s bench warrant; (2) Offender’s name, address, and date of birth; (3) Name of the law enforcement agency; (4) Location of the law enforcement agency; (5) Name of the individual law enforcement official who served the bench warrant; (6) Date the bench warrant was actually served; (7) Date(s) of attempted bench warrant service; (8) Total mileage traveled in attempting and actually serving the bench warrant; (9) Time started to serve the bench warrant and time concluded for each attempt and actual service; and (10) Signature of head of the law enforcement agency or his or her designee. (b) Using a 3-part form, the applicant shall: (1) Retain their copy of the application; (2) Submit a copy to the law enforcement official who served the bench warrant; and (3) Hand-deliver or mail the division’s copy to the business office, department of safety, James H. Hayes Safety Building, 33 Hazen Drive, Concord, N.H. 03305 within 60 days of the date of arrest. (c) If a law enforcement agency is submitting a reimbursement request for 5 or more warrants, it may submit the information specified in (a), above, on one form.

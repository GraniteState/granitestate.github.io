Saf-C 8503.01 Obtaining a Blue Envelope

Effective 2026-07-03. Expires 2026-12-30.

Saf-C 8503.01 Obtaining a Blue Envelope. (a) The following people may apply to the blue envelope program: (1) A driver licensed in New Hampshire and diagnosed with autism spectrum disorder or trauma and stressor related disorder; (2) A parent applying on behalf of a minor driver licensed in New Hampshire and diagnosed with autism spectrum disorder or trauma and stressor related disorder; or (3) A guardian applying on behalf of a driver licensed in New Hampshire and diagnosed with autism spectrum disorder or trauma and stressor related disorder. (b) The DMSV 653 “Blue Envelope Request Form” (rev 07/26) may be obtained from www.dmv.nh.gov/forms; or in person, during business hours, from the DMV locations listed at www.dmv.nh.gov/hours-and-locations. (c) To receive a blue envelope a person can submit a completed DSMV 653 “Blue Envelope Request Form” (rev 7/26) signed by a doctor. But if they have already completed and submitted a DSMV 643 “Medically Recognized Disorder Indication” (rev 07/26) form which was signed by a doctor and is on file with the DMV, then they just need to request in person, orally, to be issued a blue envelope and they will be given on based on the DSMV 643 (rev 07/26) complying with this part. (d) A parent or legal guardian requestor applying on behalf of a participating person, shall submit a completed form DMSV 653 “Blue Envelope Request Form” (rev 07/26); and (1) To receive a blue envelope a person can submit a completed DSMV 653 “Blue Envelope Request Form” (rev 07/26) signed by a doctor. But if they have already completed and submitted a DSMV 643 “Medically Recognized Disorder Indication” (rev 07/26) form which was signed by a doctor and is on file with the DMV, then they just need to request in person, orally, to be issued a blue envelope and they will be given on based on the DSMV 643 (rev 07/26) complying with this part. ; (2) Sign the form under penalty of unsworn falsification pursuant to RSA 641:3; and (3) Include the following documentation where applicable: a. If the applicant is a parent of a minor, include a copy of one of the following: 1. The minor’s birth certificate; 2. Certificate of adoption; or 3. Adoption decree; or b. If the applicant is a legal guardian, include a copy of the order appointing the guardianship identifying the powers granted to the legal guardian. (e) Participants shall submit completed requests by: (1) Mailing to: Division of Motor Vehicles Attn: Blue Envelope 27 Hazen Drive Concord, NH 03305; (2) Hand delivering during business hours to: Division of Motor Vehicles 27 Hazen Drive Concord, NH 03305; or a. Any DMV location at www.dmv.nh.gov/hours-and-locations. Source. #14643, INTERIM, eff 7-3-26, EXPIRES: 12-30-26

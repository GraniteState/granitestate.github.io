Saf-C 5607.04 Express Consent

Effective 2002-01-01.

Saf-C 5607.04 Express Consent. (a) Unless a person gives their express consent, the department shall not release motor vehicle records for the purposes specified in RSA 260:14, V(a)(8) and (9). (b) Express consent may be given by submitting in writing to the department a letter including the following information: (1) The person's name, address, and date of birth; (2) The motor vehicle record information that the department may release; (3) A statement that the person gives their express consent to the release of their motor vehicle record information for purposes specified in RSA 260:14, V(a)(8) and (9). (c) Once received, a letter giving a person's express consent to release of motor vehicle records shall remain in effect for two years. Nothing shall prevent the person from withdrawing his or her express consent at any time. Source. #7361, EXEMPT, eff 9-1-00; ss by #7621, EXEMPT, eff 1-1-02

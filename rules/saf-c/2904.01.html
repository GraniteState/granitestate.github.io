Saf-C 2904.01 Grant Conditions

Effective 2021-07-09. Earlier expiration 2020-03-31.

Saf-C 2904.01 Grant Conditions. (a) Grant funds awarded pursuant to the substance abuse enforcement program shall support activities for: (1) The investigation and apprehension of individuals or organizations that are involved in narcotic-related drug use and trafficking that is identified by: a. Using statistics and information to place additional patrol and investigative presence at locations, times, and places where there has been a significant convergence of motor vehicle crashes, crimes, and illegal drug use; and b. Using statistics and information to place additional patrol and investigative presence in corridors known to be used by drug dealers for shipment of illegal drugs into the state; (2) Absent unanticipated staff shortages, relieving dedicated patrol units from taking calls for service; (3) Promoting multi-jurisdictional law enforcement collaboration and increased information sharing through the IAC; and (4) The reduction of narcotics sales, abuse, and deaths.

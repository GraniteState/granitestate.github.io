Saf-C 5901.411 Emergency medical services instructor (EMS I)

Effective 2024-04-30.

Saf-C 5901.411 “Emergency medical services instructor (EMS I)” means the primary or assistant instructor licensed by the division to be responsible for instruction conducted in division approved training programs. Source. #13953, eff 4-30-24

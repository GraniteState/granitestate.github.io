Saf-C 1102.06 Refresher Course

Effective 2022-07-25. Earlier expiration 2022-05-23.

Saf-C 1102.06 Refresher Course. (a) The commissioner shall require any person who has been granted citation authority to complete a refresher training course pursuant to Saf-C 1102.04(b) when: (1) The person has evidenced a lack of understanding of the fire safety or applicable licensing rules; (2) The fire safety or applicable licensing rules have been amended, in order for the person to have current knowledge of the rules; or (3) The person has not maintained the continuing education requirement pursuant to Saf-C 1102.04(a)(2).

Saf-C 1806.03 Basic Control Skills Test

Effective 2024-10-18. Expires 2034-10-18.

Saf-C 1806.03 Basic Control Skills Test. (a) The department shall determine whether the applicant has the basic ability to control the vehicle by administering the basic control skills test. The examination shall consist of various exercises marked by lines, traffic cones, or rope. Each applicant shall pass the basic control skills test in the event they successfully perform the required maneuvers in their entirety in the order listed and shall score no more than 8 points: (1) Forward stop; (2) Straight-line backing; (3) Forward offset tracking; and (4) Reverse offset backing. (b) Notwithstanding (a) above, the basic control skills test examination shall be immediately discontinued and counted as a failure in the event of any of the following: (1) Performing an unsafe act; or (2) Failure to follow instructions. (c) Each applicant who fails the basic control skills examination shall not be permitted to proceed to the road skills portion of the examination.

Saf-C 513.20 Gold Star Plate

Effective 2023-01-13.

Saf-C 513.20 Gold Star Plate. (a) Gold Star plates shall be issued to a vehicle owned or held in trust by a mother or stepmother of a person killed while on duty in the United States armed forces, pursuant to RSA 261:87-c. (b) A stepmother shall be eligible for a gold star plate under this rule if she: (1) Provides a marriage license which is dated prior to the armed service member’s 15th birthday; and (2) Provides a completed Department of Defense “DD form 1300” to the director. (c) If the vehicle is held in trust, the applicant shall provide a copy of the most updated trust document that demonstrates: (1) That the vehicle is owned by the trust, except that a current registration for the vehicle demonstrating that the vehicle held in the trust may be substituted for the trust document for this requirement; and (2) That the applicant is a trustee of the trust. (d) A gold star plate shall not be issued to a stepmother if a gold star plate is already issued to another stepmother for that service member. Source. #10135, eff 5-23-12; ss by #12914, eff 11-16-19 (formerly Saf-C 514.19); ss by #13521, eff 1-13-23 (formerly Saf-C 514.20)

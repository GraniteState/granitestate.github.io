Saf-C 8403.01 Obtaining and Completing Application

Effective 2025-08-14. Expires 2035-08-14.

Saf-C 8403.01 Obtaining and Completing Application. (a) Application forms may be obtained from www.nhsp.dos.nh.gov/resources/documents-and-forms; or in person, during business hours, from the: Division of State Police Permits and Licensing 33 Hazen Dr. - 1st floor Concord, NH 03305. (b) An applicant applying on their own behalf shall submit completed Form DSSP 470 “Application Form on Behalf of Yourself”, effective 08/25 and: (1) Acknowledge they are voluntarily providing contact information for and designating the contact person(s) named to assist with communications during an encounter with law enforcement; and (2) The applicant’s signature shall be notarized. (c) A parent applying on behalf of a minor who has a disability, or a legal guardian applying on behalf of a person with a disability, shall submit completed Form DSSP 471 “Application Form for Parent of Minor or for Legal Guardian of Person with a Disability”, effective 08/25, and: (1) Acknowledge they are the parent or legal guardian of the minor or ward named on the application; (2) Acknowledge that they are voluntarily providing contact information for and designating the contact person(s) named to assist the minor or ward with communications during an encounter with law enforcement; and (3) The applicant’s signature shall be notarized. (4) Include the following documentation where applicable: a. If the applicant is a parent of a minor, include a copy of one of the following: 1. The minor’s birth certificate; 2. Certificate of adoption; or 3. Adoption decree; or b. If the applicant is a legal guardian, include a copy of the order appointing the guardianship that shows the powers granted to the legal guardian. (d) Applicants shall submit completed applications by: (1) Mailing to: Division of State Police Attn: NCIC 33 Hazen Dr. – 2nd Floor Concord, NH 03305; (2) Hand delivering during business hours to: Division of State Police Permits and Licensing 33 Hazen Dr. – 1st floor Concord, NH 03305; or b. Any troop station; or (3) Emailing to: NCIC@dos.nh.gov Source. #14340, eff 8-14-25, EXPIRES: 8-14-35

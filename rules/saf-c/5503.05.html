Saf-C 5503.05 Sexual Offenders Or Offenders Against Children Reporting Requirements

Effective 2022-12-09. Earlier expiration 2010-02-20.

Saf-C 5503.05 Sexual Offenders Or Offenders Against Children Reporting Requirements. (a) Pursuant to RSA 651-B:4, I, within 5 business days after the person’s release, or of establishing residence in New Hampshire, any sexual offender or offender against children shall report in person to the local law enforcement agency having jurisdiction over his or her primary residency. (b) Pursuant to RSA 651-B:4, II, any nonresident sexual offender or offender against children shall be required to report, pursuant to law and these rules, if the purpose of the sexual offender or offender against children's presence in the state is for: (1) Employment, with or without compensation; or (2) Attendance at any public or private educational institution. (c) The nonresident sexual offender or offender against children shall register, within 5 business days of entering the state, with the: (1) Local law enforcement agency having jurisdiction over the place of employment or school; or (2) Division, if there is no principal place of employment within the state, at: Department of Safety SOR 33 Hazen Drive Concord, N.H. 03305 (d) Each time a sexual offender or offender against children is required to report, the offender shall provide all the information required on DSSP 311 to the local law enforcement agency or division, as applicable. The sexual offender or offender against children shall sign the completed form DSSP 311, which shall be an acknowledgement of the offender’s duty to report. (e) Pursuant to RSA 651-B:5, sexual offenders or offenders against children required to register under RSA 651-B: 4, and these rules, shall also give written notification of any change of registration information within 5 business days of such change of information to the local law enforcement agency to which he or she last reported under RSA 651-B:4. For the purposes of this section, the provisions of Saf-C 5504.05 shall apply. Source. #7649, eff 2-20-02, EXPIRED: 2-20-10 New. #10121, eff 4-25-12; ss by #13504, eff 12-9-22

Saf-C 6205.02 Mobile Prop Fees

Effective 2018-02-08.

Saf-C 6205.02 Mobile Prop Fees. (a) New Hampshire fire department and fire-related state agencies shall be charged fees for the utilization of mobile props, as follows: (1) There shall be no charge for the first 2 utilizations of mobile props in a fiscal year; and (2) Each subsequent utilization of mobile props in a fiscal year shall be the actual expenses incurred by the division, as calculated per Saf-C 6202. (b) Individuals and entities that are not New Hampshire fire departments and fire-related state agencies shall be charged the actual expenses incurred by the division for utilization of mobile props. Source. #12477, eff 2-8-18 (formerly Saf-C 6204.02)

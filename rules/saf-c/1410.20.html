Saf-C 1410.20 Applicable Rules

Effective 2024-11-28. Expires 2034-11-28.

Saf-C 1410.20 Applicable Rules. Notwithstanding the rules in part Saf-C 1410, all bungee jump amusements shall conform to the rules in parts Saf-C 1402-1409, except Saf-C 1405.01(b). Source.

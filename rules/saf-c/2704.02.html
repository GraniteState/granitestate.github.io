Saf-C 2704.02 Use of Emergency Lights

Effective 2021-06-16. Earlier expiration 2004-01-26.

Saf-C 2704.02 Use of Emergency Lights. (a) All hospital emergency personnel using emergency lights when responding to a hospital emergency shall: (1) Have in their possession the hospital emergency permit issued by the commissioner; (2) Have in their possession a valid driver license for the particular class of vehicle being operated; (3) Operate a vehicle that is in safe condition for operation on public ways; (4) Operate a vehicle equipped with emergency lights meeting the requirements of Saf-C 2704.01 (a) - (d); (5) Operate a vehicle pursuant to the requirements of RSA 265:8; (6) Be aware that utilizing emergency lights shall not relieve the driver from the responsibility to drive with due regard for the safety of all persons and he or she shall not be protected from the consequences of reckless disregard for the safety of others; (7) Not equip the vehicle with or utilize a siren; and (8) Use emergency lights only when responding to a hospital emergency.

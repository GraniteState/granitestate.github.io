Saf-C 5604.02 Account Set-Up

Effective 2020-12-31.

Saf-C 5604.02 Account Set-Up. (a) A person shall contact the Department of Safety, Division of Administration, Business Office at 33 Hazen Drive, Concord, NH 03305 or accountsreceivable@dos.nh.gov to set up an account. (b) A person establishing an account shall provide their name, mailing and legal address, date of birth, company name, and phone number. (c) The department shall issue a unique four number personal identification number (PIN) for use by the account holder or a person authorized pursuant to 5604.03, when requesting information via fax or telephone. An account holder may cancel his/her PIN and obtain a new number by contacting the business office as specified in (a), above. (d) A minimum of $100.00 deposit shall be made to establish the account. (e) The department shall notify the account holder when the balance is less than $25.00.

Saf-C 1605.03 Application Procedures

Effective 2013-08-15. Earlier expiration 2005-01-10.

Saf-C 1605.03 Application Procedures. (a) Any person who wishes to apply for an original explosive license shall do the following: (1) Appear in person at the department, room 106, during normal business hours; (2) Supply proof of identification such as a valid driver's license or birth certificate; (3) If the application is for a user's license, the applicant, if a natural person, shall furnish 2 recent photographs, 1 1/4" by 1 1/4", of him or herself; and (4) Complete the application form: a. If for a user license, DSSP 110 “Application for License to Use, Purchase and Transport Explosives”, effective May 2013; or b. If for a storage license, DSSP 109, “Application for License to Store Explosive Material”, effective May 2013; or c. If for a seller’s license, DSSP 108, “Application for License to Market and Sell Explosives”, effective May 2013; and (5) Make payment of the fee as specified in RSA 158:9-c.

Saf-C 521.01 Definitions

Effective 2023-01-13. Earlier expiration 2009-07-27.

Saf-C 521.01 Definitions. The terms enumerated in this part shall have the meaning set forth herein, unless a different meaning is clearly stated in the text, as follows: (a) “Designee” means state official or employee and does not include an agent, or employee of an agent, that has contracted with the department of transportation; (b) “Electronic toll collection system” means “electronic toll collection system” as defined in RSA 236:31, I(c); (c) “Electronic toll collection monitoring system” means “toll collection monitoring system” as defined in RSA 236:31, I(d); (d) “Nonrenewal” means refusal to issue or renew a motor vehicle registration, in accordance with RSA 261; (e) “Owner” means owner as defined in RSA 259:72; (f) “Pay” means “pay” as defined in RSA 236:31, I(g); and (g) “Violation” means “violation” as defined in RSA 236:31, I(h).

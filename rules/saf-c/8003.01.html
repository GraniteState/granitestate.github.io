Saf-C 8003.01 General License Rules

Effective 2009-09-10.

Saf-C 8003.01 General License Rules. (a) No person shall engage in the business or work as a fuel gas fitter unless the person is lawfully licensed as provided by this chapter. (b) No individual license shall be granted to any person less than 18 years of age. (c) An initial individual license shall be issued for 2 years, except that it shall expire on the last day of the month of the holder’s birth. (d) Unless otherwise stated in these rules, all applications and documentation shall be signed by the applicant under the penalty of unsworn falsification pursuant to RSA 641:3. (e) An individual who meets the qualifications for service technician shall be deemed to be qualified as an installation technician, hearth system installation and service technician, and piping installer. (f) An individual who meets the qualifications for installation technician shall be deemed to be qualified as a hearth system installer and service technician and a piping installer. (g) A business entity license shall be issued for 2 years, expiring on the last day of December. (h) A license shall be issued to a business entity without examination or continuing education, provided that one or more officers of the business entity or designee hold a current and valid liquefied propane gas service technician license or a natural gas service technician license. (i) All business entities shall be registered with the NH secretary of state’s office. A foreign business entity shall be registered with the NH secretary of state’s office as a foreign business entity doing business in NH. Source. #8954, eff 7-21-07; amd by #9542, eff 9-10-09

Saf-C 1407.01 Identification Plate Required

Effective 2024-11-28. Expires 2034-11-28.

Saf-C 1407.01 Identification Plate Required. (a) A permanent New Hampshire identification number shall be assigned to each carnival or amusement ride and associated electrical equipment registered for operation in New Hampshire. (b) A numbered metal identification plate measuring 1” x 3” shall be provided by the department of safety. (c) The identification plate shall be permanently affixed to the carnival or amusement ride by the owner or operator prior to public operation. (d) The identification plate shall be placed in immediate proximity of the New Hampshire registration sticker. (e) If a permanent identification plate is lost or stolen, the department of safety, tramway and amusement ride bureau shall be notified immediately.

Saf-C 1006.04 Motorcycle Skills Examination: Description

Effective 2023-08-05.

Saf-C 1006.04 Motorcycle Skills Examination: Description. The motorcycle skills examination shall include the following: (a) Overall control and operation of the motorcycle; (b) Sharp turns; (c) Stopping at a designated point; (d) Cone weave; (e) "U" turns; (f) Quick stop; and (g) Obstacle turn. Source. #8109, eff 6-24-04; ss by #10176, eff 8-23-12; ss by #13685, eff 8-5-23 (see Revision Note at chapter heading for Saf-C 1000)

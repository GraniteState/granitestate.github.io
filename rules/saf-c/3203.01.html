Saf-C 3203.01 Items to be Inspected

Effective 2016-01-06. Earlier expiration 1998-02-15.

Saf-C 3203.01 Items to be Inspected. (a) The items to be inspected on a vehicle, which are described in detail in subsequent parts, shall include the following primary areas: (1) Registration, plates, registration certificate and vehicle identification number; (2) Steering and suspension; (3) Brakes, including parking brake; (4) Odometer and speedometer; (5) Electrical system, horn and defroster; (6) Lights and reflectors, including headlight aim; (7) Glass, glazing and mirrors; (8) Wipers; (9) Exhaust system; (10) On-board diagnostics system; (11) Body, chassis and bumper height; (12) Fuel system; and (13) Tires and wheels.

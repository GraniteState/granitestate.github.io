Saf-C 8007.01 Application for License by Non-New Hampshire License Holder

Effective 2009-09-10.

Saf-C 8007.01 Application for License by Non-New Hampshire License Holder. (a) The department shall issue an individual license to any applicant who presents evidence of being authorized to perform these duties by another state, jurisdiction or country that maintains professional standards to be equivalent or higher than those set forth in these rules provided that the other state, jurisdiction or country provides a reciprocal privilege for New Hampshire licensees. (b) An applicant shall submit the following: (1) Completed application, form DSFM 123; (2) 2 passport sized photos; and (3) Applicable fee as set forth in Saf-C 8009; (c) Evidence of the required field experience for the applicable license, as set forth in Part Saf-C 8004; and (d) Copy of: (1) The license issued by another state, jurisdiction or country; and (2) Documentation evidencing the other state, jurisdiction or country’s professional standards. Source. #8954, eff 7-21-07; amd by #9542, eff 9-10-09

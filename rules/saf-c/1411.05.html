Saf-C 1411.05 Department Inspection

Effective 2024-11-28. Expires 2034-11-28.

Saf-C 1411.05 Department Inspection. (a) Agents of the commissioner shall be subject to the standards set forth in the “ANSI/ACCT 03-2019 CHALLENGE COURSES AND CANOPY/ZIP LINE TOURS STANDARDS” (Revision of ANSI/ACCT 03-2016 Standards) or the ASTM International standards listed in Saf-C 1402.01(a), available as noted in Appendix B. (b) In addition to (a) above, agents of the commissioner shall be granted free access to any challenge course or zip line pursuant to Saf-C 1405.05 and shall be authorized to conduct inspections at any reasonable time, with or without prior notice.

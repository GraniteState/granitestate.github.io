Saf-C 5705.06 Fees

Effective 2017-03-17.

Saf-C 5705.06 Fees. (a) There shall be no fee for providing criminal history records in response to an electronic request for a record that does not involve a request to process fingerprints. (b) There shall be a fee collected by the division as the fee is established and set by the FBI, for handling a request involving fingerprint processing for noncriminal justice purposes.

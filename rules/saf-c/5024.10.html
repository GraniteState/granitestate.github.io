Saf-C 5024.10 Fire Equipment

Effective 2019-04-30. Earlier expiration 2005-01-10.

Saf-C 5024.10 Fire Equipment. (a) The sponsor of the display shall arrange for the presence of fire department personnel and extinguishing equipment. The local fire chief shall provide at least one member of the department or more that he deems necessary to operate such extinguishing equipment as outlined below. They may be on duty from the time the fireworks are delivered at the site, but shall be on duty from the time unloading of the fireworks begin until the termination of the display and removal of all fireworks and debris from the site. For the purpose of this rule debris shall include any un-ignited fireworks or remnants containing explosive material. (b) The fire chief shall designate such fire extinguishing equipment as might be required at the display site from the time the fireworks are delivered at the site until the termination of the display and removal of all fireworks and debris from the site. The head of the fire department shall designate the location and type of fire extinguishing equipment. (c) Nothing in this section is intended to supplement or substitute a fire department’s authority to charge for costs, if any, associated with detailing its personnel or equipment as described above.

Saf-C 702.03 Submitting the Application

Effective 2015-07-16. Earlier expiration 2005-03-25.

Saf-C 702.03 Submitting the Application. (a) The application shall be submitted to the: Office of the Commissioner, Department of Safety James H. Hayes Building 33 Hazen Drive Concord, New Hampshire 03305. (b) If an application is found to be incomplete, the railroad shall be notified in writing within 30 days of the date the application is received by the department.

Saf-C 1402.10 Generators and Electrical Distribution

Effective 2024-11-28. Expires 2034-11-28.

Saf-C 1402.10 Generators and Electrical Distribution. (a) All generating plants shall be designed and maintained in accordance with the provisions of the NEC. Each plant shall be individually identified by a unique identification number. (b) All generators shall be inspected and certified annually by a licensed New Hampshire electrician to comply with the NEC. This certification shall contain the unique identification number required in (a) above and shall remain with the generator and be presented in hand to any authorized agent of the commissioner upon request. (c) All distribution boxes shall be designed and maintained in accordance with the provisions of the NEC. Each box shall be individually identified by a unique identification number. All distribution boxes shall be individually certified by a third party testing agency or a licensed New Hampshire electrical engineer.

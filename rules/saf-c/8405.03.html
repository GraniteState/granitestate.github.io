Saf-C 8405.03 Removal Without Withdrawal Form

Effective 2025-08-14. Expires 2035-08-14.

Saf-C 8405.03 Removal Without Withdrawal Form. (a) If the director has reason to believe that a participating person is deceased, the director will verify such status with one or more of the participating person’s contact person(s). Upon confirmation that the participating person is deceased, the director shall remove the participating person from the program. (b) Upon the death of a participating person’s sole contact person, the director shall remove the participating person from the program. (c) A participating person, upon turning 18 years old, shall be removed from the program. Such person, however, may apply or participate in the program pursuant to Saf-C 8401.02(b)(1) or (3). (d) Annually, the director shall confirm the accuracy of the contact person’s contact information. If a contact person fails to confirm their contact information within 30 days, the director shall remove the contact person from the program and notify the participating person, unless the participating person is a minor or has a legal guardian in which case the director shall notify the participating person’s parent or legal guardian. (e) Upon removing a sole contact person from the program, the participating person shall be removed from the program. (f) When a participating person is removed from the program pursuant to Saf-C 8405.02(b) or this section, the director shall notify the participating person unless the participating person is a minor or has a legal guardian in which case the director shall notify the participating person’s parent or legal guardian.

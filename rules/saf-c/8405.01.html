Saf-C 8405.01 Obtaining and Completing Withdrawal Form

Effective 2025-08-14. Expires 2035-08-14.

Saf-C 8405.01 Obtaining and Completing Withdrawal Form. (a) A participating person who applied to the program on their own behalf may withdraw by completing section 1 of Form 474 “Withdrawal From Contact Person Notification Program Form”, effective 08/25, and: (1) The participating person’s signature shall be notarized; and (2) Notification to the contact person(s) shall not be required. (b) A parent of a minor or a legal guardian of a participating person may withdraw a participating person from the program by completing section 2 of Form 474 and: (1) The signature of the parent or legal guardian of the participating person shall be notarized; and (2) Notification to the contact person(s) shall not be required. (c) Contact persons may withdraw themselves from the program by completing section 3 of Form 474 and by acknowledging that they have notified the participating person of such withdrawal, unless the participating person is a minor or has a legal guardian in which case the contact person shall acknowledge they have notified the participating person’s parent or legal guardian. (d) The signature of the contact person pursuant to (c) above shall be notarized. (e) Form 474 shall be submitted pursuant to Saf-C 8403.01(d)(1)-(3). Source. #14340, eff 8-14-25, EXPIRES: 8-14-35

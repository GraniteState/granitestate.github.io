Saf-C 8005.02 Issuance of Original License

Effective 2009-09-10.

Saf-C 8005.02 Issuance of Original License. (a) An individual applicant who meets the educational and licensing requirements in accordance with these rules and who submits a completed application, pursuant to Saf-C 8005.01, shall be issued a pocket card and wall license. (b) The individual licensee pocket card, DSFM 126, and wall certificate, DSFM 125, shall contain at minimum the following: (1) The individual’s name; (2) The individual’s photo; (3) The license number; (4) The type of specialty license; (5) The date of issuance; and (6) The expiration date of the license. (c) The individual licensee wall certificate, DSFM 125, shall contain at minimum the following: (1) The individual’s name; (2) The license number; (3) The type of specialty license; (4) The date of issuance; and (5) The expiration date of the license. (d) All individual licenses issued shall expire on the last day of the month of the applicant’s birthday, 2 years from the date of issuance. (e) A business entity that meets the licensing requirements in accordance with these rules and who submits a completed application pursuant to Saf-C 8005.01(c), shall be issued a wall certificate. (f) The business entity wall certificate shall contain at minimum the following: (1) The business entity’s name; (2) The business entity’s license number; (3) The responsible managing employee’s name; (4) The responsible managing employee’s license number; (5) The expiration date of the responsible managing employee’s license number; and (6) The expiration date of the business entity’s license number. (g) All business entity licenses shall expire on the last day of December in each 2-year period. Source. #8954, eff 7-21-07; ss by #9542, eff 9-10-09

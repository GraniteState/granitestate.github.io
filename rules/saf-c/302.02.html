Saf-C 302.02 Alternative fuel dealer

Effective 2021-04-07. Earlier expiration 2015-06-30.

Saf-C 302.02 “Alternative fuel dealer” means “alternative fuel dealer” as defined in RSA 259:3-e. Any person who meets the criteria in RSA 259:3-e is an “alternative fuel dealer,” regardless of the ownership of the vehicles into which alternative fuels are dispensed. Source. #10753, INTERIM, eff 1-1-15, EXPIRED: 6-30-15 New. #10890, eff 7-16-15; ss by #13186, eff 4-7-21 (See Revision Note #3 at Chapter Heading for Saf-C 300)

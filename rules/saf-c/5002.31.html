Saf-C 5002.31 Special effects

Effective 2019-04-30. Earlier expiration 2005-01-10.

Saf-C 5002.31 “Special effects” means visual or sound effects introduced during a theatrical or musical production with or without an audience. Source. (See Revision Note at Chapter Heading for Saf-C 5000) #6055, eff 7-1-95, EXPIRED: 7-1-03 New. #8117, INTERIM, eff 7-14-04, EXPIRED: 1-10-05 New. #8261, eff 1-21-05; ss and moved by #9765-A, eff 8-2-10 (from Saf-C 5002.28); renumbered by #12124 (from Saf-C 5002.30); ss by #12738, eff 4-30-19

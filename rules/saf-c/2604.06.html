Saf-C 2604.06 Denial of an Exception Permit

Effective 2015-02-13. Earlier expiration 2000-03-31.

Saf-C 2604.06 Denial of an Exception Permit. (a) Each application for an exception permit shall be reviewed by the commissioner in accordance with RSA 541-A:29. (b) The applicant shall be notified of any apparent errors or omissions in the application and allowed to resubmit the application. (c) The commissioner shall deny an exception permit pursuant to RSA 160-B:12 if: (1) The fireworks are not to be used for agricultural, railroad or military purposes; (2) The applicant failed to provide evidence of local approval pursuant to Saf-C 2604.04; (3) The applicant received local approval, however, the approval was subsequently withdrawn; (4) The applicant made a false statement on the exception permit application; or (5) The date the fireworks will be displayed falls between July 1 and July 8.

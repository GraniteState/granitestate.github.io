Saf-C 513.13 Commercial Plate

Effective 2023-01-13. Earlier expiration 2012-03-15.

Saf-C 513.13 Commercial Plate. (a) Commercial plates shall be issued to commercial motor vehicles, as defined in RSA 259:12-e, excluding those plates issued pursuant to Saf-C 513.09. (b) Commercial plates shall: (1) Have 3 to 6 characters, one of which shall be the letter "A", one of which shall be a letter, and the remainder of which shall be numerals; (2) If a numerical commercial plate: a. Have the letters "COMM" on the left vertical border; and b. Have a numeral from one to 999; or (3) If a vanity plate, have characters that conform with the requirements of Saf-C 513.62(c). Source. #7972, eff 10-17-03 (See Revision Note #1 at chapter heading for Saf-C 500); ss by #9995, INTERIM, eff 9-17-11, EXPIRED 3-15-12 New. #10135, eff 5-23-12; ss by #13521, eff 1-13-23 (formerly Saf-C 514.13)

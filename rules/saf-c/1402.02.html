Saf-C 1402.02 Responsibility

Effective 2024-11-28. Expires 2034-11-28.

Saf-C 1402.02 Responsibility. (a) The responsibility for the safe installation of a carnival or amusement ride shall rest with the owner or operator. (b) The owner or operator shall provide safety devices, a safe work environment, and safety equipment which meet all state and federal regulations including but not limited to hard hats, body harnesses, and electrical installations and equipment that conform to the National Electrical Code (NEC), NFPA 70, published by the National Fire Protection Association (NFPA), as codified pursuant to RSA 155-A.

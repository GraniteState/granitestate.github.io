Saf-C 2702.07 Emergency lights

Effective 2021-06-16. Earlier expiration 2004-01-26.

Saf-C 2702.07 "Emergency lights" means lamps which emit a flashing or rotating light beam to identify an authorized vehicle responding to a hospital emergency.

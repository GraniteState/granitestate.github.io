Saf-C 9702.04 Dashboard camera

Effective 2022-04-18. Earlier expiration 2022-07-28.

Saf-C 9702.04 “Dashboard camera” means a forward-facing video recording device installed on the dashboard of a law enforcement vehicle. Source. #13338, INTERIM, eff 1-29-22, EXPIRES: 7-28-22; ss by #13365, eff 4-18-22

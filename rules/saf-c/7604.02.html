Saf-C 7604.02 Availability of ICS Training

Effective 2021-08-24.

Saf-C 7604.02 Availability of ICS Training. Information about various ICS training offered through the New Hampshire division of fire standards and emergency medical services shall be available at https://nhfa-ems.com/?s=ICS.

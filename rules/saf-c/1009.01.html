Saf-C 1009.01 High Risk Driver

Effective 2023-08-05.

Saf-C 1009.01 High Risk Driver. For the purposes of this section, "high risk driver" means a driver who, because of an apparent physical or psychological impairment or condition, needs a reexamination in order to determine their fitness to safely drive a motor vehicle so as not to endanger the public. Source. #8109, eff 6-24-04; ss by #10176, eff 8-23-12; ss by #13685, eff 8-5-23 (see Revision Note at chapter heading for Saf-C 1000)

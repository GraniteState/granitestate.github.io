Saf-C 804.01 Application, Data Required

Effective 2019-05-02. Earlier expiration 2013-07-22.

Saf-C 804.01 Application, Data Required. (a) Any person applying for permission to connect an alarm shall furnish to the receiving agency the following: (1) The name, address, and telephone number of the subscriber; (2) The type of protection afforded by the alarm, whether burglary or fire; (3) The name, address, and telephone number of 2 persons to be called in the event of alarm activation; (4) Whether the alarm is monitored by any other person or agency, and if so, the name, address, and telephone number of same; (5) The name, address, and telephone number of the alarm service company; (6) The subscriber’s signature; and (7) The approving officer's signature. (b) The information above shall be provided on a form prescribed by the receiving agency. The department shall make available standardized application forms which the receiving agency may utilize.

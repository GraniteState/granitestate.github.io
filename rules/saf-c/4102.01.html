Saf-C 4102.01 Application for Certification for Additional Truck Weights

Effective 2016-03-05. Expires 2016-09-01.

Saf-C 4102.01 Application for Certification for Additional Truck Weights. (a) An owner or lessee of a vehicle shall be eligible for an additional registration which authorizes the driving of a vehicle in accordance with the limits established by RSA 266:18, Table III and RSA 266:18-b. (b) Each applicant for certification for additional truck weights shall submit form RDMV 105 to the department, along with the appropriate fee set forth in RSA 266:18-d, II. (c) Each applicant shall furnish the following on form RDMV 105: (1) Name and address of owner or lessee; (2) Date of birth of owner or lessee; (3) Plate number, plate type and date of expiration; (4) Name and address of manufacturer of the vehicle; (5) Year of manufacture; (6) Make; (7) Model name or number; (8) Vehicle identification number; (9) Body style; (10) Number of axles; (11) Registered gross weight of vehicle; (12) Total registration weight sought for the vehicle; and (13) Certification by the applicant that the: a. Information contained on the form is correct; b. Vehicle has not been altered in a way that would reduce the gross vehicle weight rating; and c. Chassis, axles, tires, rims, brakes, steering components and suspension systems are maintained in good working order; (14) Certification from the final stage manufacturer, subject to the penalties of unsworn falsification pursuant to RSA 641:3, that the vehicle has a specified maximum gross weight or axle weight capacity at the time of manufacture as determined by the components and the summation of the manufacturer's axle design limits for each axle of the vehicle, including the specific gross vehicle weight rating and weight limits for each axle; (15) Printed name, signature and title of final stage manufacturer or representative; (16) Date of signature of final stage manufacturer; (17) Certification from the owner or lessee, subject to the penalties of unsworn falsification pursuant to RSA 641:3, as follows: “I hereby certify that the information contained on this form is correct, that the vehicle has not been altered in a way that would reduce the gross vehicle weight rating and that the chassis, axles, tires, rims, brakes, steering components and suspension systems are maintained in good working order. The power unit and trailer shall be required at all times to have a current inspection sticker or decal from an official inspection station. If a vehicle is declared out of service for a safety violation or is issued a citation for an axle distance violation, the certification shall be inoperative until the condition has been rectified.”; and (18) Signature of owner or lessee.

Saf-C 5504.06 Address Verification

Effective 2022-12-09.

Saf-C 5504.06 Address Verification. (a) Pursuant to RSA 651-B:3, III, semi-annually, the department shall verify the address at which the sexual offender or offender against children resides and shall notify the offender of the obligation to register in person. (b) Address verification shall be made by: (1) In-person contact at the home or residence of the offender by the department or local law enforcement agency; or (2) Mailing, by certified non-forwarding mail, an address verification letter to the residential address of the offender’s home or residence. (c) The offender shall sign the address verification proof of service form and return it to the officer if the address verification was made in person. (d) If the address verification letter was sent by mail, pursuant to (b)(2) above, the offender shall sign the address verification form and mail it to the department within 10 business days of receipt. (e) Notwithstanding any rule to the contrary, for sex offenders or offenders against children who are sentenced to an extended term of imprisonment pursuant to RSA 651:6, I(b), the following shall apply: (1) The department shall mail a nonforwardable address verification letter to the offender’s last reported address every 90 days after the date of the offender’s initial release or commencement of parole; (2) The offender shall state that the offender resides at the address last reported to the local law enforcement agency; and (3) The offender shall sign the verification form and mail it to the department within 10 days of receipt of the form. Source. #10121, eff 4-25-12; ss by #13504, eff 12-9-22

Saf-C 5606.04 Unauthorized Use of Personal Information

Effective 2003-01-01.

Saf-C 5606.04 Unauthorized Use of Personal Information. (a) Pursuant to RSA 263:12, X, no person shall scan, record, retain, or store, in any electronic form or format, personal information obtained from any license, unless authorized by the department. (b) Pursuant to RSA 263:12, anyone who knowingly violates (b) above shall be guilty of a misdemeanor. In addition, a person may be subject to penalties as set forth in RSA 260:14. Source. #7813, EXEMPT, eff 1-1-03

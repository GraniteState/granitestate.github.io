Saf-C 8401.02 Scope

Effective 2025-08-14. Expires 2035-08-14.

Saf-C 8401.02 Scope. (a) The rules shall apply to all applicants, contact persons, participating persons, and law enforcement agencies relative to the contact person notification program. (b) The following people may apply to the contact person notification program: (1) A person with a disability; (2) A parent applying on behalf of a minor who has a disability; and (3) A legal guardian applying on behalf of a person with a disability.

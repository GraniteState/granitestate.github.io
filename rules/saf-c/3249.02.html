Saf-C 3249.02 Mobile Service Garage Inspection Station Authorization

Effective 2024-04-05. Earlier expiration 2024-02-15.

Saf-C 3249.02 Mobile Service Garage Inspection Station Authorization. The director shall authorize as an inspection station a mobile service garage that is contracted with employers, provided: (a) Inspections shall only be performed while the mobile service garage is on an employer’s premises; (b) Inspections shall be performed solely on vehicles registered to employees of the contracted employer or registered to the employer; and (c) Each inspection station shall conspicuously post on the outside of its vehicle or trailer: (1) In a form and size approved by the director, that it is an authorized inspection station; and (2) A sign showing the inspection fee charged and the additional fee charged for the OBD II testing under RSA 266:59-b, provided that no authorized inspection station shall include the cost of the OBD II testing and reporting in the inspection fee charged for a vehicle not subject to the OBD II requirements.

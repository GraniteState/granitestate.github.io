Saf-C 3309.06 Issuance of State Labels

Effective 2019-03-08. Earlier expiration 2007-05-17.

Saf-C 3309.06 Issuance of State Labels. (a) Labels shall be issued by the department. (b) Agencies shall order labels from the department by completing, signing, and submitting the “Request & Payment for New Hampshire Labels” form DSFM-115, revised 2/2024. (c) Labels shall be sent to the agency, return receipt requested. (d) Upon issuing a label, the agency shall forward the label fee, paid for by the manufacturer in accordance with Saf-C 3309.11, to the department.

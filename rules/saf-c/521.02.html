Saf-C 521.02 Non-Renewal Upon Report

Effective 2023-01-13. Earlier expiration 2009-07-27.

Saf-C 521.02 Non-Renewal Upon Report. (a) On receipt of a report from the commissioner of the department of transportation or their designee, indicating that the owner of a vehicle has evaded tolls or charges in violation of RSA 236:31, the director of motor vehicles shall forward a notice of non-renewal to such owner by first class mail. (b) The report in (a) above shall be submitted on official letterhead and include the following: (1) The unique identification number corresponding to the name and plate associated with a violation; and (2) The identity of the owner of the vehicle that has evaded tolls or charges. (c) The effective date of such non-renewal shall be 30 days subsequent to its issuance. (d) The notice of non-renewal shall include: (1) The reasons for the non-renewal order; (2) The effective date of the non-renewal and the authority therefor; (3) The administrative fine assessed; (4) A statement indicating that the owner may request an administrative hearing no later than 30 days from the date of the notice; and (5) Motor vehicle registration number.

Saf-C 5029.03 Application for Display Permit

Effective 2019-04-30.

Saf-C 5029.03 Application for Display Permit. (a) Pursuant to RSA 160-B:7, III, each applicant for a display permit shall first complete and submit application for a permit for the display of display fireworks on form DSFM 166, “Application for Permit for the Display of Display Fireworks”, revised 01/19, to the local fire chief. (b) The applicant shall sign and date the application to certify that he or she has read the foregoing application and affirms that every statement contained therein is true and correctly set forth, under penalty of unsworn falsification. (c) A site plan containing the following information shall accompany the DSFM 166 application: (1) The dimensions of the following: a. The discharge site; b. The spectator viewing areas; c. The parking areas; d. The fallout areas; and e. All the separation distances; and (2) Location of buried utilities, if trenches or holes are to be dug in the ground to place mortars. (d) The local fire chief shall indicate on form DSFM 166 whether he or she recommends: (1) The issuance of the display permit and any other conditions imposed; or (2) Denial of the display permits and the reasons therefore. (e) The chief of police, licensing board, or governing body of the municipality shall sign and date DSFM 166, acknowledging that the signatory has reviewed the application and determined whether the permit is granted as outlined in the application, or denied. (f) An approved DSFM 166 shall be submitted to the Fire Marshal’s Office at least 3 business days prior to the intended date of display. Source. #9765-B, eff 8-2-10 (from Saf-C 5007.02); ss by #12124, eff 3-6-17; ss by #12738, eff 4-30-19

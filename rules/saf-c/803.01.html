Saf-C 803.01 Message Content and Format

Effective 2019-05-02. Earlier expiration 2013-07-22.

Saf-C 803.01 Message Content and Format. (a) The alarm system shall transmit a pre-recorded message to the receiving agency in the format prescribed in (b) below. (b) The message of the tape shall: (1) Be of a standard format as prescribed by the receiving agency; and (2) Contain a specific description of the address of the source of the alarm, including directions of how to get the address.

Saf-C 1408.03 Fees

Effective 2024-11-28. Expires 2034-11-28.

Saf-C 1408.03 Fees. (a) Pursuant to RSA 321-A:5, I, the annual registration fee for a carnival or amusement ride shall be as provided in Table 1408.1, which shall include the decal fee authorized by RSA 321-A:4. A fiscal year for the purposes of Table 1408.1 shall begin on July 1 and end on the following June 30 in the year indicated in the table, for example, FY 25 begins on July 1, 2024 and ends on June 30, 2025. (b) The registration fee shall be paid for each carnival or amusement ride registered. (c) Each registration shall be valid from January to December of each calendar year.

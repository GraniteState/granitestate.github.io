Saf-C 1410.16 Staff and Duties

Effective 2024-11-28. Expires 2034-11-28.

Saf-C 1410.16 Staff and Duties. (a) The minimum age for staff shall be 21. (b) To qualify as jump master for a bungee jumping site, a person shall supervise 500 complete jumps under supervision of a jump master and have complete knowledge of all aspects of the operation. The jumps shall be documented and shall indicate the date, time, and location of the jump. Documentation shall be available for inspection during the business owner’s hours of operation. (c) The staff of a bungee jumping operation shall include at least 4 persons, with the following responsibilities: (1) The jump master who shall: a. Have control over the operation and shall be responsible and accountable for the operation of the site; b. Take the jumper through the final stages of preparation to the jump take-off; c. Be responsible for the training of other staff and shall have a thorough knowledge of the site, equipment, procedures, and staff; and d. Be responsible for checking selection of the bungee cord and adjusting the rigging at each jump platform; (2) The jump operator who shall: a. Assist the jump master to prepare the jumper; b. Assist the jumper into harnesses, safety belts, and attach the jumper to rigging; c. Operate the lowering system; and d. Carry out landing/recovery operator duties and assist in controlling the public; (3) The landing operator who shall assist: a. The jumper to land on the landing pad or air bag; b. The jumper to the recovery area; and c. In controlling the public; and (4) The registration clerk’s duties shall include: a. Registration of the jumper; b. Weighing of the jumper; c. Controlling movement of a jumper to the jump platform; and d. Controlling or assisting to control the public. Source.

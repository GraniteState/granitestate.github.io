Saf-C 5602.08 Bulk Requests

Effective 2020-12-31.

Saf-C 5602.08 Bulk Requests. (a) The following authorized users may request bulk records from the department: (1) Motor vehicle manufacturers, or their authorized agents, for the purposes specified in RSA 260:14, IV(a)(1); (2) Insurance companies authorized to write automobile policies in New Hampshire, self-insured entities, or their authorized agents for the purposes specified in RSA 260:14, IV(a)(2); (3) Legitimate businesses as defined in Saf-C 5601.08, for the purposes specified in RSA 260:14, V(a)(1), (4), (8) and (9), but subject to the limitations of RSA 260:14, VI; and (4) Life insurance companies authorized to write life insurance policies in New Hampshire, or their authorized agents for the purposes specified in RSA 260:14, V(a)(10). (b) Requestors shall complete form DSMV 494 and submit it to the department along with the materials required in Saf-C 5602.09(a). (c) Upon receipt of form DSMV 494 and the materials required by Saf-C 5602.09(a), the commissioner shall review the request to determine whether it should be approved. If the request is approved by the commissioner pursuant RSA 260:14 and these rules, then the requestor shall comply with Saf-C 5602.09(b). If the request is not approved under RSA 260:14 and these rules, then the requestor shall be notified that the request is denied. (d) The commissioner shall determine the scope and mode of access to motor vehicle records based on the information submitted in form DSMV 494. (e) All requestors authorized to obtain motor vehicle records pursuant to (a)(4) above shall comply with Saf-C 5602.07(h).

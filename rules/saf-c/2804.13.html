Saf-C 2804.13 Appeal

Effective 2015-07-16.

Saf-C 2804.13 Appeal. (a) The recommendation resulting from an administrative review or hearing shall be final 10 days after its issuance, unless a petition for review is filed pursuant to RSA 265-A:33. (b) If a petition for review is filed, then: (1) The director’s denial shall make the recommendation final; or (2) The recommendation of the hearings examiner on the remanded issues shall be final 10 days after issuance, unless a review petition is filed by the law enforcement officer or the person whose license has been suspended or revoked. Source. #8782-A, eff 1-1-07 (formerly Saf-C 2804.09); ss by #10888, eff 7-16-15

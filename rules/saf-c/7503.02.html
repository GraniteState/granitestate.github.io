Saf-C 7503.02 Restoration: Reckless Driving

Effective 2024-04-24. Earlier expiration 2005-03-24.

Saf-C 7503.02 Restoration: Reckless Driving. Any person whose driver's license or driving privilege has been revoked pursuant to RSA 265:79, shall have such license or privilege restored if the following conditions are met: (a) The period of revocation imposed by the court has expired; (b) In a case involving reciprocity, the revocation imposed by the department has expired; (c) Any revoked license has been physically surrendered to the division as required or the respondent has notified the department that the license was lost or stolen; (d) There is no evidence of continued driving of a motor vehicle in this state during the revocation period; (e) A license application and fee are on file with the division if the driver's license has expired or if the revokee is a new resident; (f) The fee, required pursuant to RSA 263:42, V, has been paid; (g) The person's record has been reviewed to assure complete compliance with applicable statutes and rules; (h) All court fines have been paid and defaults cleared; and (i) There are no other revocations or suspensions in effect under any other statute or rule including reciprocity agreements and requirements.

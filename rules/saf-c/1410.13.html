Saf-C 1410.13 Site Requirements

Effective 2024-11-28. Expires 2034-11-28.

Saf-C 1410.13 Site Requirements. (a) The equipment shall be stored to protect it from physical, chemical, and ultra-violet ray damage. The storage area shall be secured against unauthorized entry. (b) There shall be a public address system capable of operation during all hours of business. There shall be a telecommunication link between the platform and the landing area. (c) There shall be a means of telecommunication within 200 feet of the operation to local emergency services. (d) Jumps shall only be conducted under the direct control of a jump master. (e) Adjustments for the weight of each jumper shall be made by the jump master’s selection of bungee cord and length of webbing or rope attached to the bungee cord. (f) A sign shall be erected at the place of jumper registration listing the age restrictions for jumpers. The sign shall be clearly visible. Source.

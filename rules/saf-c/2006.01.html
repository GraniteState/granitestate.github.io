Saf-C 2006.01 Repairer

Effective 2016-01-29. Earlier expiration 2006-08-17.

Saf-C 2006.01 Repairer. (a) The department shall use the following criteria to determine whether a repairer is actively and regularly engaged in business pursuant to RSA 259:87: (1) The amount and percentage of total income generated at any single business location; (2) The volume of business generated at any single business location; and (3) The percentage and amount of assets generated at any single business location.

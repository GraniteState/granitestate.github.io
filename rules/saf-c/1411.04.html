Saf-C 1411.04 Registration

Effective 2024-11-28. Expires 2034-11-28.

Saf-C 1411.04 Registration. (a) Each owner or operator of a challenge course or zip line shall register the challenge course or zip line by completing and submitting both form “Challenge Course/Zip Line Registration” (rev. 11/24), with the required fees, pursuant to Saf-C 1408.03, and form DSCO 368 “Challenge Course/Zip Line Operator’s Annual Inspection Report” (rev. 11/24), and shall certify the following by the owner’s or operator’s signature, printed name, and date on form DSCO 368 “Challenge Course/Zip Line Operator’s Annual Inspection Report” (rev. 11/24): “I certify that I have made an inspection of the amusement ride/recreational rides and devices listed above on the date referenced below and find that each complied with the Rules, Regulations and Code of New Hampshire RSA 321-A, and that to the best of my knowledge, they are safe to operate for the public, unless noted in the inspection results column. I also certify that NDT requirements, as set forth in Saf-C 1405.08, have been completed.” (b) A qualified person, in accordance with either the “ANSI/ACCT 03-2019 CHALLENGE COURSES AND CANOPY/ZIP LINE TOURS STANDARDS” (Revision of ANSI/ACCT 03-2016 Standards) or the ASTM International standards listed in Saf-C 1402.01(a), available as noted in Appendix B, shall submit an inspection report, each year, as part of registration, that demonstrates that all elements of the challenge course or zip line are in accordance with either the “ANSI/ACCT 03-2019 CHALLENGE COURSES AND CANOPY/ZIP LINE TOURS STANDARDS” (Revision of ANSI/ACCT 03-2016 Standards), or the ASTM International standards listed in Saf-C 1402.01(a), available as noted in Appendix B, and shall certify the following by the qualified person’s signature, printed name, and date on form DSCO 368 “Challenge Course/Zip Line Operator’s Annual Inspection Report” (rev. 11/24): “I certify that all elements of the challenge course(s) or zip line(s) listed above are in accordance with either the “ANSI/ACCT 03-2019 CHALLENGE COURSES AND CANOPY/ZIP LINE TOURS STANDARDS” (Revision of ANSI/ACCT 03-2016 Standards), or the ASTM International standards listed in Saf-C 1402.01(a)”. (c) The qualified person conducting the inspection and making the certification required by Saf-C 1411.04(b) above shall attach their inspection report(s) to form DSCO 368 “Challenge Course/Zip Line Operator’s Annual Inspection Report” (rev. 11/24) upon which they have made their certification. (d) For registration purposes, each set of contiguous elements shall represent a single course and be considered a single ride or device subject to the requirements of Saf-C 1408.03.

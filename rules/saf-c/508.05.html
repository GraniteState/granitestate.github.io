Saf-C 508.05 Application Approval

Effective 2023-01-13.

Saf-C 508.05 Application Approval. (a) An application for an initial or vanity registration plate shall be approved if the requested combination of characters conforms with the requirements of Saf-C 513.62(c) and it is not currently in use. (b) Upon approval, the department shall have permanent initial or vanity registration plates manufactured and forward them to the registrant. Source. #12581, eff 7-20-18 (formerly Saf-C 509.04); ss by #12914, eff 11-6-19; ss by #13521, eff 1-13-23 (formerly Saf-C 509.05)

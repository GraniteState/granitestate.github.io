Saf-C 2608.03 Period of Suspension

Effective 2015-02-13.

Saf-C 2608.03 Period of Suspension. (a) The suspension period for a single violation of RSA 160-C:2, II within a 12-month period shall be a minimum of 10 consecutive days, but no longer than 14 days. (b) The suspension period for any violation set forth in Saf-C 2608.02 , or a subsequent violation of RSA 160-C:2, II in a 12-month period, shall be a maximum of 5 years. (c) The length of the suspension period shall correlate directly with: (1) The seriousness of the offense; and (2) Whether there are aggravating factors. (d) For the purposes of (c)(2) above, aggravating factors shall include: (1) Previous license suspensions pursuant to Saf-C 2608.02; (2) Previous non-compliance with any laws, rules, or regulations related to fireworks; (3) Actions which the licensee knew or should have known were contrary to any laws, rules, or regulations related to fireworks; and (4) Actions by the licensee which create a hazard to public safety. (e) Notwithstanding any rule to the contrary, any licensee found to be selling permissible fireworks after the person’s license has been suspended pursuant to RSA 160-C:8, and Saf-C 2608.05 below, shall not have the license reinstated for a minimum of one year from the date of the immediate suspension.

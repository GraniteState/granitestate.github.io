Saf-C 3100 DRIVER EDUCATION RULES

CHAPTER Saf-C 3100 DRIVER EDUCATION RULES Statutory Authority: RSA 21-P:14, I(a) - (h) and RSA 263:45. REVISION NOTE: Document #12509, effective 4-5-18, readopted, readopted with amendments, or repealed the rules Chapter Saf-C 3100 on driver education. The former rules, prior to Document #12509, had last been filed under Document #9568, effective 10-17-09. These rules did not expire on 10-17-17 but were extended pursuant to RSA 541-A:14-a until replaced by the rules filed under Document #12509, effective 4-5-18. Document #12509 replaces all prior filings for the former rules in Chapter Saf-C 3100. The changes made by Document #12509 in Chapter Saf-C 3100 included the insertion of a new definition in Saf-C 3102.05 of “commercial motor vehicle”, the repeal of the definition in Saf-C 3102.09 of “drivers’ school”, and the replacement in other rules of references to “drivers’ school” and “commercial drivers’ school” with “approved drivers’ school”.

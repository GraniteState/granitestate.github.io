Saf-C 2606.02 Application for a Local Permit to Sell Permissible Fireworks

Effective 2015-02-13. Earlier expiration 2000-03-31.

Saf-C 2606.02 Application for a Local Permit to Sell Permissible Fireworks. (a) Each applicant for a local permit to sell permissible fireworks shall obtain a permit application from each municipality in which the person intends to sell or market permissible fireworks. (b) Each applicant for a local permit to sell permissible fireworks shall submit the following on the permit application: (1) Seller’s name, address and date of birth; (2) Business name and address; (3) That only permissible fireworks will be sold or marketed; and (4) Location(s), address(es) and physical description of all storage areas, including a site plan.

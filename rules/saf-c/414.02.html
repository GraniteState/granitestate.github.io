Saf-C 414.02 Water Event or Slalom Course Permit Application

Effective 2013-03-20. Earlier expiration 2004-12-19.

Saf-C 414.02 Water Event or Slalom Course Permit Application. (a) An applicant for a permit to conduct a water event or slalom course shall provide the commissioner with the following: (1) Name, address, date of birth, telephone number and cellular telephone number, if available, of the applicant; (2) The type of water event; (3) The name of the water event, if applicable; (4) The name of the public water on which the water event will be conducted; (5) The name of the town or city where the public water is located; (6) The date(s) the water event will be conducted; (7) The start and end time of the water event each day it is conducted; (8) A brief description of the water event; (9) The number of participants in the water event; (10) A map or sketch of the water event area, including the direction "north" as a reference; and (11) Signature of the applicant and date signed.

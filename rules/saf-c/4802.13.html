Saf-C 4802.13 Refinery

Effective 2020-04-20.

Saf-C 4802.13 “Refinery” means any facility used to process crude oil, unfinished oils, natural gas liquids or other hydrocarbons into any accountable product/motor fuel. Source. #10022, eff 11-9-11 (from Saf-C 4802.05); ss by #13039, eff 4-20-20 (formerly Saf-C 4802.12)

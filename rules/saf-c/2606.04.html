Saf-C 2606.04 Denial of Permit to Sell Permissible Fireworks by Municipality

Effective 2015-02-13. Earlier expiration 2000-03-31.

Saf-C 2606.04 Denial of Permit to Sell Permissible Fireworks by Municipality. (a) The municipality shall deny a permit pursuant to RSA 160-C:3 I, if: (1) The applicant does not have a valid permit for the sale of fireworks, pursuant to title 18 of the United States Code; (2) The applicant does not have a place of business within the municipality; (3) The business will not be located in a permanent structure; (4) The building in which the business will be located does not comply with applicable fire safety codes, building codes, zoning codes and local ordinances, including all applicable provisions of the state building code and the state fire code pursuant to Saf-C 6000; (5) The applicant has been: a. Convicted of any offense involving fireworks or explosives within 2 years prior to the date of application which has not been annulled; or b. Found to have violated fireworks or explosive laws or rules within 2 years prior to the date of application; (6) The applicant is younger than 21 years of age; or (7) Storage does not comply with applicable federal, state or local laws and rules for the storage of fireworks and related materials. Source. #5752, eff 12-2-93; ss by INTERIM #7140, eff 12-2-99, EXPIRED: 3-31-00 New. #7905, eff 6-24-03; ss by #8519, eff 12-16-05; ss by #10784, eff 2-13-15

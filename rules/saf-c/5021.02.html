Saf-C 5021.02 Position of Mortars

Effective 2019-04-30. Earlier expiration 2005-01-10.

Saf-C 5021.02 Position of Mortars. Mortars shall be positioned so that the shells are carried away from spectators and into a clear area, with the projectile going into the air as nearly vertical as possible. Source.

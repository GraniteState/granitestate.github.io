Saf-C 6003.04 Fire Investigators and Fire Inspectors

Effective 2020-08-25.

Saf-C 6003.04 Fire Investigators and Fire Inspectors. (a) Fire investigators and fire inspectors, under the direction of the state fire marshal and under the supervision of the deputy fire marshals, shall: (1) Investigate fires, structural building collapses, and incidents involving the release of carbon monoxide, other than from a motor vehicle, in accordance with procedures established by the state fire marshal; (2) Inspect state-owned buildings and other buildings in accordance with procedures established by the state fire marshal; (3) Assist local fire officials in the promulgation and enforcement of local fire codes; (4) Review architectural and engineering plans and specifications for compliance with the state fire code; and (5) Perform such other duties as directed by the state fire marshal or deputy fire marshals. Source. (See Revision Note #1 and Revision Note #2 at chapter heading for Saf-C 6000) #13094, eff 8-25-20

Saf-C 5023.01 Electrical System Requirements

Effective 2017-03-06. Earlier expiration 2009-04-25.

Saf-C 5023.01 Electrical System Requirements. All electrical firing systems shall be in accordance with NFPA 1123, Code for Display Fireworks, 2014 edition, available as noted in Appendix B. Source.

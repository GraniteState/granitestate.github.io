Saf-C 5605.23 Written Authorization

Effective 2020-12-31.

Saf-C 5605.23 Written Authorization. (a) For the purposes of RSA 260:14, V(a)(10), a written authorization shall be submitted on letterhead. (b) The request shall contain the following: (1) Date of execution; (2) Name of life insurance company authorized to write life insurance policies in the state of New Hampshire, or its authorized agent; (3) The following information about the individual whose record is being sought: a. Name; b. Address; c. Date of birth; d. Driver license number; and e. Signature of the individual whose record is being sought. (c) Approved written authorization shall be retained by the life insurance company, or its authorized agent, for a period not less than 5 years. (d) A life insurance company, or its authorized agent, shall produce approved written authorization upon request by the department, or its authorized agent. Source. #7621, EXEMPT, eff 1-1-02; ss by #13152, EXEMPT, eff 12-31-20

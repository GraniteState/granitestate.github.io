Saf-C 701.01 Chapter Definitions

Effective 2015-07-16. Earlier expiration 2005-03-25.

Saf-C 701.01 Chapter Definitions. The following terms shall be construed as set forth unless the text clearly indicates otherwise: (a) "Applicant" means a person intending to fulfill the statutory and administrative requirements to obtain an appointment within the meaning of RSA 381:2; (b) "Commissioner" means the commissioner of the department of safety or a designee thereof; (c) "Department" means the New Hampshire department of safety; (d) "Officer" means a person holding a valid commission as a railroad police officer; and (e) "Railroad" means “railroad” as defined in RSA 381:1.

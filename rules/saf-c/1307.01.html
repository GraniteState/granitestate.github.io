Saf-C 1307.01 Inspection of School Buses

Effective 2024-06-22. Expires 2034-06-22.

Saf-C 1307.01 Inspection of School Buses. All school bus inspections required by RSA 266:7 shall be conducted in accordance with the inspection criteria set forth in Saf-C 1315.

Saf-C 1400 CARNIVAL-AMUSEMENT RULES (RSA 321-A:2)

CHAPTER Saf-C 1400 CARNIVAL-AMUSEMENT RULES (RSA 321-A:2) REVISION NOTE #1: Document #5607, effective 4-2-93, adopted rules relative to bungee jumping. Document #5607 contains a new part numbered Saf-C 1410 and new and amended rules in Saf-C 1401. The insertion of the new rules in Saf-C 1401 required the renumbering editorially of most of the new and existing rules in Saf-C 1401. The text of the existing rules in Saf-C 1401 was not included in Document #5607, and therefore the effective dates for these existing rules remain unchanged by Document #5607. Document #5445, effective 8-12-92, contained emergency rules relative to bungee jumping, but these emergency rules EXPIRED: 12-10-92. Document #5445 contained new and amended rules in Saf-C 1401 and a new part numbered Saf-C 1410. REVISION NOTE #2: Document #10507, effective 1-21-14, adopted rules in Saf-C 1400 which had expired and readopted or readopted with amendments other existing rules in Saf-C 1400. Those rules with source notes which indicate that the rules or selected paragraphs had previously been filed under Document #8342, effective 4-28-05, and had expired 1-2-14, had provisions which had been subject to extension pursuant to RSA 541-A:14-a, I past their normal expiration date of 4-28-13. They had expired pursuant to RSA 541-A:14-a, II and III(a) because the rules had not been adopted by the Department of Safety within 30 days after the agency was allowed to adopt under RSA 541-A:14, I. REVISION NOTE #3: Document #14139, effective 11-28-24, readopted or readopted with amendment all of the rules in Chapter Saf-C 1400. Amendments included the renumbering of various rules as indicated in the source notes. The reserved number Saf-C 1406.02 was deleted as an editorial change during the certification process pursuant to RSA 541-A:15, I. Document #14139 replaces all prior filings for rules affecting Chapter Saf-C 1400. The former rule Saf-C 1406.02 had been titled “Requirements for Inspectors Acceptable to the Commissioner” and was repealed by Document #8145, INTERIM, effective 8-25-04. The prior filings before Document #8145 affecting the repealed Saf-C 1406.02 included the following documents: #2229, eff 12-31-82 #2950, eff 1-3-85 #4531, eff 1-1-89 #5276, eff 11-20-91 #5940, INTERIM, eff 1-1-95 #5952, eff 1-24-95, EXPIRED: 1-24-03 #7833, INTERIM, eff 2-22-03, EXPIRED: 8-21-03 #8045, eff 2-20-04

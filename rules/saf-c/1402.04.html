Saf-C 1402.04 Fences Required

Effective 2024-11-28. Expires 2034-11-28.

Saf-C 1402.04 Fences Required. (a) All carnival or amusement rides, except for challenge courses and zip lines, shall be surrounded by fencing in accordance with the requirements listed in Saf-C 1402.01(a), available as noted in Appendix B. (b) Each fence opening shall have a hinged gate adequate to prevent unauthorized persons from inadvertently entering the enclosed area. (c) All gates in the fence surrounding a carnival or amusement ride shall include a latching device that engages when the gate is closed. (d) Fences and gates shall be constructed and installed so as to prevent riders or spectators from touching the carnival or amusement ride and fences or gates at the same time. (e) All fencing and gates shall be: (1) At least 42 inches high; (2) Designed, constructed, and erected to prevent overturning or climbing by riders or spectators; and (3) Constructed so as to prevent passage of a sphere or round object 4 inches in diameter through all openings. (f) Notwithstanding the requirements of (a) above, fencing manufactured before January 1, 1993 and attached to the carnival or amusement ride by the manufacturer may remain in service. (g) Notwithstanding the requirements of (a) above, if a ride is not designed to be fenced, a certification from the manufacturer that states “No fence required” shall be kept in the ride file jacket. (h) Carnival or amusement rides in a fixed location shall be permitted to use, in lieu of fencing, existing physical characteristics which prevent unpermitted intrusion, after an inspection is conducted pursuant to Saf-C 1406.01. (i) For the purpose of (h) above, “physical characteristics” includes trees, shrubs, and water.

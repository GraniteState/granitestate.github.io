Saf-C 2306.01 Vessel Manufacturer or Dealer Registration Applications

Effective 2025-07-16. Expires 2035-07-16.

Saf-C 2306.01 Vessel Manufacturer or Dealer Registration Applications. (a) Pursuant to RSA 270-E:13, a vessel manufacturer or dealer may apply for a general distinguishing number for their vessels. (b) In order to apply for a general distinguishing number, a manufacturer or dealer shall furnish the following to the department: (1) Corporate name and federal identification number of the manufacturer's or dealer's business; (2) Trade name and federal identification number, if applicable; (3) Whether the application is an original or renewal; (4) Legal address; (5) Mailing address; (6) Telephone number; (7) Business hours; (8) Name, address, and date of birth of at least one owner, partner, or officer, and title; (9) Whether the manufacturer or dealer: a. Holds a franchise, and if so, the name of the franchise; b. Furnishes repair service; c. Has complied with all local zoning and regulatory requirements; d. Agrees to notify the department of any change of address or corporate or trade name; and e. Is principally engaged in buying, selling, or manufacturing new or used vessels, or both; (10) If a renewal: a. A list of all vessel numbers issued to the manufacturer or dealer by the department; and b. Whether a change in ownership or location has occurred; and (11) Signature of the person listed in (8) above and date signed. (c) A general distinguishing number shall be used by a manufacturer or dealer pursuant to RSA 270-E:13, II. (d) For the purposes of (b)(10)a. above, the list shall be provided in numerical and alphabetical sequence.

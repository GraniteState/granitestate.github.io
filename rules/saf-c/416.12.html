Saf-C 416.12 Squam Lake and Little Squam Lake

Effective 2017-12-11.

Saf-C 416.12 Squam Lake and Little Squam Lake. (a) There shall be an assisted lake protection program for Squam Lake and Little Squam Lake. (b) The Squam Lakes Association shall assist the department in fulfilling its obligations on Squam Lake and Little Squam Lake with regards to the enforcement of the following statutes and rules: (1) Those provisions of Saf-C 5100, regarding the specific waterbody in question, and that addresses motor boat operators exceeding no wake limits, if any; (2) RSA 270-D:2, I, regarding exceeding headway speed under bridges; (3) RSA 270-E:3, regarding unregistered boats; (4) RSA 270:44 regarding rafting violations; (5) Saf-C 403.11 regarding motor boats equipped with sound reducing devices; (6) RSA 270-E:8 and Saf-C 2306 regarding the improper display of registration; (7) Saf-C 403.01, regarding required life preservers; (8) Saf-C 403.12 regarding required lighting on boats; (9) RSA 270-D:2, VI regarding unsafe passage at less than 150 feet; (10) RSA 270:30-a regarding the transportation of a child under the age of 5 in a boat without the child wearing a life preserver; (11) RSA 270-D:5 regarding overloading; (12) RSA 163-B regarding depositing litter; and (13) RSA 270:29-a regarding the operation of a motor boat in a careless or negligent manner, or in a manner that the lives and safety of the public are endangered. (c) The manner in which assisted protection on Squam Lake and Little Squam Lake shall be implemented as set forth in the order of the department, dated January 29, 2001, as contained in Appendix I.

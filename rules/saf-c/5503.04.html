Saf-C 5503.04 Sexual Offenders Or Offenders Against Children Moving to New Hampshire

Effective 2022-12-09. Earlier expiration 2007-07-28.

Saf-C 5503.04 Sexual Offenders Or Offenders Against Children Moving to New Hampshire. (a) Upon receipt of information from any out-of-state law enforcement agency that a sexual offender or offender against children has moved into New Hampshire, the division shall: (1) Obtain the address at which the sexual offender or offender against children expects to reside and inform the local law enforcement agency; and (2) Locate and serve notice upon the sexual offender or offender against children of his/her duty to report. (b) If notice is served upon the sexual offender or offender against children, the division, pursuant to RSA 651-B: 3, II, shall complete form DSSP 311 and obtain the sexual offender or offender against children’s acknowledgement of the offender’s duty to report. (c) Pursuant to RSA 651-B:4, IV, if not already on file, the division shall obtain the following from the sexual offender or offender against children: (1) A current photograph; (2) A set of fingerprints and palm prints; (3) DNA sample, unless one has already been provided; or (4) A photocopy of a valid driver’s license or identification card. (d) The provisions of this rule shall not apply if the sexual offender or offender against children has already registered with the local law enforcement agency in which the offender resides or is located.

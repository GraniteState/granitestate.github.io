Saf-C 2308.10 Vessel Agent Fees

Effective 2025-07-16. Expires 2035-07-16.

Saf-C 2308.10 Vessel Agent Fees. (a) In addition to the fees set forth in Saf-C 2302.04, each vessel agent may charge the following fees: (1) Pursuant to RSA 270-E:5, II, $5.00 per registration decal; (2) Pursuant to RSA 72-A:4, III, $1 per boat; and (3) Pursuant to RSA 270-E:5-a, II(e), $3.00 per boat fee decal. Source. #5283, eff 11-22-91; ss by #6616, eff 10-25-97, EXPIRED: 10-25-05 New. #8506, INTERIM, eff 12-7-05, EXPIRES: 6-5-06; ss and moved by #8617, eff 4-28-06, (from Saf-C 2308.08) EXPIRED: 1-21-15 (See Revision Note at chapter heading for Saf-C 2300) New. #10787, eff 2-20-15; ss by #14321, eff 7-16-25, EXPIRES: 7-16-35

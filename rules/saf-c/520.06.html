Saf-C 520.06 Renewal of Walking Disability Plates and Placards

Effective 2023-01-13.

Saf-C 520.06 Renewal of Walking Disability Plates and Placards. (a) Walking disability plates shall be renewed in accordance with the requirements of Saf-C 511. (b) Each person who has a walking disability plate or walking disability vanity plate shall submit a form RDMV 130 (rev.8/21) to the department during the month of the applicant’s registration renewal every 5 years. (c) Walking disability placards shall be valid for 5 years from the person’s date of birth, or, if an organization, during the organization’s month of renewal set forth in Saf-C 510.08. (d) Each person who has a permanent walking disability placard shall submit form RDMV 130 (rev.8/21) to the department during the month of the person’s date of birth every 5 years. Source. #7069, eff 8-5-99 (formerly Saf-C 518, see Revision Note at Chapter Heading for Saf-C 500); ss by #8999, eff 9-29-07; ss by #10135, eff 5-23-12; ss by #13521, eff 1-13-23 (formerly Saf-C 521.07)

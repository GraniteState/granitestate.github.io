Saf-C 1407.02 Numbering

Effective 2024-11-28. Expires 2034-11-28.

Saf-C 1407.02 Numbering. The operator of any carnival or amusement ride that is composed of individual carriers, or designee, shall number each unit of the carnival or amusement ride to enable identification of each separate carrier unit. The numbers shall be at least 2 inches in height and shall be placed where they shall remain legible.

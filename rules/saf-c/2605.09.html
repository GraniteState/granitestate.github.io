Saf-C 2605.09 Records of Sale of Display or Consumer Fireworks

Effective 2015-02-13.

Saf-C 2605.09 Records of Sale of Display or Consumer Fireworks. (a) Any person who has a state license pursuant to RSA 160-B, shall only sell display fireworks to a person who shows a certificate of competency issued pursuant to RSA 158:9-f, or who shows a license for the storage of display fireworks issued pursuant to RSA 158:9-c and Saf-C 1600, or who shows a license to sell display and consumer fireworks pursuant to RSA 160-B and this chapter. (b) In the case of a sale of any display or consumer fireworks, the seller shall record the following: (1) The name and address of the purchaser of the display or consumer fireworks; (2) The purchaser’s certificate of competency license number, if applicable; (3) The purchaser’s license number for the storage of display fireworks; (4) In the case of a wholesale sale: a. The purchaser’s retail number from the state of residence; and b. IRS tax identification number; (5) The date of the transfer; (6) The type and quantity of display and consumer fireworks; (7) The serial number of the display and consumer fireworks, if any; and (8) The purpose to which the display and consumer fireworks are to be used. (c) Each record of a sale of display or consumer fireworks shall include: (1) In the case of a sale to a purchaser with a certificate of competency license: a. A copy of the purchaser’s display permit; and b. A copy of the purchaser’s certificate of competency license; c. In the case of a wholesale sale, a copy of the purchaser’s retail license or permit from the state of residence; (2) In the case of a purchaser who shows a license for the storage of display fireworks, a copy of the purchaser’s license to store display fireworks, issued pursuant to RSA 158:9-c and Saf-C 1600. (d) The records pursuant this section shall be kept by the seller for a period of 2 years. Source. #10784, eff 2-13-15

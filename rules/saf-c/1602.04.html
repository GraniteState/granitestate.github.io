Saf-C 1602.04 Bullet-resistant magazine

Effective 2013-08-15. Earlier expiration 2005-01-10.

Saf-C 1602.04 "Bullet-resistant magazine" means: (a) When used in reference to the construction of a magazine, that its side walls and doors are resistant to penetration of a 150 grain full metal case projectile fired from a standard .30-06 caliber rifle having a nominal muzzle velocity of 2,700 feet per second when the bullet is fired at right angles to the side walls or doors from a distance of between 75 and 100 feet. (b) When used in reference to the ceiling of a magazine that the ceiling is constructed to withstand penetration of a bullet, described in (a) above, when fired at an angle of 45 degrees from the perpendicular.

Saf-C 6802.05 Approval

Effective 2018-04-23.

Saf-C 6802.05 Approval. (a) A letter of approval shall be issued from the commissioner to an equipment manufacturer whose preliminary breath test device has met the requirements of Saf-C 6802.01 and Saf-C 6802.02. (b) Any device previously approved by the commissioner prior to adoption of Saf-C 6800 shall be considered valid for purposes of RSA 265-A:15. (c) The commissioner shall maintain a list of preliminary breath test devices approved for use in New Hampshire. This list shall be available to anyone upon request. Source.

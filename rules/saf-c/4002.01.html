Saf-C 4002.01 Applicant

Effective 2024-07-12. Earlier expiration 2024-05-29.

Saf-C 4002.01 “Applicant” means a state, county, or local New Hampshire law enforcement agency that has chosen to apply for grant funding under this chapter. Source. (See Revision Note at chapter heading for Saf-C 4000) #13815, INTERIM, eff 12-1-23, EXPIRES: 5-29-24; ss by #14024, eff 7-12-24

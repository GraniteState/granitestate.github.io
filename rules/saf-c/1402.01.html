Saf-C 1402.01 Standards

Effective 2024-11-28. Expires 2034-11-28.

Saf-C 1402.01 Standards. (a) All carnival or amusement rides, except as set forth in (b) below, shall conform to the standards set forth in the following ASTM International F24 Standards, available as noted in Appendix B: (1) F 747-21a “Standard Terminology Relating to Amusement Rides and Devices” (Oct 2021); (2) F 770-21a “Standard Practice for Ownership, Operation Maintenance, and Inspection of Amusement Rides and Devices” (Oct 2021); (3) F 1159-16E01 “Standard Practice for Design of Amusement Rides and Devices that are Outside the Purview of other F24 Design Standards” (Jan 2017); (4) F 1193-18a “Standard Practice for Quality, Manufacture, and Construction of Amusement Rides and Devices” (Dec 2018); (5) F 1957-99 “Standard Test Method for Composite Foam Hardness-Durometer Hardness” (Feb 2017); (6) F 2007-18 “Standard Practice for Design, Manufacture, and Operation of Concession Go-Karts and Facilities” (Dec 2018); (7) F 2137-19 “Standard Practice for Measuring the Dynamic Characteristics of Amusement Rides and Devices” (Nov 2019); (8) F 2291-21 “Standard Practice for Design of Amusement Rides and Devices” (Jun 2021); (9) F 2374-21a “Standard Practice for Design, Manufacture, Operation and Maintenance of Inflatable Amusement Devices” (Jul 2021); (10) F 2375-09 “Standard Practice for Design, Manufacture, Installation and Testing of Climbing Nets and Netting/Mesh used in Amusement Rides, Devices, Play Areas and Attractions” (Jul 2017); (11) F 2376-21a “Standard Practice for Classification, Design, Manufacture, Construction, and Operation of Water Slide Systems” (Oct 2021); (12) F 2460-19 “Standard Practice for Special Requirements for Bumper Boats” (Nov 2019); (13) F 2959-21 “Standard Practice for Aerial Adventure Courses” (Jun 2021); (14) F 2960-16 “Standard Practice for Permanent Amusement Railway Ride Tracks and Related Devices” (Nov 2016); (15) F 2970-20 “Standard Practice for Design, Manufacture, Installation, Operation, Maintenance, Inspection and Major Modification of Trampoline Courts” (Feb 2020); (16) F 2974-20 “Standard Practice for Auditing Amusement Rides and Devices” (Feb 2020); (17) F 3054-18 “Standard Practice for Operations of Amusement Railway Rides, Devices, and Facilities” (Apr 2018); (18) F 3133-21 “Standard Practice for Classification, Design, Manufacture, Construction, Maintenance, and Operation of Stationary Wave Systems” (Jun 2021); (19) F 3158-21 “Standard Practice for Patron Transportation Conveyors Used with a Water Related Amusement Ride or Device” (Jun 2021); and (20) F 3214-18 “Standard Practice for Characterization of Fire Properties of Materials Specified for Vehicles Associated with Amusement Rides and Devices” (Mar 2018). (b) Challenge courses and zip lines that are not built on concrete or steel footings shall conform to either of the following standards, available as noted in Appendix B: (1) The “ANSI/ACCT 03-2019 CHALLENGE COURSES AND CANOPY/ZIP LINE TOURS STANDARDS” (Revision of ANSI/ACCT 03-2016 Standards); or (2) The ASTM International standards listed in (a) above. (c) Challenge courses and zip lines that are built on concrete or steel footings shall conform to the standards set forth in (a) above. (d) Homemade rides or devices shall be: (1) Built to conform to or exceed the standards set forth in (a) above; and (2) Certified by the designer as meeting such standards.

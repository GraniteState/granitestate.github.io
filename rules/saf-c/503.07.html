Saf-C 503.07 Resident Registration - Address

Effective 2023-01-13. Earlier expiration 2012-03-15.

Saf-C 503.07 Resident Registration - Address. (a) A resident registration shall reflect the registrant's legal address within the State of New Hampshire. (b) In the event the director finds that a non-resident has been issued a New Hampshire registration and plates, the director shall revoke the registration and plates after notice and opportunity for a hearing. (c) In the case of registrants who are temporarily living out of state, the registration shall reflect the out of state address where the vehicle is temporarily garaged.

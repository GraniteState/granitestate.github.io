Saf-C 3206.02 Inspection Area Requirements

Effective 2016-01-06. Earlier expiration 2016-01-12.

Saf-C 3206.02 Inspection Area Requirements. (a) Each inspection station shall: (1) Have a testing space that has no obstructions and is large enough for a mechanic to walk around a vehicle with its doors open; (2) Be a minimum of 12 feet by 25 feet in length, if the station inspects motor vehicles; (3) Be a minimum of 12 feet by 50 feet in length, if the station is equipped with a headlight testing screen or board; and (4) Be a minimum of 8 feet by 12 feet in length, if the station inspects only motorcycles, and have a compliant sound inspection area per Saf-C 3236.03.

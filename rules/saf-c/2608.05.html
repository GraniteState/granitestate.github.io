Saf-C 2608.05 Immediate Suspension

Effective 2015-02-13.

Saf-C 2608.05 Immediate Suspension. (a) The commissioner shall immediately suspend the license to market or sell display and consumer fireworks, permissible fireworks, or both, if after receipt of a complaint, an investigation indicates that a licensee: (1) Has violated Saf-C 2608.02 and such violation results in jeopardizing public health, safety, or welfare; (2) Pursuant to RSA 160-B:14, has violated RSA 160-B or any rules adopted pursuant to that chapter; or (3) Pursuant to RSA 160-C:8, has violated RSA 160-C or any rules adopted pursuant to that chapter. (b) Any person whose license is suspended pursuant to this section or RSA 160-C:8 or RSA 160-B:14 shall be given the opportunity for a hearing within 10 days of the suspension. (c) The scheduling of a hearing shall not delay, postpone, or otherwise interrupt the suspension. (d) All hearings shall be conducted in accordance with Saf-C 203.

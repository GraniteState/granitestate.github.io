Saf-C 4904.01 Application for Original Certificate

Effective 2010-08-03.

Saf-C 4904.01 Application for Original Certificate. (a) An applicant for an original certificate shall submit the following to the state fire marshal’s office: (1) A completed form DSFM 87; (2) The applicable fee; (3) Evidence of 4,000 hours of field experience; and (4) A copy of a certificate evidencing successful completion of the educational and certification requirements, pursuant to Saf-C 4903. (b) An incomplete application shall be returned to the applicant with an explanation as to why the application is being returned. Source. #8650, eff 6-2-06; ss by #9766-A, eff 8-3-10

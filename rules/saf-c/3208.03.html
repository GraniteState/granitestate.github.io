Saf-C 3208.03 Inspection Fee Sign

Effective 2016-01-06. Earlier expiration 2016-01-12.

Saf-C 3208.03 Inspection Fee Sign. (a) Each sign shall be no less than 12 inches in height by 18 inches in length, bearing the words: VEHICLE INSPECTION FEE $________ (b) Each letter and number shall be at least 2 inches in height with at least a 3/8 inch brush stroke. (c) Each inspection station shall enter the appropriate fee in the space provided. (d) More than one sign may be posted. (e) If the inspection fee includes a separate fee for an OBD-II inspection, the inspection station shall break down the 2 fees on one sign, pursuant to RSA 266:1, VII. This sign shall not be subject to the requirements set forth in (b) above. Source.

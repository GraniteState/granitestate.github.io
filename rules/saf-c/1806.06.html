Saf-C 1806.06 Road Skills Examination Vehicle

Effective 2024-10-18. Expires 2034-10-18.

Saf-C 1806.06 Road Skills Examination Vehicle. (a) The applicant shall provide the test vehicle for completion of the road skills examination. (b) Test vehicles shall comply with all New Hampshire inspection laws and rules. (c) The vehicle’s size, weight, character, and equipment shall conform with the class of commercial driver license requested. The weight of the primary vehicle and any pulled unit shall have a manufacturer affixed label indicating the gross vehicle weight or be accompanied by a certificate verifying gross weight. (d) Cargo areas shall be completely empty at the time of the test. (e) Tank vehicles used for transporting hazardous material shall be accompanied by a written certification confirming the tank has been cleaned of all residual hazardous material and purged of all vapors prior to test day. (f) The vehicle shall provide the examiner with a permanently attached seat which allows the examiner accessibility to vehicle control mechanisms. (g) No person shall be allowed to ride in the test vehicle during a road skills examination, other than the applicant, the examiner, and, if applicable, an examiner in training.

Saf-C 504.03 Application, Where Submitted

Effective 2023-01-13.

Saf-C 504.03 Application, Where Submitted. (a) Except as provided hereafter, an application for a motor vehicle, trailer, or semi-trailer registration shall be submitted in person to an approved municipal agent or the division. (b) The following registrations shall be submitted to an approved municipal agent: (1) Antique registration; (2) Disabled veteran's registration; (3) Veteran’s registration; (4) Hearse registration; (5) Construction equipment registration; (6) Walking disability registration; and (7) Moped registration. (c) An application for a disabled veteran's registration pursuant to RSA 261:86 shall include an official certification from the United States government veteran's administration attesting that the applicant meets the criteria as totally or permanently disabled as a result of a war or peace time injury, unless the registration is for a motor vehicle received from the United States government, as a result of a disability. (d) An applicant for a veteran’s registration pursuant to RSA 261:87-b, shall include documentation showing proof of honorable discharge from any branch of the armed services. (e) For the purposes of (d) above, acceptable documentation may include any of the following: (1) Form DD214 from any branch of the armed services; (2) Form DD 217 from any branch of the armed services; (3) Form WDAGO 53-55 from the United States Army; (4) Form WDAGO 53-98 from the United States Army; (5) Form NAVPERS-553 from the United States Naval Service; (6) Form NAVPERS-660 from the United States Naval Service; (7) Form NAVMC 70-PD from the United States Marine Corps; (8) Form NAVMC 78-PD from the United States Marine Corps; (9) Form NAVCG-2510 from the United States Coast Guard; (10) Form GSA 6954 from the National Archives; (11) Form NA 13038 from the National Archives; (12) Form NA 13041 from the National Archives; or (13) Verification of Service letter from the United States Department of Veteran’s Affairs. (f) In the event that a document showing proof of honorable discharge is not listed in (e)(1) through (13) above, the state veteran’s council shall determine the authenticity of the document as proof of honorable discharge. (g) Each applicant for a moped registration shall furnish the following on the prescribed form: (1) Name, mailing address, and legal address; (2) The vehicle's year, make, model, color, and vehicle identification number; and (3) Signature of the applicant. (h) Upon successful completion of a moped registration application, a registration and moped plate shall be issued to the applicant. The moped plate shall conform with the requirements of Saf-C 513.25.

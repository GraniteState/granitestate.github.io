Saf-C 3206.03 Location of Inspections

Effective 2024-04-05. Earlier expiration 2024-02-15.

Saf-C 3206.03 Location of Inspections. (a) All inspections shall be conducted within the approved inspection area. (b) Temporary methods, such as tents or sheds, utilized in order to obtain additional space, shall not be permitted. (c) Off-site inspections shall not be permitted, except as approved by the Director for mobile service garages as defined under RSA 259:56-b. (d) More than one DMV licensed business located in one enclosed building shall be clearly separated by a permanent physical wall. Each shall have an independent separate exterior entrance for the public. (e) No more than one DMV licensed business shall be permitted within one business location without a clearly defined portion of space with definite boundaries and signage so as not to confuse the public. (f) The prohibition in (e) above shall not apply to more than one DMV licensed business within one business location if the business name and owners are the same for both businesses. Source.

Saf-C 5504.03 Notification by Department of Corrections, County Department of Corrections, and Department of Health and Human Services

Effective 2022-12-09.

Saf-C 5504.03 Notification by Department of Corrections, County Department of Corrections, and Department of Health and Human Services. (a) For the purposes of this section, “eligible” means the individual is qualified by applicable rules and laws and a decision has been made to allow the individual to participate in an unsupervised work detail, release into the community following secure psychiatric care, or other assignment which might bring the individual into contact with members of the public. (b) At least once each month, the commissioner of the department of corrections, and the superintendent of each county department of corrections, and the commissioner of the department of health and human services, as applicable, shall, pursuant to RSA 651-B:4,V, forward to the division a statement identifying any sexual offender or offender against children who is confined in a facility under their control and who is eligible at any time for: (1) Any unsupervised work detail; (2) Release into the community following secure psychiatric care; or (3) Other assignment which might bring him or her into contact with members of the public. (c) The statement shall include: (1) All the information set forth in RSA 651-B:4, III; (2) The information in RSA 651-B:4, IV, if the facility has the ability to collect the information; (3) The nature of the unsupervised work detail or other assignment; and (4) The location of the unsupervised work detail or other assignment which might bring the sexual offender or offender against children into contact with members of the public. (d) The commissioners of the department of corrections and department of health and human services, and the superintendent of each county department of corrections, as applicable, shall immediately notify the division when a sexual offender or offender against children is no longer on an unsupervised work detail or other assignment which might bring him or her into contact with members of the public. Source. #10121, eff 4-25-12; ss by #13504, eff 12-9-22

Saf-C 3302.18 National electrical code

Effective 2019-03-08.

Saf-C 3302.18 “National electrical code” means “national electrical code” as defined in RSA 155-A:1, IV. Source. #9752, eff 7-9-10; ss by #9752, eff 7-9-10; ss by #12737, eff 3-8-19

Authority

Saf-C 1404.03 Ride File Jacket

Effective 2024-11-28. Expires 2034-11-28.

Saf-C 1404.03 Ride File Jacket. (a) Each carnival or amusement ride shall have a current ride file jacket. (b) The file shall be kept on the grounds where the carnival or amusement ride is being operated. (c) The ride file jacket shall contain: (1) The non-destructive test report on the testing contractor’s official letterhead; (2) The name of the carnival or amusement ride, the manufacturer, and date of manufacture; (3) Maintenance records; (4) Operator training records; (5) Alteration records; (6) Ride serial number, and, in the case of a homemade ride or device, a serial number assigned by the agent of the commissioner and any information identifying such carnival or amusement ride and its manufacturer, and documentation regarding non-destructive testing requirements; (7) Daily check lists; (8) Agent of the commissioner’s reports; (9) Listing certification of distribution boxes by third party inspection agency or New Hampshire licensed electrical engineer, as applicable; (10) Listing certification of each generator by third party inspection agency or annual certification letter from New Hampshire licensed electrician for each generator as required in Saf-C 1402.10(d); (11) The manufacturer’s English-language owner or operator’s manual or, in the case of a homemade ride, air supported structure, carnival or amusement attraction, an owner or operator’s manual prepared by a state licensed engineer; and (12) Records of the continuity assurance testing that is required in Article 525.32 of the National Electric Code, NFPA 70, 2017 edition published by the National Fire Protection Association (NFPA), as adopted pursuant to RSA 155-A:1 and as amended by the building code review board and ratified by the legislature in accordance with RSA 155-A:10. (d) The ride file jacket shall be presented to the agent of the commissioner upon his or her request.

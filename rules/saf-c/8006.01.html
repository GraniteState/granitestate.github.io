Saf-C 8006.01 Request for Inactive Status of License

Effective 2007-07-21.

Saf-C 8006.01 Request for Inactive Status of License. (a) Pursuant to RSA 153:30, II, a licensed fuel gas fitter who is a member of any reserve component of the armed forces of the United States or the National Guard and is called to active duty on inactive status may request that his or her license be placed on inactive status. (b) All requests shall be sent to the address set forth in Saf-C 8005.03(b) above and shall include government documentation of armed forces activation. Source. #8954, eff 7-21-07

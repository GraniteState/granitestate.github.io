Saf-C 8401.01 Purpose

Effective 2025-08-14. Expires 2035-08-14.

Saf-C 8401.01 Purpose. The purpose of this chapter is to set forth the following: (a) The administrative requirements to implement the contact person notification program, providing law enforcement officers access to information on the person’s disability or contact information for a legal guardian or other individual who can assist law enforcement when communicating with persons who have cognitive impairment or other disability which might prevent or hinder the person’s ability to communicate; (b) The procedure for processing an application; (c) The procedure for determining the validity of identity and legal guardianship information; (d) Entering contact information into the state police online telecommunications system (SPOTS); (e) The procedures for a participating person or that person's legal guardian to withdraw from the program; and (f) The procedures for a law enforcement officer to access contact information. Source. #14340, eff 8-14-25, EXPIRES: 8-14-35

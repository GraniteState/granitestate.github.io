Saf-C 1807.02 Removal of Commercial Driver License Restriction

Effective 2024-10-18. Expires 2034-10-18.

Saf-C 1807.02 Removal of Commercial Driver License Restriction. (a) Each driver who requests to have a restriction removed from their commercial driver license shall comply with the provisions of Saf-C 1008.07. (b) Each commercial driver who requests removal of a restriction shall furnish to the division the following on form DSMV 329: (1) Legal name; (2) Legal address; (3) Mailing address; (4) Date of birth; (5) Social security number, if required pursuant to RSA 263:90, I(d); (6) Indication of the restriction to be removed; and (7) Signature of the applicant and date signed. (c) Upon the receipt of the completed form DSMV 329 by the division, the applicant shall pass: (1) The knowledge examination set forth in Saf-C 1805.01(b)(2); and (2) The road skills examination set forth in Saf-C 1806.01. (d) In the event the applicant successfully passes the restriction removal requirements, the director shall issue a new commercial driver license.

Saf-C 1405.07 Stress Analysis Required

Effective 2024-11-28. Expires 2034-11-28.

Saf-C 1405.07 Stress Analysis Required. Pursuant to RSA 321-A:6, in the case of newly developed carnival or amusement rides and altered mechanisms, except for challenge courses and zip lines, carnival or amusement ride operators shall furnish a stress analysis to the commissioner.

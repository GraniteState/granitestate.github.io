Saf-C 1806.02 Vehicle Inspection

Effective 2024-10-18. Expires 2034-10-18.

Saf-C 1806.02 Vehicle Inspection. (a) The department shall determine whether the applicant knows that the vehicle is safe to drive by requiring each applicant to pass the vehicle safety inspection test by successfully identifying and inspecting the required components applicable to the vehicle being tested for. Passing scores for each class of vehicle shall be no more than the following: (1) Combination vehicles: a. Class A: 10 points; b. Coach or transit Class A: 11 points; or c. Coach or transit Class B or C: 9 points; and (2) Straight trucks; a. Class B or C: 8 points; b. School bus Class A: 12 points; or c. School B bus Class B or C: 10 points. (b) Notwithstanding (a) above, each applicant shall immediately fail the vehicle inspection in the event they receive a failing mark in any of the following: (1) Air brake check; (2) Performs an unsafe act; or (3) Fails to follow instructions. (c) Each applicant who fails the vehicle inspection test shall not be permitted to proceed to the basic control skills test portion of the examination. Each applicant shall inspect the following items in their entirety on their vehicle if the vehicle is so equipped: (1) In vehicle components or engine start; (2) Passenger or school bus components; (3) Light operations; (4) Front of vehicle or engine area; (5) Steering axle; (6) Side of vehicle; (7) Rear of vehicle or trailer; (8) Combination vehicle specific components; and (9) Trailer specific components.

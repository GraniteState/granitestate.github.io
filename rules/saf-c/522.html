Saf-C 522 MULTI-USE DECAL PLATE

PART Saf-C 522 ELECTRONIC TOLL COLLECTION HEARINGS Revision Note: Document #12426, effective 11-30-17, readopted with amendments Saf-C 217 titled “Electronic Toll Collection Hearings” and renumbered the rule as Saf-C 522. The source note information below for the rules Saf-C 522.01 through Saf-C 522.10 in Saf-C 522 prior to Document #12426 includes the documents filed containing Saf-C 217.01 through Saf-C 217.10, respectively. The filing containing all rules in Saf-C 217 prior to Document #12426 was Document #9487, effective 6-23-09. The rules in Document #9487 did not expire on 6-23-17 but were extended pursuant to RSA 541-A:14-a until replaced by the rules in Document #12426, effective 11-30-17.

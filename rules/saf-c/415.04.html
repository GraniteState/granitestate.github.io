Saf-C 415.04 Rental Agent Registration Application/Permit Forms

Effective 2016-12-19.

Saf-C 415.04 Rental Agent Registration Application/Permit Forms. (a) “Rental Agent Registration Application/Permit” forms, DSSS 114 effective November 2016, may be obtained by writing or calling the department of safety, division of state police, marine patrol unit, 31 Dock Road, Gilford, NH 03249-7627, (603) 293-2037. (b) An applicant shall submit the completed “Rental Agent Registration Application/Permit” form, DSSS 114, effective November 2016, and the registration fee to the division of safety services. (c) An incomplete registration application shall be returned to the applicant with a letter of explanation. Source.

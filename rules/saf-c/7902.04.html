Saf-C 7902.04 Missing Vulnerable Adult Alert Activation Criteria

Effective 2025-02-25.

Saf-C 7902.04 Missing Vulnerable Adult Alert Activation Criteria. A missing vulnerable adult alert shall immediately be implemented when: (a) A local law enforcement agency receives notice that a vulnerable adult is missing; (b) The local law enforcement agency has acquired information from the vulnerable adult's family or legal guardian about the vulnerable adult's impaired mental condition, intellectual or developmental disability, brain injury, or other physical, mental, or emotional disability; and (c) The local law enforcement agency is provided a statement from the vulnerable adult's caregiver that the vulnerable adult is missing and has an impairment. Source. #14199, eff 2-25-25

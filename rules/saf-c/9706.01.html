Saf-C 9706.01 Commissioner’s Determination

Effective 2022-04-18. Earlier expiration 2022-07-28.

Saf-C 9706.01 Commissioner’s Determination. (a) Pursuant to RSA 541-A:29, within 30 days of receipt of an application, the commissioner shall review the application and notify the applicant of any apparent errors or omissions. (b) The commissioner shall review each application, and shall score each application, taking into account: (1) The number of currently deployed body-worn and dashboard cameras that the applicant presently has; (2) The percentage of the applicant’s officers who presently have body-worn and dashboard cameras; (3) The amount of the grant award as a percentage of the total project cost to implement a body-worn or dashboard camera systems; and (4) The type of cameras requested, weighted in favor of those applicants seeking to implement body-worn cameras. (c) The commissioner shall publish a scoring rubric and attach it to the application. (d) The commissioner is authorized to adjust award amounts to a lower amount than applied for when deemed appropriate based on overall impact on the greatest population, cost and budget, and alignment of application with the program guidelines. Source. #13338, INTERIM, eff 1-29-22, EXPIRES: 7-28-22; ss by #13365, eff 4-18-22

Saf-C 203.09 Representation of Parties

Effective 2004-12-17. Earlier expiration 2005-03-24.

Saf-C 203.09 Representation of Parties. (a) Parties may appear pro se or be represented by an attorney or as specified by RSA 311:1. (b) Parties or their representatives may cross-examine witnesses and present evidence and witnesses on their own behalf. (c) In the event a representative or party appears to be unable to ask pertinent questions regarding the issue(s) for which the hearing is being held, the hearings examiner shall conduct and complete such questioning to obtain all necessary information for the hearing examiner to make a fair and informed decision. (d) Appearance by a representative of a party shall not waive the party's required presence at a hearing.

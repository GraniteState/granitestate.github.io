Saf-C 6205.01 Fire Academy Facility Fees

Effective 2018-02-08. Earlier expiration 2002-03-04.

Saf-C 6205.01 Fire Academy Facility Fees. Fees for utilization of the fire academy facilities shall be as follows: (a) Use of the auditorium shall be charged at the rate of $175 per day or $125 per half-day; (b) Use of a double classroom shall be charged at the rate of $150 per day or $75 per half-day; and (c) Use of a single classroom shall be charged at the rate of $100 per day or $50 per half-day. Source. #7552, INTERIM, eff 9-5-01, EXPIRED: 3-4-02 New. #7747, eff 8-17-02; ss by #9496, eff 6-26-09; ss by #12477, eff 2-8-18 (formerly Saf-C 6204.01)

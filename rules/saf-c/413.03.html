Saf-C 413.03 Refund Procedure

Effective 2013-03-20. Earlier expiration 2003-12-21.

Saf-C 413.03 Refund Procedure. (a) A person shall be sent a blank certificate of course completion along with the notice of the requirement for a $200 administrative penalty, pursuant to RSA 270:46-a. (b) Any person who has completed an approved boat safety course within 6 months of the date of conviction, shall forward a certificate of course completion issued by the course instructor along with a letter to the director. (c) The letter required by (b) above shall include the following information: (1) Name; (2) Current mailing address; (3) Name of organization that conducted the course; (4) Title of the course; (5) Date the course was completed; and (6) Date the person was notified by the state of New Hampshire to pay the $200 administrative penalty. (d) Upon receipt of the information set forth in (c) above, the division shall send a check, in the amount of $200, to the person who was required to pay the administrative penalty.

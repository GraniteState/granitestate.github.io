Saf-C 3802.02 Information on Form DSMV 428

Effective 2015-05-14. Earlier expiration 2006-11-25.

Saf-C 3802.02 Information on Form DSMV 428. (a) The law enforcement officer shall furnish the following on form DSMV 428: (1) The name and address of the court having jurisdiction in which the violation occurred; (2) Whether the defendant is required to appear in person at the court; (3) The defendant's name, address and date of birth; (4) The defendant's sex, race, height, weight, hair color and eye color; (5) The defendant's license number, license class and state of issuance; (6) The date and location of the violation; (7) The registration number, plate, make, year and type of motor vehicle; (8) Whether the vehicle involved is a commercial vehicle; (9) Whether the defendant's license is a commercial driver license; (10) Whether the violation involved hazardous materials; (11) Whether the vehicle involved has a capacity of 16 or more passengers; (12) The specific violation; and (13) The law enforcement officer’s name, department and badge number or identification number. Source. #5539, eff 1-1-93; ss by #6891, eff 11-25-98, EXPIRED: 11-25-06 New. #8823, eff 2-21-07; ss by #10825, eff 5-14-15

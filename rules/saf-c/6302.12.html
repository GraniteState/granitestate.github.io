Saf-C 6302.12 Certification of breath test operator

Effective 2010-03-23. Earlier expiration 2004-09-28.

Saf-C 6302.12 “Certification of breath test operator” means a 12 month period in which a law enforcement officer, who meets requirements of Saf-C 6303.02, is authorized to conduct an analysis of a person’s breath for the purpose of determining its alcohol concentration.

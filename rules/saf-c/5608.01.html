Saf-C 5608.01 Single Record Requests

Effective 2025-11-06.

Saf-C 5608.01 Single Record Requests. (a) The following fees shall be charged for the specified document: (1) $20.00 for a certified copy of a driving record as specified in RSA 263:42, II; (2) $20.00 for a certified driver record provided to an insurance company as specified in RSA 260:15, II; (3) $17.00 for a non-certified driver record pursuant to RSA 260:15, II; (4) $20.00 for a certified copy of a registration as specified in RSA 263:42, II; (5) $5 for a current registration listing as specified by RSA 261:60, I and Saf-C 509.12; (6) $20 for a title search as specified by RSA 261:20, I(f); (7) $1 per page for accident reports with a minimum of $5; and (8) $1 for an insurance card filed in connection with an accident. Source. #7361, EXEMPT, eff 9-1-00; ss by #7621, EXEMPT, eff 1-1-02; ss by #13152, EXEMPT, eff 12-31-20; ss by #14423, EXEMPT, eff 11-6-25

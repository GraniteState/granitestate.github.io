Saf-C 1411.01 Operator Duties

Effective 2024-11-28. Expires 2034-11-28.

Saf-C 1411.01 Operator Duties. Each operator shall: (a) Except as otherwise required by Saf-C 1402.01(c), construct, install, maintain, and operate each challenge course or zip line in accordance with the standards set forth in either the “ANSI/ACCT 03-2019 CHALLENGE COURSES AND CANOPY/ZIP LINE TOURS STANDARDS” (Revision of ANSI/ACCT 03-2016 Standards) or the ASTM International standards listed in Saf-C 1402.01(a), available as noted in Appendix B; (b) Ensure that each challenge course or zip line is inspected, at least once each year, by a qualified person, as defined in either the “ANSI/ACCT 03-2019 CHALLENGE COURSES AND CANOPY/ZIP LINE TOURS STANDARDS” (Revision of ANSI/ACCT 03-2016 Standards) or the ASTM International standards listed in Saf-C 1402.01(a), available as noted in Appendix B, and found to be in safe condition; (c) Train employees for each challenge course or zip line in accordance with either the “ANSI/ACCT 03-2019 CHALLENGE COURSES AND CANOPY/ZIP LINE TOURS STANDARDS” (Revision of ANSI/ACCT 03-2016 Standards) or the ASTM International standards listed in Saf-C 1402.01(a), available as noted in Appendix B; (d) Procure and maintain insurance pursuant to RSA 321-A:5 and Saf-C 1405.06; (e) Retain all records pertaining to inspections and maintenance for a period of 2 years in accordance with RSA 321-A:2-a, I(a)(4); and (f) Comply with the accident procedures set forth in Saf-C 1405.04.

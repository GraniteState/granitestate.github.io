Saf-C 2603.03 Amount of Bond

Effective 2015-02-13. Earlier expiration 2000-03-31.

Saf-C 2603.03 Amount of Bond. (a) Each licensee shall file a bond in the amount of $50,000 on an annual basis. (b) A separate bond shall be required for each location in which the licensee has a place of business.

Saf-C 5503 INITIAL NOTICE OF REGISTRATION

PART Saf-C 5503 INITIAL NOTICE OF REGISTRATION REVISION NOTE: Document #13104, effective 9-15-20, readopted with amendments Form DSSP 311 “Offender Registration Information” pursuant to the expedited revisions to agency forms process in RSA 541-A:19-c. Document #13104 also updated the revision date on the form from “01/09” to “07/20” to reflect the amendments made to the form. Pursuant to RSA 541-A:19-c, the requirements in Form DSSP 311 had been set forth in Saf-C 5503.01(d)(2) and Saf-C 5503.02, but they were superseded by the requirements as stated on form DSSP 311 in Document #13104. Document #13170, effective 2-19-21, subsequently readopted with amendments Saf-C 5503.02 titled “Form DSSP 311 for Offender Registration Information and Form DSSP 311A Supplemental Form for Offender Registration Information” through the regular rulemaking process. Instead of continuing to set forth the requirements of the forms in Saf-C 5503.02, Document #13170 incorporated the Forms DSSP 311 and DSSP 311A by reference pursuant to RSA 541-A:19-b with specific revision dates of “07/20” and “02/21”, respectively, on the forms. Saf-C 5503.02 incorporates by reference Form DSSP 311 “as amended July 2020” and Form DSSP 311A “as amended February 2021”. The revision date of 07/20 on Form DSSP 311 did not change because that form has not been amended since Document #13104. But the revision date for Form DSSP 311A has been updated from 01/11 to 2/21 because that form was amended.

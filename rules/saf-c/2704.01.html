Saf-C 2704.01 Specifications for Emergency Lights

Effective 2021-06-16. Earlier expiration 2004-01-26.

Saf-C 2704.01 Specifications for Emergency Lights. (a) Emergency lights used by hospital emergency personnel on their private vehicles shall be red in color with rotating or flashing beacon. (b) Emergency lights on a private vehicle shall be mounted as follows: (1) On the roof; (2) On the front and the rear of a vehicle if single feed lights, and not less than 12 inches from the road surface; (3) On the rear window ledges of a vehicle if rear facing lights, if they do not obstruct the driver's view to the rear or cause night time interior reflection that interferes with the driver's night time vision; or (4) On the dashboard, if they do not obstruct the driver's view or cause night time interior reflection that interferes with the driver's night time vision. (c) Emergency lights on a private vehicle shall be capable of emitting a beacon: (1) At a minimum, directly ahead and directly to the rear of the vehicle; and (2) Bright enough to be seen during normal daylight hours.

Saf-C 1923.01 Junk or Dismantled Vehicles

Effective 2023-04-25. Earlier expiration 2022-09-27.

Saf-C 1923.01 Junk or Dismantled Vehicles (a) Pursuant to RSA 261:22, each notification of a junk or dismantled vehicle shall be submitted by a dealer, repair person, junk dealer, or owner within 30 days to the National Motor Vehicle Title Information System (NMVTIS). (b) The previous title, if available, shall be furnished to the bureau.

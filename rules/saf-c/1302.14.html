Saf-C 1302.14 School bus driver

Effective 2024-06-22. Expires 2034-06-22.

Saf-C 1302.14 “School bus driver” means a person, certified pursuant to RSA 263:29, who drives any school bus transporting pupils to and from any public school or school related activity. Source. #8248, eff 1-4-05 (from Saf-C 1302.13); amd by #8723, eff 9-8-06; ss by #10439, eff 10-11-13 (formerly Saf-C 1302.16) (see Revision Note #1 at chapter heading for Saf-C 1300); ss by #14005, eff 6-22-24, EXPIRES: 6-22-34 (see Revision Note #2 at chapter heading for Saf-C 1300)

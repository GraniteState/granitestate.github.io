Saf-C 510.14 Registration Refund for Members of Armed Forces

Effective 2023-01-13. Earlier expiration 2012-03-15.

Saf-C 510.14 Registration Refund for Members of Armed Forces. (a) Pursuant to RSA 261:68, any person who is ordered into or enlists in the military or naval services during a war emergency may request an abeyance of their registration(s) during such service. (b) An application may be made in writing and submitted, in person to any substation or by mail, to: The Division of Motor Vehicles, Registration Bureau 23 Hazen Drive Concord, N.H. 03305. (c) Each applicant shall submit the following: (1) The name, legal address, and date of birth of the member of the armed services; (2) A copy of military orders; and (3) The plate number of the vehicle to be considered for refund. (d) Upon receipt of the information set forth in (c) above, the department shall grant a pro rata refund, based upon the number of months the vehicle is unused.

Saf-C 513.59 United States Congressman Plate

Effective 2023-01-13. Earlier expiration 2012-03-15.

Saf-C 513.59 United States Congressman Plate. (a) United States congressman plates shall be issued to vehicles of members of the United States House of Representatives pursuant to RSA 261:90. (b) In addition to the requirements set forth in RSA 261:90, United States congressman plates shall: (1) Have the words "U.S. CONGRESS"; (2) Have the state seal; (3) Have the numeral one or 2; and (4) Have the years of term on the lower border. Source. #7972, eff 10-17-03 (See Revision Note #1 at chapter heading for Saf-C 500); ss by #9995, INTERIM, eff 9-17-11, EXPIRED: 3-15-12 New. #10135, eff 5-23-12; renumbered by #12914 (formerly Saf-C 514.58); ss by #13521, eff 1-13-23 (formerly Saf-C 514.59)

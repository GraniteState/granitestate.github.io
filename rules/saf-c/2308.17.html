Saf-C 2308.17 Revocation of Vessel Agent Authority

Effective 2025-07-16. Expires 2035-07-16.

Saf-C 2308.17 Revocation of Vessel Agent Authority. (a) The commissioner shall revoke a vessel agent’s authority, after a hearing, for failure to adhere to the contract or for any violation of this chapter or RSA 270-E. (b) All hearings shall be held in accordance with the requirements of Saf-C 200.

Saf-C 1102.04 Automatic Renewal of Authorization

Effective 2022-07-25. Earlier expiration 2022-05-23.

Saf-C 1102.04 Automatic Renewal of Authorization. (a) An authorization to issue citations shall be automatically renewed, without application, if the person: (1) Successfully completes a refresher course; or (2) Successfully demonstrates a minimum of 8 hours of continuing education on an annual basis, composed of the elements listed in (b) below. (b) For the purposes of (a)(1) above, a refresher course shall be composed of the following elements: (1) Review of search and seizure laws and procedures; (2) Review of updates and changes to fire safety rules, applicable licensing rules, and state laws and codes; (3) Review of evidence gathering procedures; and (4) Review of procedures for issuing a citation. (c) A refresher course conducted by the commission and council shall be deemed to meet the requirements of (b) above. All other refresher courses shall be approved by the commission and council, to ensure that the requirements of (b) above are met. (d) The person shall be responsible for maintaining all records of completed courses and continuing education. Such records shall be made available to the commissioner, upon request.

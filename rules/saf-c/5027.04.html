Saf-C 5027.04 Restrictions

Effective 2017-03-06.

Saf-C 5027.04 Restrictions. (a) Dense pack firing racks shall only be fired electronically from a remote location. Firing locations shall be located a minimum of 75 feet from the dense pack portable firing system. (b) Multiple dense pack firing rack systems shall be separated 2 feet or greater from one another at the discharge site. (c) Dense pack firing racks shall not be reloaded during a display. (d) Mortar size shall not exceed 6 inches. (e) Salute shells shall not be fired from any dense pack firing rack. (f) Dense pack firing racks used in conjunction with a vehicle trailer shall be secured and leveled at the discharge site and disconnected from the tow vehicle before any mortar is loaded. (g) Dense pack firing racks utilized in conjunction with a marine vessel or floating platforms shall be anchored or properly moored in accordance with Saf-C 5025.04 before any mortar is loaded. (h) Dense pack racks shall have no greater than 10 chain fused shells per ignition. (i) Dense pack firing racks that are placed on the ground shall be on a firm level surface at the discharge site before any mortar is loaded. (j) Dense pack firing racks that are placed on the ground shall be separated by a minimum of 2 feet between racks. Source. #9765-A, eff 8-2-10; ss by #12124, eff 3-6-17

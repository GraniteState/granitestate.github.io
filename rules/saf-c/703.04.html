Saf-C 703.04 Rescission of Authority

Effective 2015-07-16. Earlier expiration 2005-03-25.

Saf-C 703.04 Rescission of Authority. (a) Upon receipt of the notice of termination, the commissioner shall: (1) Rescind the former officer's commission; and (2) Give notice to the former officer thereof. (b) The former officer shall return the certificate of commission to the department within 7 days.

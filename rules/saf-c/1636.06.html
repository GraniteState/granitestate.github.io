Saf-C 1636.06 Caution

Effective 2013-08-15. Earlier expiration 2005-01-10.

Saf-C 1636.06 Caution. Caution shall be exercised in the movement of the vehicle in the blasting area to avoid driving the vehicle over or dragging hoses over firing lines, cap wires, or explosive materials. The driver, in moving the vehicle, shall obtain the assistance of any second person to guide his movements.

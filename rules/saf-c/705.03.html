Saf-C 705.03 Rule Violations

Effective 2015-07-16. Earlier expiration 2005-03-25.

Saf-C 705.03 Rule Violations. (a) The commissioner shall, after an opportunity for hearing as prescribed in Saf-C 200, suspend an officer's commission on finding that an officer has engaged in any activities that constitute a violation of these rules. (b) Such commission shall be suspended for a period not to exceed one year from the date of hearing.

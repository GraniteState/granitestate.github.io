Pos 1501.04 Application

Effective 2010-03-13.

Pos 1501.04 Application. (a) Each applicant for tuition repayment under this program shall make application on Form PEC-CART, application for CART/sign language interpreter tuition repayment, by supplying the following information: (1) Applicant’s first name and last name; (2) Applicant’s current address; (3) Applicant’s current employer, or if self-employed, applicant’s EIN number; (4) Employer’s address, if applicable; (5) Beginning and ending date of service for which an award is sought; (6) Name and title of employer’s certifying official; and (7) Signature of employer’s certifying official. (b) The application shall be signed and dated by the applicant. (c) The applicant shall attach copies of bills, or receipts, detailing the tuition paid for training as a CART provider or sign language interpreter for which the applicant is seeking repayment. (d) The applicant shall provide evidence of certification/licensure as specified in Pos 1501.03. Source. #9677, eff 3-13-10

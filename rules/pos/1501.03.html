Pos 1501.03 Approved CART and Sign Language Certification

Effective 2010-03-13.

Pos 1501.03 Approved CART and Sign Language Certification. (a) CART providers shall have current certification as a “Certified CART Provider (CCP)”, as provided by the national court reporters association (NCRA). (b) Sign language interpreters shall be: (1) Licensed by the board of licensure of interpreters for the deaf and hard of hearing under RSA 326-I; or (2) Certified by NH department of education as an educational interpreter. Source. #9677, eff 3-13-10

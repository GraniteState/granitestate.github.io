Plu 402.01 Expiration of Individual Plumbing Licenses

Effective 2009-12-02. Earlier expiration 1997-08-28.

Plu 402.01 Expiration of Individual Plumbing Licenses. (a) Pursuant to RSA 329-A:11, I, individual master plumber licenses and journeyman plumber licenses shall expire annually on the last day of the month of the licensee's birth. (b) Individuals whose plumbing licenses have not been renewed by the board before their expiration date: (1) Shall not practice plumbing until their licenses have been renewed or reinstated; and (2) Shall be subject to the provisions of RSA 329-A:18, II if they practice plumbing without a currently valid license.

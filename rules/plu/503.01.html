Plu 503.01 Obligations of Plumbing Licensees and Holders of Water Treatment Technician Certificates

Effective 2008-04-25.

Plu 503.01 Obligations of Plumbing Licensees and Holders of Water Treatment Technician Certificates. Licensees and certificate holders shall: (a) Always keep themselves informed about the state plumbing code and the administrative rules of the board; (b) Obtain local permits and inspections as required; (c) Cooperate with local code enforcement personnel; (d) Give accurate and complete information when asked to do so as part of an investigation or hearing by any governmental entity; and (e) Inform their customers or potential customers accurately about any reasonably foreseeable need for future services. Source. #9142, eff 4-25-08

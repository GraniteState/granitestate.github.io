Plu 308.04 Fees for Initial Certification of Water Treatment Businesses

Effective 2007-06-22.

Plu 308.04 Fees for Initial Certification of Water Treatment Businesses. The fees for initial certification of businesses engaged in water treatment shall be: (a) For a water treatment business with one or more certified water treatment technicians, certification shown by wall certificate and wallet card, $125.00; and (b) For a water treatment business with a certified water treatment technician as its sole owner, licensure shown by wall certificate and wallet card, no fee. Source. #8916, eff 6-22-07

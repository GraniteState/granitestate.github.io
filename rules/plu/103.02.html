Plu 103.02 Meetings of the Board

Effective 2007-09-20.

Plu 103.02 Meetings of the Board. (a) The board shall elect a chair, vice-chair and secretary at its annual meeting. (b) The chair shall preside over all meetings of the board. (c) In the absence of the chair at a meeting of the board, the vice chair shall preside. (d) Pursuant to RSA 329-A:5, the board shall meet at least 4 times annually and may meet more frequently if the business of the board so requires. (e) The annual meeting of the board shall be the first regular meeting held in each state fiscal year. (f) Meetings of the board shall be open to the public to the extent required by RSA 91-A, and notice of board meetings shall be posted at the board’s office and at the offices of 2 municipalities. Source. #8487, eff 11-17-05; ss by #8980, eff 9-20-07

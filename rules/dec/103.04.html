Dec 103.04 Contact Information for the Committee

Effective 2008-04-25.

Dec 103.04 Contact Information for the Committee. (a) The committee operates out of the offices of the public utilities commission at the following address: N.H. Public Utilities Commission 21 South Fruit Street, Suite 10 Concord, NH 03301 (b) Telephone inquiries may be made to the executive director of the public utilities commission at (603) 271-2431 or TDD Access-Relay NH: 1-800-735-296. (c) The committee’s website can be accessed through a link on the public utilities commission website at www.puc.nh.gov. Source. #9143, eff 4-25-08

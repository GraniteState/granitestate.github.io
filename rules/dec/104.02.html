Dec 104.02 Public Inspection of Minutes and Records

Effective 2008-04-25. Earlier expiration 2001-03-17.

Dec 104.02 Public Inspection of Minutes and Records. Pursuant to RSA 91-A:4, the records of the committee, including its minutes, shall be available for inspection and copying by any member of the public during regular business hours at the premises of the public utilities commission, located at 21 South Fruit Street, Suite 10, Concord, New Hampshire, from 8:00 a.m. to 4:30 p.m., Monday through Friday, except for holidays.

Lab 1305.11 Changes of Address

Effective 2025-07-11. Expires 2035-07-11.

Lab 1305.11 Changes of Address. (a) The holder of title to the building in which the elevator or accessibility lift is located shall submit to the department, in writing, any changes of address applicable to the owner or the lessee. (b) Any changes of address under Lab 1305.11(a) shall be reflected in the “Elevator Inspection Report Form” (May 2025).

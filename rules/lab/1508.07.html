Lab 1508.07 Required Response to Notice of Proposed Fine

Effective 2022-08-24.

Lab 1508.07 Required Response to Notice of Proposed Fine. (a) After receiving a notice of proposed fine, the leasing company shall respond to the notice by either: (1) Indicating that the leasing company shall attend a hearing; (2) Waiving the hearing and paying the proposed fine; or (3) Requesting an informal meeting with the department to discuss the proposed fine. (b) The leasing company shall file the response no later than 30 days from the date of the notice. (c) Any leasing company who does not waive the hearing shall inform the department of any change in the leasing company’s mailing address that occurs prior to the final resolution of the matter. (d) If the leasing company does not respond to the notice of proposed fine pursuant to Lab 1508.07 (a)(1) through to (a)(3) the department shall file a petition for hearing pursuant to Lab 200. Source. #10221, eff 11-3-12; ss by #13440, eff 8-24-22

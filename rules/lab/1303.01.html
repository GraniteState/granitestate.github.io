Lab 1303.01 Purpose

Effective 2025-07-11. Expires 2035-07-11.

Lab 1303.01 Purpose. A licensing procedure for elevator and accessibility lift mechanics and inspectors was created by the legislature. These rules are intended to carry out the intent of RSA 157-B by establishing standards for the licensing and regulation of elevator and accessibility lift mechanics and inspectors. Source. #6368, eff 10-31-96; ss by #6845, eff 9-1-98; ss by #8706, INTERIM, eff 8-19-06, EXPIRED: 2-15-07 New. #8880, eff 4-28-07; ss by #10715, eff 11-10-14; ss by #14313, eff 7-11-25, EXPIRES: 7-11-35

Lab 705.03 Payment for Provider's Services

Effective 2016-10-25.

Lab 705.03 Payment for Provider's Services. The health care provider conducting an independent examination authorized by the commissioner in Lab 705.01 shall be paid by the employer or the employer's insurance carrier. Nothing in this section shall be construed to prevent an injured employee from obtaining an examination by a health care provider of the injured employee's choice at the employee's expense.

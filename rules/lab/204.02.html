Lab 204.02 Specification of Issue

Effective 2026-03-12. Earlier expiration 2007-01-11.

Lab 204.02 Specification of Issue. Hearings before a hearing officer shall be scheduled for the determination of specified issues duly listed for hearing on the hearing notice. The department shall expand the scope of a scheduled hearing if notice of the added issues, with necessary documentation, is given to all parties at least 14 calendar days prior to the assigned hearing date. Issues may be added at any time without such notice provided all parties consent.

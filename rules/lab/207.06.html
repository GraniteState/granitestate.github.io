Lab 207.06 Public Records

Effective 2026-03-12.

Lab 207.06 Public Records. Transcripts of testimony as well as documents and materials admitted into evidence shall be public records unless the presiding officer determines that all or part of them is exempt from disclosure under RSA 281-A:21-b, RSA 91-A:5, or applicable case law.

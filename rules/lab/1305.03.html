Lab 1305.03 New Elevators and Accessibility Lifts; Construction

Effective 2025-07-11. Expires 2035-07-11.

Lab 1305.03 New Elevators and Accessibility Lifts; Construction. (a) Detailed plans for the construction and implementation of a new elevator or accessibility lift, as such unit is defined in RSA 157-B:2, shall be submitted to the department for approval before construction may begin. (b) The department shall inspect the new elevator or accessibility lift, once constructed, and the department’s approval shall be required before the new unit is released to the facility owner to be operated. (c) The owner shall coordinate the required inspection through the department. (d) Upon completing a passing inspection, the department shall issue a certificate. The department shall void that certificate if the owner fails to pay the required fee within 30 days. (e) The inspection shall not be complete until the department has issued a certificate certifying the unit. Source. #14313, eff 7-11-25, EXPIRES: 7-11-35

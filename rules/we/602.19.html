We 602.19 Private Wells on Lots Served by a Public Water System

Effective 2017-03-01.

We 602.19 Private Wells on Lots Served by a Public Water System. (a) A private well shall not be constructed on a lot served by a public water system as defined by RSA 485:1a, XV, when the provisions of RSA 485-A:30-b cannot be met, or when the construction of the well would violate subdivision approval, pursuant to RSA 485-A:29. (b) Pursuant to RSA 362:4, IV and V, when the provisions of RSA 485-A:29 and RSA 485-A:30-b are satisfied, no property owner shall be required to connect to a public water system. (c) Paragraph (a), above, shall not apply to closed loop geothermal wells. Source. (See Revision Note at chapter heading for We 600) #12083 eff 3-1-17

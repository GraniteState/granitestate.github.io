We 105.01 Summary of Duties

Effective 2008-06-13. Earlier expiration 1996-12-22.

We 105.01 Summary of Duties. In accordance with RSA 482-B the board shall: (a) Protect the ground waters of the state; (b) Regulate the construction of water wells; (c) Regulate the installation of well pumps; (d) License water well contractors; (e) License well pump installers; (f) Keep records of wells; (g) Provide for penalties for violations; (h) Charge fees for licenses; (i) Promote cooperation among water well contractors, pump installers and governmental agencies; (j) Make available to the public information relative to ground water resources of the state; and (k) Adopt rules as necessary to implement the requirements of RSA 482-B.

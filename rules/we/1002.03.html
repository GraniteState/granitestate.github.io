We 1002.03 Public Access

Effective 2017-03-01.

We 1002.03 Public Access. (a) Persons may request information from the registry either by telephone or in writing. (b) The board shall respond to a request for information from the registry within 10 working days of the date of receipt. (c) Nothing herein shall preclude any person from requesting a review of the complete record of complaints in the board’s files in accordance with the right to know law, RSA 91-A.

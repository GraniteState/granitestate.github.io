We 201.03 Governing Statutes and Rules

Effective 2008-06-13. Earlier expiration 1996-12-22.

We 201.03 Governing Statutes and Rules. The provisions of RSA 541-A, RSA 482-B and these rules shall govern proceedings before the board.

Psyc 102.05 Committees

Effective 2016-11-04.

Psyc 102.05 Committees. (a) Pursuant to RSA 329-B: 4, and in accordance with its requirements, the board shall create and administer the following standing committees: (1) The advisory committee; (2) The committee for professional conduct investigations; and (3) The professional’s health committee. (b) The members of each standing committee shall meet the eligibility requirements set forth in RSA 329-B: 6. (c) Any other committee appointed by the board to make recommendations on matters within the statutory authority of the board, shall: (1) Be deemed a special committee to exist only until completion of its assigned task; (2) Consist of one or more members of the board; and (3) Be chaired by a member of the board. (d) Standing committees and special committees shall make recommendations to the board, but shall not take final action on behalf of the board. Source. #12035-A, eff 11-4-16

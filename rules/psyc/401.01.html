Psyc 401.01 Expiration of License

Effective 2016-11-04.

Psyc 401.01 Expiration of License. (a) A license to practice psychology shall automatically expire on the last day of the month which is 2 years from the date issued by the board. (b) An expired license shall not be renewed, but upon application, may be reinstated in accordance with Psyc 401.05. (c) If a license has expired pursuant to Psyc 401.01 (a), the licensee shall not engage in the practice of psychology until such time as a new or reinstated license has been granted by the board. Source. #12035-B, eff 11-4-16

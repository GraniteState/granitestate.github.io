Psyc 306.01 Jurisprudence Examination

Effective 2026-06-30. Expires 2036-06-30.

Psyc 306.01 Jurisprudence Examination. (a) Each applicant for initial licensure or reinstatement of licensure shall be examined on the contents of RSA 329-B psychologists practice act and Psyc 100 through Psyc 500. (b) The examination shall be developed by the board of psychology and administered by the OPLC through a third party that has been contracted by the OPLC. (c) The exam shall be as follows: (1) Open book using a study guide supplied by the board; (2) Available on a secure website; and (3) Taken individually without consulting others. (d) An individual shall achieve a passing score of at least 80 percent. (e) An individual who fails the jurisprudence examination may re-take the examination as many times as it takes the individual to pass. Source. #14569, eff 6-30-26, EXPIRES: 6-30-36

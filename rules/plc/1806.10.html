Plc 1806.10 Time for Completing Continuing Education

Effective 2025-09-22. Expires 2035-09-22.

Plc 1806.10 Time for Completing Continuing Education. A certified property assessor supervisor, certified property assessor, or certified property assessor assistant shall complete all required continuing education prior to the expiration date of the individual’s current certification. Source. #14330, eff 9-22-25, EXPIRES: 9-22-35

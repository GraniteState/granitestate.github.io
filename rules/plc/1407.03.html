Plc 1407.03 Application for License Renewal

Effective 2026-01-26. Expires 2036-01-26.

Plc 1407.03 Application for License Renewal. A practitioner who wishes to renew a license shall apply by complying with Plc 308.05, subject to the following: (a) On or with the “Recreational Therapy Supplement To Universal Application For License Renewal” dated 11/2025, the applicant shall provide: (1) The documentation required by Plc 1407.04; and (2) The information and attestation relative to meeting continuing competence requirements required by Plc 1407.05; and (b) The fee shall be the application processing and licensing fee specified in Plc 1002.46, provided that an applicant who is active military or a military spouse shall not pay the fee. Source. (See Revision Note at chapter heading for Plc 1400) #13705, eff 8-1-23; ss by #14442, eff 1-26-26, EXPIRES 1-26-36

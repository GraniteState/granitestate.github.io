Plc 1804.01 General Requirements for Performing Assessing Services

Effective 2025-09-22. Expires 2035-09-22.

Plc 1804.01 General Requirements for Performing Assessing Services. (a) Individuals who, by their action(s), undertake assessing services or establish or change assessed property value(s) for any municipality in New Hampshire shall either be: (1) Certified by the DRA prior to the 2025 effective date of these rules or by the OPLC under RSA 310-A and Plc 1800; or (2) Statutorily authorized to assess property pursuant to RSA 75:1, RSA 81:1, RSA 41:2-g, or RSA 48:13. (b) When a company enters into an assessing services contract with a municipality, the company shall identify the certified individual who will be accountable under the contract for compliance with applicable provisions in Plc 1806 relative to certificant obligations.

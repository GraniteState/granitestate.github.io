Plc 1805.01 Applying for Initial Certification

Effective 2025-09-22. Expires 2035-09-22.

Plc 1805.01 Applying for Initial Certification. (a) Any person who meets the applicable qualifications in Plc 1804 and wishes to be a certified building measurer and lister, certified property assessor assistant, certified property assessor, or certified property assessor supervisor in New Hampshire who is not already certified in the classification being applied for shall file an application for initial certification in accordance with this section. (b) The applicant shall apply by complying with Plc 304.01, subject to the following: (1) The profession-specific supplement required by Plc 304.01(a) shall be the “Assessing Professions Supplement to Universal Application for Initial License” dated July 2025, that: a. Provides the information required by Plc 1805.02 and the documentation specified in Plc 1805.03; and b. Is signed and attested to as specified in Plc 1805.04; (2) A criminal records check as required by RSA 310-C:5 shall be submitted, provided that a new criminal records check shall be required only if the results of the criminal records check performed pursuant to Plc 1804.02 were submitted more than 6 months prior to the application date; and (3) The fee shall be the application processing and licensing fee specified in Plc 1002.05, provided that an applicant who is active military or a military spouse shall not pay the fee. (c) Applicants for initial certification as a certified property assessor assistant, certified property assessor, or certified property assessor supervisor shall submit a résumé with the application that explains the applicant’s education and relevant work experience in sufficient detail to show that the applicant has the qualifications necessary for certification in the classification applied for. (d) An applicant for initial certification as a certified measurer and lister who does not have the training documentation specified in Plc 1804.06 shall submit a résumé with the application that explains the applicant’s education and relevant work experience in sufficient detail to show that the applicant has the qualifications necessary for certification as a measurer and lister.

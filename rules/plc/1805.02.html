Plc 1805.02 Additional Information Required for Initial Certification Application

Effective 2025-09-22. Expires 2035-09-22.

Plc 1805.02 Additional Information Required for Initial Certification Application. In addition to the information required by Plc 304.03, the applicant for an initial certification in any of the assessing professions shall provide the following information: (a) If applying to be certified as a building measurer and lister, the following: (1) If trained in New Hampshire, the name of each field training supervisor who supervised the training required by Plc 1804.04(a); or (2) If not trained in New Hampshire, the name of each jurisdiction in which equivalent experience was attained to meet the requirements of Plc 1804.05; (b) If applying to be certified as a property assessor assistant, the following: (1) If the applicant is or was a certified building measurer and lister in New Hampshire, the certification number of the building measurer and lister certification held by the applicant, if any; or (2) If applicant is not or was not a certified building measurer and lister in New Hampshire, either: a. The name and business address of the applicant’s employer when the experience required by Plc 1804.08(a)(3) was obtained; or b. The name of each jurisdiction in which equivalent educational and professional work experience was attained to meet the requirements of Plc 1804.08(b); (c) If applying to be certified as a property assessor, the following: (1) The certification number of the property assessor assistant certification held by the applicant; (2) The name of the provider of IAAO Workshop 151 or equivalent taken as required by Plc 1804.09(b)(3); (3) The date the course in NH assessment and taxation laws required by Plc 1804.09(b)(4) was taken; and (4) The name and address of each employer for which the applicant worked to obtain the experience required by Plc 1804.09(c); and (d) If applying to be certified as a property assessor supervisor, the following: (1) The certification number of the property assessor certification held by the applicant; (2) The name and address of each employer for which the applicant worked to obtain the experience required by Plc 1804.10(a)(3) or (a)(4); (3) For each course taken to meet applicable continuing education requirements, the name, location, sponsor, and total number of hours of the course; and (4) The name and jurisdiction of each judicial or quasi-judicial tribunal in which the experience required by Plc 1804.10(a)(6) was gained.

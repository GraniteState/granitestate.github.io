Plc 1001.18 Fees for Inspections Related to Licensure

Effective 2025-07-01. Expires 2035-07-01.

Plc 1001.18 Fees for Inspections Related to Licensure. (a) Subject to (g) - (j), below, the fee for any inspection related to initial licensure shall be $150. (b) Subject to (g) - (j), below, the fee for any reinspection related to initial licensure shall be $100. (c) Subject to (g) - (j), below, the fee for any inspection related to renewal licensure shall be $150. (d) Subject to (g) - (j), below, the fee for any reinspection related to renewal licensure shall be $100. (e) Subject to (g) - (j), below, the fee for any inspection related to reinstatement of licensure shall be $150. (f) Subject to (g) - (j), below, the fee for any reinspection related to reinstatement of licensure shall be $100. (g) For general anesthesia, deep sedation, and moderate sedation permits for dentistry: (1) The fee for an initial facility inspection under Den 304.05 shall be $300; and (2) The fee for a comprehensive evaluation under Den 304.05 associated with initial licensing shall be $750. (h) The fee for inspection of an electrology school for initial licensure shall be $150. (i) The fee for an initial inspection of a funeral home, funeral chapel, or crematory for initial licensure shall be $250. (j) The fee for an initial site visit to a nursing program for initial licensure shall be $100. Source. #14289, eff 7-1-25, EXPIRES: 7-1-35

Plc 1807.01 Eligibility for Certification Renewal

Effective 2025-09-22. Expires 2035-09-22.

Plc 1807.01 Eligibility for Certification Renewal. To be eligible for renewal of a certification issued under this chapter or issued by the DRA prior to the 2025 effective date of this chapter, an individual shall: (a) Meet the qualifications stated in Plc 1804.01(a) and the applicable qualifications in Plc 1804.04, Plc 1804.08, Plc 1804.09, or Plc 1804.10; (b) Have paid all administrative and civil penalties imposed, if any; and (c) Meet the eligibility for renewal criteria specified in Plc 308.02, as applicable. Source. #14330, eff 9-22-25, EXPIRES: 9-22-35

Plc 1002.11 Application Processing and Licensing Fees for the Professions Regulated by the Board of Dental Examiners

Effective 2025-07-01. Expires 2035-07-01.

Plc 1002.11 Application Processing and Licensing Fees for the Professions Regulated by the Board of Dental Examiners. The application processing and licensing fees found in Table 1002.11, below, shall apply to the professions regulated by the board of dental examiners under RSA 317‑A: Table 1002.11 Application Processing and Licensing Fees for the Professions Regulated by the Board of Dental Examiners Category | License Duration | Fee Dentist license or dental hygienist license, application processing and licensing: Active: initial, renewal, or reinstatement after expiration of license | 2 years | $187 Inactive: initial, renewal, or reinstatement after expiration of license | 2 years | $93 Change inactive to active | 2 years | $93 Licensed dental hygienist permit to administer nitrous oxide application processing and licensing: Initial, renewal, or reinstatement after expiration of license | attached to license | $27 Licensed dental hygienist permit for certified public health dental hygienist (CPHDH) or expanded function dental auxiliary (EFDA) application processing and licensing: Initial, renewal, or reinstatement after expiration of license | attached to license | $27 Temporary license to provide voluntary services application processing and licensing | max. 12 months | $40 Temporary license to teach or participate in research project per Den 301.05 application processing and licensing | max. 12 months | $40 General anesthesia, deep sedation, and moderate sedation permits application processing and licensing: Initial, renewal, or reinstatement after expiration of license | 2 years | $187 Facility license application processing and licensing: Initial, renewal, or reinstatement after expiration of license | 2 years | $170 Source. #13260, EMERGENCY RULE, eff 9-10-21, EXPIRED: 3-9-22 New. #13420, eff 8-8-22; ss by #13703, eff 8-1-23 (formerly Plc 1002.10); ss by #14290, eff 7-1-25, EXPIRES: 7-1-35

Plc 1806.06 Certified Property Assessor Duties

Effective 2025-09-22. Expires 2035-09-22.

Plc 1806.06 Certified Property Assessor Duties. (a) A certified property assessor may: (1) Perform assessing services that include: a. The annual maintenance of assessments by using the base year data collection manual and USPAP-compliant report; and b. The use of the computer-assisted mass appraisal system existing models and cost data established by a certified property assessor supervisor during the year of the last revaluation for new appraisals; (2) Perform abatement reviews and the defense of value under the supervision of a certified property assessor supervisor; (3) Perform sale validation; and (4) Submit a signed and dated statement to the OPLC attesting to the qualifications of a building measurer and lister, or a property assessor assistant, working under the certified property assessor’s supervision to be true, accurate, and correct. (b) A certified property assessor under the supervision of a certified property assessor supervisor may: (1) Adjust the cost, land, depreciation, or other computer-assisted mass appraisal tables resulting in a change to the values; and (2) Conduct a final field review. (c) A certified property assessor shall not: (1) Establish and certify values; or (2) Sign the USPAP-compliant report. (d) A certified property assessor may participate in informal hearings, but shall not supervise informal hearings. Source. #14330, eff 9-22-25, EXPIRES: 9-22-35

Plc 2003.03 Qualifying Training

Effective 2026-09-21. Expires 2036-09-21.

Plc 2003.03 Qualifying Training. (a) Program curriculum designed to educate individuals seeking certification as a CHW shall provide training in each of the following core competencies: (1) Communication skills; (2) Interpersonal and relationship-building skills; (3) Service coordination and navigation skills; (4) Education and facilitation skills; (5) Advocacy skills; (6) Building individual and community capacity; (7) Individual and community assessment; (8) Outreach skills; (9) Professional skills and conduct; (10) Evaluation and research skills; and (11) Knowledge of social determinants of health and cultural competency and the public health structure. (b) The applicant shall demonstrate completion of the training required by (a), above, by submitting an official transcript as required by Plc 304.01(c), from any of the following training organizations: (1) North Country Health Consortium, available at https://nchcnh.org; (2) Southern New Hampshire Area Health Education Center, available at https://www.snhahec.org/; (3) White Mountains Community College at https://www.wmcc.edu; or (4) Any other training organization with a curriculum meeting the criteria of (a) above. (c) An applicant who has received training from an organization not listed in (b), above, may request a waiver under Plc 211 to allow the alternative organization’s training to be accepted. The executive director shall grant the requested waiver if the applicant demonstrates that either: (1) The alternative organization offers CHW training that meets the standards of the National C3 Council; or (2) The alternative organization’s training is accepted by at least 10 other U.S. jurisdictions that require or offer CHW certification.

Plc 1804.05 Substantially-Similar Professional Experience

Effective 2025-09-22. Expires 2035-09-22.

Plc 1804.05 Substantially-Similar Professional Experience. In lieu of the specific training required by Plc 1804.04, an individual shall have substantially-similar professional work experience that provides training and experience in the topics listed in Plc 1804.04(b), in a similar field such as real estate brokerage or real estate appraisals for non-assessing purposes, in any assessing jurisdiction that is within the definition of “another jurisdiction” in Plc 1802.03. Source. #14330, eff 9-22-25, EXPIRES: 9-22-35

Plc 1603.02 Applying for Initial Registration

Effective 2024-05-06.

Plc 1603.02 Applying for Initial Registration. (a) Any person who meets the qualifications in Plc 1603.01 and wishes to operate as nurse agency in New Hampshire shall file an application for initial registration in accordance with this section. (b) The applicant shall submit the following to the licensing bureau: (1) A completed “Universal Application for Initial License” dated April 2024, together with the “Nurse Agency Registration Supplement to Universal Application for Initial License” dated March 2024, that contains the information specified in Plc 304.03 and Plc 1603.03, and is signed and attested to as specified in Plc 304.05 and Plc 1603.04; (2) The documentation specified in Plc 304.04, as applicable; and (3) The application processing and licensing fee specified in Plc 1002, provided that if no application processing and licensing fee is specified in Plc 1002, no fee shall be charged. Source. #13914, eff 5-6-24

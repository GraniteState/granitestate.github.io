Plc 310.06 Settlement Procedures; Non-Disciplinary Remedial Actions

Effective 2024-05-03.

Plc 310.06 Settlement Procedures; Non-Disciplinary Remedial Actions. The provisions of Plc 205 shall apply to: (a) Any disciplinary matter that does not proceed to an adjudicative hearing or that is otherwise settled by agreement between the parties; and (b) Any proceeding to impose non-disciplinary remedial measures. Source. #13955, eff 5-3-24 (see Revision Note #2 at chapter heading for Plc 300)

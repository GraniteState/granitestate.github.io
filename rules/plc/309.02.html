Plc 309.02 Profession-Specific Ethical and Professional Standards

Effective 2024-05-03.

Plc 309.02 Profession-Specific Ethical and Professional Standards. Each licensee shall adhere to the code of ethics required by rules of the applicable board or other applicable law, including any codes adopted by a regional or national certifying organization if incorporated by reference in rules of the applicable board. Source. (See Revision Note #1 at chapter heading for Plc 300) #13606, eff 5-1-23; ss by #13955, eff 5-3-24 (formerly Plc 310.02) (see Revision Note #2 at chapter heading for Plc 300)

Plc 904.08 Processing of Applications for Initial Licensure; Decisions

Effective 2025-10-17. Expires 2035-10-17.

Plc 904.08 Processing of Applications for Initial Licensure; Decisions. (a) Within 30 days of receipt of an application for initial licensure submitted pursuant to Plc 904.06, the licensing bureau shall process the application as provided in Plc 304.06. (b) The application shall be subject to the abandonment provisions of Plc 304.06(h) and the withdrawal provisions of Plc 304.07. (c) After determining that an application is complete, the licensing bureau shall review the application in accordance with Plc 304.08. (d) The licensing bureau shall make a decision and notify the applicant in accordance with Plc 304.09, subject to (e), below. (e) The licensing bureau shall approve an application for initial licensure and issue a license to practice massage therapy if the applicant: (1) Has submitted an application that meets the requirements of Plc 904.06; (2) Meets the qualifications for granting a license specified in Plc 904.01; and (3) Has not answered “yes” to any of the background and character questions in Plc 304.03(e) or has provided an explanation of each “yes” answer pursuant to Plc 304.04 demonstrating that similar circumstances are not likely to arise in New Hampshire.

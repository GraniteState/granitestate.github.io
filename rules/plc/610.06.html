Plc 610.06 Additional Documentation Required for Initial Microblading Certificate Application

Effective 2026-09-21. Expires 2036-09-21.

Plc 610.06 Additional Documentation Required for Initial Microblading Certificate Application. In addition to the documentation required by Plc 304.04, the applicant for a microblading certificate shall provide: (a) A microblading training completion certificate that states the name and address of the training provider, the organization that accredited the training instructor, the date the training was completed, and the number of hours of training received; and (b) Documentation of the completion of a course in the prevention of disease transmission and blood borne pathogens. Source. (See Revision Note #2 at chapter heading for Plc 600) #13337, eff 1-27-22; ss by #14631, eff 9-21-26, EXPIRES: 9-21-36 (formerly Plc 605.04)

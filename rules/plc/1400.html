Plc 1400 RECREATIONAL THERAPISTS

CHAPTER Plc 1400 RECREATIONAL THERAPISTS Statutory Authority: RSA 326-J:1-a, II; RSA 310:6, II REVISION NOTE: The Governing Board of Recreational Therapists was established under RSA 328-F:3, I pursuant to 2006, 220:4 and under RSA 326-J pursuant to 2006, 220:1, to be effective 7-1-07. Pursuant to 2022, 72:4 and 72:5, effective 7-19-22, the Governing Board of Recreational Therapists was renamed and re-established as the Advisory Board of Recreational Therapists under RSA 326-J:1, I and RSA 326-J:1-a. The former rulemaking authority of the Governing Board of Recreational Therapists was transferred to the Executive Director of the Office of Professional Licensure and Certification under RSA 326-J:1-a, II and RSA 326-J:3, I. The Executive Director of the OPLC adopted Chapter Plc 1400 governing recreational therapists as an interim rule in Document #13512, effective 12-30-22. The Executive Director subsequently adopted and filed Document #13566, effective 2-23-23, repealing Chapter Rec 100 through Chapter Rec 500 of the former Governing Board of Recreational Therapists. The repeal of Chapter Rec 100 through Chapter Rec 500 meant that Document #13512 replaced all prior filings affecting rules of the former Governing Board of Respiratory Care Practitioners.

Plc 1607.03 Rehearings and Appeals

Effective 2024-05-06.

Plc 1607.03 Rehearings and Appeals. (a) As provided in RSA 310:14, I: (1) Any person who has been denied a registration under this chapter shall have the right to a rehearing; and (2) Requests for a rehearing shall be made within 30 days of receipt of the original final decision. (b) Pursuant to RSA 310:14, II, any person who has been disciplined shall have the right to petition in writing for a rehearing within 30 days of receipt of the original final decision. (c) Petitions or requests for rehearing shall be made in the form of a motion as provided in Plc 206.12. (d) Pursuant to RSA 310:14, III: (1) Appeals from a decision on rehearing shall be by appeal to the supreme court pursuant to RSA 541; and (2) No sanction shall be stayed during an appeal.

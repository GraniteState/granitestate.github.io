Plc 1001.01 Purpose

Effective 2025-07-01. Expires 2035-07-01.

Plc 1001.01 Purpose. The purpose of this chapter is to: (a) In Plc 1001, establish the fees charged by the office of professional licensure and certification (OPLC) that are related to: (1) Services provided by or for the OPLC and by or for: a. The boards listed in RSA 310:2, II, hereinafter referred to as “supported boards”; and b. The professions listed in RSA 310:2, II that do not have an associated board; (2) Inspections, including inspections related to licensure and inspections not related to licensure; (3) Obtaining approval from a supported board for a candidate supervision agreement, where such approval is required by applicable law; (4) Obtaining approval from a supported board for a continuing competence provider, program, or instructor, where such approval is required by appliable law based on an application being submitted to the board for review and a decision regarding whether to approve a specific provider, program, or instructor; (5) Audits of continuing competence activities and records; and (6) Attending a board-sponsored program; (b) In Plc 1002, establish the fees charged by the OPLC for application processing and licensing; (c) In Plc 1003, establish the process for paying per diems and providing reimbursements to board members; and (d) In Plc 1004, establish fees for the various examinations required for licensure.

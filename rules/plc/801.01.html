Plc 801.01 Purpose

Effective 2024-04-15.

Plc 801.01 Purpose. The purpose of this chapter is to implement RSA 327-A relative to regulating the profession of ophthalmic dispensing, by establishing requirements for: (a) Obtaining and renewing a certificate of registration to practice ophthalmic dispensing in New Hampshire; (b) Obtaining the reinstatement of a certificate of registration to practice ophthalmic dispensing that has expired or been suspended or revoked; and (c) The regulation of the practices of registered ophthalmic dispensers, including ethical and professional standards required to be met by registrants. Source. (See Revision Note at chapter heading for Plc 800) #13885 eff 4-15-24

Plc 1001.19 Fees for Site Visits and Inspections Not Related to Licensure

Effective 2025-07-01. Expires 2035-07-01.

Plc 1001.19 Fees for Site Visits and Inspections Not Related to Licensure. (a) The fee for any inspection by the board of barbering, cosmetology, and esthetics that is not related to initial licensing shall be $100. (b) The fee for a comprehensive evaluation under Den 304.05 that is not related to initial licensing shall be $750. (c) The fee for a routine inspection of a funeral home, funeral chapel, or crematory not related to licensure shall be $200. (d) The fee for re-inspection of a funeral home, funeral chapel, or crematory after deficiencies are found shall be $150. (e) The fee for an inspection of installed housing by the manufactured housing installation board shall be $250. (f) The fee for an inspection of a program by the board of nursing that is not related to initial approval shall be $100. Source. #13677, eff 7-1-23; ss by #14289, eff 7-1-25, EXPIRES: 7-1-35 (formerly Plc 1001.17)

Plc 900 MASSAGE THERAPISTS

CHAPTER Plc 900 MASSAGE THERAPISTS REVISION NOTE #1: Pursuant to 2015, 276:103 and 104, effective 7-1-15, rulemaking authority under RSA 328-B:4 and RSA 328-B:11-a over massage therapy was transferred to the Executive Director of the Office of Professional Licensure and Certification from the Commissioner of the Department of Health and Human Services. The existing rules of the Department numbered Chapter He-P 900 and titled “Massage Therapists” were also transferred pursuant to 2015, 276:45 under RSA 310-A:1-a, III. The former Chapter He-P 900 subsequently expired 12-11-17. Document #12621, effective 9-21-18, adopted a new Chapter Plc 300 titled “Massage Therapists”. Document #12621 replaced all filings affecting the former Chapter He-P 900. The filings affecting the former He-P 900 included the following documents: #2348, eff 4-28-83, EXPIRED 4-48-89 #5467, eff 9-15-92, EXPIRED 9-15-98 #5849, eff 6-22-94 #6246, eff 5-15-96 #7293, eff 5-24-00 #7471, eff 3-30-01 #8033, eff 2-7-04 #8489, eff 11-18-05 #9612, eff 12-11-09, EXPIRED 12-11-17 REVISION NOTE #2: Document #13383, effective 5-26-22, readopted with amendment Part Plc 303 titled “License by Reciprocity” in Chapter Plc 300. Document #13383 made extensive changes to the wording and numbering of the former Part Plc 303 in Document #12621, including inserting 3 new rules Plc 303.01, Plc 303.02, and Plc 303.03, and readopting with amendment and renumbering Plc 303.01 as Plc 303.04. Document #13383 thereby replaced the filing for Part Plc 303 in Document #12621.

Diet 403.06 Activation of a License That Lapsed for More Than One Year

Effective 2025-09-09. Expires 2035-09-09.

Diet 403.06 Activation of a License That Lapsed for More Than One Year. A licensee who wishes to reactivate a license lapsed for more than one year shall apply for an initial license complying with Diet 302 and provide documentation of completion of a minimum of 20 hours of continuing education in accordance with Diet 405 within the immediately preceding 2 years. Source. #14314, eff 9-9-25, EXPIRES: 9-9-35

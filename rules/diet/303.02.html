Diet 303.02 Denial of Initial Registration

Effective 2025-06-10. Expires 2035-06-10.

Diet 303.02 Denial of Initial Registration. A registration shall be denied if the OPLC finds that the applicant has: (a) Failed to file completed initial application forms required by Diet 302.02 and all required supporting documentation required by Diet 302.03; (b) Failed to submit the fees required by Plc 1001.08 and Plc 1002.12; (c) Failed to show they are of good character as evidenced by the “yes” answer to the question on the initial application form required at Plc 308.06(e); or (d) Failed to fulfill any other condition of registration, including the terms of any settlement or agreement with any other jurisdiction.

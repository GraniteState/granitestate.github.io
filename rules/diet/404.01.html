Diet 404.01 Definitions

Effective 2025-09-09. Expires 2035-09-09.

Diet 404.01 Definitions. (a) “Sexual misconduct” means engaging in one or more of the following activities with respect to a current client who is not a spouse: (1) Engaging in sexual relations, whether consensual or non-consensual; (2) Making verbal or physical sexual advances; (3) Using sexual language or phrases; (4) Requesting sexual favors; or (5) Engaging in physical contact of a sexual nature. Source. #14314, eff 9-9-25, EXPIRES: 9-9-35

Pol 302.15 Behavior in Training Facility

Effective 2016-11-16.

Pol 302.15 Behavior in Training Facility. The following shall apply to behavior in the training facility: (a) Persons using the police standards and training facility shall, as a condition of their use of the facility, be neatly attired in their official department uniforms or otherwise, as follows: (1) For male personnel a suit or sports jacket with slacks, dress shirt and tie, or sports shirt and sweater shall be acceptable; and (2) For females, a dress or skirt and blouse or sweater combination or slacks and coordinated blouse shall be acceptable; and (b) Persons using the facility shall exhibit courtesy and abide by all laws and facility rules at all times, and shall be required to leave the premises or be denied admittance if in violation of this rule, and shall be subject to dismissal. Source. #10048, eff 12-20-11 (from Pol 302.15); renumbered by #10126 (formerly Pol 302.16); ss by #12041, eff 11-16-16

Pol 602.01 Training Records

Effective 2016-11-16. Earlier expiration 2008-11-30.

Pol 602.01 Training Records. The council shall maintain a file on each officer including: (a) Employee status notification forms; (b) Proof of high school diploma or equivalent; (c) Administrative correspondence; (d) Class records of attendance and grades at any training programs conducted by the council; and (e) The medical evaluation forms submitted for officers.

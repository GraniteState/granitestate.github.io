Pol 206.02 Scheduling of Hearing

Effective 2008-08-01. Earlier expiration 2008-11-30.

Pol 206.02 Scheduling of Hearing. The date set for hearing shall allow sufficient and reasonable time for preparation of the case by council and all parties. Source. #5836, eff 6-15-94; ss by #7302, eff 6-8-00; ss by #9167, INTERIM, eff 6-3-08, EXPIRES: 11-30-08; ss by #9223-A, eff 8-1-08

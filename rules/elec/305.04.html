Elec 305.04 Application Fees and Refunds

Effective 2017-07-22. Earlier expiration 2013-12-03.

Elec 305.04 Application Fees and Refunds. (a) Application fees shall be imposed as follows: (1) $50.00 for a journeyman or master application; and (2) $25.00 for a relicensing application. (b) All fees received by the board shall be deposited with the treasurer, state of NH. (c) No refunds of application fees shall be granted. Source. #10356, INTERIM, eff 6-6-13, EXPIRES: 12-3-13 (See Revision Note #1 and Revision Note #2 at chapter heading for Elec 300); ss by #10420, eff 9-25-13; ss by #12334, eff 7-22-17 (formerly Elec 304.04) (See Revision Note #3 at chapter heading for Elec 300)

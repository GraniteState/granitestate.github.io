Elec 305.01 Format and Subject Matter

Effective 2021-05-11. Earlier expiration 2013-12-03.

Elec 305.01 Format and Subject Matter. (a) For exams taken after January 1, 2018, all questions on the National Electrical Code shall be based upon the edition of the National Electrical Code, copyrighted by the National Fire Protection Association, NFPA-70 that has been adopted by the state of New Hampshire pursuant to RSA 155-A:1, IV. (b) The journeyman examination shall consist of 110 questions, as follows: (1) Fifty questions based upon the National Electrical Code as described in (a) above; (2) Fifty questions based upon practical electrical installations as defined in RSA 319-C:2, III; and (3) Ten questions based upon RSA 319-C and the administrative rules. (c) The master examination shall consist of 125 questions, as follows: (1) Fifty questions based upon the National Electrical Code as described in (a) above; (2) Fifty questions based upon practical electrical installations as defined in RSA 319-C:2, III; and (3) Twenty-five questions based upon RSA 319-C and the administrative rules. (d) Practical questions shall be based upon the American Electricians Handbook published by McGraw-Hill, current edition.

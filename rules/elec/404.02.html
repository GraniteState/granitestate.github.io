Elec 404.02 Change in Master Electrician or High/Medium Voltage Electrician Responsible for a Corporation

Effective 2024-05-04. Earlier expiration 2013-12-03.

Elec 404.02 Change in Master Electrician or High/Medium Voltage Electrician Responsible for a Corporation. (a) Within 30 days of a change in the master electrician, or high/medium voltage electrician responsible for the affairs of a corporation or partnership, the president, director, or partner of the corporation or partnership shall furnish the following: (1) A letter on company letterhead that states the reason(s) for the change and the effective date of the change; and (2) The corporation or partnership identification card and license issued by the OPLC. Source. #10356, INTERIM, eff 6-6-13, EXPIRES: 12-3-13 (See Revision Note #1 and Revision Note #2 at chapter heading for Elec 300); ss by #10420, eff 9-25-13; ss by #13898, eff 5-4-24

Fam 403.02 Sanctions

Effective 2018-10-02. Earlier expiration 2018-10-01.

Fam 403.02 Sanctions. Pursuant to RSA 328-C:7, IV, disciplinary measures available to the board to sanction misconduct shall be one or more of the following: (a) Revocation of certification; (b) Suspension of certification; (c) A written warning for inadvertent misconduct; (d) A civil fine up to $300; (e) A written reprimand for knowing misconduct; (f) The imposition of supplemental training requirements; (g) The imposition of supervised training requirements; (h) The imposition of supplemental education requirements; and (i) The imposition of treatment and counseling requirements, including treatment and counseling for alcohol and substance abuse. Source. #9672, eff 4-2-10; ss by #12507, INTERIM, eff 4-2-18, EXPIRES: 10-1-18 New. #12635, eff 10-2-18

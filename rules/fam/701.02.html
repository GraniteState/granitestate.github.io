Fam 701.02 Duration of Certification

Effective 2018-10-02. Earlier expiration 2018-10-01.

Fam 701.02 Duration of Certification. Renewed certification of a training program issued pursuant to this chapter shall be valid for a period of 3 years unless earlier terminated by the board for: (a) Failure of the program to maintain the program components and standards described in Fam 604; (b) Failure of the program to comply with the ongoing requirements set forth in Fam 706; (c) Failure of the program to have as a director an individual meeting the requirements of Fam 604.09; (d) Suspension of the family mediator certification of the director; (e) Violation of Fam 500 by the director or members of the training staff; (f) Fraud or deceit in obtaining or attempting to obtain certification; or (g) Violation by the program, the director, or a member of the training staff of federal or state law prohibiting discrimination or other applicable federal or state law. Source. #9156, eff 5-10-08; ss by #9676, eff 4-2-10; ss by #12507, INTERIM, eff 4-2-18, EXPIRED: 10-1-18 New. #12638, eff 10-2-18

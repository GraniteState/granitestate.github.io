Abfd 301.02 Scope

Effective 2023-06-23. Earlier expiration 2011-12-19.

Abfd 301.02 Scope. Pursuant to RSA 275-C:10, II, this chapter shall apply to buildings, facilities, and their appurtenant grounds and curbs: (a) Which are used or to be used by the public; and (b) The cost of the construction, rehabilitation, or substantial remodeling of which is to be paid for, in whole or in part, by federal, state, county, or municipal funds.

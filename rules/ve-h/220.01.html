Ve-H 220.01 Requests for Explanation of Adopted Rules

Effective 2019-01-15. Earlier expiration 2018-12-26.

Ve-H 220.01 Requests for Explanation of Adopted Rules. Any interested person may, within 30 calendar days of the final adoption of a rule, request a written explanation of that rule by making a written request to the board including: (a) The name and address of the person making the request; or (b) If the request is that of an organization or other entity, the name and address of such organization or entity and the name and address of the representative authorized by the organization or entity to make the request. Source. #8952, eff 7-21-07, EXPIRED: 7-21-15 New. #12563, INTERIM, eff 6-28-18, EXPIRED: 12-26-18 New. #12711, eff 1-15-19

Ve-H 204.01 How to Submit Complaints

Effective 2019-01-15. Earlier expiration 2018-12-26.

Ve-H 204.01 How to Submit Complaints. (a) A complaint against the home, its staff, or a resident may be submitted by any: (1) Resident; (2) Legal representative of a resident; or (3) Applicant for residency or such applicant's legal representative. (b) Complaints shall be addressed to the commandant in a letter containing the following information: (1) The name and address of the complainant; (2) The name and address of the complainant's legal representative, if any; (3) A concise statement of the facts complained of; (4) The identification of any statutes, rules, orders, or other authority which the complainant alleges have been violated, if known by the complainant; and (5) The signature of the complainant.

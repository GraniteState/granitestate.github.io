Agr 3501.01 Maximum fees

Effective 2021-06-01.

Agr 3501.01 Maximum fees. The daily maximum allowable fees shall be: (a) No more than $20 per non-livestock animal, using the definition of livestock in RSA 21:34-a,(II),(a),(4), regardless of species; (b) No more than $40 per non-poultry livestock animal; and (c) No more than $5 per poultry animal. Source. #13208, eff 6-1-21 (see Revision Note at chapter heading for Agr 3500)

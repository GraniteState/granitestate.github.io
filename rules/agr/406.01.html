Agr 406.01 Exception for Feeding of Household Garbage to Swine

Effective 2022-11-08.

Agr 406.01 Exception for Feeding of Household Garbage to Swine. Agr 402 thru Agr 405 shall not apply to a person who feeds exclusively his or her own household garbage to swine which are raised for his or her own use.

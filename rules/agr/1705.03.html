Agr 1705.03 Written Records

Effective 2024-12-31.

Agr 1705.03 Written Records. (a) Each license holder shall keep a written record of all individual animals entering the licensed premises including: (1) Date of purchase or acceptance; (2) Name and address of seller, consignor, or donor; and (3) A description of the animals, including the species, age, and sex. (b) Each license holder shall keep a written record of transfers of all animals leaving their facilities including: (1) Date of transfer; (2) Name and address of recipient; and (3) Species, age, sex, and description of animals. (c) Records shall be maintained at the facility for a minimum of one year after the date of transfer. Such records shall be available upon demand for inspection at the licensed premises by the department during reasonable times. Source. #14164, eff 12-31-24 (formerly Agr 1704.08)

Agr 2110.02 Brucellosis

Effective 2026-05-04. Expires 2026-10-31.

Agr 2110.02 Brucellosis. All swine, unless consigned to slaughter, shall: (a) Originate from a validated brucellosis-free state; (b) Originate from a validated brucellosis-free herd; or (c) Be individually tested for brucellosis within 30 days prior to entry.

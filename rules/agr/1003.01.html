Agr 1003.01 Apples

Effective 2026-04-07. Expires 2036-04-07.

Agr 1003.01 Apples. The following requirements shall apply for use of the seal of quality design on apples: (a) A packer of New Hampshire apples may use the state of New Hampshire seal of quality design after receiving a yearly permit from the department, and only while said yearly permit is active; (b) A packer of New Hampshire apples shall only use the state of New Hampshire seal of quality design on apples in compliance with RSA 426:11-12, Agr 901, and Agr 1003; (c) Apples shall be marked in accordance with the provisions of the U.S. Standards for Grades of Apples; 7 CFR, Section 51.300-51.322, effective October 31, 2019; (d) Apples shall meet the requirements of one of the following grades, and associated color and tolerances, as specified in U.S. Standards for Grades of Apples, 7 CFR, Section 51.300-51.322, effective October 31, 2019: (1) U.S. Extra Fancy; (2) U.S. Fancy; or (3) Combination of U.S. Extra Fancy and U.S. Fancy; (e) Apples shall meet the “Condition After Storage” or “Transit Standards” as specified in the U.S. Standards for Grades of Apples, 7 CFR, Section 51.300-51.322, effective October 31, 2019; and (f) Apples shall be packaged in accordance with the “Packing Requirements” standards as specified in the U.S. Standards for Grades of Apples, 7 CFR, Section 51.300-51.322, effective October 31, 2019.

Agr 503.17 Information About Nursery Stock

Effective 2009-12-22.

Agr 503.17 Information About Nursery Stock. The information submitted about nursery stock for sale shall include: (a) The division of plant and industry nursery license number; or (b) A statement that the plant material for sale was inspected by a plant industry division inspector on a specified date and found free of injurious insects and diseases. Source. #9616, eff 12-22-09 Appendix

Agr 1404.13 Hearings

Effective 2017-12-01. Earlier expiration 2009-09-09.

Agr 1404.13 Hearings. Every applicant for, or holder of, a certificate of registration as a service technician shall be entitled to a hearing pursuant to Agr 200 if his or her application for an initial or renewed certificate of registration is denied. Source.

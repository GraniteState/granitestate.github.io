CHAPTER Agr 3500 COST OF CARE Statutory Authority: RSA 437:9, V and RSA 437-B:1, IV REVISION NOTE: Document #13208, effective 6-1-21, adopted Chapter Agr 3500 titled “Cost of Care”, relative to implement RSA 437:9, V and RSA 437-B:1 on the Cost of Care Fund to assist municipalities in covering the costs of care incurred from caring for animals pending the resolution of any action brought for animal cruelty under RSA 644:8 or RSA 644:8-a.

Agr 700 ACQUISITION OF AGRICULTURAL LAND DEVELOPMENT RIGHTS

CHAPTER Agr 700 ACQUISITION OF AGRICULTURAL LAND DEVELOPMENT RIGHTS Statutory Authority: RSA 432:21 REVISION NOTE: Document #13910, effective 3-21-24, adopted all the previously expired rules in Chapter Agr 700 titled “Acquisition of Agricultural Land Development Rights”. Agr 705.02 titled “Land Evaluation” was readopted with amendment. The former Chapter Agr 700 had expired 3-21-09, and the only rule effective after that date was Agr 705.02 titled “Land Evaluation”, adopted by Document #13804, effective 11-9-23. Document #13910 made changes to the expired rules, including the division of Agr 704.01 titled “Acceptance for Filing” into Agr 704.01 titled “Acceptance for Filing” and Agr 704.02 titled ‘Completed Application”, and the renumbering of the expired rules Agr 704.02 through Agr 704.05 as Agr 704.03 through Agr 704.06. Document #13910 replaces all prior filings for rules in this chapter. The prior filings included the following documents: #1601, eff 7-1-80 #2237, eff 1-1-83 #2887, eff 10-22-84 #4098, eff 7-22-86, EXPIRED: 7-22-92 #7463, eff 3-21-01, EXPIRED: 3-21-09 #13804, eff 11-9-23 (Agr 705.02 only)

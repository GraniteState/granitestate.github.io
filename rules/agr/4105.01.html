Agr 4105.01 Review of Program Data

Effective 2025-01-07.

Agr 4105.01 Review of Program Data. (a) The department shall collect and monitor all animal records information required by RSA 437:8-a through software provided by the program vendor. (b) The department shall review and evaluate the collected information for errors or completeness. Source. #14163, eff 1-7-25

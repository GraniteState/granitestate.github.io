Agr 1403.06 Retention of Copies of Weight Certificates

Effective 2017-12-01. Earlier expiration 2009-09-09.

Agr 1403.06 Retention of Copies of Weight Certificates. (a) Each public weighmaster shall keep and preserve for a period of 2 years all copies of voided certificates and a true legible copy of each weight certificate issued. (b) These records shall be available for inspection by any inspector of the division of weights and measures during normal business hours. Source.

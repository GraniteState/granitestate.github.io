Agr 1708.04 Abandonment of an Application

Effective 2024-12-31.

Agr 1708.04 Abandonment of an Application. An application shall be deemed abandoned when the applicant fails to complete the application and licensure process within 6 months of the department’s initial receipt of any required information unless that time period is extended pursuant to Agr 1708.01 or Agr 1708.03(c). Source. #10834, eff 5-21-15; ss by #14164, eff 12-31-24 (formerly Agr 1707.03)

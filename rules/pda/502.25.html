Pda 502.25 Shorefront property mooring

Effective 2019-12-24.

Pda 502.25 “Shorefront property mooring” means a mooring permitted by the division pursuant to Pda 500 within a shorefront property mooring area and owned by a shorefront property owner. Source. #7940, eff 8-23-03; renumbered by #8775 (from Pda 502.25); ss by #9975, eff 8-20-11 (formerly Pda 502.28) (See Revision Note at chapter heading for Pda 500); ss by #12958, eff 12-24-19

Pda 510.04 Moving or Interfering With Moorings

Effective 2019-12-24.

Pda 510.04-Pda 510.08 Pda 510.03-Pda 510.07 Document #9975 replaces all prior filings for rules in the former Chapter Pda 500 except for rules in Part Pda 512, on mooring fees, which are exempt from RSA 541-A pursuant to RSA 12-G:42, XI. The filings affecting the former rules Pda 502.03, 502.06, and 502.13 that had reserve numbers are as follows: #7940, eff 8-23-03 (Pda 502.03, 502.06, and 502.13) #8321, eff 10-1-05 (repealed Pda 502.03, 502.06, and 502.13) The prior filings affecting the rules which were repealed by Document #9975 are as follows: #7940, eff 8-23-03 (Pda 508.04, 509.09, and 510.03) #8184, eff 10-1-04 (Pda 510.03) #8775, eff 12-16-06 (Pda 509.09 and Pda 510.03)

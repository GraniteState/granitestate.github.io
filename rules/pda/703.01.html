Pda 703.01 Use of State-Owned Restricted Piers

Effective 2025-09-20. Expires 2035-09-20.

Pda 703.01 Use of State-Owned Restricted Piers. A vessel without a slip permit issued under Pda 706 may be secured to a state-owned restricted pier, if the vessel operator requests permission to be so secured and the division director or an employee of the division: (a) Determines that the securing of the vessel would not interfere with the use of the pier by another vessel approaching, departing from, or already secured to the pier and that one or more of the following applies: (1) The vessel requires emergency repairs; (2) Weather or tide conditions make it hazardous for the vessel not to be secured to the pier; (3) A medical emergency exists involving a passenger or crew member; (4) The crew or passengers, or both, of the vessel are making use of state-owned facilities for official government business; or (5) Failure to secure the vessel to the pier would result in an imminent and substantial hazard to navigation or to the safety of any person on board such vessel; and (b) Gives the operator of the vessel oral permission to be secured to the pier, but only for as long as the situation creating the reason for the stay exists. Source. #8845, eff 3-21-07; ss by #10949, eff 10-20-15; ss by #14376, eff 9-20-25, EXPIRES: 9-20-35

Pda 101.25 Mooring buoy

Effective 2006-11-18.

Pda 101.25 “Mooring buoy” means any float or marker which is attached to a mooring anchor that is either suitable for attachment to a vessel through the use of a pennant or other device or facilitates the attachment of the vessel to the mooring anchor. Source. #8760, eff 11-18-06 (formerly Pda 101.17)

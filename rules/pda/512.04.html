Pda 512.04 Mooring Fees; Payment of Fees

Effective 2026-04-01.

Pda 512.04 Mooring Fees; Payment of Fees. (a) All mooring fees shall be nonrefundable, except as provided in Pda 506.01. (b) The fee(s) paid by check or money order shall be made payable to “Pease Development Authority, Division of Ports and Harbors or “PDA-DPH.” PEASE DEVELOPMENT AUTHORITY - DIVISION OF PORTS AND

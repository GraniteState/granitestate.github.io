Pda 507.04 Hearings; Notice of Denial

Effective 2019-12-24.

Pda 507.04 Hearings; Notice of Denial. (a) Any hearing required pursuant to Pda 507.03(a) shall be held by the director or designee. (b) If a mooring permit is denied, or revoked under Pda 507.03 after notice and opportunity for a hearing, notice of the denial or revocation and the reason(s) therefore shall be sent to the applicant in writing within 10 business days of the decision. Source. #7940, eff 8-23-03; ss by #9975, eff 8-20-11 (See Revision Note at chapter heading for Pda 500); ss by #12958, eff 12-24-19

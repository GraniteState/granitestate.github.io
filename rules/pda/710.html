Pda 710 titled “Permit Fees”, adopted by the Pease Development Authority, is exempt from RSA 541-A pursuant to RSA 541-A:21, I(n), RSA 12-G:42, XI, and RSA 12-G:53, III

Part Pda 710 titled “Permit Fees”, adopted by the Pease Development Authority, is exempt from RSA 541-A pursuant to RSA 541-A:21, I(n), RSA 12-G:42, XI, and RSA 12-G:53, III. Exempt rules do not expire as they are not subject to RSA 541-A:16, III and RSA 541-A:17, I. Document #8846, effective 3-21-07, adopted Part Pda 710 as a rule exempt from RSA 541-A. Document #10818, effective 4-17-15, readopted with amendment Pda 710.01 titled “Fee Schedule”, also as an exempt rule. However, Document #14376, effective 9-20-25, readopted or readopted with amendment all of the rules in Chapter Pda 700 as regular rules subject to RSA 541-A, including Part Pda 710. Nevertheless, pursuant to RSA 541-A:21, I(n), RSA 12-G:42, XI, and RSA 12-G:53, III, the rules in Part Pda 710 filed in Document #14376 remain exempt from RSA 541-A and therefore do not expire like the rules in Part Pda 701 through Pda 709, as indicated in the source notes for the rules.

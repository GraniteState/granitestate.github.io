Pda 101.13 Commercial fishing vessel

Effective 2006-11-18.

Pda 101.13 "Commercial fishing vessel" means a commercial vessel outfitted and utilized for the taking of any marine species for purposes of sale. Source. #7847, eff 3-8-03; ss and renumbered by #8146, eff 8-28-04 (from Pda 101.01); renumbered by #8298 (from Pda 101.11); ss and moved by #8760, eff 11-18-06 (from Pda 101.08)

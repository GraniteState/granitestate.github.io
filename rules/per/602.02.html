Per 602.02 Part-time, Temporary, and Seasonal Employees

Effective 2025-12-01. Expires 2035-12-01.

Per 602.02 Part-time, Temporary, and Seasonal Employees. Part-time, temporary, and seasonal employees who have not been converted to permanent employees in accordance with RSA 98-A:2 and Per 601 shall be considered at-will employees not subject to a probationary period or to the procedures for discipline and termination in Per 1000 unless expressly specified in this chapter. Source. #14433, eff 12-1-25, EXPIRES 12-1-35

Per 601.04 Seasonal Appointment

Effective 2025-12-01. Expires 2035-12-01.

Per 601.04 Seasonal Appointment. (a) In accordance with RSA 98-A:3, an appointing authority shall designate as seasonal those positions that can reasonably be anticipated as likely to recur each year for a period of 6 months or more, not necessarily consecutively, in any 12 month period. (b) Pursuant to RSA 98-A:3, a person assigned to a position under a seasonal or a temporary assignment who works the equivalent of 6 months or more, not necessarily consecutively, in any 12 month period in that position shall be determined to be either a permanent temporary employee or a permanent seasonal employee. Source. #8738, eff 10-18-06; ss by #10817, eff 5-17-15; ss by #14433 (formerly Per 601.03), eff 12-1-25, EXPIRES 12-1-35

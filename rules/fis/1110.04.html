Fis 1110.04 Archery and Muzzleloader

Effective 2024-02-23. Earlier expiration 2015-12-28.

Fis 1110.04 Archery and Muzzleloader. In addition to the agent fee required by RSA 214-A:4, I: (a) The fee for an archery license, pursuant to RSA 208:5, I, shall be shall be $31 for residents and $82 for nonresidents; and (b) The fee for a muzzleloader license, pursuant to RSA 208:5-a, shall be shall be $15 for residents and $40 for nonresidents. Source. #10874, EMERGENCY RULE, eff 7-1-15, EXPIRED: 12-28-15 New. #10981, eff 1-1-16; ss by #13874, eff 2-23-24

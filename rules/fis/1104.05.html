Fis 1104.05 Fishing Tournament Permit

Effective 2026-01-02. Expires 2036-01-02.

Fis 1104.05 Fishing Tournament Permit. (a) Effective January 1, 2026, an applicant for a fishing tournament shall complete the on-line form “Application for Permit to Conduct a Fishing Tournament”, Rev. 01/26, available at https://www.wildlife.nh.gov/fishing-new-hampshire/tournaments, and submit the required fee, in accordance with Fis 1110.05. If payment of the fee is not received within 10 days of the date of the notice of approval, the application(s) shall be denied for non-payment. (b) An applicant for a kids’ derby shall complete the on-line form “Application to Conduct a Kids Fishing Derby”, Rev. 01/26, available at https://www.wildlife.nh.gov/fishing-new-hampshire/tournaments.

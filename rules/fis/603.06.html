Fis 603.06 Salt Water Smelt

Effective 2024-11-25. Expires 2034-11-25.

Fis 603.06 Salt Water Smelt. (a) Taking of salt water smelt, by use of net or weir, shall not be permitted between March 1 and December 15, in the waters under the jurisdiction of the state which lie inland of the so-called Memorial Bridge in Portsmouth. (b) The daily limit for salt water smelt, taken by any method from coastal and estuarine waters, shall be 4 quarts liquid measure of whole, head and tail intact, smelt. .

Fis 406.07 Wild Trout Waters

Effective 2023-07-26.

Fis 406.07 Wild Trout Waters. (a) The taking of fish from this waterbody as referenced in Table 400.1 in Fis 406.01(b) shall be subject to the following restrictions: (1) The open season for taking all species shall be the 4th Saturday in April through Labor Day; (2) Only single hook artificial lures and flies shall be used and all hooks shall be barbless or have all barbs pinched; and (3) All fish shall be immediately released unharmed to the water. Source. #7763, eff 1-1-03; ss by #8442, eff 1-1-06; ss by #10425, eff 1-1-14; ss by #13698, eff 7-26-23 (see Revision Note #2 at chapter heading for Fis 400)

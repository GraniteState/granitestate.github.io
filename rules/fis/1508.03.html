Fis 1508.03 Duplicate Certificates

Effective 2018-07-25.

Fis 1508.03 Duplicate Certificates. Students who have successfully completed an OHRV or snowmobile safety course may obtain a duplicate certificate of completion without charge by contacting the law enforcement division of the department. Source. (See Revision Note #1 and Revision Note #2 at chapter heading for Fis 1500) #12590, eff 7-25-18

Fis 502.06 Dealing in Live Fishing Bait

Effective 2023-11-01. Earlier expiration 2006-03-11.

Fis 502.06 Dealing in Live Fishing Bait. Any premises used by persons licensed to take and hold live finfish or aquatic invertebrates for bait shall be open for inspection by the executive director or designee at any time. Source.

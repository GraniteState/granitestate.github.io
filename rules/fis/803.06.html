Fis 803.06 Controlled Species - Importation

Effective 2026-04-21. Expires 2036-04-21.

Fis 803.06 Controlled Species - Importation. (a) A permit to import wildlife shall be required for all species, including hybrids, designated as controlled as cited in Table 800.01.

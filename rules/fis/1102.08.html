Fis 1102.08 Moose Lottery Application

Effective 2018-07-25.

Fis 1102.08 Moose Lottery Application. Applicants for the moose lottery for moose permits pursuant to Fis 1102.09 shall make application on the “Moose Lottery Application” form provided by the department for the current year’s lottery by supplying the following: (a) Complete name and mailing address; (b) Town and state of legal residence at the time of application; (c) Current driver’s license number or state-issued non-driver identification number and an indication as to whether this number has changed since the applicant last applied; (d) Date of birth; (e) Home telephone number and cell or work number; (f) Moose hunting unit preferences; (g) Whether he or she is willing to accept an antlerless-only permit; and (h) Whether he or she wishes to apply for a bonus point only. Source. #8019, eff 12-24-03; (See Revision Note at chapter heading for Fis 1100) renumbered by #8183 (formerly Fis 1102.20); ss by #8517, eff 12-17-05; ss by #9800-B, eff 1-1-11; ss by #12591, eff 7-25-18

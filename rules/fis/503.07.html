Fis 503.07 Intra-scholastic Fishing Tournaments

Effective 2023-11-01.

Fis 503.07 Intra-scholastic Fishing Tournaments. (a) Schools holding intra-scholastic fishing tournaments shall provide the New Hampshire fish and game department, fisheries division, with the following information at least 3 days prior to the beginning of their intra-scholastic tournament: (1) School name; (2) Contact name; (3) Contact phone number and email address; (4) Tournament date; (5) Water body; (6) Maximum number of boats; (7) Species targeted; and (8) If tournament operates under a format of catch, measure, and immediate release. (b) Intra-scholastic fishing tournaments shall be conducted as specified in Fis 503.02(d), Fis 503.05, and Fis 503.06(a), (d) and (e). (c) Schools shall report as specified in Fis 503.04. Source. #10541, eff 4-1-14; ss by #13798, eff 11-1-23

Fis 213.03 Filing and Content of Motion

Effective 2010-01-12. Earlier expiration 2009-05-26.

Fis 213.03 Filing and Content of Motion. (a) A motion for rehearing shall be filed within 30 days of the date of the department decision or order. (b) A motion for rehearing shall: (1) Identify each error of fact, error of reasoning, or error of law which the moving party wishes to have reconsidered; (2) Describe how each error causes the department’s decision to be: a. Unlawful, unjust or unreasonable; b. Or illegal in respect to jurisdiction, authority or observance of the law; c. An abuse of discretion; d. Or arbitrary or capricious; (3) State concisely the factual findings, reasoning or legal conclusion proposed by the moving party; and (4) Include any argument or memorandum of law the moving party wishes to file.

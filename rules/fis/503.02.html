Fis 503.02 Permit Required

Effective 2026-01-02. Expires 2036-01-02.

Fis 503.02 Permit Required. (a) No person shall conduct a fishing tournament without obtaining a fishing tournament permit pursuant to in Fis 1104.05. (b) Effective January 1, 2026, applicants for a fishing tournament shall complete the on-line form “Application for Permit to Conduct a Fishing Tournament”, Rev 01/26, available at: https://www.wildlife.nh.gov/fishing-new-hampshire/tournaments. (c) Applications shall be accepted up to one year prior to the tournament start date. (d) A permit issued for a fishing tournament shall be valid for no longer than 3 consecutive days. (e) A person shall be deemed to have a fishing tournament permit by rule for fishing tournaments that meet the following conditions: (1) The fishing tournament is held entirely on private ponds and no state fish are provided by the department; (2) The fishing tournament is an intra-scholastic fishing tournament; or (3) The proposed tournament is to take place entirely within the coastal and estuarine waters as defined in Fis 601.01 and targets only species of fish that spend all or a portion of their existence in the Atlantic Ocean.

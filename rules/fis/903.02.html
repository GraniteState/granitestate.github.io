Fis 903.02 Webster WMA

Effective 2026-01-27. Expires 2036-01-27.

Fis 903.02 Webster WMA. Webster WMA in Canaan shall be subject to the following deed restrictions: (a) Hunting shall only be permitted with bow and arrow, muzzle loader, or shotgun; and (b) Shooting within 300 yards of U.S. Highway Route 4 shall not be permitted.

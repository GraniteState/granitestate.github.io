Fis 302.11 Pheasant

Effective 2025-07-10. Expires 2035-07-10.

Fis 302.11 Pheasant. (a) The open season for pheasant shall be October 1 through December 31, however the pheasant season shall be closed until 2:00 pm each Thursday and Friday as specified in Table 300.02 below: Table 300.02 Days Pheasant Season Closed Until Noon If the October 1 Opening Day of Season Occurs on a: | On the Following Days the Season is Closed Until 2:00 pm: Sunday | Thursday and Friday, October 5-6, 12-13, and 19-20 Monday | Thursday and Friday, October 4-5, 11-12, and 18-19 Tuesday | Thursday and Friday, October 3-4, 10-11, and 17-18 Wednesday | Thursday and Friday, October 2-3, 9-10, and 16-17 Thursday | Thursday and Friday, October 8-9, 15-16, and 22-23 Friday | Thursday and Friday, October 7-8, 14-15, and 21-22 Saturday | Thursday and Friday, October 6-7, 13-14, and 20-21 (b) The daily bag limit shall be 2 pheasants, and the season limit shall be 10 pheasants.

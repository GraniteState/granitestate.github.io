Fis 303.07 American Marten or Canada Lynx

Effective 2017-04-18.

Fis 303.07 American Marten or Canada Lynx. (a) There shall be no open season for taking American marten. (b) There shall be no open season for taking Canada lynx as it is listed as an endangered species in Fis 1001.01. (c) Any American marten or Canada lynx trapped shall be reported immediately by contacting the fish and game department at 603-271-3361 on business days during the hours of 8:00 AM to 4:30 PM, or if the fish and game department is closed, by contacting the nearest conservation officer.

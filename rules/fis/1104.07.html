Fis 1104.07 Importing Bait Fish

Effective 2024-02-23.

Fis 1104.07 Importing Bait Fish. (a) Applicants for an annual permit to import bait fish shall complete and submit a “N.H. Fish and Game Department Application to Import Fin Fish for Bait” form, Rev. 12/2022, available at import-bait-fish.pdf (nh.gov), to the fish and game department at 11 Hazen Drive, Concord, NH 03301. (b) All permits to import bait fish shall expire on December 31 of the year noted on the permit. (c) No permit to import bait fish under RSA 214:34-d shall be issued to an applicant if: (1) That person, as a holder of a permit for a previous year, has failed to file all of the reports required by Fis 502.08; (2) The applicant has failed to pay the required fee; (3) The applicant does not already possess a valid annual New Hampshire bait dealer license; or (4) The applicant’s bait dealer license is under suspension or revocation in accordance with Fis 502.09. Source. (See Revision Note at chapter heading for Fis 1100) #8183, eff 9-28-04 (formerly Fis 1104.03); ss by #9773-B, eff 1-1-11; ss by #12768, eff 5-3-19; ss by #13874, eff 2-23-24

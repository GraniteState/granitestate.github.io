Fis 815.03 Petitions to the Fish and Game Commission

Effective 2026-04-21. Expires 2036-04-21.

Fis 815.03 Petitions to the Fish and Game Commission. (a) Any person aggrieved by the decision of the executive director with regards to a permit denial pursuant to Fis 815.02 may appeal such decision by written petition to the fish and game commission (commission). (b) Pursuant to RSA 206:4-a, VI, the commission shall hear the appeal within 45 days of receiving the written request. (c) A majority vote of a quorum of commissioners shall be required to reverse the decision of the executive director. (d) The commission’s ruling shall be in writing and issued within 14 days of the hearing.

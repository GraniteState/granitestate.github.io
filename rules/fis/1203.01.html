Fis 1203.01 Free Fishing Day

Effective 2017-02-16. Earlier expiration 2006-07-26.

Fis 1203.01 Free Fishing Day. No fishing license shall be required to take fish, by angling, in any public waters within the jurisdiction of the state on the first Saturday of June and the third Saturday in January, except that those anglers participating in a fishing tournament on either day shall be required to possess a valid fishing license.

Fis 1303.01 Record Keeping

Effective 2025-10-22. Expires 2035-10-22.

Fis 1303.01 Record Keeping. (a) Each licensed guide shall keep written records for all guiding trips completed. (b) The records shall include the following for each trip: (1) The date(s) on which guiding occurred; (2) For each person guided on the trip: a. The client’s address; b. The client’s hunting or fishing license number; and c. The client’s phone number; (3) The towns in which guiding occurred; and (4) The species of game or fish sought. (c) Records shall be updated within 10 days of the conclusion of the guided trip. (d) Such records shall be kept for a minimum of 5 years and shall be made immediately available to the executive director or designated agent upon request.

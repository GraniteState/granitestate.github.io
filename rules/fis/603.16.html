Fis 603.16 Weakfish

Effective 2004-05-24. Earlier expiration 2000-02-18.

Fis 603.16 Weakfish. (a) No person shall take or possess weakfish (Cynoscion regalis) less than 16 inches total length. (b) No person shall possess more than 10 weakfish per day. Source. #5521, eff 12-3-92; ss by #5789, eff 2-18-94, EXPIRED: 2-18-00 New. #7215, eff 3-16-00; ss by #8088, EXEMPT, eff 5-24-04

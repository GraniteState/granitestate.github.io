Fis 603.26 Atlantic Mackerel

Effective 2026-05-18.

Fis 603.26 Atlantic Mackerel. (a) For the purposes of this section, “recreational take” means the taking of finfish by any method for personal use only and which are not sold, traded, or bartered. (b) The recreational take or possession of Atlantic mackerel (Scomber scombrus) shall be no more than 25 per person per day, with the exception that a person angling from a licensed saltwater for hire fishing vessel may take or possess 50 Atlantic mackerel per day. (c) Notwithstanding paragraph (b) above, Atlantic mackerel purchased from a commercial dealer, with proof of purchase, do not count towards the possession limit of 25 Atlantic mackerel per day. Source. #13652, EXEMPT, eff 5-25-23; ss by #14606, EXEMPT, eff 5-18-26

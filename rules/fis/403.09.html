Fis 403.09 Northern Pike (Esox lucius)

Effective 2023-07-26. Earlier expiration 1997-01-01.

Fis 403.09 Northern Pike (Esox lucius). (a) The minimum length for northern pike shall be 28 inches, except in Jericho Lake, Berlin as specified in Fis 406.05(f)(10). (b) The daily bag limit for northern pike shall be one fish, except in Jericho Lake, Berlin as specified in Fis 406.05(f)(9). Source.

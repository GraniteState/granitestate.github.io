Fis 302.07 Common Snipe

Effective 2026-04-18.

Fis 302.07 Common Snipe. (a) Pursuant to authority granted by RSA 209:6-b, and in accordance with 50 CFR Part 20, the season for taking common snipe shall open on September 15 and close on November 14 each year. (b) Taking of common snipe shall be limited to a daily bag of 8, and a possession limit of 24. (c) Daily shooting hours shall begin one-half hour before sunrise and end at sunset.

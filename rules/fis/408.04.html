Fis 408.04 Black Bass

Effective 2025-10-22. Expires 2035-10-22.

Fis 408.04 Black Bass. (a) There shall be no closed season for taking black bass by all legal methods, except as otherwise provided in Fis 408.10. (b) The daily bag limits shall be as follows: (1) From January 1 to May 14, 2 fish; (2) From May 15 to June 15, all black bass shall be immediately released unharmed at the site of catch to the waters from which taken; and (3) From June 16 to December 31, 5 fish. (c) From May 15 to June 15, black bass shall be taken only by artificial lures and flies.

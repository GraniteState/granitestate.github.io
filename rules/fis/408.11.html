Fis 408.11 Liability for Prosecution

Effective 2023-07-26. Earlier expiration 2005-01-21.

Fis 408.11 Liability for Prosecution. Violators of the provisions of this part shall be liable to prosecution under the laws of New Hampshire or, alternatively, may be liable to prosecution under Vermont law.

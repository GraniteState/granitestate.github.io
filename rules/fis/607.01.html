Fis 607.01 Northern Shrimp

Effective 2014-01-01.

Fis 607.01 Northern Shrimp. (a) No person shall fish for, take, land, transport, or have in possession any northern shrimp (Pandalus borealis) while on or leaving the waters of the state. (b) Nothing in this section shall be construed to restrict the possession of shrimp for use as bait on vessels not equipped with an otter trawl.

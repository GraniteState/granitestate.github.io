Fis 1503.02 Annual Registration Electronic Application Process

Effective 2025-07-09. Expires 2035-07-09.

Fis 1503.02 Annual Registration Electronic Application Process. (a) The annual registration described in Fis 1503.03 shall be applied for either electronically, in person at an agent location or headquarters, or through regular mail. If the applicant wishes to apply electronically they shall do so by accessing the application on the department’s online website at https://www.wildlife.nh.gov/highway-recreational-vehicles-ohrv-and-snowmobiles/registering-your-ohrv-or-snowmobile. (b) An applicant shall provide, in addition to the information required on the form required in Fis 1503.03, the following: (1) An email address; and (2) Credit card information required to pay fees. (c) The electronic application shall include a check box for the applicant to indicate understanding that operation of the registered vehicle shall not be allowed until registration and decals have been received via mail and placed on the vehicle(s), in accordance with RSA 215-A:21, VI or RSA 215-C:36, IX. (d) The applicant shall pay a processing fee and credit card fee, in addition to the fees specified in Fis 1503.03. (e) A minimum of 5 business days shall be expected for processing by the department. Source.

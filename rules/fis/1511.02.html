Fis 1511.02 Definitions

Effective 2018-07-25.

Fis 1511.02 Definitions. (a) “All-Terrain Vehicle (ATV)” means “all-terrain vehicle” as defined in RSA 215-A:1, I-b. (b) “Muffler” means “muffler” as defined in RSA 259:66. (c) “Off Highway Recreational Vehicle (OHRV)” means “off highway recreational vehicle” as defined in RSA 215-A:1, VI. (d) “Operate" means “operate” as defined in RSA 215-A:1, VII. (e) “Operator” means “operator” as defined in RSA 215-A:1, VII. (f) “Sound level" means total noise emission from the entire vehicle. (g) “Trail bike” means “trail bike” as defined in RSA 215-A:1, XIV. (h) “Utility Terrain Vehicle (UTV)” means “utility terrain vehicle” as defined in RSA 215-A:1, XXIV. Source. (See Revision Note #1 and Revision Note #2 at chapter heading for Fis 1500) #12590, eff 7-25-18

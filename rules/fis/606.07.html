Fis 606.07 Display of Clam Licenses

Effective 2018-10-27. Earlier expiration 2018-10-26.

Fis 606.07 Display of Clam Licenses. Clam licensees shall wear said licenses in plain view, either on the cap or hat, or on the front of the outer garment, while engaged in taking clams.

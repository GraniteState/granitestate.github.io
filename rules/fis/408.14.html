Fis 408.14 Fishways Closed

Effective 2023-07-26. Earlier expiration 2004-05-22.

Fis 408.14 Fishways Closed. Fishways on the Connecticut River shall be closed as follows: (a) Vernon Dam in Vernon, Vermont and Hinsdale, New Hampshire shall be closed to all fishing 150 feet below the dam; and (b) Wilder Dam in Wilder, Vermont and Lebanon, New Hampshire shall be closed to all fishing 150 feet below the dam.

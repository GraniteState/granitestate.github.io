Fis 500 BAIT FISH, ORGANIZED AND/OR COMMERCIAL ACTIVITIES

CHAPTER Fis 500 BAIT FISH, ORGANIZED AND/OR COMMERCIAL ACTIVITIES Statutory Authority: REVISION NOTE: Document #5016 made extensive changes to the wording, format and structure of Fis 400 and Fis 500. Document #5016 supersedes all prior filings for the sections in these chapters except for Parts Fis 408 and 410, which were not included in Document #5016.

Occ 405.07 Qualifications to be a Supervisor

Effective 2026-03-13. Expires 2036-03-13.

Occ 405.07 Qualifications to be a Supervisor. To qualify to supervise occupational therapy assistants individuals shall be: (a) Currently licensed in New Hampshire as an occupational therapist for a minimum of one year; (b) Not under disciplinary investigation by the OPLC, another state or jurisdiction, or under pending disciplinary charges in the facilities where supervision is to take place; and (c) Not related in any of the following ways to the occupational therapy assistants being supervised: (1) Spouse; (2) Parent, step-parent, parent-in-law, or step-parent in-law; (3) Natural, foster or adopted child, or stepchild; (4) Sibling, brother-in-law, or sister-in-law; (5) First or second cousin; (6) Grandparent; or (7) Aunt or uncle.

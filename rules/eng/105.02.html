Eng 105.02 Quorum

Effective 2023-10-24.

Eng 105.02 Quorum. A quorum of the board shall consist of a majority of the members appointed by Governor and Counsel. A quorum of the board shall be necessary to pass a motion unless otherwise specified by law. In the absence of the chair or vice chair the chair shall designate a pro tempore officer for the officer or officers absent. Source. #6910, eff 12-15-98; ss by #13736, eff 10-24-23

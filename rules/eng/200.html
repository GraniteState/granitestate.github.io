Eng 200 RULES OF PRACTICE AND PROCEDURE

CHAPTER Eng 200 RULES OF PRACTICE AND PROCEDURE REVISION NOTE: Document #13890, effective 2-24-24, repealed Part Eng 201 through Part Eng 217 in Chapter Eng 200, titled “Practice and Procedure”, and renamed the chapter as “Rules of Practice and Procedure”. Document #13890 also adopted a new Part Eng 201 titled “Applicability and Waiver of Substantive Rules”, containing Eng 201.01 titled “Applicability of Plc 200” and Eng 201.02 titled “Waiver of Administrative Rules.” Document #13890 replaces all prior filings affecting the rules in the former Chapter Eng 200. The prior filings included the following documents. Italics indicate the rules were subject to expiration only pursuant to RSA 541-A:17, II: #5397, eff 5-20-92 #6752, INTERIM, eff 5-20-98, EXPIRED: 9-17-98 #7468-A, eff 3-24-01 (Eng 201-Eng 216 only) #10044, eff 1-1-12 (Eng 217 only) As practice and procedure rules, the rules in Document #13890 will not expire except pursuant to RSA 541-A:17, II.

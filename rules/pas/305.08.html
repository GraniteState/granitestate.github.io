Pas 305.08 General Acceptance Testing of New Passenger Tramways

Effective 2024-12-06. Expires 2034-12-06.

Pas 305.08 General Acceptance Testing of New Passenger Tramways. (a) All new passenger tramways shall be subject to an inspection and a running test in accordance with ANSI B77.1-2022 standards effective 2022 and available as noted in Appendix B. The test shall be witnessed by an authorized representative or designee of the board. (b) An authorized representative of the board pursuant to (a) above shall include: (1) Engineers as designated by the board; (2) Members of the tramway board; and (3) The state of New Hampshire's chief of tramway safety or state tramway inspector. (c) In the event of a modification to an existing passenger tramway, the testing requirements of the qualified engineer shall be completed. The board shall require testing of one or more components beyond that required by the qualified engineer if the board determines that the modification adversely affects the safety of the lift. For fiber rope tows, testing shall be completed by the designer. (d) The board shall be given 10 days advance notice that the tramway shall be ready for testing.

Pas 307.03 Training

Effective 2022-08-06. Earlier expiration 2010-12-11.

Pas 307.03 Training. (a) All personnel employed in connection with lift operations at any passenger tramway shall be trained in their duties. (b) The area operator shall be responsible for carrying out such training as is necessary to permit the safe performance of all duties assigned to passenger tramway personnel.

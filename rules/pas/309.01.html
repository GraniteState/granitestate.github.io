Pas 309.01 Notice

Effective 2022-08-06. Earlier expiration 2010-12-11.

Pas 309.01 Notice. Notice of an emergency shutdown order issued by the board as set forth in RSA 255-A:18-a shall include the following: (a) Date; (b) Name of person to whom the order is directed; (c) Order to shutdown tramway; (d) Tramway plate number; (e) Statement of duration of the order; (f) Signature of board member issuing the order, or his or her agent; and (g) Affidavit of service, including: (1) Name of person to whom the order is served; (2) Date and time of service; and (3) Signature of person making service.

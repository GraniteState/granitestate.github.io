Pas 304.03 Wire Rope Splicers

Effective 2022-08-06. Earlier expiration 2010-12-11.

Pas 304.03 Wire Rope Splicers. (a) The board shall qualify a wire rope splicer by demonstration of professional competence, education and experience as evidenced by, but not limited to the applicant’s: (1) Resume; (2) Experience with rope wire; (3) Education by a wire rope manufacturer; (4) Ability to splice and knowledge of wire rope; and (5) Certification or diploma from a wire rope company stating the applicant’s level of training. Source. #7800, eff 12-11-02, EXPIRED: 12-11-10 New. #10072, eff 1-11-12; ss by #13429-B, eff 8-6-22 (see Revision Note #2 at chapter heading for Pass 300)

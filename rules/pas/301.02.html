Pas 301.02 Area operator

Effective 2022-08-06. Earlier expiration 2010-12-11.

Pas 301.02 “Area operator” means the person who owns or is responsible for the operation of a facility using a passenger tramway. Source. #2297, eff 2-7-83; ss by #2704, eff 5-9-84, EXPIRED 5-9-90 (formerly Saf-S 1001.02) New. #4862, eff 7-10-90; ss by #5434, eff 7-8-92; ss by #6020, eff 4-14-95; ss by #6284, eff 7-10-96; ss by #7800, eff 12-11-02, EXPIRED: 12-11-10 New. #9354-B, eff 1-3-09; ss by #10072, eff 1-11-12 (from Pas 301.03); ss by #13429-B, eff 8-6-22 (see Revision Note #2 at chapter heading for Pas 300)

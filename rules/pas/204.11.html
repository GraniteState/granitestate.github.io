Pas 204.11 Exceptions

Effective 2024-12-06.

Pas 204.11 Exceptions. The board shall be authorized to grant exceptions from the design, operations, and safety-related requirements of the passenger tramway rules or permit the use of other devices or methods that provide features or functions comparable to those required by the passenger tramway rules in accordance with the American National Standards for Passenger Ropeways – Aerial Tramways, Aerial Lifts, Surface Lifts, Tows and Conveyors – Safety Requirements (ANSI B77.1-2022), section 1, “General Requirements”, effective 2022 and available as noted in Appendix B. The operator requesting an exception shall apply in writing to the board and the request shall contain: (a) A written description of the device(s) or method(s) for which the exception is requested, including a reference to the administrative rules that normally apply; (b) A written description of how the use of alternative device(s) or method(s) will maintain the tramway in question in a state that is comparable to full conformance; and (c) The period of time for which the exception will be in effect. Source. #14144-A, eff 12-6-24

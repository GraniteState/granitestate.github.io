Tra 202.19 Standard and Burden of Proof

Effective 2003-07-26.

Tra 202.19 Standard and Burden of Proof. (a) A party requesting relief from the department shall bear the burden of producing evidence to show, as a matter of law, an entitlement to such relief. (b) A party asserting a fact shall bear the burden of proving the truth of the fact by a preponderance of the evidence, meaning proof that the proposition is more likely true than not true. Source. #7927-A, eff 7-26-03

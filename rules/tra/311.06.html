Tra 311.06 Manufacture

Effective 2023-03-22.

Tra 311.06 Manufacture. (a) Unless otherwise specified, the specifications for message panels and name plaques, shall conform to the pertinent requirements of signs as set forth in the 2016 edition or as amended, of the department’s “Standard Specifications for Road and Bridge Construction” available on-line or from the department; (b) Message panel text shall be limited to the phrase “PLEASE DON’T DRINK AND DRIVE”; and (c) Memory panels shall comply with the following: (1) Text shall be limited to the phrase “IN MEMORY OF” with the name of the victim(s), limited to 4 names per plate; and (2) A DWI sign assembly installed without at least one name plaque shall not include a memory panel. Source. #10165, eff 7-27-12; ss by #13588, eff 3-22-23

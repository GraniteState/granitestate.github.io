Lot 8108.02 Cybersecurity Audit

Effective 2025-12-03. Expires 2035-12-03.

Lot 8108.02 Cybersecurity Audit. Pursuant to RSA 284:22-c, IV(g), each ADW provider shall file with the commission by April 15 of each year for the previous calendar year in which ADW was offered a copy of the annual independent third-party cybersecurity audit which shall include, at a minimum, the following: (a) A PCI attestation of compliance; (b) A penetration test of all digital platforms, mobile applications, internal, external, and wireless networks to confirm if identified vulnerabilities of all devices, the servers, and applications are susceptible to compromise; and (c) Recommended corrective action, and remediation plans, if applicable. Source. #14448, eff 12-3-25, EXPIRES: 12-3-35

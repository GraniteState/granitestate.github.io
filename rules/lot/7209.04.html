Lot 7209.04 MEAL Requirements

Effective 2026-02-04. Expires 2036-02-04.

Lot 7209.04 MEAL Requirements. (a) Each EGD shall have and each individual who gains entry to the device shall complete a MEAL. (b) The MEAL shall document: (1) The serial number or other unique ID of the device being entered; (2) The identification of the individual who entered the device; (3) The date and time of entry; (4) The duration of entry; (5) The reason for entry; and (6) Activity while entered, including the specific areas accessed and changes made. (c) Entries in the MEAL shall not be required when removing a stacker or drop box from gaming equipment during normal drop procedures. (d) The game operator shall retain the MEAL for a period of 2 years from the date of the last entry and make it available to the commission upon request. Source. #14503, eff 2-4-26, EXPIRES: 2-4-36

Emab 206.02 Contents of Explanation

Effective 2014-06-10.

Emab 206.02 Contents of Explanation. The appellate board shall, within 90 days of receiving a request in accordance with Emp 206.01, provide a written response which: (a) Concisely states the meaning of the rule adopted; (b) Concisely states the principal reasons for and against the adoption of the rule in its final form; and (c) States, if applicable, why the appellate board overruled any arguments and considerations presented against the rule. Source. #10622, eff 6-10-14 Appendix

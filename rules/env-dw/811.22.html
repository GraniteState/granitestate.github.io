Env-Dw 811.22 Contaminant Source Information

Effective 2019-09-30.

Env-Dw 811.22 Contaminant Source Information. (a) If a CWS O/O reports in the CCR a detection of any contaminant as specified in Env-Dw 811.09, the owner shall also report the likely source of each detected contaminant to the best of the O/O’s knowledge.

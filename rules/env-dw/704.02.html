Env-Dw 704.02 MCLs and MCLGs for Health-Related Regulated IOC Contaminants

Effective 2020-05-28.

Env-Dw 704.02 MCLs and MCLGs for Health-Related Regulated IOC Contaminants. The MCLs and MCLGs for health-related regulated IOC contaminants shall be as listed in Table 704-1, below: Table 704-1: IOC MCLs and MCLGs IOC Contaminant | MCL (mg/L unless otherwise specified) | MCLG (mg/L unless otherwise specified) Antimony | 0.006 | 0.006 Arsenic through June 30, 2021 | 0.010 | Zero Arsenic as of July 1, 2021 | 0.0050 | Zero Asbestos | 7 million fibers/L (longer than 10 μm) | 7 million fibers/L (longer than 10 μm) Barium | 2 | 2 Beryllium | 0.004 | 0.004 Cadmium | 0.005 | 0.005 Chromium | 0.1 | 0.1 Copper | See Env-Dw 704.03 | 1.3 Cyanide (as free Cyanide) | 0.2 | 0.2 Fluoride (also see Env‑Dw 706) | 4.0 | 4.0 Lead | See Env-Dw 704.03 | Zero Mercury | 0.002 | 0.002 Nitrate (as N) | 10 | 10 Nitrite (as N) | 1 | 1 Total Nitrate + Nitrite | 10 | 10 Selenium | 0.05 | 0.05 Thallium | 0.002 | 0.0005 Source. #9699, eff 5-1-10; ss by #12665, eff 1-1-19; ss by #13044, eff 5-28-20

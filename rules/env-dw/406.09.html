Env-Dw 406.09 Application for Part II, Final Design

Effective 2024-12-25.

Env-Dw 406.09 Application for Part II, Final Design. (a) An applicant requiring final design of a non-community water system, including reactivation or registration, shall provide the following information constituting part II of the application: (1) A corrected copy of part I of the application previously submitted, which clearly identifies any changes from the original submittal, if applicable; (2) A revised plan, if different than that submitted with part I of the application pursuant to Env-Dw 406.06(a); (3) The well completion report for each well as required in We 800; (4) The water quality analysis results of the water from each well as specified in Env-Dw 406.16; (5) The well capacity of each well proposed to be connected to the PWS determined pursuant to Env-Dw 406.15; (6) The name of a designated primary operator certified in accordance with Env-Dw 502 or a person that has taken or arranged to take the operator certification exam as required under Env-Dw 502; (7) For NTNC water systems, a business plan pursuant to Env-Dw 602; (8) An emergency plan pursuant to Env-Dw 503; (9) The following design information, as applicable: a. For storage tank(s), the information in Env-Dw 406.20; b. For pump controls and auxiliary equipment, the information in Env-Dw 406.18; c. For a pumphouse or pump room, the information in Env-Dw 406.17; d. For well appurtenances and pumps, the information in Env-Dw 406.19; e. For wiring and control devices, the information in Env-Dw 406.21; f. For water treatment facilities, the information in Env-Dw 406.22, Env-Dw 406.23, and Env-Dw 406.25; and g. For water distribution systems, the information in Env-Dw 406.26; and (10) For well source(s) constituting a large groundwater withdrawal as defined in RSA 485-C:2, a copy of the large groundwater withdrawal permit issued by the department in accordance with Env-Wq 403. (b) Any material, product, or equipment incorporated into a PWS shall comply with the appropriate standard(s) of the AWWA, as specified in Env-Dw 402 if such a standard exists, and shall be specified in the design information accordingly. (c) The applicant shall sign and date the application to certify that the information provided is accurate. (d) The design review fee required by Env-Dw 402.03 shall be paid in conjunction with submission of part II of the application. Source. #14158, eff 12-25-24

Env-Dw 405.14 Determination of the SPA

Effective 2024-12-25.

Env-Dw 405.14 Determination of the SPA. (a) The purpose of the SPA shall be to protect the long-term quality of each PWS well. (b) The SPA for any well supplying water to small CWSs shall be established in accordance with Env-Dw 302 or Env-Dw 305, as applicable. (c) Land use within the SPA for small CWSs shall be in accordance with Env-Dw 302, Env-Dw 305, or both as applicable. (d) Notwithstanding the requirements of Env-Dw 302 and Env-Dw 305, no well connected to a PWS shall be located closer to an aboveground storage tank or underground storage tank than the applicable distances specified in Env-Or 300 and Env-Or 400, as applicable. Source. #14158, eff 12-25-24

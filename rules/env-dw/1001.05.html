Env-Dw 1001.05 Eligibility for Surface Water Treatment Supplemental Grants

Effective 2024-11-23. Expires 2034-11-23.

Env-Dw 1001.05 Eligibility for Surface Water Treatment Supplemental Grants. (a) In order to be eligible for surface water treatment supplemental grants in the amount of 10% of the total eligible surface water treatment costs, the water system shall: (1) Demonstrate to the department that construction will result in user fees for the average single family home that are 20% above the statewide average for residential customers as established by the department pursuant to Env-Dw 1001.06; and (2) Meet all eligibility requirements of Env-Dw 1001.03. (b) The effect of the 10% supplemental grant shall not be included in the amount used to calculate eligibility under this section. (c) Where a system uses the fixture rate method, a list of all customers and their charges for the previous year shall be submitted to the department. Source.

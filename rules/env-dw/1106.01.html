Env-Dw 1106.01 Final Application Requirements

Env-Dw 1106.01 Final Application Requirements. An applicant whose project is on the PPL who wishes to obtain funding shall submit to the department the following: (a) The information required for a final application specified in Env-Dw 1106.03 on or with “Form 1: Final Application”, NHDES-W-03-064, December 2020, available at https://onlineforms.nh.gov/?FormTag=NHDES-W-03-064; (b) Confirmation of the applicant’s authority to borrow as specified in Env-Dw 1106.04; (c) Information regarding the environmental review required by Env-Dw 1108, as specified in Env-Dw 1106.05; (d) A capacity assurance self-assessment as specified in Env-Dw 1106.06; (e) The bank information specified in Env-Dw 1106.07; (f) A planning document as specified in Env-Dw 1106.08; and (g) Verification of a revenue program as specified in Env-Dw 1106.09.

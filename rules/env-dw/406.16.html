Env-Dw 406.16 Water Supply Quality Sampling

Effective 2024-12-25.

Env-Dw 406.16 Water Supply Quality Sampling. (a) The owner shall take a water sample from each source and have the sample analyzed for quality by a laboratory accredited by the New Hampshire environmental laboratory accreditation program for the analysis requested. Results from unaccredited laboratories shall not be accepted. (b) Water quality for NTNC water systems shall meet the applicable standards established in chapter Env-Dw 700 or shall be capable of meeting these standards with approved treatment. (c) Wells proposed to be connected to any non-community water systems shall be sampled for: (1) Arsenic; (2) Total coliform bacteria and E. coli; (3) Chloride; (4) Copper; (5) Fluoride; (6) Hardness; (7) Iron; (8) Lead; (9) Manganese; (10) Nitrate and nitrite; (11) pH; (12) Sodium; (13) Per- and Polyfluoroalkyl Substances (PFAS); and (14) Radon. (d) In addition to the sampling required by (c) above, wells proposed to be connected to a NTNC water system shall be sampled for radionuclides as required by Env-Dw 710.05(c). (e) The department shall require testing for additional contaminants if review of available data identifies contamination sources that might contaminate the well. (f) The collection date of the samples taken in accordance with (a) above shall be no greater than 6 months prior to the time the final design application is submitted to the department. (g) Samples taken in accordance with (a) above shall be taken after at least 3 well volumes have been pumped from the well. (h) The owner shall have the laboratory results submitted to the department as originals or copies of the original. Re- typing or re-writing the data shall not be acceptable. Source.

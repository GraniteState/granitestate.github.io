Env-Dw 504.03 General System Maintenance

Effective 2024-06-25.

Env-Dw 504.03 General System Maintenance. (a) Each PWS shall have a maintenance schedule for each of the following: (1) Wells, reservoirs, and intakes; (2) Pump stations; (3) Electrical equipment and controls; (4) Water storage tanks; (5) Distribution system; (6) Water treatment facilities; and (7) Water meters and instrumentation. (b) The schedule shall be based on: (1) The maintenance frequencies recommended by the manufacturer, for those components for which such recommendations exist; and (2) For all other components, the frequencies recommended by the American Water Works Association or commonly used in the water works industry. Source. (See Revision Note at part heading for Env-Dw 504) #10465, eff 12-2-13; ss by #14006, eff 6-25-24

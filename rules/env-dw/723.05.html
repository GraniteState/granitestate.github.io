Env-Dw 723.05 Non-Central Treatment Implementation Requirements

Effective 2024-11-23. Expires 2034-11-23.

Env-Dw 723.05 Non-Central Treatment Implementation Requirements. (a) The requirements of this section shall apply after a PWS owner receives approval for the installation and use of non-central treatment devices pursuant to Env-Dw 723.02 and Env-Dw 723.03 and complies with Env-Dw 723.04. (b) With respect to the contract required by Env-Dw 723.02(i), the PWS owner shall: (1) Not allow the contract to lapse for greater than 15 days; and (2) Make the contract available for review by department staff upon request. (c) With respect to the non-central treatment devices installed, the owner shall: (1) Operate and maintain each non-central treatment device, including those installed in private homes or businesses, as specified in the monitoring plan approved under Env-Dw 723.04, by maintaining a contract with a certified operator for specific implementation of operation and maintenance; (2) Submit data documenting that every building and every device within such building is subject to treatment and monitoring; and (3) Not separate the non-central treatment device from the PWS by sale, lease, or other conveyance of the property. (d) Monitoring shall be in accordance with Env-Dw 723.08. Source. #10618, eff 6-1-14, EXPIRED 6-1-24 New. #14119, eff 11-23-24, EXPIRES: 11-23-34

Env-Dw 406.03 Approval Required for New Non-Community Water Systems

Effective 2024-12-25.

Env-Dw 406.03 Approval Required for New Non-Community Water Systems. (a) No person shall connect a new or existing well that is intended to serve a PWS without prior approval obtained in accordance with this part. (b) Any existing water supply source for a PWS that has not previously been approved by the department shall meet the requirements specified in this part. (c) No person shall commence operation of a PWS or connect to any other PWS without obtaining approval for the final design in accordance with Env-Dw 406.10 and without completing the post- construction procedures required by Env-Dw 406.27. Source. #14158, eff 12-25-24

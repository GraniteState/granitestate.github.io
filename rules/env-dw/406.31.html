Env-Dw 406.31 Waivers

Effective 2024-12-25.

Env-Dw 406.31 Waivers. (a) Any applicant or supplier of water who would be adversely impacted by the strict application of a rule in this part and who wishes to request a waiver of the rule shall do so in accordance with Env-Dw 202. (b) Waivers shall not be granted for criteria which pose a direct risk to public health, including but not limited to, proper well construction, multiple wells where required, water quality standards, or the integrity of water storage tank(s). (c) A waiver requests for a non-community water system’s SPA shall be submitted by the owner as follows: (1) All requests for waivers shall be submitted as specified in Env-Dw 202 and shall be signed by the owner and the water system’s designer; and (2) The request shall include information on technically-important factors for a waiver of the SPA such as historical water quality, if applicable, overburdened soil depth, soil type, height and consistency of water table, direction of slope, and potential contamination sources.

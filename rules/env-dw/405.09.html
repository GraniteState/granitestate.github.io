Env-Dw 405.09 Application for Part II, Final Design

Effective 2024-12-25.

Env-Dw 405.09 Application for Part II, Final Design. (a) An applicant requiring final design of a small CWS, including reactivation or registration, shall provide the following information constituting part II of the application: (1) A corrected copy of part I of the application previously submitted, which clearly identifies any changes from the original submittal, if applicable; (2) A revised plan, if different than that submitted with part I of the application pursuant to Env-Dw 405.06(a); (3) The well driller’s well completion report for each well as required in We 800; (4) The water quality analysis results of the water from each well as specified in Env-Dw 405.15; (5) The permitted production volume of each well proposed to be connected to the small CWS, approved pursuant to Env-Dw 302, Env-Dw 305, or both as applicable; (6) The name of a designated primary operator certified in accordance with Env-Dw 502 or a person that has taken or arranged to take the operator certification exam as required under Env-Dw 502; (7) A business plan pursuant to Env-Dw 602; (8) A draft copy of the legal documents related to the creation, ownership, and management of the proposed CWS; (9) An emergency plan pursuant to Env-Dw 503; and (10) The following design information, as applicable: a. For storage tank(s), the information in Env-Dw 405.16 and Env-Dw 405.21; b. For booster pumps, pump controls, and auxiliary equipment, the information in Env-Dw 405.17 and Env-Dw 405.19; c. For a pumphouse or pump room, the information in Env-Dw 405.18; d. For well appurtenances and pumps, the information in Env-Dw 405.20; e. For wiring and controls, the information in Env-Dw 405.22; f. For water treatment facilities, the information in Env-Dw 405.23, Env-Dw 405.24, and Env-Dw 405.26; and g. For water distribution systems, the information in Env-Dw 405.27. (b) Any material, product, or equipment incorporated into a PWS shall comply with the appropriate standard(s) of AWWA, as specified in Env-Dw 402, if such a standard exists, and shall be specified in the design information accordingly. (c) The applicant shall sign and date the application to certify that the information provided is accurate. (d) The design review fee required by Env-Dw 402.03 shall be paid in conjunction with submission of part II of the application. Source. #14158, eff 12-25-24

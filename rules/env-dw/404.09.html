Env-Dw 404.09 Modifications and Expansions

Effective 2024-12-25.

Env-Dw 404.09 Modifications and Expansions. (a) Any person seeking to expand a large CWS shall submit the following to the department: (1) A water audit conducted in accordance with the standards of the AWWA; (2) The proposed increase in average day and maximum day demand due to the proposed expansion; and (3) Documentation demonstrating the existing source(s) have the required supply capacity as required by Env-Dw 404.04 and Env-Dw 404.05. (b) Any person seeking to modify or expand a large CWS in accordance with Env-Dw 404.02 above shall submit the following to the department: (1) A description of the proposed modifications or expansions; (2) An explanation of why the modifications or expansions are needed; (3) Plan sheets, including revisions to previously approved plans, and specifications for the proposed modifications or expansion as required by Env-Dw 404.04 and Env-Dw 404.05; (4) Revised calculations, as applicable; (5) If infrastructure is decommissioned, photo documentation showing a minimum 12-inch gap in the piping from the original well(s), storage tank(s), and any other decommissioned infrastructure; (6) If connection to another PWS is proposed, documentation from the supplying owner certifying that: a. Adequate quantities of water are available to serve the proposed PWS; and b. With the proposed PWS online, adequate system pressures and source capacity will be maintained in the supplying PWS; and (7) The application fee as required by Env-Dw 402.03. (c) For deepening or hydrofracturing of wells at CWSs, the applicant shall comply with Env-Dw 302 and Env-Dw 305, as applicable. (d) Replacement in-kind shall not require department approval, except that replacement of a well shall comply with the requirements of Env-Dw 302 or Env-Dw 305, as applicable. (e) The plans and specifications for modifications to large CWSs shall be sealed by a professional engineer licensed in the state of New Hampshire. (f) The department shall approve the application to modify the CWS if the design meets the criteria in this part. Source.

Env-Dw 1100 DRINKING WATER STATE REVOLVING LOAN FUND PROGRAM

CHAPTER Env-Dw 1100 DRINKING WATER STATE REVOLVING LOAN FUND PROGRAM Statutory Authority: RSA 486:14, I(a) & (c) Revision Note #1: Document #8702, effective 8-24-06, readopted with amendments and renumbered former Parts Env-C 510 through Part Env-C 521, the State Drinking Water Revolving Loan Fund Program, under a new subtitle as Chapter Env-Dw 1100, with a revised heading “Drinking Water State Revolving Loan Fund Program.” The redesignation from subtitle Env-C to subtitle Env-Dw was done pursuant to a rules reorganization plan for Department rules approved by the Director of the Office of Legislative Services on 9-7-05. Document #8702 replaces all prior filings for rules formerly in Env-C 510 through Env-C 521. The prior filings for rules in former Env-C 510 through Env-C 521 included the following documents: #6620, INTERIM, eff 11-15-97, EXPIRES: 3-15-9 #6697, eff 2-25-98 #8575, INTERIM, eff 2-25-06, EXPIRED: 8-24-06 REVISION NOTE #2: Document #13160, effective 1-20-21, readopted with amendments Chapter Env-Dw 1100, which included inserting new rules and extensively renumbering the existing rules. Document #13160 replaces all prior filings since Document #8702, which included only Document #10772, effective 1-24-15.

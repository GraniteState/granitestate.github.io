Env-Dw 1307.04 Signature Required

Effective 2019-10-23.

Env-Dw 1307.04 Signature Required. (a) The authorized representative of the applicant or recipient that is requesting the waiver shall sign the request. (b) The authorized representative’s signature shall constitute certification that: (1) The information provided is true, complete, and not misleading to the best of the signer’s knowledge and belief; (2) The signer understands that any waiver granted based on false, incomplete, or misleading information is subject to revocation; and (3) The signer is subject to the penalties specified in New Hampshire law for falsification in official matters, currently RSA 641. (c) If the signer is or is acting on behalf of a listed engineer as defined in Env-C 502.10, the signature also shall constitute certification that the signer understands that the submittal of false, incomplete, or misleading information could constitute grounds for debarring the listed engineer from the roster. Source. #12903, eff 10-23-19

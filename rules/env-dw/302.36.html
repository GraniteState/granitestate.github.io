Env-Dw 302.36 Waivers

Effective 2016-04-01.

Env-Dw 302.36 Waivers. Any applicant or supplier of water who would be adversely impacted by the strict application of a rule in this part and who wishes to request a waiver of the rule shall do so in accordance with Env-Dw 202. Source. (See Revision Notes #1 and #2 at part heading for Env-Dw 302) #11061, eff 4-1-16

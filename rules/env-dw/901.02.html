Effective 2025-06-27. Expires 2035-06-27.

Env-Dw 901.02 Applicability. The rules in this part shall apply to: (a) Any person seeking a reclassification of groundwater as allowed under RSA 485-C:9; and (b) Any local entity that has requested and obtained a groundwater reclassification for which RSA 485-C:8 requires on-going management of potential contamination sources.

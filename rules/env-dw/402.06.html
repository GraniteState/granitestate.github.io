Env-Dw 402.06 Quality of Products and Workmanship

Effective 2024-12-25.

Env-Dw 402.06 Quality of Products and Workmanship. The quality, materials, and workmanship standards of the American Water Works Association (AWWA), specified in Table 402-1 below and available as noted in Appendix B, shall apply to all public water systems relative to construction, operation, and maintenance activities regulated by Env-Dw 300 and Env-Dw 401 through Env-Dw 406.

Env-Dw 901.15 Performing an Inventory of Potential Contamination Sources

Effective 2025-06-27. Expires 2035-06-27.

Env-Dw 901.15 Performing an Inventory of Potential Contamination Sources. A local entity requesting reclassification to GAA or to GA1 shall: (a) Perform the required inventory of potential contamination sources by: (1) Reviewing human activities and operations to determine whether potential contamination sources such as those listed in RSA 485-C:7, II are located within the area proposed to be reclassified; and (2) Recording the information specified in RSA 485-C:8, II for each potential contamination source; and (b) Submit an inventory report in accordance with Env-Dw 901.16. Source. (See Revision Note at part heading for Env-Dw 901) #8747, eff 10-28-06; ss by #10749, eff 12-19-14 (from Env-Dw 901.14); ss by #14296, eff 6-27-25, EXPIRES: 6-27-35

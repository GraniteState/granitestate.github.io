Env-Dw 405.12 Calculation of Peak Flow for CWSs

Effective 2024-12-25.

Env-Dw 405.12 Calculation of Peak Flow for CWSs. Peak flow shall be calculated as design flow times the multiplier in Table 405-1 with the number of residential-equivalent units being calculated in accordance with Env-Dw 405.06(a)(9)a.: Table 405-1: Peak Flow Number of Residential Service Connections or Residential-Equivalent Units | Peaking Factor (Peak Flow / Design Flow) Less than 50 | 10 50 to 99 | 9 100 to 149 | 7 150 to 199 | 5 Greater than 200 | 4 Source. #14158, eff 12-25-24

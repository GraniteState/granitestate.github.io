Env-Dw 721.12 Bottled Water and Point-of-Use or Point-of Entry Devices

Effective 2014-06-01.

Env-Dw 721.12 Bottled Water and Point-of-Use or Point-of Entry Devices. (a) The department shall require a public water system to provide bottled water or point-of-use or point-of-entry devices or other means of response as a condition for granting an exemption from the requirements of Env-Dw 703 through Env-Dw 706. (b) The owner of a public water system that uses bottled water as a condition of obtaining an exemption shall ensure the bottled water source meets the requirements specified in Env-Dw 303. (c) The owner of a public water system that uses point-of-use or point-of-entry devices as a condition for receiving an exemption shall meet the requirements specified in Env-Dw 723. Source. #10617, eff 6-1-14

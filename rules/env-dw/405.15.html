Env-Dw 405.15 Water Supply Quality Sampling

Effective 2024-12-25.

Env-Dw 405.15 Water Supply Quality Sampling. (a) Water quality sampling for new, hydrofractured, or deepened community wells shall comply with Env-Dw 302 or Env-Dw 305, as applicable. (b) The collection date of the samples taken in accordance with (a) above, shall be no greater than 6 months prior to the time the final design application is submitted to the department. (c) Samples taken in accordance with (a) above, shall be taken after at least 3 well volumes have been pumped from the well. (d) The owner shall have the laboratory results submitted to the department as originals or copies of the originals. Re-typing or re-writing the data shall not be acceptable. Source.

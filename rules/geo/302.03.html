Geo 302.03 References Required

Effective 2017-03-24.

Geo 302.03 References Required. (a) Each applicant for licensure shall provide the board with the names and addresses of at least 5 individuals, who shall provide references, and who are not related to the applicant. At least 3 of the references shall be individuals having personal or professional knowledge of the applicant's professional geology experience. At least 3 of the references shall be licensed professional geologists as defined by RSA 310-A:118, IV, having detailed knowledge of the applicant's geology experience. (b) The board shall use as references any individuals, whose names appear in any part of the completed application. Source. #7526, eff 7-1-01; ss by #9439, eff 3-25-09; ss by #12152, eff 3-24-17

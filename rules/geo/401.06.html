Geo 401.06 Denial of Reinstatement

Effective 2017-03-24.

Geo 401.06 Denial of Reinstatement. Reinstatement shall be denied if, after notice and an opportunity for hearing, the board finds: (a) Noncompliance with the continuing education requirements of Geo 403; (b) Any unethical act for which discipline shall be imposed under Geo 500; (c) Reasons for which an initial application could be denied; or (d) Failure to furnish complete or accurate information on a license reinstatement application. Source. #9439, eff 3-25-09; ss by #12152, eff 3-24-17

Res 8408.02 Time Limit for Reimbursements

Effective 2015-06-17. Earlier expiration 2008-12-24.

Res 8408.02 Time Limit for Reimbursements. All requests for reimbursement shall be submitted within the contract completion date as specified in the approved grant-in-aid agreement.

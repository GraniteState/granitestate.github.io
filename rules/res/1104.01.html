Res 1104.01 Petition for Adding and Deleting from the Natural Heritage Inventory

Effective 2009-06-01. Expires 2017-06-01.

Res 1104.01 Petition for Adding and Deleting from the Natural Heritage Inventory. (a) All petitions for adding and deleting species from the natural heritage inventory as "endangered", "threatened" or "special concern" shall be made through the petition process as described in Res 202.01. (b) The petitioner shall provide the following information: (1) Petitioner's name and address; (2) Date of petition; (3) Scientific and common name of species; (4) Taxonomic distinctiveness/taxonomic problem; (5) Applicant's reasons for petitioning; (6) Documentation of occurrence; (7) Locality of occurrence; and (8) Signature of applicant.

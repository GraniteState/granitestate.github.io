Res 5603.04 Rule Violation

Effective 2014-03-21. Earlier expiration 2005-10-01.

Res 5603.04 Rule Violation. The local forest fire warden or the forest ranger or both shall bring to the attention of the selectmen, mayors of cities, or town and city managers any violation of these rules so that expeditious corrective action shall be effected.

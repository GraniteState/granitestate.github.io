Res 103.01 Requests for Information

Effective 2000-11-01. Expires 2018-07-01.

Res 103.01 Requests for Information. (a) Requests for information, including but not limited to, requests made pursuant to RSA 91-A:4, the Right-to-Know Law, shall be made directly to the division described in Res 102 which is responsible for the operation of the program for which information is being requested. (b) All requests for information shall be addressed directly to the division as follows: 172 Pembroke Road PO Box 1856 Concord, NH 03302-1856 (c) Telephone inquiries for information shall be addressed directly to the division as set forth in table 101.03 department telephone numbers. Table 101.03 Department Telephone Numbers Office of the Commissioner (603) 271-2411 Business Office (603) 271-3728 Design, Development and Maintenance (603) 271-2606 Division of Forests and Lands (603) 271-2215 Forest Protection Bureau (603) 271-2217 Forest Information and Planning Bureau (603) 271-3457 Land Management Bureau (603) 271-3457 Forest Management Bureau (603) 271-3456 Division of Parks and Recreation (603) 271-3556 Trails Bureau (603) 271-3254 Parks Bureau (603) 271-3627 State Ski Operations Bureau (603) 271-2006 Division of Economic Development (603) 271-2341 Office of Business and Industrial Development (603) 271-2591 Office of International Commerce (603) 334-6074 Division of Travel and Tourism Development (603) 271-2665 (d) All actual costs associated with providing requested information shall be paid by the requesting party.

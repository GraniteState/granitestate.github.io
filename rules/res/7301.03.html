Res 7301.03 Authority of DRED Personnel

Effective 2014-03-01. Earlier expiration 2005-01-23.

Res 7301.03 Authority of DRED Personnel. Persons shall obey all requests made by authorized DRED personnel in matters of public interest, public health and safety, or resources protection.

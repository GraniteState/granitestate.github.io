Res 102.02 Office of the Commissioner

Effective 2000-11-01. Expires 2018-07-01.

Res 102.02 Office of the Commissioner. (a) The office of the commissioner shall include the commissioner and 2 sections which shall be directly responsible to the commissioner. (b) The commissioner shall be ultimately responsible for organizing and directing all department policies, activities, projects and programs. Directors of divisions shall be subject to the supervisory authority of the commissioner who shall establish policy as well as to control the actual operations of the department and its divisions. Rulemaking authority for the entire department shall be vested exclusively in the commissioner. As authorized by RSA 12-A:1-d, the commissioner shall have police powers and shall confer same upon certain individuals in the department of resources and economic development. (c) The business management section shall be responsible for all financial accounting for the department, human resource management, data management, information technology management as well as management of the department's warehouse and supply depot. (d) The design, development and maintenance section shall be responsible for all the engineering and construction needs of the department. The section shall also administer all contracts necessary to adequately maintain and construct department facilities.

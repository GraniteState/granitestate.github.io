Env-Hw 1407.05 Delivery

Effective 2025-08-01. Expires 2035-08-01.

Env-Hw 1407.05 Delivery. (a) A used oil transporter shall deliver all used oil received to the following entities provided the entity has obtained an EPA identification number: (1) Another used oil transporter, subject to (b), below; (2) A used oil processor/re-refiner; or (3) A used oil burner. (b) For the purposes of (a)(1), above, if the transfer occurs within New Hampshire: (1) The used oil transporter receiving the used oil shall also possess a current and valid New Hampshire hazardous waste transporter registration; and (2) The transfer shall occur at either a used oil transfer facility or a permitted hazardous waste transfer facility. (c) If a shipment of used oil subject to regulation under this chapter is accompanied by a manifest, the NH01 hazardous waste number shall not be recorded on the manifest. Source. #14282, eff 8-1-25, EXPIRES: 8-1-35

Env-Hw 514.05 Interest on Unpaid Fees

Effective 2025-08-01. Expires 2035-08-01.

Env-Hw 514.05 Interest on Unpaid Fees. (a) As specified in RSA 147-A:5, IV(c), if the self-certification fee is not paid within 60 days of the due date, interest shall be assessed. (b) The interest rate shall be the rate established by RSA 336:1, II. Source. #10205, eff 10-19-12; ss by #12347, eff 8-14-17; ss by #14280, eff 8-1-25, EXPIRES: 8-1-35 (formerly Env-Hw 514.07)

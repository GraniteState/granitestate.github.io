Env-Hw 808.05 Speculative Accumulation of Recyclable Materials Used for Precious Metal Recovery

Effective 2026-02-28. Expires 2036-02-28.

Env-Hw 808.05 Speculative Accumulation of Recyclable Materials Used for Precious Metal Recovery. Recyclable materials used for precious metal recovery that are accumulated speculatively, as determined under Env-Hw 807, shall be regulated as hazardous wastes under Env-Hw 100-700 and not regulated as recyclable materials under this chapter.

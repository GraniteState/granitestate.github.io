Env-Hw 1408.09 Recordkeeping and Reporting

Effective 2025-08-01. Expires 2035-08-01.

Env-Hw 1408.09 Recordkeeping and Reporting. (a) A used oil processor/re-refiner shall keep the following records for at least 3 years from the date of acceptance or shipment, as applicable: (1) A record of acceptance for each used oil shipment accepted for processing/re-refining, in the form of a log, invoice, manifest, bill of lading, or other shipping document, that includes: a. The name, address, and EPA identification number of the transporter that delivered the used oil to the processor/re-refiner; b. The name, address, and if applicable, EPA identification number of the generator or processor/re-refiner from which the used oil was sent; c. The quantity of used oil accepted; and d. The date of acceptance; (2) A record of each shipment of used oil that is shipped to a used oil burner or processor/re-refiner, in the form of a log, invoice, manifest, bill of lading, or other shipping document, that includes: a. The name, address, and EPA identification number of the transporter that will deliver the used oil to the burner or processor/re-refiner; b. The name, address, and EPA identification number of the burner or processor/re-refiner that will receive the used oil; c. The quantity of used oil shipped; and d. The date of shipment; and (3) Pursuant to Env-Hw 512.01(a)(1), all manifest copies, including the original generator copy and the copy certified by the designated facility or the foreign receiving facility, for shipments of used oil to a disposal facility. (b) The owner or operator shall record, as it becomes available, and maintain the following information in a written operating record until closure of the facility: (1) Information gathered and records and results of used oil analyses performed as described in the analysis plan required under Env-Hw 1408.08; and (2) Summary reports and details of all incidents that require implementation of the contingency plan as specified in Env-Hw 1408.05 and Env-Hw 1408.06. (c) A used oil processor/re-refiner shall complete and submit, by March 1 of each year, Form HWM-12 “Annual Used Oil Processor/Re-refiner Report,” as amended August 2025, that includes information concerning used oil activities during the previous calendar year. (d) The 3-year record retention period in (a), above, shall be extended automatically while any enforcement action is pending. Source. #14282, eff 8-1-25, EXPIRES: 8-1-35

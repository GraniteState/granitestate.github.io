Env-Hw 904.15 Fines Relating to Hazardous Waste Pharmaceuticals

Effective 2025-08-01. Expires 2035-08-01.

Env-Hw 904.15 Fines Relating to Hazardous Waste Pharmaceuticals. (a) If a healthcare facility fails to notify the department that it is operating under Env-Hw 1300 in violation of Env-Hw 1302.02(c)(1), the proposed fine shall be $1,000. (b) If a healthcare facility fails to notify the department that it is withdrawing from operating under Env‑Hw 1300 in violation of Env-Hw 1302.02(c)(1), the proposed fine shall be $250. Source. #13784, eff 10-21-23; ss by #14283, eff 8-1-25, EXPIRES: 8-1-35 (formerly Env-Hw 904.19)

Env-Hw 103.47 EPA identification number

Effective 2025-08-01.

Env-Hw 103.47 “EPA identification number” means the site-specific number assigned by the department upon receipt of a complete Form HWM-2, “RCRA C Site Identification Form” dated August 2025, submitted by a: (a) Generator; (b) Recognized trader; (c) Transporter; (d) Transfer facility; (e) Treatment, storage, or disposal facility; (f) Universal waste handler; (g) Healthcare facility operating under Env-Hw 1300; (h) Reverse distributor; or (i) Used oil handler. Source. (See Revision Note #1 and Revision Note #2 at chapter heading for Env-Hw 100) #12343, eff 8-14-17; (formerly Env-Hw 103.45, renumbered by #12920) (See Revision Note #3 at chapter heading for Env-Hw 100); ss by #14278, eff 8-1-25

Env-Hw 1004.02 Eligible Applicants and Purposes of Funding

Effective 2025-08-01. Expires 2035-08-01.

Env-Hw 1004.02 Eligible Applicants and Purposes of Funding. (a) A political subdivision or other government entity that is in compliance with the hazardous waste rules shall be eligible to apply for a grant from the HWCF for costs associated with: (1) The establishment or improvement of household DIY used oil collection centers; and (2) The offsite transportation and recycling of household DIY used oil. (b) A non-profit organization that focuses on waste management and recycling issues, or a private business that is a registered state motor vehicle inspection station, shall be eligible to apply for a grant from the HWCF for costs to establish a household DIY used oil collection center, provided the organization or business is in compliance with the hazardous waste rules. (c) Grant awards shall not exceed $2,500 on an annual basis. (d) Political subdivisions that use the same household DIY used oil collection center may combine grant awards, however in no case shall the grant awarded to a household DIY used oil collection center exceed $5,000. Source. #14284, eff 8-1-25, EXPIRES: 8-1-35

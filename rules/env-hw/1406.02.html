Env-Hw 1406.02 Requirements for Used Oil Generators, Household DIY Used Oil Collection Centers, Commercial Used Oil Collection Centers, and Used Oil Aggregation Points

Effective 2025-08-01. Expires 2035-08-01.

Env-Hw 1406.02 Requirements for Used Oil Generators, Household DIY Used Oil Collection Centers, Commercial Used Oil Collection Centers, and Used Oil Aggregation Points. Used oil generators, household DIY used oil collection centers, commercial used oil collection centers, and used oil aggregation points shall comply with the requirements of Env-Hw 1405 and, as applicable: (a) Env-Hw 1406.03 through Env-Hw 1406.07; (b) Env-Hw 1407 for transporting used oil and operating a used oil transfer facility, except as provided under Env-Hw 1406.05(c) and (d); (c) Env-Hw 1408 for processing or re-refining used oil, except as provided under Env-Hw 1406.03; (d) Env-Hw 1409 for burning used oil for energy recovery, except as provided under Env-Hw 1406.04; and (e) Env-Hw 1410 for acting as a used oil fuel marketer. Source. #14282, eff 8-1-25, EXPIRES: 8-1-35

Env-Hw 904.08 Fines Relating to Hazardous Waste Transporter Requirements

Effective 2025-08-01. Expires 2035-08-01.

Env-Hw 904.08 Fines Relating to Hazardous Waste Transporter Requirements. (a) For transporting hazardous waste into or within the state of New Hampshire without having a transporter registration issued by the department in violation of Env-Hw 602.01, the proposed fine shall be $2,000 per load transported. (b) For failing to notify the department prior to conducting hazardous waste activities in violation of Env-Hw 602.02(a), the proposed fine shall be $2,000. (c) For transporting hazardous waste without having received an EPA identification number in violation of Env-Hw 602.02(d), the proposed fine shall be $1,000 per load transported. (d) For failing to transport hazardous waste in accordance with the manifest requirements of 40 CFR 263.20(a) through (g) in violation of Env-Hw 604.01(a), the proposed fine shall be $2,000 per load transported. (e) For failing to verify the generator EPA identification number in violation of Env-Hw 604.01(b), the proposed fine shall be $250 per manifest used without a valid generator EPA identification number. (f) For failing to immediately report a discharge of hazardous waste or material in violation of Env-Hw 608.01(a), the proposed fine shall be $2,000 per discharge. Source. #13784, eff 10-21-23; ss by #14283, eff 8-1-25, EXPIRES: 8-1-35 (formerly Env-Hw 904.12)

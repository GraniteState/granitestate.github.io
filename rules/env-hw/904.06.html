Env-Hw 904.06 Fines Relating to Manifest and Delivery Requirements for Generators

Effective 2025-08-01. Expires 2035-08-01.

Env-Hw 904.06 Fines Relating to Manifest and Delivery Requirements for Generators. (a) For transporting, offering hazardous waste for transport, or otherwise causing hazardous waste to be sent to a facility that is not a designated facility or not otherwise authorized to receive the generator’s hazardous waste in violation of Env-Hw 501.01(d), the proposed fine shall be $2,000 per load delivered to an unauthorized facility. (b) For failing to use a manifest for shipments of hazardous waste in violation of Env-Hw 510.01(a), the proposed fine shall be $2,000 per shipment. (c) For failing to forward one legible copy of the manifest, with signatures, to the department within 5 days of shipment in violation of Env-Hw 510.02(a)(5)b. and Env-Hw 510.02(b)(5)b., the proposed fine shall be $100 per manifest. (d) For failing to properly complete a manifest for shipments of hazardous waste in violation of Env‑Hw 510.03, the proposed fine shall be $500 per shipment. (e) For a small or large quantity generator delivering or offering hazardous waste to a facility that has not obtained an EPA identification number in violation of Env-Hw 511.01(b)(1), the proposed fine shall be $2,000 per load delivered or offered. (f) For a small or large quantity generator delivering or offering hazardous waste to a transporter that does not possess an EPA identification number or a current and valid New Hampshire hazardous waste transporter registration in violation of Env‑Hw 511.01(b)(2), the proposed fine shall be $2,000 per load delivered or offered. (g) For a very small or small quantity generator failing to submit the required documentation when the generator has not received a copy of the signed manifest within 60 days of shipment in violation of Env-Hw 511.01(c) or (e), the proposed fine shall be $100 per submittal not made. (h) For a large quantity generator failing to submit an exception report when the generator has not received a copy of the signed manifest within 60 days of shipment in violation of Env-Hw 511.01(g) or (h), the proposed fine shall be $100 per report not submitted. Source. #13784, eff 10-21-23; ss by #14283, eff 8-1-25, EXPIRES: 8-1-35 (formerly Env-Hw 904.09)

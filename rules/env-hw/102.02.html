Env-Hw 102.02 Modifications to Federal Hazardous Waste Definitions

Effective 2025-08-01.

Env-Hw 102.02 Modifications to Federal Hazardous Waste Definitions. The following terms, when used in federal regulations incorporated by reference, shall be substituted with the analogous New Hampshire terms as follows: (a) Any reference to the “Regional Administrator,” “Administrator,” “EPA Regional Administrator for the Region in which the healthcare facility is located,” or “EPA Regional Administrator for the Region in which the reverse distributor is located” in federal rules adopted by reference shall be deemed to mean the “commissioner, or commissioner’s designee,” except when used in 40 CFR 262 Subpart H, 264.12(a), 265.12(a), 268.5, 268.6, 268.13, 268.40(b), 268.42(b), 268.44(a) through (g), 270.11(a)(3), 270.14(b)(20), 270.32(b)(2), and 270.51; (b) Any reference to the “U.S. Environmental Protection Agency,” “Agency,” “United States Environmental Protection Agency,” “EPA Headquarters,” “EPA Region(s),” or “EPA” in federal rules adopted by reference shall be deemed to mean the “department,” except when used in: (1) 40 CFR 260.2(c) and (d), 261.39(a)(5), 261.41, 262 Subpart H, 264.12(a)(2), 265.12(a)(2), 268.1(e)(3), 268.2(j), 268.5(g), 268.44(a) through (g), 270.11(a)(3), 270.32(a), 270.32(c), 270.51, 270.72(a)(5), and 270.72(b)(5); or (2) Any reference to EPA identification numbers, EPA hazardous waste numbers, EPA test methods, EPA forms, any EPA acknowledgment of consent, any EPA publications, manuals, or guidance, and EPA’s electronic manifest system; and (c) Any reference to any provision of the Resource Conservation and Recovery Act (RCRA) in federal rules incorporated by reference shall be deemed to be a reference to the comparable New Hampshire statutory provision, except in 40 CFR 270.72(a)(5) and (b)(5) and 40 CFR 270.51(d) relative to EPA-issued RCRA permits, as listed in Table 1.1, below: Table 1.1 Comparison of Federal and State Statutory Provisions RCRA (P.L. 94-580) | U.S.C. | NH RSA 1004(5) | 42 U.S.C. 6903(5) | 147-A:2, VII 1004(27) | 42 U.S.C. 6903(27) | 147-A:2, XVIII 3004 | 42 U.S.C. 6924 | 147-A:3, IV 3005 | 42 U.S.C. 6925 | 147-A:3, IX;

Env-Hw 602.04 Notification Determination

Effective 2017-08-14.

Env-Hw 602.04 Notification Determination. (a) If the department, upon examination of a notification submitted as specified in Env-Hw 602.03, determines that the transporter has not provided all of the information required by Env-Hw 602.03, the department shall notify the transporter in writing of each deficiency. (b) The transporter shall, no later than 30 days from receipt of the notice of deficiency, explain or correct the alleged deficiency. (c) If the transporter does not respond within 30 days, the transporter shall submit a new notification prior to engaging in any regulated hazardous waste activities. (d) If a transporter's notification is deemed complete, the department shall assign an EPA identification number to the transporter.

Env-Hw 304.13 Pre-Submittal Conference

Effective 2017-08-14.

Env-Hw 304.13 Pre-Submittal Conference. Upon the applicant’s request, the department shall afford the applicant an opportunity to meet with the department before submitting an application in order to review the department’s permit application requirements, including, if applicable, the siting requirements of Env-Hw 304.09. Source. (See Revision Note at chapter heading for Env-Hw 300) #9362, eff 1-28-09; ss by #12345, eff 8-14-17 (formerly Env-Hw 304.14)

Env-Hw 904.04 Fines Relating to Generator Accumulation of Hazardous Waste

Effective 2025-08-01. Expires 2035-08-01.

Env-Hw 904.04 Fines Relating to Generator Accumulation of Hazardous Waste. (a) For failing to position each container of hazardous waste so that its hazardous waste label or marking is not hidden by walls or other containers in violation of Env-Hw 507.02(a), the proposed fine shall be $100 per container. (b) For storing hazardous waste containers on surfaces that are not impervious in violation of Env‑Hw 507.02(b), the proposed fine shall be between $250 and $2,000, based upon the volume of hazardous waste stored in each non‑conforming storage area. (c) For storing hazardous waste containers in an area with a functional floor drain or manhole or in or near a sink with a functional drain present without secondary containment capable of containing the volume of the largest capacity hazardous waste container present in violation of Env-Hw 507.02(c), the proposed fine shall be between $250 and $2,000, based upon the volume of hazardous waste stored in each non-conforming storage area. (d) For storing hazardous waste containers outdoors without being covered to prevent precipitation from coming in contact with the top of the containers in violation of Env-Hw 507.02(e)(1), the proposed fine shall be between $250 and $2,000, based upon the volume of hazardous waste stored in each non-conforming storage area. (e) For failing to provide adequate secondary containment for hazardous waste containers stored outdoors in violation of Env-Hw 507.02(e)(2)a., the proposed fine shall be $1,000 per storage area. (f) For failing to keep secondary containment covered for hazardous waste containers stored outdoors in violation of Env-Hw 507.02(e)(2)b., the proposed fine shall be $500 per secondary containment structure. (g) For outdoor storage of hazardous waste containers within any of the distances in Env-Hw 507.02(e)(3), the proposed fine shall be between $250 and $2,000 per container based on the volume of each container.

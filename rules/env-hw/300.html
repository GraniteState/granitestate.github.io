Env-Hw 300 PERMITS

CHAPTER Env-Hw 300 PERMITS Statutory Authority: RSA 147-A:3; RSA 541-A:16, I(b) REVISION NOTE: Document #9362, effective 1-28-09, readopted with amendments and renumbered the permit rules on hazardous waste management, formerly in Part Env-Wm 301 and Part Env-Wm 351 through Part Env-Wm 353 (Part Env-Wm 352 reserved), under a new subtitle as Env-Hw 300. The redesignation from subtitle Env-Wm to Env-Hw was done pursuant to a rules reorganization plan for Department rules approved by the Director of the Office of Legislative Services on 9-7-05. Document #9362 replaced all prior filings for hazardous waste rules formerly in Chapter Env-Wm 300. The prior filings for the former rules in Env-Wm 300 on hazardous waste included the following documents: #5053, eff 1-24-91 #5886, eff 8-26-94 #6619-B, eff 10-29-97 #7207-B, eff 2-26-00 #7333, eff 8-1-00 #7578, eff 10-13-01 #8462-A, eff 10-28-05 #9215, INTERIM, eff 8-1-08

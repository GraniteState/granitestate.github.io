Env-Hw 104.78 Universal waste

Effective 2022-07-23.

Env-Hw 104.78 “Universal waste” means any of the following hazardous wastes that may be managed in accordance with Env-Hw 1100 in lieu of Env-Hw 300 through Env-Hw 700: (a) Batteries; (b) Pesticides; (c) Mercury-containing devices; (d) Lamps; (e) Cathode ray tubes; (f) Antifreeze; and (g) Aerosol cans. Source.

Env-Hw 1407.02 Requirements for Used Oil Transporters

Effective 2025-08-01. Expires 2035-08-01.

Env-Hw 1407.02 Requirements for Used Oil Transporters. Used oil transporters shall comply with the requirements of Env-Hw 1405 and, as applicable: (a) Env-Hw 1407.03 through Env-Hw 1407.09; (b) Env-Hw 602, Env-Hw 609, and Env-Hw 610 for hazardous waste transporter registration and notification; (c) Env-Hw 603.02, Env-Hw 603.03, Env-Hw 603.05, Env-Hw 603.07, and Env-Hw 603.08 for vehicle identification, security, and safety; (d) US DOT regulations in 49 CFR parts 171 through 180; (e) Rules adopted by the New Hampshire department of safety; (f) Env-Hw 1406 for generating used oil; (g) Env-Hw 1408 for processing or re-refining used oil, except as provided under Env-Hw 1407.03; (h) Env-Hw 1409 for burning used oil for energy recovery; and (i) Env-Hw 1410 for acting as a used oil fuel marketer. Source. #14282, eff 8-1-25, EXPIRES: 8-1-35

Puc 203.17 Orders Nisi

Effective 2025-01-24.

Puc 203.17 Orders Nisi. (a) In a docketed matter, the commission shall elect to issue a final order with a delayed effective date referred to as an order nisi. (b) If permitted under New Hampshire law. (c) The commission determines that the record is sufficient to support a commission decision without further process. (d) Any order nisi shall include a notice and publication requirement to allow interested parties an opportunity to object or request a further hearing. (e) An interested party that fails to timely intervene or participate in a proceeding shall demonstrate good cause for late-intervention, as provided by RSA 541-A:32, II. Source. #14184, eff 1-24-25

Puc 203.16 Public Comment

Effective 2025-01-24. Earlier expiration 2006-02-19.

Puc 203.16 Public Comment. The commission shall provide persons who are not a party or a participant in a proceeding, but having interest in the subject matter, with an opportunity to state their position.

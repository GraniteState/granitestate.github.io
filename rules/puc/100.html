Puc 100 ORGANIZATIONAL RULES

CHAPTER Puc 100 ORGANIZATIONAL RULES REVISION NOTE 1: Document #6470, effective 3-18-97, made changes to the wording, format, and numbering of rules in Chapter Puc 100. Former Part Puc 101, Definitions, was amended and renumbered as Part Puc 102. Former Part Puc 102, Description and Contact Information for the Commission, was amended and renumbered as Part Puc 103. Part Puc 101, and Part Puc 104 are new rules adopted in Document #6470. Document #6470 supersedes all prior filings for the sections in this chapter. The prior filings for former Chapter Puc 100 include the following documents: #2011, eff 5-4-82 #2173, eff 11-11-82 #2487, eff 7-27-84 #2912, eff 11-26-84 #4997, eff 11-26-90 #6365, INTERIM, eff 11-18-96 REVISION NOTE 2: Document #14185, effective 1-24-25, made extensive changes to chapter Puc 100 by adopting, readopting with amendment, and repealing various sections of the chapter, as well as reorganizing the structure of the chapter. Document #14185 repealed Puc 101.01, “Applicability”, Puc 102.01 the definition of “adjudicative proceeding”, Puc 102.03 through Puc 102.22 including the definitions of “complaint”, “contested case”, “hearing”, “motion”, “order”, “party”, “petition”, prehearing conference”, prehearing order”, “presiding officer”, “proceeding”, “public statement hearing”, “respondent”, “routine filings”, “rulemaking”, “staff”, and “technical session”. Puc 103.01, “Commission Functions”, was also repealed. The prior filings for former Puc 100 include the documents above in Revision Note 1 and the following documents: #6470, eff 3-18-97 #8656, eff 6-10-06 #9897, INTERIM, eff 3-26-11 #10101, eff 3-24-12 As procedural rules the rules in Document #14185 will not expire except pursuant to RSA 541-A:17, II.

Puc 1300 UTILITY POLE ATTACHMENTS

CHAPTER Puc 1300 UTILITY POLE ATTACHMENTS Statutory Authority: RSA 374:34-a REVISION NOTE: Document #13498, effective 12-1-22, readopted with amendments Chapter Puc 1300 titled “Utility Pole Attachments.” Pursuant to 2021, 91:187, effective 7-1-21, the Department of Energy was established by the adoption of RSA 12-P. Pursuant to RSA 12-P:11, “All of the functions, powers, duties, records, personnel, and property of the public utilities commission incorporated in the statutes establishing the department of energy and which replace the authority of the commission with the authority of the department of energy, are hereby transferred, as of July 1, 2021, to the department of energy.” Pursuant to RSA 12-P:14, “Existing rules, orders, and approvals of the public utilities commission which are associated with any functions, powers, and duties, transferred to the department of energy pursuant to RSA 12-P:11 or any other statutory provision, shall continue in effect and be enforced by the commissioner of the department of energy until they expire or are repealed or amended in accordance with applicable law.” The amendments in Document #13498 were made pursuant to RSA 374:34-a on pole attachments, as amended by 2021, 91:271, effective 7-1-21, to the extent of the provisions which are under the authority of the Public Utilities Commission. The amendments in Document #13498 included, but were not limited to, the deletion of Puc 1302.02 defining “boxing”; the adoption of Puc 1302.03 defining “Department” as the Department of Energy; the deletion of Puc 1302.05 defining “extension arm(s)”; the deletion of Puc 1302.08 defining “make-ready work”; the deletion of Puc 1302.09 defining “overlash”; the renumbering of other rules within Part Puc 1302 titled “Definitions”; the deletion of Part Puc 1303 titled “Access to Poles”; and the amendment and renumbering of Part Puc 1304 titled “Dispute Resolution” as Part Puc 1303. The source notes of the rules indicate the former rule numbers where applicable. Document #13498 replaced all prior filings affecting the former Chapter Puc 1300. The prior filings affecting the deleted rules in the former Chapter Puc 1300 included the following documents: #9703, INTERIM, effective 1-17-08 (rules Puc 1302.02 and Puc 1303.01-Puc 1303.04) #9614, effective 12-12-09 (all deleted rules) #12574, effective 9-1-18 (all deleted rules) The Department of Energy adopted Chapter En 1300 titled “Utility Pole Attachments” in Document #13497, effective 12-1-22, which included Part En 1303 titled “Access to Poles” based on the former Part Puc 1303.

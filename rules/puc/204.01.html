Puc 204.01 Applicability

Effective 2025-01-24.

Puc 204.01 Applicability. Part 204 applies to: (a) Proceedings conducted pursuant to RSA 365 other than rulemakings; (b) Proceedings conducted pursuant to RSA 369; (c) Proceedings to acquire property or rights pursuant to RSA 371; (d) Proceedings conducted pursuant to RSA 374:3-a, RSA 374:7, RSA 374:22 -:36, RSA 374:34-a; RSA 374:7, RSA 374:37-40, RSA 374:41-47, and RSA 374:47-a. (e) Proceedings related to the implementation or enforcement of RSA 374-F, and RSA 374-G; (f) Rate proceedings conducted pursuant to RSA 378 when the commission exercises its rights to suspend a proposed rate schedule pursuant to RSA 378:6; (g) Commission reviews of integrated distribution plans pursuant to RSA 378:40; (h) Any other proceeding in which a person is entitled to notice and hearing; (i) Any other docket when the commission determines it would be just and reasonable and in the public interest to conduct the case as an adjudicative proceeding per RSA 541-A; (j) Proceedings conducted pursuant to RSA 362-A; (k) Proceedings conducted pursuant to RSA 362-I:2; (l) Proceedings upon the commission’s own motion pursuant to RSA 366:7; and (m) Proceedings initiated by the department related to affiliate contracts pursuant to RSA 366:9. Source. #14184, eff 1-24-25

Puc 302.08 Utility

Effective 2024-10-25. Expires 2034-10-25.

Puc 302.08 “Utility” means any public utility as defined in RSA 362:2 owning, operating, or managing any plant or equipment or any part of the same for the transmission, distribution, or generation of electricity ultimately sold to the public within New Hampshire except: (a) Municipal corporations, to the extent exempt pursuant to RSA 362:4-a; (b) Entities specifically exempted from the definition of “public utility” pursuant to RSA 362:4-c; and (c) Competitive energy suppliers that have not requested public utility status pursuant to RSA 374-F:7, I. Source. #10603, eff 5-21-14 (from Puc 302.22); ss by #14106, eff 10-25-24, EXPIRES: 10-25-34 (formerly Puc 302.23) (see Revision Note at chapter heading for Puc 300)

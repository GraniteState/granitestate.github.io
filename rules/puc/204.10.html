Puc 204.10 Settlement

Effective 2025-01-24.

Puc 204.10 Settlement. (a) Parties to the docket that participate in settlement discussions shall treat such discussions as confidential and shall not disclose the contents of such discussions. (b) The commission shall approve a disposition of any contested case by settlement agreement, if it determines the settlement agreement is consistent with applicable law, is just and reasonable, and serves the public interest. (c) A proposed settlement agreement shall be filed no less than 15 business days prior to the hearing unless otherwise ordered, except as provided in (d). If a settlement agreement is filed late, the commission shall determine whether to reschedule the hearing based on the determination in (d). (d) The commission shall accept a late-filed settlement agreement when such acceptance: (1) Promotes the orderly and efficient conduct of the proceeding; and (2) Will not impair the rights of any party to the proceeding. (e) In addition to, or in lieu of, filing a proposed settlement agreement, parties may file proposed findings of facts and conclusions of law for the commission's consideration. Source. #14184, eff 1-24-25 (formerly Puc 203.20)

Puc 2106.03 Relation to Antitrust Laws

Effective 2026-04-15. Expires 2036-04-15.

Puc 2106.03 Relation to Antitrust Laws. (a) Nothing in this chapter shall be construed to confer immunity from state and federal antitrust laws. (b) A penalty assessed pursuant to Puc 2106.02 shall not affect or preempt antitrust liability but rather is in addition to any antitrust liability that might apply to the activity.

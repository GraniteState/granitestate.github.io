Puc 2508.04 Audit

Effective 2018-02-01.

Puc 2508.04 Audit. A recipient of any monies disbursed from the renewable energy fund shall make its books, records, and facilities available to the commission for the purpose of allowing the commission to discharge its audit responsibilities pursuant to RSA 362-F:10, I.

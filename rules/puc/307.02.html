Puc 307.02 Obligations

Effective 2024-10-25. Expires 2034-10-25.

Puc 307.02 Obligations. When a utility is notified by ISO-NE that it must assume the load obligation of a CEPS, customer authorization to transfer service pursuant to Puc 307.01 shall not be required.

Puc 606.04 E-25 Water - Report of Proposed Changes in Depreciation Rates

Effective 2026-01-02. Expires 2036-01-02.

Puc 606.04 E-25 Water - Report of Proposed Changes in Depreciation Rates. (a) Each utility shall file with the commission and the department “Form E-25 Water Report of Proposed Changes in Depreciation Rates” when it proposes any change in depreciation rates. (b) A utility shall include on form E-25: (1) A caption identifying the report as “E-25 Water Report of Proposed Changes in Depreciation Rates” along with the name of the company; (2) The date; (3) The account number and title; (4) The estimated life, both present and proposed, in years; (5) The net salvage, both present and proposed, by percentage; (6) The depreciation rate, both present and proposed, by percentage; (7) The net annual change in dollars; (8) The reasons for the proposed changes; and (9) The signature, full name, and title of the utility employee who supervised the preparation of the report. (c) A utility shall not implement any change in depreciation rates until the proposed change has been approved by the commission in an adjudicatory proceeding pursuant to Puc 200. Source. #13887, eff 2-22-24; ss by #14460, eff 1-2-26, EXPIRES 1-2-36

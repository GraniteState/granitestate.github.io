Puc 2002.08 Principals

Effective 2026-05-13. Expires 2036-05-13.

Puc 2002.08 “Principals” means, for a corporation, any of its officers, directors, or controlling shareholders, for a limited liability company, any of its managers or controlling members, for a partnership, any of its general partners, and for any other business entity, any of its personnel exercising executive functions and any of its controlling equity owners. Source. #12372, eff 11-1-17; ss by #14576, eff 5-13-26, EXPIRES: 5-13-36 (formerly Puc 2002.19)

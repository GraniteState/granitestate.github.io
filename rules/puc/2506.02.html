Puc 2506.02 Metering of Customer-Sited Sources

Effective 2018-02-01.

Puc 2506.02 Metering of Customer-Sited Sources. (a) A customer-sited source shall use a revenue quality meter or other meter tested and certified by the manufacturer to meet ANSI C-12 standards to measure the electricity generated. (b) A customer-sited source shall certify to the independent monitor that the meter operates to the manufacturer’s standards. (c) A customer-sited source shall maintain the meter according to the manufacturer’s recommendations. Source. #9169, eff 6-3-08; ss by #10741, eff 12-5-14 (from Puc 2505.10); ss by #12473, eff 2-1-18

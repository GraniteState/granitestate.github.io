Puc 607.04 Eligibility

Effective 2024-02-22.

Puc 607.04 Eligibility. A small water system shall not be eligible for rate relief pursuant to this section within 2 years after the issuance of a final order under Puc 607 or Puc 1600. Source. (See Revision Note # 1 and Revision Note #2 at chapter heading for Puc 600) #13887, eff 2-22-24

Puc 2205.12 Complaints and Dispute Resolution

Effective 2022-10-12.

Puc 2205.12 Complaints and Dispute Resolution. (a) The commission shall hear and decide complaints or disputes between committees, CPAs, and utilities, as well as complaints regarding a CPA’s or utility’s compliance with the requirements of RSA 53-E and these rules. (b) Complaints submitted to the commission under these rules shall be made pursuant to Puc 204, and, consistent with RSA 53-E:7, X, shall not be subject to RSA 541-A:29 or RSA 541-A:29-a.

Puc 1607.01 Effective Date of Special Contract

Effective 2024-07-25.

Puc 1607.01 Effective Date of Special Contract. A utility shall not provide service under a special contract until the commission has issued written approval of the terms of the proposed special contract pursuant to this part or by operation of RSA 378:18.

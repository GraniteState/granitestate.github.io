Puc 2605.03 Rebate to Electric Ratepayers

Effective 2009-12-12. Expires 2017-12-12.

Puc 2605.03 Rebate to Electric Ratepayers. The commission shall order and provide for the rebate of any monies eligible for rebate under this part in a timely manner to be set forth by commission order.

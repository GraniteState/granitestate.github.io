Env-Wq 1503.10 Signatures and Certifications Required

Effective 2026-08-06. Expires 2036-08-06.

Env-Wq 1503.10 Signatures and Certifications Required. (a) Each document that is required by this chapter to be submitted to the department, including but not limited to applications, requests, and reports, shall: (1) Be signed and dated by: a. For any document submitted prior to a permit being issued, the applicant or agent; and b. For any document submitted after a permit has been issued, each permit holder or agent; and (2) Show the typed or printed name and title, if applicable, of the individual who signed. (b) Each signature required by (a), above, shall constitute certification by the signer that: (1) The information contained in or otherwise submitted with the document is true, complete, and not misleading to the best of the signer’s knowledge and belief; (2) The signer understands that the submission of false, incomplete, or misleading information constitutes grounds for the department to: a. Deny the application; b. Revoke any permit that is granted based on the information; c. If the signer is a professional engineer, file a complaint with the office of professional licensure and certification pursuant to RSA 310:9; or d. Undertake any combination of a. through c., above; and (3) The signer understands that the documents being submitted are considered public records under New Hampshire’s Right-to-Know Law, RSA 91-A, and shall be made available for public inspection and copying.

Env-Wq 304 CERTIFICATION OF WASTEWATER TREATMENT PLANT OPERATORS

PART Env-Wq 304 CERTIFICATION OF WASTEWATER TREATMENT PLANT OPERATORS Statutory Authority: RSA 485-A:5-a; RSA 485-A:6, XI and XI-b; RSA 485-A:7-a; RSA 485-A:7-b; RSA 485-A:7-c; RSA 485-A:7-d; RSA 486:9; RSA 486:10, III Revision Note #1: Document #10392, effective 8-1-13, readopted with amendments and renumbered former Part Env-Ws 901 titled “Certification of Wastewater Treatment Plant Operators” under a new subtitle as Part Env-Wq 304. The redesignation from subtitle Env-Ws to subtitle Env-Wq was done pursuant to a rules reorganization plan for Department rules approved by the Director of the Office of Legislative Services on 9-7-05. Document #10392 replaced all prior filings for rules formerly in Env-Ws 901. The prior filings for rules in the former Env-Ws 901 included the following documents: #1774, eff 7-15-81 #2352, eff 4-26-83 #2851, eff 9-21-84, EXPIRED: 9-21-90 #6326, INTERIM, eff 9-3-96, EXPIRED: 1-1-97 #6502, eff 4-25-97 #8327, eff 4-23-05 REVISION NOTE #2: Document #13755, effective 9-27-23, readopted with amendment Part Env-Wq 304 titled “Certification of Wastewater Treatment Plant Operators.” Document #13755 was the first filing since Document #10392 that affected Part Env-Wq 304 and replaces all prior filings for rules in the former Part Env-Wq 304. The rules in Document #10392 did not expire on 8-1-23 but were extended pursuant to RSA 541-A:14-a until replaced by the rules in Document #13755, effective 9-27-23. The amendments in Document #13755 made extensive changes to the former Part Env-Wq 304, including the readoption with amendment and renumbering of Env-Wq 304.06 titled “Operator in Responsible Charge - Multiple Plants” as Env-Wq 304.26 titled “Request for Multi-Plant Certified Operator Endorsement” Document #13755 also deleted Env-Wq 304.11 titled “Information Required for Application” and inserted a new rule Env-Wq 304.25 titled “Wastewater Treatment Plant Operator Code of Ethics. These changes necessitated the renumbering by Document #13755 of existing rules in Part Env-Wq 304 as indicated in the source notes.

Env-Wq 502.43 Priority system

Effective 2022-12-20.

Env-Wq 502.43 “Priority system” means the criteria for ranking projects on the priority list. Source. (See Revision Notes #1-#3 at chapter heading for Env-Wq 500) #10984, eff 12-1-15; ss by #13511, eff 12-20-22 (formerly Env-Wq 502.41)

Env-Wq 907.03 Required Health Equipment: Day Camps

Effective 2017-09-01.

Env-Wq 907.03 Required Health Equipment: Day Camps. (a) A day camp shall have either: (1) A first aid cabinet as specified in Env-Wq 907.06(a)(1); or (2) At least one first aid kit containing such items as health staff deem necessary to address health issues likely to arise at the YRC. (b) Excluding epinephrine auto-injectors or asthma inhalers possessed pursuant to RSA 485-A:25-b through RSA 485:25-g, all medications or prescription drugs shall be kept in a container that is: (1) Inaccessible to campers and unauthorized YRC staff; (2) Stored in a secondary container separate from food if in a refrigerator; and (3) Labeled with the camper’s name to ensure identification of the medication. (d) All medications belonging to YRC staff shall be stored separately from the campers’ medications in a container or area that is inaccessible to unauthorized individuals. (e) The YRC shall comply with Env-Sw 904 relative to storage and disposal of infectious waste, including sharps. Source.

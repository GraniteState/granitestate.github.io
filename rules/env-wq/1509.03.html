Env-Wq 1509.03 Content and Format of Waiver Requests

Effective 2026-08-06. Expires 2036-08-06.

Env-Wq 1509.03 Content and Format of Waiver Requests. (a) The person requesting the waiver shall provide the following information to the department through NHEnviro: (1) The submission number assigned pursuant to Env-Wq 1503.13(b); (2) The specific rule section or paragraph for which a waiver is being requested; and (3) A full explanation of why the applicant believes that having the waiver granted will meet the criteria in Env-Wq 1509.04. (b) The applicant or the applicant’s agent shall sign the request as specified in Env-Wq 1503.10.

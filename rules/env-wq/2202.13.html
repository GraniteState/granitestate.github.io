Env-Wq 2202.13 Salt alternative

Effective 2024-05-24. Earlier expiration 2014-04-30.

Env-Wq 2202.13 “Salt alternative” means “salt alternative” as defined in RSA 489-C:1, VI, as reprinted in Appendix B.

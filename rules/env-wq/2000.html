Env-Wq 2000 COASTAL PROGRAM GRANT RULES

CHAPTER Env-Wq 2000 COASTAL PROGRAM GRANT RULES REVISION NOTE: Effective July 1, 2004, Chapter 257:20 of the Laws of 2004 transferred Pln 900 Coastal Program Grant Rules from the Office of Energy and Planning, formerly the Office of State Planning and Energy Programs, to the Department of Environmental Services (Department). The Department subsequently filed Env-Ws 2000 in Document #8411, effective 8-19-05 which made extensive changes to the numbering, wording, and format of Pln 900 and supersedes all prior filings for Pln 900. The prior filings affecting rules in Pln 900 include the following: #4526, eff 11-3-88 #5010, eff 11-27-90 #5509, eff 11-25-92 #6430, eff 1-18-97 #6610, eff 10-22-97 #7992, eff 12-1-03 This chapter was subsequently redesignated editorially as Env-Wq 2000 pursuant to a rules reorganization plan for Department rules approved by the Director of the Office of Legislative Services on 9-7-05.

Env-Wq 1503.25 Permit Amendment to Modify the Approved Project

Effective 2026-08-06. Expires 2036-08-06.

Env-Wq 1503.25 Permit Amendment to Modify the Approved Project. (a) If the amendment request is to modify the approved project, the applicant shall do the following in addition to submitting the form in Env-Wq 1503.24(b): (1) If any portion of a project is located within a river corridor of a designated river as defined in RSA 483:4, VIII, notify the appropriate local river advisory committee that an amendment request is being submitted through NHEnviro and include the permit number; (2) If the revised area of disturbance is outside the area allowed to be disturbed under the original permit, submit through NHEnviro a DataCheck request results letter, dated no later than one year from the date the amendment request form is submitted, from the department providing information on recorded occurrences of threatened or endangered species; (3) Submit through NHEnviro revised plans with the changes highlighted; (4) Submit through NHEnviro revised calculations, if applicable; and (5) Pay the fee required by RSA 485-A:17, II(b). (b) The amendment request shall be approved if: (1) All required information is submitted; (2) The modifications do not rise to the level of significant deviation pursuant to Env-Wq 1503.22(d); and (3) The project as modified would have been approved originally. (c) If the department determines that proposed amendments qualify as significant deviations to the approved plans pursuant to Env-Wq 1503.22(d), the department shall deny the request for an amended permit and the permit holder shall submit a new application if the permit holder wants to proceed with the project as modified.

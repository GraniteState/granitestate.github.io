Env-Wq 2002.06 Practicable

Effective 2009-10-17.

Env-Wq 2002.06 “Practicable" means available and capable of being done after taking into consideration cost, existing technology, and logistics in light of overall project purposes. Source. (See Revision Note at chapter heading for Env-Wq 2000) #8411, eff 8-19-05; ss by #9570, eff 10-17-09

Env-Wq 1611.02 Label Requirements

Effective 2024-04-27.

Env-Wq 1611.02 Label Requirements. (a) A generator of EQ solids shall submit a proposed label to the department with the application for EQ certification, and with the annual report required under Env-Wq 1610.06. (b) The proposed label shall include the following: (1) The name, address, and telephone number of the treatment facility where the EQ solids were produced; (2) A brief description of the EQ solids product, including the process employed to treat or stabilize the solids or filtrate; (3) Recommended uses and appropriate application rates; (4) Average nutrient analysis of the final EQ solids product for nitrogen, phosphorus, and potassium based upon the analytical results required under Env-Wq 1610.05 from the previous year; and (5) Average concentration for the compounds listed in Env-Wq 1610.03(c) and the average concentrations of the regulated groundwater PFAS compounds based upon the analytical results required under Env-Wq 1610.05 from the previous year. (c) The department shall approve the label within 30 days of receipt if it determines that the information in the proposed label is complete and correct. (d) A generator of EQ solids shall include the label approved in (c) above, with every shipment. Source. (See Revision Note #1 and Revision Note #2 at chapter heading for Env-Wq 1600) #13944, eff 4-27-24

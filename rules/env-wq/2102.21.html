Env-Wq 2102.21 Public Water Suppliers

Effective 2017-03-21.

Env-Wq 2102.21 Public Water Suppliers. Any public water supplier that provides water use data to the department under subtitle Env-Dw shall be deemed to be in compliance with this part if the information being provided includes the information required by Env-Wq 2102.18. Source. #9279, eff 9-24-08; ss by #12135, eff 3-21-17 (from Env-Wq 2102.20)

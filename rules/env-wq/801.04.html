Env-Wq 801.04 Co-Disposal of Wastes Prohibited

Effective 2026-05-15. Expires 2036-05-15.

Env-Wq 801.04 Co-Disposal of Wastes Prohibited. Hazardous waste as defined in RSA 147-A or solid waste as defined in RSA 149-M, except for wood ash certified for use under Env-Sw 1700 and waste derived products certified for use under Env-Sw 1500, shall not be disposed or processed at a site or facility permitted under this chapter. Source. #10998, eff 1-1-16; ss by #14571, eff 5-15-26, EXPIRES: 5-15-36

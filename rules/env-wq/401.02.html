Env-Wq 401.02 Applicability

Effective 2025-08-23. Expires 2035-08-23.

Env-Wq 401.02 Applicability. (a) Subject to (b) below, these rules shall apply only to persons who use, store, or otherwise handle any regulated substances in regulated containers. (b) As provided in RSA 485-C:11, I, these rules shall not apply to: (1) Potential contamination sources listed in RSA 485-C:7, II(j), which are subject to requirements of RSA title XL and the department of agriculture, markets, and food; or (2) Those regulated substances defined as pesticides under RSA 430:29, XXVI. (c) These rules also shall not apply to: (1) Aboveground storage tanks regulated under RSA 146-A and Env-Or 300; (2) Underground storage tanks regulated under RSA 146-C and Env-Or 400; or (3) On-premise-use facilities as defined in RSA 146-E:2, III.

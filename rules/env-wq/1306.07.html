Env-Wq 1306.07 Reporting Requirements

Effective 2025-01-25.

Env-Wq 1306.07 Reporting Requirements. (a) For maintenance projects using aquatic herbicides, the grant recipient shall submit a report at the end of the growing season that identifies the type of herbicide, rate of chemical application, success of the treatment, total project cost, and impacts to any non-target aquatic or land-based plant or animal species, and provides a summary of IPM strategies used during the grant period. (b) To satisfy the reporting requirement of (a), above, the grant recipient may submit a photocopy or electronic copy of the aquatic pesticide project report submitted to the department of agriculture, markets and foods pursuant to Pes 603.03(c)(27), or any successor rule, that the grant recipient has supplemented with any information required by (a), above, that is not already in the report.

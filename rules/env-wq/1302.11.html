Env-Wq 1302.11 Maintenance project

Effective 2025-01-25.

Env-Wq 1302.11 “Maintenance project” means a project designed to remediate a water impairment through the short-term control of an exotic aquatic weed infestation or a water quality problem by treating the problem but not the underlying cause. Source. (See Revision Note #1 at chapter heading for Env-Wq 1300) #8703-A, eff 9-5-06; ss by #10696, eff 10-18-14 (See Revision Note #2 at chapter heading for Env-Wq 1300); ss by #14186, eff 1-25-25

Env-Wq 1503.05 AOT Permit Application Procedures

Effective 2026-08-06. Expires 2036-08-06.

Env-Wq 1503.05 AOT Permit Application Procedures. (a) As specified in RSA 485-A:17, I, an application for an AOT permit shall be filed at least 30 days prior to the proposed starting date of the proposed activities, and no activities shall commence without prior approval of the application by the department. (b) The applicant for an AOT permit shall submit a complete application, as specified in (c), below, to the department online through NHEnviro. (c) A complete application shall include: (1) A completed application form as specified in Env-Wq 1503.06, signed as specified in Env-Wq 1503.10; (2) The plans and other information specified in Env-Wq 1503.07 and Env-Wq 1503.09, as applicable; (3) If the applicant has authorized another to act as the applicant’s agent, a copy of the written authorization; (4) The information required in (e)(2), below, if the application is for a permit by notification and the project is located within a river corridor of a designated river as defined in RSA 483:4, VIII; and (5) The fee required by RSA 485-A:17, II(a). (d) If any portion of a project is located within a river corridor of a designated river as defined in RSA 483:4, VIII, the applicant for a standard permit shall: (1) Submit a copy of the plans required pursuant to Env-Wq 1503.11 and the application receipt with the NHEnviro submission number to the appropriate local river advisory committee no later than 3 days after submitting the application in NHEnviro; and (2) Obtain proof of delivery in accordance with (g), below, and submit this documentation within 3 days of receipt of a request from the department. (e) If any portion of a project is located within a river corridor of a designated river as defined in RSA 483:4, VIII, the applicant for a permit by notification shall: (1) Submit a copy of the plans required pursuant to Env-Wq 1503.11 and a copy of the stormwater management report of the project to the appropriate local river advisory committee at least 40 days prior to filing the application with the department; and (2) Obtain proof of delivery in accordance with (g), below, and submit this documentation in NHEnviro when applying for a permit by notification. (f) An applicant for a permit by notification may submit their application before the 40 days in (e), above, has ended if the applicant has: (1) Received comments from the local river advisory committee and provided a response letter to the local river advisory committee confirming that the comments were addressed in a manner that the proposed activity would not violate a protection measure as specified in RSA 483:12-a; or (2) Received written documentation from the local river advisory committee that it does not have any comments for consideration. (g) To satisfy the proof of delivery requirements in (d)(2) and (e)(2), above, acceptable proof of delivery shall include: (1) A copy of the certified mail receipt for each copy that was sent by certified mail; (2) A copy of the delivery confirmation for each copy that was sent via a private delivery service; or (3) A statement signed by the applicant that the copy was delivered in hand for each copy that was hand-delivered. (h) Upon receipt of a complete application for an AOT permit, the department shall send notice through NHEnviro that includes a link to the application to the affected municipalities in accordance with RSA 541-A:39.

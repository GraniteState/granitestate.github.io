Env-Wq 907.08 Communicable Diseases Isolation and Reporting

Effective 2017-09-01.

Env-Wq 907.08 Communicable Diseases Isolation and Reporting. (a) As required by He-P 301.03, any case or suspected case of a reportable disease, as listed in He-P 301.02, shall be reported by: (1) The physician or licensed health care practitioner who assessed, diagnosed, or treated the individual believed to have or suspected of having a reportable disease; or (2) When no physician or licensed health care practitioner is present, the director. (b) The report identified in (a), above, shall include all of the information required by He-P 301.03, including the name and home address of the individual known to have or suspected of having the reportable disease. (c) An individual with a communicable disease shall be placed in isolation and not leave or be removed from strict isolation without permission of the YRC health staff. Source.

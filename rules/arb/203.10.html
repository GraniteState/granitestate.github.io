Arb 203.10 Withdrawal of Request for Hearing

Effective 2007-09-28. Earlier expiration 2007-11-01.

Arb 203.10 Withdrawal of Request for Hearing. (a) A consumer or lessee may withdraw his/her request for a hearing in writing to the board. (b) Pursuant to RSA 357-D:4, V, if a consumer or lessee withdraws his/her request for a hearing due to corrective work done by the manufacturer, the consumer or lessee may recommence his/her request for a hearing, if the corrective work is unsatisfactory.

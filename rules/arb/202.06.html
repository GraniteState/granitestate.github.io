Arb 202.06 Lessor

Effective 2007-09-28.

Arb 202.06 "Lessor" means a person who leases a motor vehicle to a lessee pursuant to a written agreement for a term of 2 or more years. Source. #8996, eff 9-28-07 (from Arb 202.01)

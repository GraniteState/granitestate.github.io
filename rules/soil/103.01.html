Soil 103.01 Duties and Responsibilities

Effective 2026-04-01. Earlier expiration 1995-09-20.

Soil 103.01 Duties and Responsibilities. The board shall administer the provisions of RSA 310-A:75 through RSA 310-A:95 which include but are not limited to fostering intelligent application of the knowledge of soil properties and wetland characteristics in planning and implementing land use decisions in this state.

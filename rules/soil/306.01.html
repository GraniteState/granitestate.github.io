REVISION NOTE #2: Document #14019, effective 9-6-24, adopted and readopted with amendments all of the rules in Chapter Soil 300. Paragraph (b) was amended and incorporated into the former Soil 301.06, which was renumbered as Soil 301.04, and now titled “Information from References for Soil Scientist and Wetlands Scientist Candidates.” Document #14019 also renumbered expired Soil 301.04 as Soil 301.03, expired Soil 301.08 as Soil 301.05, expired Soil 304.03 as Soil 304.04, and expired Soil 304.04 as Soil 304.05.

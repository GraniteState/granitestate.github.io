Soil 103.05 Office Hours, Office Location, Mailing Address and Telephone

Effective 2026-04-01.

Soil 103.05 Office Hours, Office Location, Mailing Address and Telephone. (a) The board’s office shall be located at the OPLC, 7 Eagle Square, Concord, N.H. and shall be open to the public weekdays, excluding state holidays, from 8:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m. (b) Correspondence shall be addressed to the board at: Office of Professional Licensure and Certification 7 Eagle Square Concord, N.H. 03301 (c) The board’s telephone number shall be (603)-271-2152. Source. #8006, eff 12-18-03; ss by #13640, eff 7-18-23; ss by #14535, eff 4-1-26 (formerly Soil 103.07)

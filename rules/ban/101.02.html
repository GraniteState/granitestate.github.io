Ban 101.02 Scope

Effective 2024-10-03. Earlier expiration 1990-04-20.

Ban 101.02 Scope. These rules shall apply to financial institutions that are either chartered, registered, or licensed in New Hampshire, including, without limitation: (a) Banks; (b) Trust companies; (c) Family trust companies; (d) Credit unions; (e) Small loan companies; (f) Mortgage bankers, brokers, originators, and loan servicers; (g) Motor vehicle sales finance companies; (h) Retail sellers of motor vehicles; (i) Debt adjusters; (j) Money transmitters; and (k) Other financial institutions organized and operating under the banking laws of the state of New Hampshire.

Med 413.01 Negotiating a Settlement Agreement

Effective 2020-01-10.

Med 413.01 Negotiating a Settlement Agreement. A licensee may engage in settlement negotiations with hearing counsel at any time until the board issues a final order in accordance with Med 208.01(a). Source. #9900, eff 4-12-11 (see Revision Note at chapter heading for Med 400); ss by #12972, eff 1-10-20

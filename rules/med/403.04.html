Med 403.04 Noncompliance

Effective 2020-01-10. Earlier expiration 2006-12-13.

Med 403.04 Noncompliance. Failure to complete continuing medical education requirements or to submit documentation of such completion in a timely fashion, shall result in denial of license renewal.

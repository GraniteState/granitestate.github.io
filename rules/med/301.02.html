Med 301.02 Application Process

Effective 2020-01-10. Earlier expiration 2006-12-13.

Med 301.02 Application Process. (a) Persons wishing to practice medicine in New Hampshire shall submit both the Federation of State Medical Boards’ Uniform Application, available on the board’s website, and a “State Addendum,” revised 11/2019, which contains the information specified in Med 301.03, and the application fee specified in Table 3.6.1. in Med 306.01. (b) An application which is not signed by the applicant shall not be accepted and shall be returned to the applicant. (c) The board shall acknowledge receipt of an application within 60 days and shall notify the applicant of any deficiencies in the application, including the absence of the application fee, or any further information needed to clarify the applicant's qualifications. Failure to remedy the deficiencies within 52 weeks of the board’s initial receipt of the application shall result in dismissal of the application. (d) Applications shall be granted by the board pursuant to the requirements set forth in RSA 329:14, II. (e) If the application is denied, the applicant shall be provided an opportunity to request a hearing for reconsideration pursuant to Med 208 on the deficiency issues identified by the board. Any such request shall be received by the board within 30 days.

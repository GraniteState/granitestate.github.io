Med 403.01 Severance of Connection

Effective 2023-07-02. Earlier expiration 2006-12-13.

Med 403.01 Severance of Connection. All licensees shall notify the board in writing within 30 days after they sever connection with any commitment to practice medicine for any reason, either personal, professional, or disciplinary.

Med 401.01 Expiration of License

Effective 2016-03-02. Earlier expiration 2016-03-02.

Med 401.01 Expiration of License. Each license shall automatically expire on July 1 of the year in which the licensee's renewal is set to occur, unless the licensee has applied to the board for renewal of license by June 30 of the year in which the licensee’s renewal is set to occur.

Bar 303.01 Scheduling Examinations

Effective 2017-11-01. Earlier expiration 2017-07-13.

Bar 303.01 Scheduling Examinations. Each practical and written exams shall be held in accordance with RSA 313-A:15. The exams shall be held once a month. The location of exams shall, include but not be limited to, the Courtyard Marriott& Grappone Conference Center in Concord and a school licensed by the board.

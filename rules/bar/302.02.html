Bar 302.02 Shop and Licensee Equipment

Effective 2023-10-20.

Bar 302.02 Shop and Licensee Equipment. (a) A barber, cosmetology, esthetics, or manicuring shop shall have equipment necessary to safely perform barber, cosmetology, esthetics, or manicuring services. (b) All shampoo services shall be performed in a shampoo sink.

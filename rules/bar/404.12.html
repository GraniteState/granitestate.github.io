Bar 404.12 Multiple Violations

Effective 2017-06-30.

Bar 404.12 Multiple Violations. (a) The board shall assess separate fines against a party for multiple violations of the statutes, rules, or both, provided the board makes a separate finding of fact from the record for each violation. (b) Noncompliance which was carried out over a period of days shall be a separate violation for each such day. (c) Nonpayment of a fine by a licensee in contravention of an order, agreement or promise to pay, shall result in separate discipline by the board and shall result in denying a license until paid.

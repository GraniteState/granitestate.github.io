Bar 602.01 Application for a Tanning Facility Registration

Effective 2026-07-17. Expires 2036-07-17.

Bar 602.01 Application for a Tanning Facility Registration. Each applicant applying for their initial tanning facility registration shall submit: (a) The “Universal Application for Initial Licensure” as required by Plc 304.01(a)(1); (b) The following information on the “Tanning Facility Addendum to the Universal Application for Initial Licensure” revised 5/2026: (1) The name and business address of the owner(s), in accordance with RSA 313-A:28, II; (2) A list of equipment including the number for each type of tanning device; and (3) Signature of owner(s) and current date; and (c) The fee required by Plc 1002.07.

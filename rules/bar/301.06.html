Bar 301.06 Completion of Apprenticeship

Effective 2015-04-02. Earlier expiration 2014-06-09.

Bar 301.06 Completion of Apprenticeship. The apprentice shall complete and file an application for licensure in accordance with Bar 301.07(b) upon completion of the apprenticeship.

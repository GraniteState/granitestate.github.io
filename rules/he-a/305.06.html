He-A 305.06 Requirements for Organizational or Service Change

Effective 2025-05-22. Expires 2035-05-22.

He-A 305.06 Requirements for Organizational or Service Change. (a) All registered recovery houses shall provide the department with written notice at least 30 days prior to changes for any of the following: (1) Ownership; (2) Physical location; (3) Name; (4) Number of beds; and (5) Changes in services available to residents. (b) All registered recovery houses shall provide the department with written notice within 5 days of any changes to the status of their certification by the certifying body. (c) All registered recovery houses shall complete and submit a new application for registry prior to operating under a: (1) Change in ownership; or (2) Change in physical location.

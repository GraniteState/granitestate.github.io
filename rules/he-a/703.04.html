He-A 703.04 Administrative Fee for Program Transfer

Effective 2006-11-16. Earlier expiration 2006-11-16.

He-A 703.04 Administrative Fee for Program Transfer. If, after paying the program fee and prior to the start of the first session, a client wishes to transfer to another program in another catchment area, he or she may do so, subject to the following conditions: (a) The original provider shall retain an administrative fee pursuant to 703.03(a) or (b) from the full program fee that was paid and shall return the remainder of the program fee to the client; and (b) The client shall pay the provider to which he or she transfers the entire program fee.

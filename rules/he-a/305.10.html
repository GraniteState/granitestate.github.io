He-A 305.10 Referral to Registered Recovery Houses

Effective 2021-06-26. Earlier expiration 2021-06-28.

He-A 305.10 Referral to Registered Recovery Houses. A state agency or vendor with a state or federally funded contract that is providing treatment or recovery support services to a person shall not refer the person to recovery housing unless the recovery housing is registered pursuant to this part.

He-A 907.02 Services Prohibited

Effective 2008-01-30. Earlier expiration 2008-01-09.

He-A 907.02 Services Prohibited. Each phase II program shall offer only intervention and educational services for its clients convicted of DWI, but shall not provide treatment services for the alcohol and drug problems of said clients within the context of phase II programming. Source. #7058, eff 7-21-99; ss by #8902, INTERIM, eff 7-13-07, EXPIRED: 1-9-08 New. #9082, eff 1-30-08

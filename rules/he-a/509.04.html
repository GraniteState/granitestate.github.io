He-A 509.04 Certification Period

Effective 2024-01-06. Expires 2034-01-06.

He-A 509.04 Certification Period. A certificate for instructor certification shall expire after 5 years on the last day of the month it was issued unless a completed application for renewal has been timely received by the department. This shall not be the case if certification is revoked prior to the end of the 5-year period pursuant to He-A 509.06. Source. #10240, eff 1-1-13; ss by #13846, eff 1-6-24, EXPIRES: 1-6-34

He-A 904.01 New Hampshire Residents: Program Options

Effective 2009-10-23. Expires 2010-04-21.

He-A 904.01 New Hampshire Residents: Program Options. (a) Upon conviction under RSA 265-A:2 or RSA 265-A:3, and pursuant to RSA 265-A:42 and RSA 265-A:39, I, a person who is a resident of the state of New Hampshire shall attend a phase II program in the state, as assigned by the court or the department. (b) If a New Hampshire resident lives in another state for at least 9 months out of a year for the purpose of formal study in a state certified educational institution or is in active military duty, such person shall be considered a non-resident for the purpose of these rules.

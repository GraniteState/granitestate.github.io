He-A 903.02 Administrative Fee for Phase II Programs

Effective 2008-01-30. Earlier expiration 2008-01-09.

He-A 903.02 Administrative Fee for Phase II Programs. Unless otherwise specified herein, if any He-A 900 rule mandates an administrative fee, as defined in He-A 902.01(b) and allowed by RSA 265-A:39, IV(d), the administrative fee to be paid by the client for a phase II program shall be $150.00.

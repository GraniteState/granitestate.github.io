He-A 707.09 Films and Resource Materials

Effective 2006-11-16. Earlier expiration 2006-11-16.

He-A 707.09 Films and Resource Materials. (a) Providers shall use only those films and resource materials approved by the department, according to the procedure and criteria described in He-A 707.05(b) and (c). (b) New films or resource materials may be used subsequent to department approval.

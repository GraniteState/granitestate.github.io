He-A 305.09 Publication and Dissemination of Recovery House Registration List

Effective 2021-06-26. Earlier expiration 2021-06-28.

He-A 305.09 Publication and Dissemination of Recovery House Registration List. The department shall: (a) Compile and maintain a list of registered recovery houses; (b) Publish the list on the bureau of drug and alcohol website found at https://www.dhhs.nh.gov/dcbcs/bdas/recovery-house-registry.htm; (c) Distribute the list directly to the bureau of drug and alcohol services’ contracted vendors; and (d) Make the list available to anyone upon request.

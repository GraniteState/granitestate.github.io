He-M 401.01 Purpose

Effective 2013-07-23. Earlier expiration 2013-07-23.

He-M 401.01 Purpose. The purpose of these rules is to establish the requirements and procedures for determining eligibility for state-funded community mental health services and for developing and monitoring the individual service plan.

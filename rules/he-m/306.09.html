He-M 306.09 Treatment Limitations

Effective 2026-06-26. Expires 2036-06-26.

He-M 306.09 Treatment Limitations. (a) No involuntary treatment shall be imposed pursuant to a request for authority to administer emergency treatment prior to a decision being rendered by the presiding officer in accordance with He-M 306.07 except in accordance with He-M 305, personal safety emergencies. (b) Treatment shall not be administered to a voluntarily admitted individual who refuses to accept it. (c) The chief medical officer of New Hampshire hospital shall monitor treatment provided pursuant to He-M 306.03 at least every 7 days through review with the attending licensed practitioner and order discontinuation of the treatment authorization upon determination that the criteria for treatment authorization no longer exist. The chief medical officer may delegate these functions to another qualified hospital psychiatrist who is a board-certified physician and not the attending physician for the patient. (d) An authorization for emergency treatment shall not exceed 90 days. An authorization shall be renewed for subsequent 90-day periods if: (1) A request for extension is filed before the current authorization period expires; and (2) The licensed practitioner demonstrates that continued treatment is necessary to prevent a recurrence of the conditions or symptoms that led to the psychiatric or medical emergency. (e) The authorization to provide emergency treatment to the patient shall immediately expire if a guardian over the person of the patient with authority to make treatment decisions is appointed during the period of emergency treatment authorized by the presiding officer.

He-M 311.01 Purpose

Effective 2024-07-30. Earlier expiration 2015-05-14.

He-M 311.01 Purpose. The purpose of these rules is to define the rights of individuals receiving services in state mental health facilities which include New Hampshire hospital, Glencliff home, Hampstead hospital and residential treatment facility, and any receiving facility so designated by the commissioner pursuant to RSA 135-C:26.

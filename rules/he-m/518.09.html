He-M 518.09 Records and Reporting

Effective 2024-08-01. Earlier expiration 2014-02-18.

He-M 518.09 Records and Reporting. Each provider agency shall: (a) Maintain records and provide documentation as outlined in He-M 504.03 and He-M 504.04 and He-M 517.10 for all individuals receiving services pursuant to He-M 518.05 and He-M 518.07; and (b) At least annually, assess the employment service through interviews with employers, individuals, and guardians.

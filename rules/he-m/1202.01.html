He-M 1202.01 Purpose

Effective 2017-05-26. Earlier expiration 2012-03-19.

He-M 1202.01 Purpose. The purpose of the rule is to ensure the safe administration of medications by providers to individuals who reside in community residences certified under He-M 1002. Source. #7957, eff 9-19-03; ss by #9978, INTERIM, eff 9-19-11, EXPIRED: 3-19-12 New. #12192, eff 5-26-17

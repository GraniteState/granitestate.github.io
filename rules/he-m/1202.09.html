He-M 1202.09 Designation of Nurse Trainers

Effective 2017-05-26. Earlier expiration 2012-03-19.

He-M 1202.09 Designation of Nurse Trainers. (a) The director shall, upon request, grant designation as a nurse trainer to nurses who: (1) Have a license as a registered nurse in the State of New Hampshire that is current and unencumbered; (2) Have 2 years of licensed nursing experience, at least one of which has been as a registered nurse, within the past 5 years; and (3) Have completed a 6 hour orientation program approved by the director. (b) The director shall, upon request, grant a conditional designation effective until the next scheduled training as a nurse trainer to nurses who fulfill the requirements of (a)(1) and (2) above but have not yet completed the orientation required by (a)(3) above. (c) A nurse granted conditional designation shall not authorize or re-authorize providers to administer medications but may supervise currently authorized providers.

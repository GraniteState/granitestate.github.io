He-M 613.06 Discharge from NHH Pursuant to Voluntary Admission

Effective 2016-09-20. Earlier expiration 2007-06-29.

He-M 613.06 Discharge from NHH Pursuant to Voluntary Admission. (a) Pursuant to RSA 135-C:16, if a person is a patient on a voluntary basis, he or she may withdraw from the receiving facility, whether or not such withdrawal is made against medical advice. (b) A patient who has requested to withdraw shall: (1) Be released upon request if an examination conducted in accordance with RSA 135-C:17 is not ordered; (2) Be released if an examination is ordered but does not occur within the 24-hour period; (3) Be released if an examination determines that the criteria specified in RSA 135-C:27 are not met; or (4) Be held and treated in accordance with the provisions of RSA 135-C:27-33 if an examination determines that the criteria are met.

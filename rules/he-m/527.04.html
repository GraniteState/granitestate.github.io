He-M 527.04 Transfers to or from a DRF

Effective 2016-07-01. Earlier expiration 2016-07-01.

He-M 527.04 Transfers to or from a DRF. (a) A DRF may accept the transfer of a person who is admitted to the secure psychiatric unit pursuant to RSA 171-B, in accordance with RSA 622:48, I(b). (b) A DRF may transfer a person admitted to the DRF pursuant to RSA 171-B, to the secure psychiatric unit pursuant to RSA 171-B:15, I, RSA 622:45, and He-M 611. (c) Transfers from one DRF to another shall be conducted in accordance with He-M 529. (d) Transfers from a DRF for medical treatment or security reasons shall be conducted in accordance with He-M 529.

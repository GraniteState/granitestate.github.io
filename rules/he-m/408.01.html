He-M 408.01 Purpose

Effective 2017-10-24. Earlier expiration 2008-05-23.

He-M 408.01 Purpose. These rules establish the required components of the clinical records maintained by community mental health programs and their subcontracted service providers for persons eligible to receive state-funded services pursuant to RSA 135-C:13 and He-M 401 and identify the contents of those components.

He-M 426.01 Purpose

Effective 2017-03-28. Earlier expiration 2017-03-28.

He-M 426.01 Purpose. The purpose of these rules is to describe the services provided by CMHPs and community mental health providers that are offered to persons eligible for services pursuant to He-M 401 and are reimbursable under the medicaid program.

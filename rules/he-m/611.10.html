He-M 611.10 Readmissions to the Secure Psychiatric Unit

Effective 2017-07-25. Earlier expiration 2017-08-13.

He-M 611.10 Readmissions to the Secure Psychiatric Unit. Persons transferred to a mental health or developmental services DRF from the unit pursuant to RSA 622:48 who are transferred back to the unit shall be transferred under the provisions of He-M 611.

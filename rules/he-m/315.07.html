He-M 315.07 Personal Rights

Effective 2017-05-26. Earlier expiration 2013-10-06.

He-M 315.07 Personal Rights. (a) Persons receiving services from a PSA shall have the right to be treated by PSA staff, volunteers, and consultants with dignity and respect at all times. (b) Staff, volunteers, and consultants of a PSA shall not abuse, neglect, or exploit agency members or participants. (c) Staff, volunteers, and consultants of a PSA shall respect the privacy of members and participants. (d) A PSA shall maintain the confidentiality of membership records except as in (e) below. (e) A PSA may disclose to law enforcement personnel that information necessary to report a crime committed at the agency. (f) The PSA shall disclose the information specified in (e) above: (1) To the department of health and human services; (2) When required to report abuse or neglect of a child pursuant to RSA 169-C; (3) When required to report abuse, neglect or exploitation of an adult pursuant to RSA 161-F; and (4) As otherwise required by law. (g) Members and participants of a PSA shall have the right to privacy including the following: (1) The right to courtesies such as knocking on closed doors before entering; and (2) The right to have reasonable access to a telephone and a computer, if one is available for use, with such privacy as agency facilities allow, provided that members and participants may be required to pay for long distance calls. Source. #8445, eff 10-6-05, EXPIRED: 10-6-13 New. #12191, eff 5-26-17

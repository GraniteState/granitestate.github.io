He-M 523.11 Voluntary Withdrawal from Services

Effective 2018-12-28. Earlier expiration 2018-12-24.

He-M 523.11 Voluntary Withdrawal from Services. (a) A young adult or family may withdraw voluntarily from services at any time. (b) The PIH coordinator shall document the withdrawal in the record. (c) A young adult or family who has withdrawn from services may reapply for services at any time.

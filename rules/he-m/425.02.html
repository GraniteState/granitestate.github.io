He-M 425.02 Definitions

Effective 2018-04-20. Earlier expiration 2014-02-17.

He-M 425.02 Definitions. The words and phrases in this rule shall mean the following: (a) "Commissioner" means the commissioner of the department of health and human services. (b) "Community mental health program (CMHP)” means a program operated by the state, city, town, or county, or a community based New Hampshire nonprofit corporation for the purpose of planning, establishing, and administering an array of community-based, mental health services pursuant to He-M 403 and as defined in RSA 135-C:2, IV. (c) "Region" means a geographic area designated in He-M 425.03 for the purpose of assigning primary responsibility for providing mental health services to the residents of certain communities.

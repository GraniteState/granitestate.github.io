He-M 402.08 Quality Improvement

Effective 2017-05-26. Earlier expiration 2013-10-06.

He-M 402.08 Quality Improvement. (a) The department shall conduct announced or unannounced quality assurance reviews of PSAs to assure that such services and programs are operated in accordance with the department's rules and contract provisions. (b) A PSA shall perform active monitoring and comprehensive quality assurance activities including, at a minimum: (1) Participation in quality improvement reviews conducted by the department; (2) Member satisfaction surveys; (3) Review of personnel files for completeness; and (4) Review of the complaint process.

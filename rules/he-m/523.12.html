He-M 523.12 Designation of Region Boundaries

Effective 2018-12-28. Earlier expiration 2018-12-24.

He-M 523.12 Designation of Region Boundaries. (a) An eligible young adult or family may request to SMS to receive services from a region other than the one in which they reside. (b) A lead agency may request from SMS, with the approval of the eligible young adult or family, that the young adult or family receive services from another region other than the one in which they reside. (c) Requests made in (a) and (b) above shall be submitted in writing to SMS and include supporting information that explains why the family is better served by another region.

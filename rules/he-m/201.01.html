He-M 201.01 Purpose

Effective 2008-01-03. Expires 2016-01-03.

He-M 201.01 Purpose. The purpose of these rules is to provide any person employed by a state institution an opportunity to challenge any finding that she or he has abused, neglected or exploited a client.

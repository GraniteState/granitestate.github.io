He-M 519.08 State Family Support Council

Effective 2019-05-21. Earlier expiration 2011-02-13.

He-M 519.08 State Family Support Council. The state family support council shall: (a) Be comprised of one voting delegate appointed by each of the 10 regional family support councils; (b) Be assisted by the family support administrator or designee and bureau support staff; (c) Elect a new chairperson at least every 2 years; (d) Hold meetings every other month to discuss agenda items formulated by members of the council; (e) Be a forum for exchanging, sharing, and distributing information to each regional council; (f) Be an avenue for arbitration and mediation of conflict resolution between area agencies and regional councils when requested by both parties and after processes identified pursuant to He-M 519.05(c)(4)b. have been exhausted; and (g) Provide information and feedback on issues and concerns of regional councils to the bureau.

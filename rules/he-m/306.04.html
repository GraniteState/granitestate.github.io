He-M 306.04 Criteria for Emergency Treatment

Effective 2026-06-26. Expires 2036-06-26.

He-M 306.04 Criteria for Emergency Treatment. A licensed practitioner requesting authorization to administer emergency treatment shall demonstrate, by clear and convincing evidence, that: (a) A medical or psychiatric emergency exists; (b) The patient’s admission status is one of the following: (1) A district court found probable cause for involuntary emergency admission pursuant to RSA 135-C:27-33; (2) A probate court ordered involuntary admission pursuant to RSA 135-C:34-45; or (3) New Hampshire hospital has absolutely revoked the patient’s conditional discharge in accordance with RSA 135-C:51 and He-M 609.06 due, at least in part, to the patient’s dangerousness; (c) The patient does not have a guardian authorized to make medical decisions; (d) The involuntarily admitted patient cannot make a decision regarding their treatment due to lack of capacity; and (e) A reasonable person would consent to the administration of emergency treatment.

He-M 201.05 Hearing Process

Effective 2008-01-03. Expires 2016-01-03.

He-M 201.05 Hearing Process. (a) An employee may request a review hearing to appeal a finding pursuant to He-M 201.03(c) or a reconsideration determination pursuant to He-M 201.04(c). (b) The purpose of a review hearing shall be to determine if the finding of abuse, neglect or exploitation is supported by the evidence collected during the investigation. (c) Hearing requests and all other hearing related matters shall take place in accordance with He-C 200.

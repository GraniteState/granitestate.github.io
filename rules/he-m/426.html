He-M 426 COMMUNITY MENTAL HEALTH SERVICES

PART He-M 426 COMMUNITY MENTAL HEALTH SERVICES REVISION NOTE: Document #5433, effective 7-2-92, readopted with amendments Part He-M 426. Document #5433 superseded all prior filings for the section in this Part. The prior filings for former Part He-M 426 included the following documents: #4313, eff 9-25-87 #4798, eff 4-24-90 Interim #4921, eff 8-22-90 #5178, eff 7-19-91 Document #5589, effective 2-25-93, and Document #5703, effective 9-17-93, superseded or amended different sections in He-M 426 after Document #5433 as noted in the source notes. Document #5971, effective 2-1-95, superseded or amended several sections in He-M 426. Document #5971 also adopted a new section He-M 426.13 entitled "Case Management Services" and renumbered existing sections He-M 426.13 through He-M 426.20 as He-M 426.14 through He-M 426.21. The source notes for He-M 426.14 through He-M 426.21 therefore now contain the filing history of rules which had been numbered He-M 426.13 through He-M 426.20 prior to Document #5971.

He-M 524.01 Purpose

Effective 2022-06-18. Earlier expiration 2011-11-17.

He-M 524.01 Purpose. The purpose of these rules is to establish minimum standards for the provision of Medicaid-covered home- and community-based in home residential habilitation, including personal care and other related supports and services that promote greater independence and skill development for a child, adolescent, or young adult who: (a) Has a developmental disability; (b) Has significant medical or behavioral challenges as determined pursuant to He-M 524.03 (a)(4) and (5) a.; and (c) Lives at home with his or her family.

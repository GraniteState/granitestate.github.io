He-M 203.10 Civil Action

Effective 2013-04-26. Earlier expiration 2008-10-19.

He-M 203.10 Civil Action. Any party aggrieved by the findings and decisions of the appointed impartial person in the due process proceedings as set out in He-M 203.03 through He-M 203.08 may immediately bring a civil action in state or federal court under § 639 (a) (1) of the IDEIA.

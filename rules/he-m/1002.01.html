He-M 1002.01 Purpose

Effective 2019-11-16. Earlier expiration 2019-09-16.

He-M 1002.01 Purpose. The purpose of these rules is to: (a) Define the standards and procedures for the certification of community residences funded by the state of New Hampshire for persons with a mental illness; and (b) Establish minimum standards governing the operation and continued certification of such residences.

He-M 609.01 Purpose

Effective 2022-01-25. Earlier expiration 2008-09-21.

He-M 609.01 Purpose. The purpose of these rules is to define the criteria and procedures for conditional discharge of a person involuntarily admitted to a facility and for the revision and revocation of the conditional discharge.

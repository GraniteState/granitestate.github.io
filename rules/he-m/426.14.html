He-M 426.14 Services to Determine Medicaid Eligibility

Effective 2017-03-28. Earlier expiration 2017-03-28.

He-M 426.14 Services to Determine Medicaid Eligibility. (a) Copying a portion of a recipient’s record to be used for medicaid eligibility determination shall: (1) Be a covered CMHP service; and (2) Be limited to one unit per 6 month period. (b) Psychiatric evaluation for medicaid eligibility shall: (1) Be a covered CMHP service; (2) Refer to one evaluation session of any duration; and (3) Be limited to one session per recipient per 6-month period. (c) Psychiatric evaluation for medicaid eligibility shall include the following: (1) History of present illness; (2) Family and social history; (3) Current mental status examination; (4) Psychiatric diagnosis; (5) Associated medical problems; and (6) An assessment of disability including a suggested individual treatment plan and further diagnostic evaluation studies, with a written report to the office of family services.

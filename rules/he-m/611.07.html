He-M 611.07 Transfer from the Unit to New Hampshire Hospital

Effective 2022-08-21.

He-M 611.07 Transfer from the Unit to New Hampshire Hospital. (a) A person shall have the right to request a transfer to the least restrictive environment necessary to achieve the purposes of treatment. (b) Transfers from the unit to New Hampshire hospital shall be made pursuant to Cor 504.11. (c) If the commissioner denies a transfer, the denial shall be communicated to the person and his or her guardian, if applicable, and include notice of the person’s rights and the procedures to appeal the transfer decision. (d) If the person is denied a requested transfer to the New Hampshire hospital, the person may appeal the decision to the commissioner and be afforded a hearing in accordance with the procedures set forth in He-M 611.08 and He-C 200 within 5 business days after the transfer has been denied or suspended, pending bed availability. Source. #13255, eff 8-21-22

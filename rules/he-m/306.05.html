He-M 306.05 Request for Emergency Treatment Authorization

Effective 2026-06-26. Expires 2036-06-26.

He-M 306.05 Request for Emergency Treatment Authorization. A licensed practitioner shall submit, to the department of health and human service’s administrative appeals unit, a written request for emergency treatment authorization which shall establish the criteria enumerated in He-M 306.04 and which shall contain the following information: (a) A statement of facts demonstrating that a medical or psychiatric emergency exists, including the harm that would likely result from a delay in treatment; (b) A description of the patient’s admission status; (c) A description of the patient’s guardianship status; (d) A statement of facts demonstrating the patient’s lack of capacity to make an informed decision with respect to the medical or psychiatric treatment offered, including: (1) A description of the efforts that have been made to inform the patient of the nature, effects, and risks of the proposed treatment; and (2) A description of the treatment, if any, that the patient has agreed to accept and an explanation of why such treatment would not ameliorate the medical or psychiatric emergency; and (e) An explanation as to why a reasonable person would consent to the proposed treatment, based on its anticipated therapeutic benefit, the potentially significant risks, and the nature and severity of possible side effects. Source. #14637, eff 6-26-26, EXPIRES: 6-26-36

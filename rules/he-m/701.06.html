He-M 701.06 Appeals

Effective 2018-04-25. Earlier expiration 2018-04-22.

He-M 701.06 Appeals. An applicant or anyone authorized to act on behalf of an applicant may appeal any decision rendered under these rules in accordance with He-M 202 and He-M 204, rights protection procedures, and He-C 200, rules of practice and procedure.

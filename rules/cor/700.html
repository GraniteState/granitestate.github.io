Cor 700 PHYSICAL PLANT MANAGEMENT

CHAPTER Cor 700 PHYSICAL PLANT MANAGEMENT REVISION NOTE: Document #12892, effective 9-28-19, contained new Chapter Cor 700 titled “Physical Plant Management” by adopting Part Cor 701 titled “purpose and Applicability”, adopting Part Cor 702 titled “Definitions”, and readopting with amendment and renumbering existing rules Cor 301.01, Cor 301.02, Cor 301.03, and Cor 301.04 as, respectively, Cor 703.01, Cor 703.02, Cor 704.01, and Cor 703.03. Document #12892 replaces all prior filings for rules Cor 301.01 through Cor 301.04. The prior filings affecting these and other rules in Chapter Cor 300 are listed in Revision Note #1 and Revision Note #2 at the chapter heading for Chapter Cor 300.

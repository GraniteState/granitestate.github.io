Cor 412.01 Permission for Release of Information

Effective 2020-08-06.

Cor 412.01 Permission for Release of Information. Residents shall complete and sign a “Release of Information Form” (revised 5/2019) as referenced in Cor 412.03 prior to the release of information from their non-medical client records. A copy of the completed form shall be maintained in the client record. The release shall expire 6 months from the date of issue. Source. #13082, eff 8-6-20

Cor 305.04 Visitor Requirement to Follow Rules

Effective 2021-01-05.

Cor 305.04 Visitor Requirement to Follow Rules. (a) All visitors who visit, or go on, or cross the grounds of a facility or area under the control of the NHDOC shall be subject to the rules established in Cor 305. Failure to follow such rules shall subject the visitor to removal from the grounds, arrest, or prosecution. (b) Everyone on prison grounds or in NHDOC facilities, regardless of whether they are a resident, visitor, staff, or anyone defined in some other category, shall be subject to search without warning of their vehicles, possessions, and persons pursuant to Cor 306.

Env-Wt 515.01 Applicability

Effective 2025-09-25. Expires 2035-09-25.

Env-Wt 515.01 Applicability. (a) Subject to (b), below, the rules in this part shall apply to any person who wishes to: (1) Construct a boathouse; (2) Modify, repair, or replace a dug-in basin or boathouse in, over, or in the bank of, non-tidal waters; or (3) Modify, repair, or replace any structures regulated under RSA 482-A:26. (b) The rules in this part shall not apply to maintenance and repairs undertaken pursuant to a statutory exemption as clarified in Env-Wt 308.02.

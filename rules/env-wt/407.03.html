Env-Wt 407.03 Jurisdictional Area Size Thresholds; Measuring Watercourse Length

Effective 2020-10-23.

Env-Wt 407.03 Jurisdictional Area Size Thresholds; Measuring Watercourse Length. (a) Subject to (b), below, projects shall be classified based on size as shown in Table 407-1, below: Table 407-1: Classification Based on Size of Impact Type of Jurisdictional Area | Minimum | Minor | Major Other than watercourse | < 3,000 square feet (SF) | ≥ 3,000 SF to < 10,000 SF | ≥ 10,000 SF Watercourse | < 50 linear feet (LF) | ≥ 50 LF to < 200 LF | ≥ 200 LF (b) Projects shall not be classified based on Table 407-1 if the project: (1) Is subject to an adjustment under Env-Wt 407.02; (2) Qualifies for a PTE under Env-Wt 407.04; or (3) Qualifies for project-specific size criteria as identified in Env-Wt 407.04, Table 407-2. (c) Unless otherwise specified for a particular purpose, watercourse length measurements shall be made as follows: (1) For intermittent watercourses, the distance shall be measured along the thread of the channel; and (2) For perennial watercourses, the total distance shall be calculated by summing the lengths of the disturbance to the channel and the banks. Source. #12804, eff 12-15-19; amd by #13124, eff 10-23-20

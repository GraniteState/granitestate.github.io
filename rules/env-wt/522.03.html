Env-Wt 522.03 Application Requirements for Agricultural Projects

Effective 2019-12-15.

Env-Wt 522.03 Application Requirements for Agricultural Projects. The project-specific information required by Env-Wt 310.01(c)(1) or Env-Wt 311.03(b)(11), as applicable, for an agricultural project shall be as follows: (a) A conservation plan prepared by NRCS, a county conservation district or a plan of conservation practices for proposed wetland impacts prepared by a certified wetland scientist; (b) Aerial photography with site impact located; (c) NRCS soil survey reports for the project area; (d) A wetland delineation and impact plan prepared by an NRCS soil scientist or certified wetland scientist; (e) A sediment and erosion control plan; (f) A construction sequence detailing timing of the work proposed, including dewatering plans, construction of access routes, and temporary impacts; and (g) All of the information and documentation specified in Env-Wt 522.06(a). Source. #12805, eff 12-15-19

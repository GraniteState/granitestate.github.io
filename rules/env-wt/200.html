Env-Wt 200 HEARINGS, APPEALS, AND WAIVER REQUESTS

CHAPTER Env-Wt 200 HEARINGS, APPEALS, AND WAIVER REQUESTS REVISION NOTE #1: Pursuant to a rules reorganization plan approved by the Director of the Office of Legislative Services on September 7, 2005, Department rules in Chapter Wt 200 filed under Document #8341, effective 4-25-05, were editorially renumbered subsequently with subtitle Env-Wt denoting the wetlands program area. The title Wt was discontinued. REVISION NOTE #2: Document #12802, effective 12-15-19, readopted or readopted with amendment all of the rules in Chapter Env-Wt 200, and changed the chapter heading from “Rules of Practice and Procedure” to “Hearings, Appeals, and Waiver Requests.” Env-Wt 202.02, Env-Wt 203.01, Env-Wt 203.04, and Env-Wt 203.05 were new rules, which necessitated renumbering in Part Env-Wt 202 and Env-Wt 203 as indicated in the source notes. As practice and procedure rules the rules in Chapter Env-Wt 200 will not expire except pursuant to RSA 541-A:17, II.

Env-Wt 103.25 Jetty

Effective 2024-04-27.

Env-Wt 103.25 “Jetty” means a structure extending into a body of water that is designed to prevent shoaling of a channel and to direct and confine the stream or tidal flow. Jetties are generally built at the mouths of rivers or tidal inlets to maintain the depth of and stabilize a channel. Source.

Sw 4001.10 Sizzler Promotion

Effective 2008-05-31.

Sw 4001.10 Sizzler Promotion. (a) The sizzler promotion shall be a limited extension of the hot lotto game and shall be conducted during a period of time designated by the commission in a public announcement. (b) A qualifying play shall be any single hot lotto play for which the player shall pay an extra dollar for the sizzler option and which shall be recorded in the central computer system. (c) A hot lotto sizzler ticket shall have the caption “Sizzler: Yes” on the ticket. (d) A hot lotto sizzler that wins one of the 8 regular set prizes, as set forth in Table 3.1.1 shall be multiplied by 3. The jackpot shall not be a set prize and shall not be multiplied. (e) The hot lotto game group may change the sizzler multiplier number for special promotions, for a specified period of time, to be announced by the commission in a public announcement. (f) Except as provided in these rules, all hot lotto sizzler prizes awarded shall be paid as lump sum set prizes as set forth in Table 3.1.2, Expected Sizzler Prize Payout, below: TABLE 3.1.2 Expected Sizzler Prize Payout Match | Regular Prize Amount | Sizzler 3X 5 of first set and none of second set | $10,000.00 | $30,000 4 of first set plus 1 of second set | 500.00 | $1,500 4 of first set and none of second set | 50.00 | $150 3 of first set plus 1 of second set | 50.00 | $150 3 of first set and none of second set | 4.00 | $12 2 of first set plus 1 of second set | 4.00 | $12 1 of first set plus 1 of second set | 3.00 | $9 0 of first set plus 1 of second set | 2.00 | $6 (g) Pursuant to Sw 4001.03 (c), if the set prize amounts are paid pari-mutuel the sizzler prizes shall be a multiple of the new hot lotto prize amount. (h) Sizzler prizes valued at $100,000 or more shall be paid within 5 business days of the date of claim with the commission. Source. #9165, eff 5-31-08 APPENDIX

Sw 703.01 Quarterly Incentive

Effective 2012-05-23. Earlier expiration 2012-06-29.

Sw 703.01 Quarterly Incentive. Quarterly incentives shall be awarded as follows: (a) A sales rep whose quarterly instant ticket sales exceed the same quarter from the prior fiscal year, by the established sales goal or greater, shall receive $1000.00; (b) A sales rep whose quarterly instant ticket sales exceed the same quarter from the prior fiscal year’s sales, by the sales goal and an additional 60% or greater, shall receive $2000.00; and (c) A sales rep shall be eligible to receive 4 quarterly incentives per fiscal year. Source. #10035, INTERIM, eff 1-1-12, EXPIRES: 6-29-12; ss by #10132, eff 5-23-12

Par 201.02 Scope

Effective 2017-04-07. Earlier expiration 2008-07-09.

Par 201.02 Scope. (a) These rules shall apply to all hearings required by state law to be conducted by the board at which public comment shall be solicited under RSA 541-A:11. (b) If any requirement set forth by these rules conflicts with an applicable statute, such other authority shall control.

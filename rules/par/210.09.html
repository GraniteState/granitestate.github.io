Par 210.09 Decisions

Effective 2017-04-07. Earlier expiration 2008-07-09.

Par 210.09 Decisions. In accordance with RSA 651-A:3 II, a panel of 3 board members, including the presiding officer, shall make a decision after personally hearing the testimony in the case.

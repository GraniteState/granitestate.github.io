Par 203.08 Reconsideration Hearings

Effective 2017-04-07. Earlier expiration 2008-07-09.

Par 203.08 Reconsideration Hearings. The board shall hold a reconsideration hearing for any inmate previously approved for parole who receives a disciplinary write-up prior to release. At the reconsideration hearing the board shall determine the effect of the disciplinary upon the inmate’s release date. The standard setback shall be 60 days for a minor disciplinary and 90 days for a major disciplinary.

Par 202.01 Rulemaking Petitions

Effective 2017-04-07. Earlier expiration 2008-07-09.

Par 202.01 Rulemaking Petitions. (a) Any interested person may petition the board, for the adoption, amendment, or repeal of any board rule under RSA 541-A:4. (b) Within 30 days of the receipt of the completed petition required by Par 202.03, the board shall either: (1) Initiate rulemaking procedures in compliance with the petition and in accordance with RSA 541-A:3; or (2) Deny the petition in writing, stating the reasons for the denial. (c) The board shall initiate a requested procedure or deny a petition, based on whether: (1) The requested change is consistent with the duties of the board as contained in RSA 651-A; and (2) The requested change improves the efficiency of board procedures.

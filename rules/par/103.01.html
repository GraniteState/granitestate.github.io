Par 103.01 Jurisdiction

Effective 2017-04-07. Earlier expiration 1999-12-10.

Par 103.01 Jurisdiction. (a) Pursuant to RSA 651-A:4, I, the board has the sole authority to grant parole to a New Hampshire state prison inmate. (b) Pursuant to RSA 651-A:4, II, the board has legal custody of all persons released on parole until they receive their discharge or are recommitted to the prison. (c) Pursuant to RSA 651-A:18, the board has the sole authority to revoke the parole privilege of any person in its custody and recommit that person to the prison.

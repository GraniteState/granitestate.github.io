Par 303.03 Conduct of Medical Parole Hearings

Effective 2017-04-07. Earlier expiration 2016-09-06.

Par 303.03 Conduct of Medical Parole Hearings. The board shall ask the director of medical and forensic services or designee to attend the hearing and answer questions from board members. Source. #9255, eff 9-6-08, EXPIRED: 9-6-16 New. #12158, eff 4-7-17

Par 103.02 Rulemaking Authority

Effective 2017-04-07. Earlier expiration 1999-12-10.

Par 103.02 Rulemaking Authority. The board is required by RSA 651-A:4, III to adopt rules relative to the parole process, including: (a) The conduct of parole hearings; (b) Criteria used to evaluate prospective parolees; (c) Conditions for the conduct of parolees; and (d) Procedures for revocation of parole and procedures for medical parole.

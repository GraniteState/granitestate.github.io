Vet 200 RULES OF PRACTICE AND PROCEDURE

CHAPTER Vet 200 RULES OF PRACTICE AND PROCEDURE REVISION NOTE: Document #13936, effective 6-23-24, repealed Part Vet 201 through Part Vet 207 in Chapter Vet 200, titled “Rules of Procedure”, and renamed the chapter as “Rules of Practice and Procedure”. Document #13936 also adopted a new Part Vet 201 titled “Applicability and Waiver of Substantive Rules”, containing Vet 201.01 titled “Rules of Practice and Procedure” and Vet 201.02 titled “Waiver of Administrative Rules.” Document #13936 replaces all prior filings affecting the rules in the former Chapter Vet 200. The prior filings included the following documents. Italics indicate the rules were subject to expiration only pursuant to RSA 541-A:17, II: #1557, eff 4-1-80 #2294, eff 2-1-83 #2892, eff 10-26-84 #4430, eff 6-02-88 #5024, eff 12-4-90, EXPIRED: 12-4-96 #6450, INTERIM, eff 2-8-97 #6481, eff 3-29-97 #7418, eff 12-21-00, EXPIRED: 12-21-08 #9464-A, eff 4-23-09 (Vet 201-Vet 205) #9468-B, eff 4-23-09 (Vet 206), EXPIRED: 4-23-17 #12842, INTERIM, eff 8-1-19, EXPIRED: 1-18-20 (Vet 206) #13269, eff 9-29-21 (Vet 201-Vet 206) #13522, eff 1-19-23 (Vet 207) As practice and procedure rules, the rules in Document #13936 will not expire except pursuant to RSA 541-A:17, II.

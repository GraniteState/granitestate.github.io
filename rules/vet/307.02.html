Vet 307.02 Conditional License Duration

Effective 2025-11-23. Expires 2035-11-23.

Vet 307.02 Conditional License Duration. (a) Conditional licenses shall be valid for 2 years from the date of issuance, or until the licensee obtains ECFVG certification and receives a full license. (b) An ECFVG candidate, or a candidate for another certification program that meets the ECFVG or PAVE program minimum standards, who does not pass a CPE examination may apply to renew or reinstate their conditional license, however, a conditional license shall not be renewed or reinstated more than once. Source. #14397, eff 11-23-25, EXPIRES: 11-23-35

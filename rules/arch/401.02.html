Arch 401.02 Renewal of License

Effective 2026-03-24. Expires 2036-03-24.

Arch 401.02 Renewal of License. Any licensee wishing to renew a license shall submit: (a) The “Universal Application for License Renewal” required by Plc 308.05; (b) The fee required by Plc 1002.04; and (c) A completed “NH Architect Addendum to the Universal Application for License Renewal” that provides the information and documentation required by Arch 401.03.

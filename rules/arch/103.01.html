Arch 103.01 Duties and Responsibilities

Effective 2003-08-27. Earlier expiration 1995-04-13.

Arch 103.01 Duties and Responsibilities. The board shall administer the provisions of RSA 310-A:28-52 which include but are not limited to safeguarding property, to promoting public welfare and maintaining a high standard of integrity, skills and practice in the profession of architecture in this state.

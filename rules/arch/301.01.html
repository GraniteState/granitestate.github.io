Arch 301.01 Application Process

Effective 2026-06-02. Expires 2036-06-02.

Arch 301.01 Application Process. (a) Each person wishing to become licensed as an architect shall complete and submit the “Universal Application for Initial License” as required by Plc 304.01(a)-(c), and the application fee as specified in Plc 1002.04. (b) If the application for licensure is denied, the applicant shall be provided an opportunity to request a hearing pursuant to Plc 304.10.

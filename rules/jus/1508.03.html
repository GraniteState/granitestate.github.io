Jus 1508.03 Content of Written Demand

Effective 2004-05-08.

Jus 1508.03 Content of Written Demand. The written demand shall include the following: (a) A statement indicating that the person making the written demand is authorized to make such a demand, pursuant to a delegation of authority by the attorney general or county attorney under RSA 7:6-b, III; (b) A statement that the person making the written demand has reasonable grounds for belief that the service being furnished to the targeted person or location by the communications common carrier has been, is being, or may be used for an unlawful purpose; (c) A list of the specific information being sought from the communications common carrier, as designated in RSA 7:6-b, I(a) - (f); (d) A statement indicating that the written demand shall constitute an administrative subpoena for purposes of determining compliance with federal law; and (e) A statement indicating to whom the requested information shall be provided and the requested format in which the information shall be furnished. Source. #8082, eff 5-8-04

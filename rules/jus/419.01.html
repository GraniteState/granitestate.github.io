Jus 419.01 Definitions

Effective 2024-12-27.

Jus 419.01 Definitions. For purposes of RSA 292:6-a, the following terms shall have the following meanings: (a) “Of the same immediate family” means a spouse, parent, grandparent, child, grandchild, sibling, or half-sibling, including a stepparent, step grandparent, stepchild, step grandchild, stepsibling, and step half-sibling. (b) “Related by blood or marriage” means, in addition to any member of the same immediate family, as that term is defined in Jus 419.01(a), the following: i. An uncle, aunt, nephew, niece, or first cousin; ii. A step uncle, step aunt, step nephew, step niece, or first step cousin; and iii. A parent-in-law, child-in-law, sibling-in-law, half-sibling-in-law, grandparent-in-law, grandchild-in-law, uncle-in-law, aunt-in-law, nephew-in-law, niece-in-law, or first cousin-in-law. Source. #14162, eff 12-27-24

Jus 604.05 Verification of Employment and Lost Wages Form

Effective 2019-12-09. Earlier expiration 2017-09-25.

Jus 604.05 Verification of Employment and Lost Wages Form (a) If a claimant seeks compensation for lost wages, the victims’ assistance unit shall request that the victim’s employer complete and submit a “Verification of Employment and Lost Wages Form,” revised 8/23/2019. (b) In addition to the form, the victim’s employer shall provide the victims’ assistance unit with payroll information for the month preceding the date of the crime and one month after the crime. Source. #4864, eff 7-13-90; ss by #6052 eff 7-1-95, EXPIRED: 7-1-03 New. #9553, eff 9-25-09, EXPIRED: 9-25-17 New. #12938, eff 12-9-19 (formerly Jus 604.06)

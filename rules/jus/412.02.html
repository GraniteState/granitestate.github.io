Jus 412.02 Form NHCT-50 Authorization for Electronic Filing by Agent

Effective 2024-12-27.

Jus 412.02 Form NHCT-50 Authorization for Electronic Filing by Agent. (a) Form NHCT-50 shall be signed in ink on behalf of a charitable trust by one of the following: (1) A trustee if the charitable trust is an express trust; (2) The presiding officer or treasurer of the governing board; or (3) If the organization is not New Hampshire-based, the executive director of the organization. (b) Form NHCT-50 shall be signed in ink on behalf of fundraising counsel or a paid solicitor by an officer of the fundraising counsel or paid solicitor. Source. #13455, eff 10-7-22; ss by #14162, eff 12-27-24

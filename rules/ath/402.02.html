Ath 402.02 License Renewal Application Packet

Effective 2025-05-16. Expires 2035-05-16.

Ath 402.02 License Renewal Application Packet. Applicants for license renewal shall submit to the OPLC: (a) The information required by Plc 308.06 on the “Universal Application for License Renewal”, dated April 2024, as described in Plc 308.05, that contains the information specified in Plc 308.06, the information and documentation required in Plc 308.07, and that is signed and attested to as required in Plc 308.08; (b) An attestation or proof of having met applicable continuing competence requirements, as required by applicable law; (c) A copy of a certificate issued by the Board of Certification (BOC) verifying certification; and (d) The application-related fees for renewal required by Plc 1002.06, except that applicants applying for facilitated licensure as active military or a military spouse shall not pay the fee. Source. #9099, eff 3-1-08; ss by #10921, eff 8-29-15; ss by #13307, eff 12-17-21 (formerly Ath 402.03) (see Revision Note at chapter heading for Ath 400); ss by #14247, eff 5-16-25, EXPIRES: 5-16-35

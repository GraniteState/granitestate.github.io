Ath 405.01 Procedures for, and Effect of, Voluntary Surrender of License

Effective 2025-05-16. Expires 2035-05-16.

Ath 405.01 Procedures for, and Effect of, Voluntary Surrender of License. Pursuant to RSA 310:4, II (d) and RSA 310:9, the procedures for, and effect of, the voluntary surrender of a license shall be governed by Plc 205. Source. #10228 (formerly Ath 218.01); ss by #10921, eff 8-29-15; ss by #14247, eff 5-16-25, EXPIRES: 5-16-35

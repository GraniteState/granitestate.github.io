En 1005.01 Applicable Standards

Effective 2026-09-01. Expires 2036-09-01.

En 1005.01 Applicable Standards. The applicant and DER shall comply with the utility’s TIR as defined in En 1002.53 and the standards identified in this section, as applicable, unless waived by the utility. If the utility’s published technical interconnection standards specify a more recent industry standard, then the industry standard cited in the utility’s technical interconnection standards shall prevail. Applicable standards include the following: (a) IEEE Std 1547-2018, “IEEE Standard for Interconnection and Interoperability of Distributed Energy Resources with Associated Electric Power Systems Interfaces” dated 4/6/2018 as available in Appendix B; (b) IEEE Std 1547.1™-2020, “IEEE Standard Conformance Test Procedures for Equipment Interconnecting Distributed Energy Resources with Electric Power Systems and Associated Interfaces” dated 5/21/20 as available in Appendix B; (c) ANSI C84.1-2020, “Electric Power Systems and Equipment-Voltage Ratings (60 Hertz)” dated 3/10/2020 as available in Appendix B; (d) IEEE Std 1547.2™-2023, “IEEE Application Guide for IEEE Std 1547™-2018, IEEE Standard for Interconnection and Interoperability of Distributed Energy Resources with Associated Electric Power Systems Interfaces” dated 5/20/2024 as available in Appendix B; (e) IEEE Std 1547.3™-2023, “IEEE Guide for Cybersecurity of Distributed Energy Resources Interconnected with Electric Power Systems” dated 12/11/2023 as available in Appendix B; (f) IEEE Std 1547.7™-2013, “IEEE Guide for Conducting Distribution Impact Studies for Distributed Resource Interconnection” dated 2/28/2014 as available in Appendix B; (g) IEEE Std 519™-2022, “IEEE Standard for Harmonic Control in Electric Power Systems” dated 8/5/2022 as available in Appendix B; (h) IEEE Std 1453™-2022, “IEEE Standard for Measurement and Limits of Voltage Fluctuations and Associated Light Flicker on AC Power Systems” dated 2/27/2023 as available in Appendix B; (i) IEEE Std C37.90.2™-2024, “IEEE Standard for Relays, Relay Systems, and Control Devices used for Protection and Control of Electric Power Apparatus-Radiated Electromagnetic Interference Withstand Capability Requirements and Tests” dated 1/31/2025 as available in Appendix B; (j) IEEE C37.95™-2014, “IEEE Guide for Protective Relaying of Utility-Consumer Interconnections” dated 5/5/2014 as available in Appendix B; (k) IEEE Std C50.13™-2014, “IEEE Standard for Cylindrical-Rotor 50 Hz and 60 Hz Synchronous Generators Rated 10 MVA and Above” dated 5/9/2014 as available in Appendix B; (l) IEEE Std C62.92.1™-2016, “IEEE Guide for the Application of Neutral Grounding in Electric Utility Systems-Part I: Introduction” dated 3/31/2017 as available in Appendix B; (m) IEEE Std C62.92.2™-2017, “IEEE Guide for the Application of Neutral Grounding in Electric Utility Systems, Part II-Synchronous Generator Systems” dated 5/19/2017 as available in Appendix B; (n) IEEE Std C62.92.6™-2017, “IEEE Guide for Application of Neutral Grounding in Electrical Utility Systems, Part VI-Systems Supplied by Current-Regulated Sources” dated 12/22/2017 as available in Appendix B; (o) UL 1741, “Inverters, Converters, Controllers and Interconnection System Equipment for Use With Distributed Energy Resources” dated 4/22/2025 as available in Appendix B; (p) Certification Requirement Decision for Power Control Systems, March 8, 2019, Inverters, Converters, Controllers and Interconnection System Equipment for Use With Distributed Energy Resources found in UL 1741, “Inverters, Converters, Controllers and Interconnection System Equipment for Use With Distributed Energy Resources” dated 4/22/2025 as available in Appendix B; and (q) UL 3141 “UL LLC Outline of Investigation for Power Control Systems” dated 10/9/2024 as available in Appendix B. Source. #14657, eff 9-1-26, EXPIRES: 9-1-36

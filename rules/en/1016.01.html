En 1016.01 Informal Dispute Resolution Efforts Required

Effective 2026-09-01. Expires 2036-09-01.

En 1016.01 Informal Dispute Resolution Efforts Required. (a) In the event of a dispute regarding any issue arising under this chapter, the utility and the applicant shall make a good faith effort to first resolve the dispute through informal means. (b) If the efforts of informal dispute resolution are unsuccessful, the applicant may make a formal complaint with the department pursuant to RSA 365:1-4. (c) Informal dispute resolution efforts shall not suspend, extend, or delay any timeframes or deadlines applicable to the application(s) at issue, except upon mutual agreement by the utility and the applicant(s). (d) All timelines and deadlines shall remain in place and enforceable, including all consequences of violating the applicable timeline or deadline, which may be addressed in the informal dispute resolution process or brought as a formal complaint to the department. (e) If a timeline or deadline is part of a good-faith dispute or will be materially impacted as a consequence of a good-faith dispute, the utility and the applicant shall make their best-faith efforts to accommodate modifications to the relevant timelines or deadlines. (f) No application shall be deemed withdrawn or removed from the queue solely because of a missed deadline that is associated with or results from a good-faith dispute undergoing the process of this section. (g) An applicant’s execution of an IA or payment for system modifications in accordance with applicable deadlines shall not preclude the applicant from disputing any system modification or associated cost required under the IA.

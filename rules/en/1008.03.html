En 1008.03 Required Screens

Effective 2026-09-01. Expires 2036-09-01.

En 1008.03 Required Screens. The utility shall evaluate the application using the following screens: (a) For minimum load screen for interconnection of a DER to a radial distribution circuit, the DER’s export capacity, aggregated with the export capacity of all other DERs on a line section, shall not exceed 100 percent of the line section’s relevant minimum load as most recently measured at the substation or calculated for the line section. If the utility does not have relevant minimum load data, 25 percent of the peak feeder load shall be used instead; (b) For inadvertent export screen for interconnection of a proposed DER that can introduce inadvertent export, where the nameplate rating of the DER minus the export capacity is greater than 250 kW, the power change equal to the nameplate rating minus the export capacity, the change in voltage at the point on the medium voltage (primary) level nearest the POI shall not exceed 3 percent. Voltage change will be estimated applying the following formula: Formula | (RSOURCE × ∆𝑷) – (XSOURCE × ∆𝑸) V2 Where: ∆𝑷 = (DER apparent power Nameplate Rating – Export Capacity) × PF, ∆𝑸 = (DER apparent power Nameplate Rating – Export Capacity) × , RSOURCE is the grid resistance, XSOURCE is the grid reactance, V is the grid voltage, PF is the power factor (c) For fault current contribution screen, the DER, aggregated with other generation on the distribution circuit, shall not contribute more than 10 percent to the distribution circuit’s maximum fault current at the point on the primary nearest the proposed POI; (d) For short circuit interrupting capability screen, the DER, aggregated with other generation on the distribution circuit, shall not cause any distribution protective devices and equipment, including but not limited to substation breakers, fuse cutouts, and line reclosers, or utility customer equipment on the system, to exceed 90 percent of the short circuit interrupting capability, nor is the interconnection proposed for a circuit that already exceeds 90 percent of the short circuit interrupting capability; (e) The line configuration screen shall include a review of the type of electrical service provided to the interconnecting customer, including line configuration and the transformer connection to limit the potential for creating over-voltages on the utility’s EDS due to a loss of ground during the operating time of any anti-islanding function. The DER shall comply with the applicable type of interconnection, based on Table 1008 below: Table 1008: Line Configuration for DER Interconnection Primary Distribution Line Configuration | Type of Interconnection to Primary Distribution Line | Results/Criteria 3-phase, 3-wire | 3-phase or single-phase, phase-to-phase | Pass Screen 3-phase, 4-wire | 3-phase or single-phase, line-to-neutral | Pass Screen (f) For shared secondary transformer screens, if the facility is to be interconnected on a single-phase shared secondary, then the aggregate export capacity on the shared secondary, including the facility’s export capacity, shall not exceed 25 kVA. The utility shall consider voltage control capabilities of the facility to determine if such controls can alleviate the voltage concern identified by this screen; (g) For service imbalance screens, if the DER is single-phase and is to be interconnected on a transformer center tap neutral of a 240-volt service, its addition shall not create an imbalance between the 2 sides of the 240-volt service of more than 20 percent of nameplate rating of the service transformer; (h) For transient stability screens, the DER’s nameplate rating, in aggregate with other generation interconnected to the distribution low-voltage side of the substation transformer feeding the distribution circuit where the DER proposes to interconnect shall not: (1) Exceed 10 MW in an area where there are known or posted transient stability limitations to generating units located in the general electrical vicinity for example 3 or 4 transmission voltage level busses from the POI; and (2) Have interdependencies, known to the utility, with earlier queued interconnection requests, that would necessitate further study; (i) For stiffness screens, the ratio of the utility short circuit current at the PCC to the proposed DER’s nameplate rated current shall not be less than 25; (j) For transmission screens, the DER’s PCC shall not be on a transmission line; (k) For network screens for interconnection of a DER within a spot network or area network, the aggregate nameplate rating including the DER’s nameplate rating shall not exceed 50 percent of the spot network or area network’s relevant minimum load. Alternately, if the utility does not have minimum load data for the spot or area network, the utility may select any of the following methods to determine anticipated minimum load: (1) Five percent of the spot network or area network’s maximum load in the previous year; (2) The applicant’s good faith estimate, if provided; or (3) The utility’s estimate if provided in writing to the applicant along with the reasons why the utility considered the other methods to estimate minimum load inadequate; and (l) For effective grounding screens, the proposed facility in aggregation with other generation in the vicinity of the proposed POI shall not exceed the utility’s threshold for requiring effective grounding established in the utility’s current TIR.

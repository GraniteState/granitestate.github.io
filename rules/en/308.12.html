En 308.12 Quarterly Reporting of Electric Utility Reliability Measures

Effective 2024-10-22. Expires 2034-10-22.

En 308.12 Quarterly Reporting of Electric Utility Reliability Measures. Each utility shall on a quarterly basis complete a report of electric utility reliability measures on Form E-38 dated 10/2024, available at the department’s website at www.energy.nh.gov, and file one signed electronic copy with the department.

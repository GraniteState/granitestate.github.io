En 512.10 Department Action

Effective 2023-10-25.

En 512.10 Department Action. (a) The NOV shall become final and the department shall act upon it unless the respondent requests a hearing pursuant to En 512.09(b). (b) The department shall forward hearing requests pursuant to En 512.09(b) to the commission.

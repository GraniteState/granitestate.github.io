En 511.01 Compliance with Federal Standards Required

Effective 2023-10-25.

En 511.01 Compliance with Federal Standards Required. (a) All LPG operators and landfill gas operators shall comply with those pipeline safety regulations established by the United States Department of Transportation as set forth in 49 C.F.R. Parts 191 and 192. (b) All LPG operators shall comply with the LP Gas Code (NFPA 58) as referenced by 49 C.F.R. § 192.7. (c) LPG operators shall employ the guidelines contained in the Training Guide for Operators of Small LP Gas Systems, written by the United States Department of Transportation and the National Association of Regulatory Utility Commissioners, printed April 2001, as found in Appendix B. Source. #13796, eff 10-25-23

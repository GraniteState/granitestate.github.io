En 1011.07 Detailed Study

Effective 2026-09-01. Expires 2036-09-01.

En 1011.07 Detailed Study. (a) If the utility determines that necessary modifications to the utility’s EDS are substantial, the SIS report shall include a high-level estimate of the system modification costs and an estimate of the cost of an optional detailed study. The optional detailed study shall provide granular costs of any EDS modifications necessary to interconnect the applicant’s proposed facility. (b) If the applicant elects to participate in a group study pursuant to En 1012, the utility shall waive the detailed study. (c) If the utility determines that EDS modifications required to accommodate the facility are not substantial, the SIS shall identify the scope and cost of the EDS modifications defined in the SIS results, and no detailed study shall be required. (d) If the applicant elects to proceed with the detailed study, within 10 business days of the issuance of the SIS report, the utility shall provide a detailed study agreement, including a good faith estimate of the cost and time to complete the detailed study. (e) The utility shall execute the detailed study once the applicant completes the detailed study agreement and submits payment. The detailed study shall include a granular list of necessary EDS modifications, estimated milestones for completion of modifications, and a non-binding, itemized cost estimate detailing equipment, labor, operation and maintenance, contingencies, and other costs. (f) The utility shall complete the detailed study within 30 business days of execution of the detailed study agreement and receipt of study payment if no substation upgrades are required. If substation upgrades are required, the timeline for detailed study shall be established by mutual agreement. (g) After the results of the detailed study are delivered, if an applicant requests a meeting to discuss the results within 10 business days, the utility shall conduct a results meeting with the applicant within 15 business days of providing the results to the applicant. The results meeting shall take place in person, by telephone, or virtually. The utility and the applicant shall bring to the results meeting personnel, including system engineers, and other resources as might be reasonably required to accomplish the purpose of the meeting. (h) Within 10 business days after the results meeting or the applicant’s decision to waive the results meeting, the applicant shall notify the utility of its decision to proceed with the IA, if applicable, or request a modification pursuant to En 1004.04, or withdraw the application. Failure of the applicant to notify the utility of its decision or request a modification shall result in the withdrawal of the application. Source. #14657, eff 9-1-26, EXPIRES: 9-1-36

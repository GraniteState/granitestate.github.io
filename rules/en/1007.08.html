En 1007.08 Final Utility Review and Metering

Effective 2026-09-01. Expires 2036-09-01.

En 1007.08 Final Utility Review and Metering. (a) If the utility determines that a review of the facility or changes to the applicant’s meter are necessary, the utility shall complete this work within 10 business days of receiving the complete facility as-built documentation from the applicant. If the facility passes the review, the utility shall provide written notice of the passage within 3 business days. (b) If the facility initially fails a utility review, the utility shall offer to reinspect the facility at the applicant’s expense at a time mutually agreeable to both the utility and the applicant. If the utility determines that the facility fails the review, including the discovery of unapproved changes to the facility, the utility shall provide the applicant with a written explanation detailing the reasons for the failure and any standards violated within 3 business days. (c) If the utility determines no inspection or changes to the applicant’s meter are necessary, it shall notify the applicant within 3 business days of receiving the notice of the completion of construction. Source. #14657, eff 9-1-26, EXPIRES: 9-1-36

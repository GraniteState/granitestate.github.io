En 1802.06 “Residential building,” as referenced in RSA 155-A New Hampshire building code, means

Effective 2025-09-22. Expires 2035-09-22.

En 1802.06 “Residential building,” as referenced in RSA 155-A New Hampshire building code, means: (a) Any detached one- or 2-family dwelling; (b) Any other dwelling as defined and prescribed by the New Hampshire building code. Source. #14381, eff 9-22-25, EXPIRES: 9-22-35

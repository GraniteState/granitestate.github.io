En 1501.02 Applicability

Effective 2024-04-26.

En 1501.02 Applicability. En 1500 shall apply to: (a) Public utilities within the meaning of RSA 362:2; (b) Rural electric cooperatives for which a certificate of deregulation is on file pursuant to RSA 301:57; (c) Municipal corporations and county corporations regardless of whether operating within their corporate limits; and (d) Every other corporation or other entity, and every individual desiring to cross any public water or land for any purpose set forth in RSA 371:17. Source. #13939, eff 4-26-24

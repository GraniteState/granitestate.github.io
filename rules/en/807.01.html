En 807.01 Investigation of Complaints

Effective 2024-08-21.

En 807.01 Investigation of Complaints. (a) The notification center shall institute procedures to receive and resolve complaints of excavators, operators, owners of underground facilities, members, and the general public, but nothing contained herein precludes any private cause of action for damages, including for wanton, intentional, or other unlawful conduct. (b) Owners and operators of underground facilities shall institute procedures to receive and resolve complaints of excavators and the general public. (c) The department staff shall consider and decide unresolved complaints pursuant to the procedures established in En 807.02 through 807.07. (d) An unresolved dispute relating to En 800 which is appealed in writing shall be treated as a request for an adjudicatory proceeding pursuant to the procedures established in En 200. Source. #14062, eff 8-21-24

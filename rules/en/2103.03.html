En 2103.03 Annual Certifications

Effective 2026-02-26. Expires 2036-02-26.

En 2103.03 Annual Certifications. No later than July 1 of each year subsequent to filing its initial compliance plan, a utility shall: (a) Certify that its compliance plan continues to meet the requirements of En 2103, Puc 2100, and RSA 366; and (b) If necessary, file an updated compliance plan which meets the requirements of En 2103.04.

En 1011.04 Affected System Operator

Effective 2026-09-01. Expires 2036-09-01.

En 1011.04 Affected System Operator. (a) The utility shall coordinate with the applicant, ASO, and any affected system owners if an ASO study is required. (b) The utility shall not be responsible for the timing of an ASO study. (c) Within 10 business days following receipt of complete information from the ASO, the utility shall provide the applicant with information related to the plan for conducting the ASO study, including the responsibilities of each party, the scope of the study, the expected timeline for completion, and the estimated cost. (d) The applicant shall be directly responsible to the ASO and affected system owner for all costs of any additional studies required to evaluate the impact of the interconnection on the affected systems. (e) The applicant shall be directly responsible for any resulting affected system costs, including, without limitation, modifications to the electric power system of the ASO or affected system owner and operation and maintenance costs. (f) The utility may, in its sole discretion, elect to include the additional ASO study costs and affected system costs in the utility’s cost estimates. If the utility elects to include these costs in its estimates, the utility shall specify the ASO study costs and the affected system costs in its estimates. The applicant shall pay the utility for those costs, and the utility shall pay those costs to the ASO and affected system owner. (g) The applicant shall be responsible for all actual affected system costs necessitated as a result of the ASO requirements, none of which shall be subject to any cost caps or limitations under this chapter. (h) Within 5 business days of notice from the ASO that additional information or technical data about the facility is required for the ASO study, the utility shall notify the applicant. The applicant shall provide the requested information to the utility or request additional time from the utility within 15 business days of the notification from the utility. If the applicant fails to provide the requested information within the allowed time, the applicant shall have the opportunity to cure the failure ahead of the application being removed from its queue position pursuant to the required notification period established in 1014.02(c). If the applicant does not cure the failure within the required notification period, the application shall be withdrawn unless the applicant and the utility mutually agree to extend the deadline. (i) Within 5 business days of notice from the ASO of any ASO study costs, the utility shall notify the applicant. The applicant shall provide payment within 15 business days of the notification from the utility. If the applicant fails to provide payment within the allowed time, the applicant shall have the opportunity to cure the failure ahead of the application being removed from its queue position pursuant to the required notification period established in 1014.02(c). If the applicant does not cure the failure within the required notification period, the application shall be withdrawn unless the applicant and the utility mutually agree to extend the deadline.

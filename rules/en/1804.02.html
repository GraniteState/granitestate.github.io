En 1804.02 Department Responsibilities

Effective 2025-09-22. Expires 2035-09-22.

En 1804.02 Department Responsibilities. The department shall review the application and shall: (a) Determine whether the applicant has demonstrated, pursuant to RSA 155-A, that the design of the residential building complies with the energy code; and (b) If the department determines compliance with the energy code, the department shall issue a verification letter to the municipality pursuant to RSA 155-A:10-a with a copy to the applicant. Source. #14381, eff 9-22-25, EXPIRES: 9-22-35

En 1011.08 Interconnection Agreement

Effective 2026-09-01. Expires 2036-09-01.

En 1011.08 Interconnection Agreement (a) Within 15 business days of completion of the last study or the meeting to discuss study results, whichever is later, the utility shall provide the applicant with a signed IA using the current standard agreement, which shall incorporate the milestones for completing EDS modifications, if any, from the detailed study. The IA shall include a good faith, non-binding estimate of all costs associated with interconnection of the applicant’s proposed facility to the utility EDS including an itemized quote including contingencies. (b) Within 15 business days of the receipt of the IA, the applicant shall either execute or provide comments on the IA and return it to the utility. The utility shall have 15 business days to respond to any applicant IA comments. Upon the applicant’s receipt of the utility’s responses, the applicant and utility shall attempt to resolve any issues identified by the applicant. The applicant shall either execute the IA within 15 business days of the receipt of comments by the utility, or the applicant may initiate informal dispute resolution efforts pursuant to En 1016, or withdraw its IA at any time. (c) Payment of 25 percent of the utility’s good faith, non-binding estimate shall be due within 45 business days of applicant signature of the IA with the remaining 75 percent due within 90 business days of applicant signature of the IA. Nothing in this provision shall prevent the parties from establishing mutually agreed upon payment terms or the applicant making payment sooner than 45 business days to maintain queue position. (d) If the applicant’s system modification costs exceed $500,000 or the estimated construction schedule for system modifications exceeds 2 years from the signed IA, the applicant shall be eligible for a payment plan. This plan shall be included with the IA and shall include a payment and construction schedule with milestones for both the utility and the applicant that correlates to the schedule for payments with design, procurement, and construction of the EDS modifications. (e) If the applicant fails to pay the utility within the timelines defined in (c) and (d) above in this section, the IA shall be deemed withdrawn, subject to the notice requirement of En 1014.02. (f) Upon execution of the IA, the applicant shall notify the utility of the anticipated date the facility shall commence operations. Unless the utility agrees to a later date or requires more time for necessary modifications to its EDS, the applicant shall identify an anticipated in-service date that is within 24 months of the applicant’s execution of the IA. The utility and the applicant may mutually agree to an extension of this time if needed beyond the 24-month time period. (g) The applicant shall notify the utility if there is any change in the anticipated in-service date of interconnected operations of the facility. (h) The IA milestone schedule for system modifications shall commence upon the utility’s receipt of the applicant’s initial 25 percent payment. The utility shall make its best efforts to adhere to the IA milestone schedule provided in the IA or detailed study. Source. #14657, eff 9-1-26, EXPIRES: 9-1-36

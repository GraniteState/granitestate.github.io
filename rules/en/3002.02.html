En 3002.02 Aggregate

Effective 2026-05-21. Expires 2036-05-21.

En 3002.02 “Aggregate” means to combine the natural gas loads of 2 or more customers or a single customer with multiple meters or multiple locations. Source. #14593, eff 5-21-26, EXPIRES: 5-21-36

En 1004.04 Modifications to a Proposed Facility or an Existing DER

Effective 2026-09-01. Expires 2036-09-01.

En 1004.04 Modifications to a Proposed Facility or an Existing DER. (a) At any time after an application is deemed complete, including after the receipt of fast track, supplemental review, group study, SIS, or detailed study results, the applicant or the utility may identify modifications to the proposed facility. Existing DERs may also propose modifications. The applicant for a proposed facility or interconnecting customer, for an existing DER, shall submit to the utility, in writing according to each utility’s processes, all proposed modifications to the facility. The utility shall not unilaterally apply any modification to an application or facility. (b) Within 10 business days of receipt of a proposed modification, the utility shall notify the applicant whether the proposed modification constitutes an exempt modification, member impact modification, or material modification as outlined below: (1) If the proposed modification is determined by the utility to be an exempt modification, then the utility shall notify the applicant in writing that the modification has been accepted and that the applicant shall retain its eligibility for interconnection, including its place in the queue; (2) If the application proposed to be modified is part of a group study, and the proposed modification is determined to be a member impact modification, written approval shall be required from all other applicants in the group and the utility as follows: a. The applicant shall submit written approval from all other applicants in the group within 10 business days of notification from the utility that the modification is a member impact modification; b. Within 20 business days of receipt of written authorization from all other applicants in the group, the utility shall provide the applicant with estimated costs, timelines, and requirements for revising the group study process; c. Within 10 business days of notification from the utility related to estimated costs, timelines, and requirements for revising the group study process, the applicant shall submit written approval from all other applicants in the group and pay the utility pursuant to the term of the group study agreement. If the applicant does not provide such approval and payment, the proposed modification shall be deemed withdrawn; d. Within the timeline specified in (b)(2)b. above in this section, the utility shall provide the applicant with a description of the utility’s analysis, conclusions, and the reasoning supporting those conclusions; and e. Within 10 business days of receipt of the results from the updated group study, the applicant shall notify the utility of its election to proceed with the modification and provide written approval of the modification from all other applicants in the group. If the applicant does not provide its written approval, the proposed modification, shall be deemed withdrawn; (3) If the proposed modification is determined to be a material modification, the utility shall notify the applicant in writing that the applicant shall provide the utility with its decision in writing by either withdrawing the proposed modification, or by notifying the utility that it wishes to proceed with the modification; (4) The applicant shall provide its decision within 10 business days after the material modification determination notification, which shall note the due date for a response from the applicant. If the applicant does not provide its decision by the date provided in the notification, the proposed modification shall be deemed withdrawn by the applicant, the application shall remain unchanged and retain its queue position; and (5) If the applicant notifies the utility that it wishes to proceed with the modification within the timeframe indicated in (b)(4) above in this section then the utility shall add the modification to the application and place the application at the end of the queue. (c) Any change to the application or proposed facility not previously agreed upon by both the applicant and the utility shall be prohibited and shall result in the application being eligible for removal from the queue. (d) Each utility shall provide a process for interconnecting customers to propose changes to existing DERs. Applications for modifications to existing DERs, whether changes to the existing facility itself or the addition of an energy storage device, shall follow this process. Proposed changes that are determined to be an exempt modification shall not require processing through the queue. Source. #14657, eff 9-1-26, EXPIRES: 9-1-36

En 1011.03 Scoping Meeting

Effective 2026-09-01. Expires 2036-09-01.

En 1011.03 Scoping Meeting. (a) The utility shall conduct an initial review that includes a scoping meeting with the applicant within 15 business days of determining that an application is complete. The purpose of the scoping meeting shall be to discuss the application, the RPA, and review existing studies relevant to the application. (b) The scoping meeting shall take place in person, by telephone, or electronically by a means mutually agreeable to the utility and applicant. (c) At the scoping meeting, the utility shall provide pertinent information such as: (1) The available fault current at the proposed location; (2) The existing peak loading on the lines in the general vicinity of the proposed facility; and (3) The configuration of the distribution line at the proposed POI, and whether the proposed facility may be required to participate in an ASO study. (d) The utility and the applicant shall bring to the meeting personnel, including system engineers, and other resources as might be reasonably required to accomplish the purpose of the meeting. (e) By mutual agreement of the parties, the scoping meeting, SIS, or detailed study may be waived. Source. #14657, eff 9-1-26, EXPIRES: 9-1-36

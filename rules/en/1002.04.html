En 1002.04 Application

Effective 2026-09-01. Expires 2036-09-01.

En 1002.04 “Application” means a request to have a proposed facility interconnect to a utility EDS, or a request to modify an interconnected facility. Source. #14657, eff 9-1-26, EXPIRES: 9-1-36

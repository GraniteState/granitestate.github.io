En 1002.20 Exempt modification

Effective 2026-09-01. Expires 2036-09-01.

En 1002.20 “Exempt modification” means a modification to an application or facility that does not increase the export capacity of the facility, relocate the point of interconnection (POI), or otherwise qualify as a material modification. These include, but are not limited to: (a) A change of ownership of a facility; (b) A change or replacement of generating equipment, including inverters and transformers, that is a like-kind substitution in size, ratings, impedances, efficiencies, or capabilities of the equipment; (c) Replacement of existing inverters with inverters that conform to more recent standards after the state has adopted their use, as long as the export capacity does not increase; (d) Any change to equipment on the direct current (DC) side of the applicant’s equipment, including DC coupled storage; (e) Any change to relays or reclosers that do not alter trip settings; (f) Modification to the grounding configuration that remains in compliance with utility standards; (g) A change where the inverter dynamic simulation performance is equivalent to the existing inverter; and (h) A reduction in the size of the facility. Source. #14657, eff 9-1-26, EXPIRES: 9-1-36

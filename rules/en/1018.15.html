En 1018.15 Energy Storage

Effective 2026-09-01. Expires 2036-09-01.

En 1018.15 Energy Storage. (a) If the applicant is seeking approval for an energy storage device, that is co-located with load, other than station service load, pursuant to an application governed by this chapter, the utility shall not require a separate and additional request for new service for the interconnection of new or modified load. The utility shall review the co-located energy storage device pursuant to the processes in this chapter and not pursuant to En 300. (b) If the applicant is seeking approval for an energy storage device not co-located with load, other than station service load, the utility shall apply the interconnection requirements established in the utility TIR. (c) Energy storage devices shall be screened or studied according to the proposed facility’s design and operating characteristics. If the facility limits export or import pursuant to En 1013, screening or study of the device shall use export capacity or import capacity instead of nameplate rating, except when assessing fault current contribution.

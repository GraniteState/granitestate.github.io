Env-Wr 506.01 Notification Test

Effective 2024-05-24.

Env-Wr 506.01 Notification Test. (a) Prior to conducting a test of the notification flowchart, the owner shall review the EAP and if any changes are needed, the owner should proceed in accordance with Env-Wr 506.02 before initiating the test. (b) The owner shall conduct a test of the notification flowchart within one month of approval of the EAP and every 2 years thereafter for a high hazard dam and every 4 years thereafter for a significant hazard dam. (c) The test shall consist of the owner making the primary contact as shown on the flowchart and indicating that a required test of the EAP is being conducted. (d) Within 7 days of conducting the test, the owner shall either: (1) Notify the department after verifying that a successful test occurred by contacting the final contact in each flowline of the notification flowchart. The portion of the notification flowchart with calls initiated by the New Hampshire state police need not be included in the verification process; or (2) Notify the department that the test was unsuccessful and within 30 days of the notification, determine where problems occurred, modify the notification flowchart and other portions of the EAP, as necessary, distribute updated material to all EAP holders, and repeat test. Following completion, notify the department pursuant to (d)(1), above. Source. #8414, eff 8-20-05; ss by #10510, eff 1-22-14; ss by #13980, eff 5-24-24 (formerly Env-Wr 507.01)

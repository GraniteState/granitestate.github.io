Env-Wr 101 DEFINITIONS

PART Env-Wr 101 DEFINITIONS REVISION NOTE: Document #13979, effective 5-24-24, readopted with amendment Chapter Env-Wr 100 and Chapter Env-Wr 200. Document #13979 inserted the following new definitions in Part Env-Wr 101: Env-Wr 101.01 (“1,000-year design event”), Env-Wr 101.04 (“250% of the 100-year design event”), Env-Wr 101.06 (“campsite”), Env-Wr 101.13 (“drainage area”), Env-Wr 101.16 (“engineer”), Env-Wr 101.17 (“exempt dam”), Env-Wr 101.26 (“manual operations”), Env-Wr 101.27 (“maximum discharge capacity”), Env-Wr 101.28 (“maximum pool level”), Env-Wr 101.29 (“maximum storage capacity”), Env-Wr 101.34 (“normal pool”), Env-Wr 101.36 (“overtopping”), Env-Wr 101.41 (“regulatory discharge capacity”), and Env-Wr 101.48 (“top of dam”). Document #13979 deleted the following definitions: Env-Wr 101.04 (“applicant”), Env-Wr 101.05 (“breached dam”), Env-Wr 101.07 (“construction engineer”), Env-Wr 101.13 )”discharge capacity”), Env-Wr 101.24 (“low economic loss”), Env-Wr 101.37 (“reversible environmental losses”), Env-Wr 101.40 (“specifications”), Env-Wr 101.42 (“storage capacity”), Env-Wr 101.43 (“stream”), and Env-Wr 101.45 (“wave run up). Document #13979 also deleted Part Env-Wr 102 titled “Duties of the Department” and Part Env-Wr 103 titled “Access to Files.” Document #13979 replaces all prior filings affecting the rules in the former Chapter Env-Wr 100 and former Chapter Env-Wr 200. Due to the changes made by #13979, the existing rules in Part Env-Wr 101 were extensively renumbered as indicated in the source notes.

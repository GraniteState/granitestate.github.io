Env-Wr 602.03 Attachments for Dam Removal Projects

Effective 2024-05-24. Earlier expiration 1990-11-07.

Env-Wr 602.03 Attachments for Dam Removal Projects. The following information shall be provided as an attachment to the form required in Env-Wr 602.02(b): (a) A description of the proposed methods to remove the dam, including construction sequence, so as to meet the criteria of Env-Wr 602.01 and Env-Wr 604.01(a); (b) An explanation of the purpose for removing the dam; (c) Plans showing removal design and restoration of site, including existing conditions, plan view, and a cross section of dam to be removed; (d) Description of method and location of disposal of dam materials; (e) USGS topographic map with property lines indicated; (f) Tax map showing property lines and the abutters' properties labeled; (g) Original, dated photos mounted or digital copies on 8.5" x 11" sheets of paper clearly illustrating the project impact area with locations of photographs noted on the plans, required in (c) above; and (h) Plans depicting the information required in Env-Wr 602.01(a).

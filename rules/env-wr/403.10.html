Env-Wr 403.10 Earth Embankment Design Criteria

Effective 2024-05-24.

Env-Wr 403.10 Earth Embankment Design Criteria. The department shall use one or more of the following texts in evaluating the competence of the submitted design: (a) USDA NRCS Earth Dams and Reservoirs Technical Release 210-60 (TR 210-60), 2019, available as noted in Appendix B; (b) Evaluation and Monitoring of Seepage and Internal Erosion” FEMA P-1032, May 2015, available as noted in Appendix B; (c) Filters for Embankment Dams – Best Practices for Design and Construction” FEMA, 2011, available as noted in Appendix B; (d) “Technical Manual: Conduits through Embankment Dams” FEMA L-266, September 2005, available as noted in Appendix B; (e) “Technical Manual: Overtopping Protection for Dams” FEMA P-1015, 2014, available as noted in Appendix B; (f) “Technical Manual: Plastic Pipe Used in Embankment Dams” FEMA P-675, November 2007, available as noted in Appendix B; or (g) NRCS “National Engineering Handbook”, Part 628, Articulated Concrete Block Armored Spillways, March 2019, as noted in Appendix B. Source. #8414, eff 8-20-05; ss by #10510, eff 1-22-14; ss by #13980, eff 5-24-24

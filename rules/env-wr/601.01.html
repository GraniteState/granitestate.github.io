Env-Wr 601.01 Purpose

Effective 2024-05-24. Earlier expiration 1990-11-07.

Env-Wr 601.01 Purpose. The purpose of the rules in this chapter is to specify the procedures to be followed when proposing to remove a dam under the auspices of the department’s river restoration program and the criteria that will be applied in reviewing such proposals.

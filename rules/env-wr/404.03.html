Env-Wr 404.03 Conditions of Permit

Effective 2024-05-24. Earlier expiration 1990-11-07.

Env-Wr 404.03 Conditions of Permit. The following conditions shall apply to all permits to construct or reconstruct a dam: (a) The permit shall be valid for 5 years from the date of issuance; (b) Work under the permit shall be completed within 5 years unless the owner has requested and received a time extension in accordance with Env-Wr 404.04; (c) All work on the dam shall comply with the approved plans and specifications, subject to design changes approved and documented in accordance with Env-Wr 404.05; (d) The engineer shall provide a final inspection report in accordance with Env-Wr 405.07; (e) The engineer shall certify to the department that the work was completed in accordance with the approved plans and specification and design intent as required by Env-Wr 405.08; (f) Inspections shall be performed in accordance with the plan submitted pursuant to Env-Wr 405.04; (g) The owner shall notify the department upon completion of the work in accordance with Env-Wr 405.10(a); (h) The owner shall maintain a current mailing and email address with the department; (i) The owner shall operate and maintain the structure in accordance with the written approved OMRF procedures submitted in accordance with Env-Wr 402.06(a)(8); and (j) The department shall include in the permit any other conditions necessary to ensure protection of public safety that are based on the specific design, site, and watershed.

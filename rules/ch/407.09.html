Ch 407.09 Preparatory and Complementary Procedures

Effective 2017-12-08. Earlier expiration 2017-08-20.

Ch 407.09 Preparatory and Complementary Procedures. A licensee shall not use preparatory and complementary procedures unless the licensee learned such procedures at an accredited chiropractic college or through a program of continuing education sponsored by an accredited chiropractic college.

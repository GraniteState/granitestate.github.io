He-W 641.06 Medical Coverage for Pregnant Women

Effective 2007-06-07. Expires 2015-06-07.

He-W 641.06 Medical Coverage for Pregnant Women. For purposes of Section 1902(l) of the Social Security Act relative to medical assistance for pregnant women, the following shall apply: (a) Pregnant women, who apply for or receive medical coverage for pregnant women (MCPW) as defined in He-W 601.05(q), shall not be subject to the maximum resource limits for medical assistance; and (b) The income of pregnant women shall be compared to a percentage of the federal poverty income guidelines, as required under section 1902 (1)(2)(A)(i) of the Social Security Act, for the appropriate budgetary unit size. In order for a pregnant woman to be income eligible for medical coverage, monthly income of the budgetary unit, as defined in He-W 601.02(e), shall not exceed the current percentage of the poverty income guidelines established under the Social Security Act or RSA 167:68, whichever is higher, for the appropriate budgetary unit size.

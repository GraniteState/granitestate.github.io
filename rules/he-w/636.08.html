He-W 636.08 Disclosure of Paternity Information

Effective 2022-08-20.

He-W 636.08 Disclosure of Paternity Information. (a) All FANF applicants and recipients shall identify the father of a child for whom assistance is being sought as specified in RSA 167:79, III (c), unless good cause pursuant to RSA 167:82, III (b) exists. (b) Individuals who do not identify the father at the personal interview shall: (1) Be referred to the bureau of child support services (BCSS); (2) Be contacted and interviewed by a BCSS child support worker for the purpose of understanding the importance of child support and establishing paternity; and (3) Participate in an interview with the BCSS child support worker within 10 days from the contact. (c) Individuals who do not participate in the interview with child support within 10 days from the date of the BCSS contact shall be denied financial assistance. Source.

He-W 806.22 Verification of Unemployment Status

Effective 2023-04-26. Earlier expiration 1997-06-26.

He-W 806.22 Verification of Unemployment Status. For the determination of eligibility for parent or other caretaker relative medical assistance based on deprivation due to unemployment of at least one parent pursuant to He-W 828.03, the applicant shall provide written documentation or self-attest that at least one parent is unemployed or working less than 100 hours per month.

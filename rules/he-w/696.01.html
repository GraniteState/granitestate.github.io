He-W 696.01 Purpose

Effective 2021-11-24. Earlier expiration 2005-10-24.

He-W 696.01 Purpose. The purpose of Nutritional Supplement for Working Families (NSWF) is to offer working recipients of Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program (SNAP) benefits a supplemental monthly assistance pursuant to 45 CFR 260.31(a)(1) to be used only to augment their families’ nutritional needs and help offset their other financial obligations. Recipients of NSWF shall be counted in the department’s work participation rate pursuant to RSA 167:77-a,I(e). Source.

He-W 655.02 Availability of Funds

Effective 2025-07-01. Expires 2034-07-01.

He-W 655.02 Availability of Funds. (a) New Hampshire employment program (NHEP) activities as described in He-W 637 shall only be required as budgeted funding and resources within the current state fiscal year are available for a particular employment and training support service. (b) NHEP participants, as defined in He-W 637.01, shall be the only individuals eligible for assistance for fees, supplies, and tuition as described in He-W 655.06 and He-W 655.08, respectively.

He-W 589.03 Student Eligibility

Effective 2025-11-25. Expires 2035-11-25.

He-W 589.03 Student Eligibility. To be eligible for medicaid reimbursement for covered services, a student shall: (a) Have a care plan; (b) Be between the ages of 3 and 22, provided that the students aged 21 through 22 are not eligible for medicaid reimbursement for services covered only under the EPSDT benefit; (c) Be a medicaid recipient; and (d) Be served by an LEA or SAU that is an enrolled school provider.

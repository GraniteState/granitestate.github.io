He-W 504.02 Medical Eligibility

Effective 2013-04-25.

He-W 504.02 Medical Eligibility. To be medically eligible for MEAD: (a) An individual who is currently a medicaid recipient shall: (1) Have been determined eligible for aid to the permanently and totally disabled (APTD) according to RSA 167:6, VI, or aid to the needy blind (ANB) according to RSA 167:6, IV, within the 12 months prior to application for MEAD; and (2) Not have been terminated from APTD or ANB due to medical improvement during the previous 12 months; or (b) An individual who is not currently an APTD or ANB recipient shall: (1) Be employed, or self-employed, for pay, pursuant to the provisions of He-W 641.03(b)-(c), on the date of application and during the time eligibility for MEAD is being determined; (2) Have a medical impairment that meets the Social Security Administration’s Listing of Impairments in accordance with 20 CFR 404, Subpart P, Appendix I; and (3) Have a medical impairment that has persisted or is expected to persist for a minimum of 48 consecutive months. Source. #7644, eff 2-8-02; ss by #8292, eff 2-24-05; ss by #10321, eff 4-25-13

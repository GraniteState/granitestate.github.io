He-W 552.04 Provider Participation

Effective 2014-09-26. Earlier expiration 2014-09-26.

He-W 552.04 Provider Participation. (a) All providers of PCA services shall be enrolled as a New Hampshire medicaid provider to provide PCA services. (b) All personal care attendants shall: (1) Be employed by or under contract with a New Hampshire medicaid provider in (a) above; (2) Meet the training and experience requirements specified in He-W 552.07; and (3) Not be a member of the recipient’s family, per 42 CFR 440.167(a)(2).

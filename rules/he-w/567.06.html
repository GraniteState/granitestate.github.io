He-W 567.06 Non-Covered Services

Effective 2018-05-25. Earlier expiration 2003-12-29.

He-W 567.06 Non-Covered Services. Non-covered services shall include, but not be limited to: (a) Replacement of hearing aids due to loss, misuse, or abuse, except as noted in He-W 567.05(e)(3); (b) FM systems, if the systems are for the sole purpose of recipient use in an educational setting and are coverable under He-M 1301; (c) Repair of hearing aids which are covered under a warranty; (d) Pocket talker repairs, batteries, accessories, except those listed in He-W 567.05(j) above, and optional telelinks; (e) A pocket talker if a hearing aid is already covered by medicaid; (f) A hearing aid if a pocket talker is already covered by medicaid, unless the criterion in He-W 567.05(i)(1) is met; and (g) Binaural hearing aids for recipients 21 years of age or over when the requirements set forth in He-W 567.05(c)(2) or He-W 567.07 have not been met. Source. (See Revision Note at chapter heading He-W 500); ss by #6157, eff 12-29-95, EXPIRED: 12-29-03 New. #9480, eff 5-23-09; amd by #10342, eff 6-1-13; ss by #12538, eff 5-25-18

He-W 856.06 Resource Limits

Effective 2016-10-25.

He-W 856.06 Resource Limits. (a) The resource limit for categorically needy medical assistance for home care for children with severe disabilities (HC-CSD) shall be $1,000, regardless of assistance group size. (b) The resource limit for adult categorically-needy medical assistance shall be $1,500, with the exception of individuals eligible for medicaid for employed adults with disabilities (MEAD) pursuant to He-W 641.03. (c) Except as described in (d) below, the resource limit for all categories of medically-needy medical assistance shall be as follows, based on the number of individuals in the assistance group: (1) For a single individual, $2,500; (2) For 2 individuals, $4,000; and (3) For 3 or more individuals, add an additional $100 for each additional individual in the assistance group to the resource limit in (2) above. (d) The resource limit for applicants for medical assistance long-term care services shall be $2,500 plus the value of coverage provided in a conforming long-term care insurance policy, pursuant to RSA 167:4, IV(d) and 42 USC 1396p(b). Source. #12017, eff 10-25-16

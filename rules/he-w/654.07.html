He-W 654.07 Income-In-Kind

Effective 2024-04-24. Earlier expiration 1997-10-25.

He-W 654.07 Income-In-Kind. (a) For all adult categories of financial assistance, earned income-in-kind received on a regular basis shall be assigned a dollar value and shall be treated as countable earned income as follows: (1) The dollar value shall be determined by a statement from the employer indicating the frequency and dollar value of earned income-in-kind, or, if unable to obtain such a statement, by multiplying the number of hours worked times the hourly wage as provided by the employer; (2) If the employer is unable to provide an approximate hourly wage, or the client is self-employed, the current minimum hourly wage shall be used; and (3) From the dollar value amount determined as referenced in (1) or (2) above, any monetary payment received as a supplement for the same job shall be subtracted, resulting in gross earned income-in-kind. (b) With the exception of payments made from trusts or similar legal devices, for adult category financial assistance, unearned income-in-kind shall represent a component of the adult category standard of need and shall not be counted as income.

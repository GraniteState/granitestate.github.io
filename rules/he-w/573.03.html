He-W 573.03 Provider Participation

Effective 2014-05-23. Earlier expiration 2006-02-16.

He-W 573.03 Provider Participation. All participating wheelchair van service providers shall: (a) Be enrolled as a NH medicaid provider; (b) At the time of enrollment, provide proof of vehicle registration in accordance with He-W 573.07(a); (c) At the time of enrollment, provide proof of vehicle insurance in accordance with He-W 573.07(b); and (d) Comply with all applicable requirements of RSA 153-A:1, Saf-C 5919, 49 CFR 37, and 49 CFR 38.

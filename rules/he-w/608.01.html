He-W 608.01 Use of Money Payments

Effective 2023-09-28. Earlier expiration 1997-10-25.

He-W 608.01 Use of Money Payments. (a) The individual shall be responsible for the satisfaction of the individual’s creditors to the same extent as any other citizen. (b) The department shall not intervene in disputes involving the individual and a provider in matters relating to financial obligations. Source.

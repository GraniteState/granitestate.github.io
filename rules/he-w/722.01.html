He-W 722.01 Interview Requirements

Effective 2023-07-22. Earlier expiration 2005-08-01.

He-W 722.01 Interview Requirements. (a) In accordance with 7 CFR 273.2(e)(2), all households shall have the option to participate in a telephone interview in lieu of a face-to-face interview. (b) The casehead, as defined in He-W 701.01, or authorized representative shall review the summary of the information provided during the interview with the department’s representative who conducted the interview, and make any corrections to the information, if needed.

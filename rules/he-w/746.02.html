He-W 746.02 Establishing Certification Periods

Effective 2026-07-17. Expires 2036-07-17.

He-W 746.02 Establishing Certification Periods. (a) Thirty-six months certification shall be allowed for households in which: (1) There is no earned income; and (2) All adult household members are age 65 or older or disabled as defined in 7 CFR 271.2. (b) In accordance with 7 CFR 273.10(f)(3), up to 6 months certification shall be allowed for households in which: (1) There is earned income; or (2) At least one adult household member is not age 65 or older or disabled as defined in 7 CFR 271.2. (c) In accordance with 7 CFR 273.10(f)(3), a certification period of less than 6 months shall be allowed for households where the household’s situation is unstable. Source.

He-W 658.02 Financial Assistance To Needy Families Payment Standard

Effective 2023-09-28. Earlier expiration 2008-09-20.

He-W 658.02 Financial Assistance To Needy Families Payment Standard. The payment standard for financial assistance to needy families financial assistance shall be based upon the size of the assistance group (AG) and be equal to 60% of the federal poverty guideline for the AG size.

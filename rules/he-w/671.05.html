He-W 671.05 Electronic Stolen Benefit Replacement

Effective 2025-10-24. Expires 2035-10-24.

He-W 671.05 Electronic Stolen Benefit Replacement. (a) Electronic benefit transfer (EBT) financial benefits shall be replaced by the department if they are stolen through card skimming, card cloning, or similar fraudulent methods. (b) Replacement benefits shall be issued if the individual: (1) Reports the electronic stolen benefits to the department within 30 calendar days from the date the household discovered the transaction; and (2) Provides, within 10 days of the report, a signed statement attesting to electronic stolen benefits, including the amount of the benefits stolen, the date, time, and location of the fraudulent transactions, and the date, time, and location where they used their EBT card. (c) Replacement financial benefits shall be issued for the actual amounts stolen or the amount equal to 2 monthly allotments prior to the date the benefits were stolen, or whichever is less. (d) The department shall not replace electronic stolen benefits more than twice in one federal fiscal year for any household. (e) Only electronic stolen benefits shall be eligible for replacement. Benefits shall not be replaced if an individual loses their card or gives someone they know their card and personal identification number, even if the EBT card was used without the individual’s permission. (f) The department shall immediately cancel and replace compromised cards when electronic stolen benefits are reported. Source. #14411, eff 10-24-25, EXPIRES: 10-24-35

He-W 654.16 Adult Standard Disregard

Effective 2025-07-01. Expires 2035-07-01.

He-W 654.16 Adult Standard Disregard. (a) For adult category financial assistance applicants or recipients, a standard disregard shall be subtracted from income as described in He-W 654.02(c)(5). (b) The amount of the disregard shall depend on the number of individuals whose needs are considered when determining eligibility, as follows: (1) The standard disregard for one individual shall be $13.00; (2) The standard disregard shall be $20.00 for an individual and applicant or nonapplicant spouse, or an individual and a needy essential person; and (3) The standard disregard shall be $25.00 for an individual and applicant or nonapplicant spouse and a needy essential person.

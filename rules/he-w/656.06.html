He-W 656.06 Resource Limits

Effective 2023-05-24. Earlier expiration 1997-10-25.

He-W 656.06 Resource Limits. (a) When determining eligibility for Financial Assistance to Needy Families (FANF): (1) Applicant households that have received financial assistance under FANF in any one of the previous 6 months shall be considered a recipient household in determining the resource limit to be used; and (2) The resource limits shall be: a. For applicant households, $1,000; and b. For recipient households, $5,000. (b) The resource limit for adult category financial assistance shall be $1,500. Source.

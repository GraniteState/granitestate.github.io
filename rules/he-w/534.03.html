He-W 534.03 Provider Participation

Effective 2014-01-24. Earlier expiration 2005-02-28.

He-W 534.03 Provider Participation. Each participating APRN shall: (a) Be a New Hampshire (NH) enrolled medicaid provider; (b) If practicing in NH, be licensed as an APRN by the NH board of nursing in accordance with RSA 326-B:18; and (c) If practicing in another state, submit current proof of meeting that state’s advanced education and clinical requirements to practice as a registered nurse in an advance practice role.

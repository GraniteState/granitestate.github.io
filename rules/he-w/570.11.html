He-W 570.11 Utilization Review and Control

Effective 2018-05-22. Earlier expiration 2002-12-19.

He-W 570.11 Utilization Review and Control. The department’s provider integrity unit shall monitor utilization of pharmaceutical services to identify, prevent, and correct potential occurrences of fraud, waste, and abuse in accordance with 42 CFR 455, 42 CFR 456, and He-W 520.

He-W 693.04 Waiver of the Administrative Disqualification Hearing

Effective 2019-10-22. Earlier expiration 2011-08-23.

He-W 693.04 Waiver of the Administrative Disqualification Hearing. (a) An individual accused of an IPV may waive his or her right to the ADH. (b) An individual who chooses to waive his or her right to an ADH shall do so in writing by submitting the waiver form provided by the department entitled “Administrative FANF Disqualification Hearing Waiver” Form 402 (May 1996). (c) If an individual waives his or her right to an ADH, the department shall impose the disqualification penalty and provide notice of the disqualification penalty pursuant to He-W 693.06. Source.

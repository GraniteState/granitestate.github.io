He-W 804.02 Notice of Decision

Effective 2023-10-05.

He-W 804.02 Notice of Decision. (a) Except where otherwise specified, increases in the level of eligibility shall take effect on the first of the following month after the change is processed, as determined by computer processing cut-off dates. (b) When positive and negative changes occur or are reported simultaneously, the changes shall be processed together and the combined effect of the changes shall determine the advance notice period requirements as described in He-W 804.03. Source. #13765, eff 10-5-23

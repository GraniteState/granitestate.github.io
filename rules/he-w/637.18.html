He-W 637.18 Interim Activities

Effective 2024-11-26. Expires 2034-11-26.

He-W 637.18 Interim Activities. New Hampshire employment program (NHEP) participants shall be required to participate in the specific interim activities, including the allowable federal activities pursuant to 45 CFR 261.30, identified in the NHEP participant’s employability plan as defined in He-W 637.01.

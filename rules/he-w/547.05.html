He-W 547.05 Non-Covered Services

Effective 2025-08-01. Expires 2035-08-01.

He-W 547.05 Non-Covered Services. (a) Medical services covered under the NH medicaid program pursuant to He-W 530 through He-W 590 and He-M 400 through He-M 700 shall not be covered as part of a home visit. (b) If during the home visit a medical necessity for other services is assessed, those services shall be billed separately from the home visit service. Source.

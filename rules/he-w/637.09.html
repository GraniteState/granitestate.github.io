He-W 637.09 Initial Assessment

Effective 2023-02-01. Earlier expiration 2007-04-11.

He-W 637.09 Initial Assessment (a) An individual shall participate in an initial assessment interview with department staff. (b) Participation in an initial assessment interview shall be required for all NHEP participants pursuant to 45 CFR 261.11. (c) The NHEP participant shall provide information, pursuant to 45 CFR 261.13 to the NHEP representative resulting in the development of the employability plan as outlined in He-W 637.12. (d) An initial assessment shall be completed by an NHEP representative within 90 days of the date an individual becomes eligible for financial assistance. Source.

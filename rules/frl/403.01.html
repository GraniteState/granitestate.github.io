Frl 403.01 Purpose

Effective 2026-09-07. Expires 2036-09-07.

Frl 403.01 Purpose. The purpose of Frl 403 is to require all licensed embalmers and funeral directors to obtain continuing education as a condition for renewal of their licenses in order to maintain the high standards of the profession and business of funeral directing and embalming, and to establish criteria for approval of continuing education programs to be used in fulfilling such continuing education requirements.

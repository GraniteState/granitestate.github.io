Frl 103.03 Office Hours, Office Location, Mailing Address, and Telephone

Effective 2026-01-01.

Frl 103.03 Office Hours, Office Location, Mailing Address, and Telephone. (a) The board’s office shall be located at the office of professional licensure and certification, 7 Eagle Square, Concord, NH 03301, and shall be open to the public during the normal business hours established in Plc 103.03(b). (b) Correspondence, filings, and other communications intended for the board shall be addressed to the board at the address in paragraph (a) above. (c) The board’s telephone number is (603) 271-2152. Source. #6927, eff 1-1-99; ss by #14456, eff 1-1-26 (formerly Frl 103.04)

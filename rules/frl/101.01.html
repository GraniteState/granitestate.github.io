Frl 101.01 Purpose and Scope

Effective 2026-01-01. Earlier expiration 1994-01-20.

Frl 101.01 Purpose and Scope. The rules of this title implement the statutory responsibilities of the New Hampshire board of registration of funeral directors and embalmers created by RSA 325.

Frl 603.01 Funeral Home and Funeral Chapel Inspections

Effective 2026-09-04. Expires 2036-09-04.

Frl 603.01 Funeral Home and Funeral Chapel Inspections. (a) All funeral homes and funeral chapels where funeral directing is conducted or where embalming is practiced shall submit to an inspection at least once every 2 years. The owner or a licensed New Hampshire funeral director employed by the funeral home or chapel shall be present for the inspection. (b) The applicant shall notify the OPLC that the establishment for which they have requested an initial license is ready for the inspection. (c) The inspection checklist referenced in Plc 403.08 shall evaluate the following: (1) Whether the funeral home or funeral chapel displays certificates of inspection and certificates of licensed individuals in accordance with Frl 602.04(b) and Frl 603.01(f); (2) Whether the funeral home or funeral chapel complies with the Americans with Disabilities Act; (3) Whether the funeral home or funeral chapel complies with Frl 602.05 through Frl 602.09; (4) Whether the funeral home or funeral chapel maintains a clearly legible exterior sign in accordance with Frl 602.12; (5) Whether the funeral home maintains a preparation room in compliance with Frl 701.01; (6) Whether the funeral home or funeral chapel maintains proper sanitation and universal precautions in compliance with Frl 701.02; (7) Whether the funeral home or funeral chapel maintains a general price list, casket price list, outer burial container price list, and statement of funeral goods and services selected disclosures in compliance with 16 CFR 453.6; (8) Whether the funeral home or funeral chapel complies with the web site requirements in Frl 602.10(d); and (9) Whether the funeral home or funeral chapel complies with the pre-need funeral contracts requirements in RSA 325:46-a I and funds deposited in accordance with RSA 325:45. (d) Fees for routine inspections related to licensure of funeral homes and funeral chapels shall be as required by Plc 1001.18(i). (e) Fees for site visits and inspections not related to licensure shall be the fees required by Plc 1001.19(d). (f) Upon receiving a certificate of inspection, the funeral home or funeral chapel shall display the certificate in a place viewable by the general public within the funeral home or funeral chapel. (g) Pursuant to RSA 310:12, IV, in instances involving imminent danger to the public health, safety, or welfare, where the board finds that emergency action is required, the board shall comply with the requirements of RSA 541-A:30.

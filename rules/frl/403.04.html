Frl 403.04 Waivers

Effective 2026-09-07. Expires 2036-09-07.

Frl 403.04 Waivers. (a) Any licensee with a serious illness, disability, hardship, or extenuating circumstances may request a waiver of Frl 403, in accordance with Plc 211, not less than 30 days prior to date of license expiration. (b) Applicants shall provide a plan for obtaining the required CE credits that includes the date that the applicant will complete the requirements. (c) Decisions on waiver requests shall be issued in accordance with Plc 211.08 Source. #6516, eff 5-24-97, EXPIRED: 5-24-05 New. #9387, eff 2-12-09 (from Frl 403.08); ss by #12129, eff 3-10-17; ss by #14645, eff 9-7-26, EXPIRES: 9-7-36 (formerly Frl 403.06)

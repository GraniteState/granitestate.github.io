Pdab 202.13 Limiting Number of Witnesses

Effective 2022-12-02.

Pdab 202.13 Limiting Number of Witnesses. To avoid unnecessary cumulative evidence in any proceeding, the board may limit the number of witnesses or the time for testimony upon a particular issue in the course of any hearing. Source. #13500-A, eff 12-2-22

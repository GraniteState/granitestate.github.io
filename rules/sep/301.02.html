Sep 301.02 Application for Licensure

Effective 2025-10-26. Expires 2035-10-26.

Sep 301.02 Application for Licensure. Each applicant for licensure shall provide the following information to the licensing bureau on the “Addendum to the Universal Application for Initial License for Septic System Evaluators”, revised August 2025: (a) A “yes” or “no” answer to the question “Does the applicant hold a Granite State septic system certified evaluator designation pursuant to RSA 310-A:210, II?”; (b) A “yes” or “no” answer to the questions “Has the applicant completed the classroom and field training from a board approved septic evaluator provider, pursuant to RSA 310-A:210, I(a)? If “yes,” has the applicant provided documentation issued by the course provider that includes the following information: (1) The date of the course; (2) Where the course was taken; and (3) Name of the course provider; (c) A “yes” or “no” answer to the question “Is the applicant at least 18 years old as required by RSA 310-A:210, I(c)?”. (d) A “yes” or “no” answer to the question “Has the applicant passed a board approved examination required by Sep 303.01?”.

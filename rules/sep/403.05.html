Sep 403.05 Exemptions

Effective 2025-10-26. Expires 2035-10-26.

Sep 403.05 Exemptions. (a) A licensee may request a waiver from the continuing education requirements, pursuant to Sep 201.02(j), for any of the following reasons: (1) A licensee serving on temporary active duty in the armed forces of the United States for a period of time exceeding 120 consecutive days in a year shall be exempt from obtaining the continuing education hours required during that year; or (2) Licensees experiencing disability, illness, or other extenuating circumstances which would prevent the licensee from completing the required continuing education hours. (b) A licensee seeking a waiver shall file the request as required by Plc 211.03 through Plc 211.05. Source. #12654, eff 10-31-18; ss by #14361, eff 10-26-25, EXPIRES: 10-26-35

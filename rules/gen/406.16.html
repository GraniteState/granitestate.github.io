Gen 406.16 Peer Supervision Groups

Effective 2025-10-07. Expires 2035-10-07.

Gen 406.16 Peer Supervision Groups. (a) The licensee shall receive continuing professional education credit for participation in a peer supervision group so long as: (1) The peer supervision group consists of genetic counselors or other counseling professionals; (2) It operates according to a contract or other written guidelines that outline the rules, procedures, and expectations for member’s participation in the group; and (3) The group meets on a regular basis according to a published schedule of dates, times, and locations. (b) The licensee shall receive continuing professional education of credit for every 25 hours of participation for a maximum of 2 hours per renewal cycle. (c) Documentation of peer supervision groups shall be: (1) A photocopy of the contract or written guidelines for operation showing the name of the peer supervision group; (2) A photocopy of the schedule of meetings; and (3) A photocopy of the dated sign-in sheets documenting attendance. Source. #10687, eff 10-7-14; ss by #13556, eff 4-11-23 (see Revision Note #2 at chapter heading for Gen 400); ss by #14338, eff 10-7-25, EXPIRES: 10-7-35

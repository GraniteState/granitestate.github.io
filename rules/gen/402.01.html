Gen 402.01 License Renewal Procedure

Effective 2025-10-07. Expires 2035-10-07.

Gen 402.01 License Renewal Procedure. (a) A licensee wishing to renew their current license shall submit the completed renewal application as specified in Plc 308.05, that contains the information required by Plc 308.06, and the information and documentation described in Plc 308.07 and Gen 402.02. (b) Pursuant to RSA 310:8, III, licenses to be renewed shall lapse if the renewal applicants do not submit the completed renewal application, renewal fee, and any supporting documentation on or before license expiration. (c) Renewal applicants whose licenses have lapsed shall not provide genetic counseling until their licenses have been reinstated. Source. #10687, eff 10-7-14; ss by #13556, eff 4-11-23 (formerly Gen 402.02) (see Revision Note #2 at chapter heading for Gen 400); ss by #14338, eff 10-7-25, EXPIRES: 10-7-35 (formerly Gen 402.02)

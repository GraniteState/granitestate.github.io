Gen 407.01 Administrative Obligations of Licensees

Effective 2025-10-07. Expires 2035-10-07.

Gen 407.01 Administrative Obligations of Licensees. (a) Plc 307 shall govern the administrative obligations of licensees. (b) Licensees shall retain documentation of their continuing professional education and professional activities for at least 3 years. (c) Licensees shall cooperate with all investigations and requests for information from the OPLC and the board, wherein “cooperate” means to: (1) Provide accurate factual information; (2) Respond to any questions posed in a timely fashion; and (3) Assist in any other way requested by the OPLC or the board.

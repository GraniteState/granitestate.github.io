Gen 100 ORGANIZATIONAL RULES

CHAPTER Gen 100 ORGANIZATIONAL RULES REVISION NOTE: Document #13554, effective 4-11-20, adopted, readopted and renumbered, readopted with amendments and renumbered, or repealed various rules in Chapter Gen 100. Specifically, Gen 103.02 titled “Officers”, Gen 103.06 titled “Attendance at Meetings by Members of the Public”, Gen 103.07 titled “Notice of Meetings”, Gen 103.08 titled “Minutes of Board Meetings”, and Part Gen 104 titled “Public Information” were repealed. Gen 103.03 titled “Relationship of the Board to the Office of Licensed Allied Health Professionals” was readopted with amendments and renumbered as Gen 103.02. Gen 103.04 titled “Responsibilities of the Board” and Gen 103.05 titled “Board Meetings and Quorum” were readopted and renumbered as Gen 103.03 and Gen 103.04, respectively. A new Gen 103.05 titled “Other Organizational Rules” was adopted. Document #13554 replaced the prior filings for the various, former rules. The prior filings affecting the former rules in Chapter Gen 100 included only Document #10650, effective 8-6-14. As organizational rules the rules in Chapter Gen 100 in Document #13554 will not expire except pursuant to RSA 541-A:17, II.

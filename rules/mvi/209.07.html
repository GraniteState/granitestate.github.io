Mvi 209.07 RSA 357-C:9, Relocation or Establishment of Dealerships

Effective 2017-03-02. Earlier expiration 2017-04-03.

Mvi 209.07 RSA 357-C:9, Relocation or Establishment of Dealerships. (a) The scope of hearings held pursuant to RSA 357-C:9 shall be limited to whether the manufacturer complied with the requirements of RSA 357-C:9. (b) When it assesses whether a manufacturer had good cause to relocate or not enter into an agreement, the Board shall consider the existing circumstances, including, but not limited to the criteria specified in RSA 357-C:9, II.

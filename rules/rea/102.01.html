Rea 102.01 Description of Agency

Effective 2025-04-16. Earlier expiration 2000-04-16.

Rea 102.01 Description of Agency. (a) The commission consists of 5 members appointed by the governor with the advice and consent of the council pursuant to RSA 331-A:5. (b) Its functions include the examination and licensing of persons applying for salesperson or broker status, and the regulation of salespersons and brokers.

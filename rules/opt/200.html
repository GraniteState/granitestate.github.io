Opt 200 RULES OF PRACTICE AND PROCEDURE

CHAPTER Opt 200 RULES OF PRACTICE AND PROCEDURE REVISION NOTE: Document #13899, effective 5-11-24, repealed Part Opt 201 through Part Opt 217 in Chapter Opt 200, titled “Practice and Procedure”, and renamed the chapter as “Rules of Practice and Procedure”. Document #13899 also adopted a new Part Opt 201 titled “Applicability and Waiver of Substantive Rules”, containing Opt 201.01 titled “Rules of Practice and Procedure.” Document #13899 replaces all prior filings affecting the rules in the former Chapter Opt 200. The prior filings included the following documents. Italics indicate the rules were subject to expiration only pursuant to RSA 541-A:17, II: #1711, eff 1-25-81 #2290(E), eff 12-30-82 #2291, eff 1-19-83 #3108, eff 8-28-85 #5839, eff 6-17-94, EXPIRED: 6-17-00 #7370, INTERIM, eff 9-30-00, EXPIRED: 3-29-01 #7608-A, eff 12-5-01 #7608-B, eff 12-5-01 (Opt 216 and Opt 217 only) #9558, eff 10-6-09 (Opt 216 and Opt 217 only) #12382, eff 9-16-17 (Opt 216 and Opt 217 only) As practice and procedure rules, the rules in Document #13899 will not expire except pursuant to RSA 541-A:17, II.

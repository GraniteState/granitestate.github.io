Opt 102.01 Terms Used

Effective 2025-05-10. Earlier expiration 2001-03-29.

Opt 102.01 Terms Used. As used in these rules, the following terms shall have the meanings indicated: (a) “Board” means the board of registration in optometry created by RSA 327:2; (b) “Licensee” means a person holding a license, certificate, or registration issued by the office of professional licensure and certification (OPLC); (c) “Out of state contact lens dispenser” means any non-pharmacy business organization physically located outside of New Hampshire that offers contact lenses for retail sale to customers inside New Hampshire; (d) “Prescription” means the written specifications for a spectacle lens or contact lens, necessary for the manufacture of such lens; (e) “Pharmaceutical agent” means “pharmaceutical agent” as defined in RSA 327:1, III, namely, “the following pharmaceutical products: (f) Non-legend, over the counter, agents; (g) Mydriatic and cycloplegic agents which are topically applied. (h) Miotic agents approved pursuant to RSA 327:6-a, VI. (i) Antibiotics, sulfonomides, and combinations thereof, which are topically applied or orally administered to treat or alleviate the effects of disease or abnormal conditions of the human eye, adnexa, and eyelids, or structures posterior to the iris, approved pursuant to RSA 327:6-a, VI. (j) Anti-allergy medications, including but not limited to antihistamines, decongestants, and mast-cell stabilizers which are topically applied. (k) Anesthetics and dyes which are topically applied. (l) Ocular lubricants and hypertonic agents which are topically applied. (m) Orally administered analgesic agents used for the purpose of alleviating pain caused by a disease or abnormal condition of the human eye or eyelid, or structures posterior to the iris. This may include class III and IV controlled substances approved pursuant to RSA 327:6-a, VI and included in the formulary. (n) Other pharmaceutical agents, any solely diagnostic agents, and diagnostic agents combined with pharmaceutical agents as defined in this paragraph and as approved pursuant to RSA 327:6-a, VI. (o) Non-steroidal anti-inflammatory agents approved pursuant to RSA 327:6-a, VI. (p) Anti-glaucoma agents provided that an optometrist may dispense or prescribe such agents if the optometrist has met the requirements of RSA 327:6-c. (q) Corticosteroids which are topically applied, as approved by the board. (r) Antivirals which are approved pursuant to RSA 327:6-a, VI. (s) Corticosteroids or antivirals, provided that optometrists with patients on corticosteroids or antivirals who demonstrate no improvement in 10 days shall be referred to an ophthalmologist. (t) Influenza, COVID-19, and shingles vaccines which have been approved by the Food and Drug Administration to adults.” (u) “Therapeutic pharmaceutical agents (TPA)” means such agents used in the diagnosis and treatment of illnesses of the eyes; (v) “TPA-certified” means that the licensee has obtained the necessary training and qualifications to administer therapeutic pharmaceutical agents in the practice of optometry; and (w) “TPA/G-certified” means that the licensee has obtained the necessary training and qualifications to administer therapeutic pharmaceutical agents in the practice of optometry, including the treatment of glaucoma. ` Source.

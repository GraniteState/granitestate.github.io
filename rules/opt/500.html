Opt 500 ETHICAL STANDARDS AND OPIOID PRESCRIBING

CHAPTER Opt 500 ETHICAL STANDARDS AND OPIOID PRESCRIBING REVISION NOTE: Document #14260, effective 8-2-25, made extensive changes to chapter Opt 500 by readopting with amendment, repealing various sections of the chapter, as well as and reorganizing the structure of the chapter. Document #14260 repealed part Opt 501, “Disciplinary Matters”, and part Opt 503, “Inactive License and Disciplinary Actions”. Document #14260 also renumbered previous part Opt 502 as part Opt 501, “Ethical Standards”, and part Opt 504 as part Opt 502, “Opioid Prescribing.” The prior filings for former Opt 500 include the following documents: #2770, eff 7-6-84 #5667, eff 7-15-93 #6098-B, eff 10-1-95 #6185, eff 2-16-96 #6717, eff 3-24-98 #7889, eff 5-20-03 #9342, eff 12-9-08 #9756, eff 7-20-10 #10243, eff 12-18-12 #12060, eff 1-1-17 #12108, eff 2-21-17 #12383, eff 9-16-17 #13848, eff 3-9-24

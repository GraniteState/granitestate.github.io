Mil 103.01 Record of Official Actions

Effective 2000-07-01.

Mil 103.01 Record of Official Actions. (a) Minutes shall be kept of all board meetings and all official actions taken by the board. (b) These minutes shall record those members who participate in each vote and separately record the position of any members who choose to dissent, abstain, or concur. (c) Board minutes shall be public records and be available for inspection within 144 hours of the meeting or vote in question. Source. #7313, eff 7-1-00

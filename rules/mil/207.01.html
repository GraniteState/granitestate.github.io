Mil 207.01 Explanation After Adoption

Effective 2001-07-12. Expires 2009-07-12.

Mil 207.01 Explanation After Adoption. (a) Any person may request an explanation regarding adoption of the rules pursuant to RSA 541-A:11, VII by submitting a request to the board. (b) The request shall be considered and the board shall issue a response within 45 days after consideration. Source. #7529, eff 7-12-01, EXPIRED: 7-12-09

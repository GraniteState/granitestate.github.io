Ncr 844.12 Change in Project Scope Request

Effective 2025-06-25. Expires 2035-06-25.

Ncr 844.12 Change in Project Scope Request. Applicants can request a specific GIA trail project to be changed as necessary. Applicants shall make efforts to complete approved projects before requesting a change in project scope unless field conditions prevent approved projects from being completed. Applicants shall submit a “Grant In Aid Change in Project Scope Request Form”, 2025 June, in writing to the bureau chief for review. All applicable rules per Ncr 844.02 apply to these change in project scope requests. Source. #14293, eff 6-25-25, EXPIRES: 6-25-35

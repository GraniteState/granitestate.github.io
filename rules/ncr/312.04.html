Ncr 312.04 Extirpated Plant Species

Effective 2020-01-01.

Ncr 312.04 Extirpated Plant Species. (a) For the purposes of Table 312.4, “extirpated species” means a native plant taxon presumed to be extirpated from the state because the taxon has not been located despite intensive searches of historical sites and other appropriate habitat, and has virtually no likelihood it will be rediscovered.

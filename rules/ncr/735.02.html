Ncr 735.02 Trail Use Limitations

Effective 2025-06-25. Expires 2035-06-25.

Ncr 735.02 Trail Use Limitations. No person shall use the following equipment or animals on the Franconia Notch recreational trail: (a) Skateboards; (b) Roller skates, rollerblades, or roller skis; (c) Children’s tricycles, except these shall be permitted from the parking lot to the Old Man Viewing Area; (d) Motorized bicycles; (e) Electronic Personal Assistive Mobility Devices (EPAMD), such as Segways; or (f) Horses. Source. #14055 EMERGENCY, EXPIRED: 2-15-25 New. #14291, eff 6-25-25, EXPIRES: 6-25-35

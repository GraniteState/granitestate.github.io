Ncr 845.01 OHRV Application for Grant-in-Aid

Effective 2025-06-25. Expires 2035-06-25.

Ncr 845.01 OHRV Application for Grant-in-Aid. OHRV clubs and political subdivisions applying for GIA for summer OHRV trail projects or summer grading operations shall complete, and submit the “OHRV Application for Grant-In-Aid Summer” form, 2025 June. Source. #14293, eff 6-25-25, EXPIRES: 6-25-35

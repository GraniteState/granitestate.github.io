Den 301.09 Restricted License

Effective 2015-12-09.

Den 301.09 Restricted License. (a) A restricted license shall limit a dentist or dental hygienist to performing those procedures specified by the board. (b) Restricted licenses shall be issued: (1) To dentists and dental hygienists who are limited to performing some, but not all, procedures to acceptable professional standards thereby protecting the public; and (2) To dentists and dental hygienists who are in need of remedial training in specific areas to reach professional standards that protect the public welfare.

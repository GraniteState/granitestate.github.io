Den 503.07 Ongoing Requirements for the Use of General Anesthesia, Deep Sedation, and Moderate Sedation, Moderate Sedation Only, or Moderate Sedation with Pediatric Qualif

Effective 2025-03-07.

Den 503.07 Ongoing Requirements for the Use of General Anesthesia, Deep Sedation, and Moderate Sedation, Moderate Sedation Only, or Moderate Sedation with Pediatric Qualification. No dentist shall use general anesthesia, deep sedation, or moderate sedation on an outpatient basis for dental patients except pursuant to Den 304.

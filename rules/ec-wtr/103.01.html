Ec-Wtr 103.01 Office Hours, Electronic Mail Address, and Location

Effective 2024-04-11.

Ec-Wtr 103.01 Office Hours, Electronic Mail Address, and Location. (a) The council’s office is located at: Department of Environmental Services 29 Hazen Drive P.O. Box 95 Concord, NH 03302-0095 (b) The council’s electronic email address is appeals@des.nh.gov. (c) The council’s regular business hours shall coincide with those of the department. Source.

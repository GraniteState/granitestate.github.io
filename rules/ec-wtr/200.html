Ec-Wtr 200 PROCEDURAL RULES

CHAPTER Ec-Wtr 200 PROCEDURAL RULES Statutory Authority: RSA 21-O:7, IV; RSA 21-O:14, IV; RSA 541-A:16, I(b)–(d); RSA 541-A:30-a REVISION NOTE #1: Document #5469, effective 9-17-92 readopted with amendments Chapter Env-WC 200, which made extensive changes to the wording and numbering. The filings prior to Document #5469 are as follows: #4657, effective 7-28-89 #5351, effective 3-11-92 REVISION NOTE #2: Document #13923, effective 4-11-24, readopted with amendments and renumbered the former Chapter Env-WC 100 and Chapter Env-WC 200 as Chapter Ec-Wtr 100 and Chapter Ec-Wtr 200. Document #13923 also changed the title of the former Chapter Env-WC 100 from “Organizational Rules” to “Definitions, Organization, and Public Information”. The title of the former Chapter Env-WC 200 remained as “Procedural Rules”. The renumbering of rules with the subtitle “Env-WC” to “Ec-Wtr” was done pursuant to a rules redesignation plan for the Council’s rules approved by the Director of the Office of Legislative Services in 2021. Document #13923 replaced all prior filings affecting the rules in the former Chapter Env-WC 100 and Chapter Env-WC 200. The prior filings since Document #5469 which affected the rules in the former Chapter Env-WC 200 included the following documents. Italics indicate the rules were subject to expiration only pursuant to RSA 541-A:17, II: #6860, INTERIM eff 10-1-98, EXPIRED 1-29-99 #7011, eff 6-9-99 #8892, INTERIM eff 5-31-07, EXPIRED 12-6-07 #8976 eff 9-14-07 #8977-A, eff 9-14-07 (Env-WC 201-Env-WC 206 only) #8977-B, eff 9-14-07 (Env-WC 207 only) As practice and procedure rules, the rules in Chapter Ec-Wtr 200 in Document #13923 will not expire except pursuant to RSA 541-A:17, II.

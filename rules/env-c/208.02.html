Env-C 208.02 Applicability

Effective 2015-03-24.

Env-C 208.02 Applicability. This part shall apply to any person submitting information to the department who wishes to assert a claim of confidentiality for some or all of the information being submitted. Source. #6960, eff 3-25-99; ss by #8851-B, eff 3-25-07; ss by #10802-A, eff 3-24-15

Env-C 205.04 Notice

Effective 2007-03-25.

Env-C 205.04 Notice. Notice of the date, time, and place of an oral public hearing shall be given as follows: (a) For a rulemaking hearing held pursuant to RSA 541-A:3, IV, by publication as specified in RSA 541-A:6; (b) For an oral public hearing held on a license application, as specified in the department’s rules specific to such license; or (c) For any other oral public hearing, by such means as the commissioner determines will notify those persons likely to be interested in the most cost-effective manner. Source. #5860, eff 7-1-94; ss by #6960, eff 3-25-99; ss by #8851-A, eff 3-25-07

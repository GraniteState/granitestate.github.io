Env-C 802.16 Protected species

Effective 2026-01-19. Expires 2036-01-19.

Env-C 802.16 “Protected species” means both protected plant species and protected wildlife species. Source. #14374, INTERIM, eff 8-26-25; ss by #14473 (formerly Env-C 802.13), eff 1-19-26, EXPIRES 1-19-36

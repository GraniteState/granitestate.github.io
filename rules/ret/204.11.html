Ret 204.11 Motion for Rehearing

Effective 2019-01-09.

Ret 204.11 Motion for Rehearing. (a) All motions for rehearing or reconsideration of a board of trustees' decision shall be in writing and filed within 30 days of the date of the order of notice of the board's decision. (b) All motions for rehearing or reconsideration shall fully set forth every ground upon which a party claims the board’s decision is unlawful or unreasonable. (c) A motion for rehearing shall suspend the order or decision complained of until such time as it has been acted upon by the board of trustees. Source. #7477, eff 4-10-01; ss by #7572, eff 10-10-01; ss by #12709, eff 1-9-19 (formerly Ret 204.10)

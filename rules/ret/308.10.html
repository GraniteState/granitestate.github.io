Ret 308.10 Notice Of Finding On Petition For Relief Due To Financial Inability To Pay

Effective 2019-01-09. Earlier expiration 2018-10-08.

Ret 308.10 Notice Of Finding On Petition For Relief Due To Financial Inability To Pay. (a) The board of trustees or its designee shall act upon any petition for relief filed pursuant to Ret 308.08 in accordance with this section. (b) Upon a finding in favor of the eligible employee on a petition for relief filed pursuant to Ret 308.08, the board of trustees or its designee shall provide the eligible employee and the employer with a written notice of its finding. The employer may appeal and receive a hearing set forth in Ret 308.13. Within 45 days after the date of the notice the employer shall make a direct payment, pursuant to Ret 308.11, of the employer portion of the cost of prior service credit as determined in accordance with Ret 308.03(I). As a result of the receipt of a direct payment the eligible employee shall not receive credit for prior service for the time period for which the eligible employee was found eligible. (c) Upon a finding against the eligible employee on a petition for relief filed pursuant to Ret 308.08, the board of trustees or its designee shall provide the eligible employee and the employer with a written notice of its finding. The eligible employee may appeal and receive a hearing set forth in Ret 308.13. Upon a final administrative determination against the eligible employee, the eligible employee may make a lump sum payment or file an election to pay by installments in accordance with Ret 308.03(d) within 90 days after the date of the final administrative decision.

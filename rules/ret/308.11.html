Ret 308.11 Direct Payment

Effective 2019-01-09. Earlier expiration 2018-10-08.

Ret 308.11 Direct Payment. A direct payment made pursuant to Ret 308.10(a) shall be in the form of: (a) An immediate lump sum payment to the eligible employee, if the eligible employee, upon receipt of the prior service credit for which he or she had been found eligible, would be eligible for present or vested future retirement benefits pursuant to RSA 100-A:10; or (b) A lump sum payment held in an employer provided escrow account, if the eligible employee is in service with the employer and does not qualify for immediate payment as provided in (a) above.

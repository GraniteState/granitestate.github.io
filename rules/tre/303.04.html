Tre 303.04 Return of Report

Effective 2009-04-25. Expires 2009-10-22.

Tre 303.04 Return of Report. (a) Abandoned property reports submitted to the department shall be returned to the holder for correction when: (b) The report is not in form specified in Tre 303.01 (f) above; (c) The report fails to identify category(ies) of unclaimed or abandoned property contained in the report; (d) The report fails to provide the date of last transaction or first payable date for property reported; or (e) The report fails to include any other information required under the act. Source. #7435, eff 1-25-01, EXPIRED: 1-25-09 New. #9465, INTERIM, eff 4-25-09, EXPIRED: 10-22-09

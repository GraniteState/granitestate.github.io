Tre 303.02 Determination of Abandoned Property Due and Deliverable

Effective 2009-04-25. Expires 2009-10-22.

Tre 303.02 Determination of Abandoned Property Due and Deliverable. Upon examination of the properly completed and filed T-1 report(s) or as the result of an audit/examination, unclaimed or abandoned property shall be identified and deemed due. Source. #7435, eff 1-25-01 New. #9465, INTERIM, eff 4-25-09, EXPIRED: 10-22-09

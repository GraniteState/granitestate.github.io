Ph 704.02 Prospective Drug Review

Effective 2022-06-22. Earlier expiration 2013-03-26.

Ph 704.02 Prospective Drug Review. (a) A pharmacist shall review the patient record and each prescription presented for dispensing for purposes of identifying: (1) Over-utilization or under-utilization; (2) Therapeutic duplication; (3) Drug-disease contraindication; (4) Drug-drug interactions; (5) Incorrect drug dosage or duration of drug treatment; (6) Drug-allergy interactions; and (7) Clinical abuse or misuse. (b) Upon recognizing any of the above, the pharmacist shall take appropriate steps to avoid or resolve the problem which might include consultation with the prescriber.

Ph 812.01 Determination of a Certified Pharmacy Technician’s Duties

Effective 2018-11-17.

Ph 812.01 Determination of a Certified Pharmacy Technician’s Duties. (a) Any certified pharmacy technician who does not maintain national certification shall notify the board and the pharmacist–in-charge immediately of the lapse of certification. Those whose certification has lapsed shall be permitted to perform the duties of a registered pharmacy technician, but shall no longer perform the additional duties of a certified pharmacy technician. (b) A certified pharmacy technician shall only perform such tasks and duties which are prescribed by the permit holder or pharmacist-in-charge based upon the needs of the pharmacy. (c) A certified pharmacy technician’s duties may be further limited by the pharmacist on duty or the supervising pharmacist. (d) Any training given under the direction of a pharmacist shall be documented by the pharmacist-in-charge and be retrievable upon inspection. Source. #12671, eff 11-17-18

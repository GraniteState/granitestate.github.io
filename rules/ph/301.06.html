Ph 301.06 Issuance or Denial of Original License

Effective 2016-01-29. Earlier expiration 2015-04-21.

Ph 301.06 Issuance or Denial of Original License. (a) If candidate timely files an application, complete in all respects, successfully completes all examinations required by Ph 301 and demonstrates the complete fulfillment of the requirements of these rules, RSA 318, and RSA 318-B, the board shall issue a license to practice pharmacy. (b) In the event a candidate for an original license to practice pharmacy in New Hampshire fails to meet the requirements of these rules, or RSA 318, or both, the board shall deliver to the applicant a written denial of the application, specifying in detail the requirement which the candidate failed to meet, and how the candidate is deficient.

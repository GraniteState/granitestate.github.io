Ph 1202.02 Central prescription processing

Effective 2018-02-24. Earlier expiration 2017-05-16.

Ph 1202.02 “Central prescription processing” means “central prescription processing” as defined in RSA 318: 1, XXIII, namely, “the processing by a pharmacy of a request from another pharmacy to fill or refill a prescription drug order or to perform processing functions, such as dispensing, drug utilization review, claims adjudication, refill authorizations, and therapeutic interventions.” Source. #9469, eff 5-16-09, EXPIRED: 5-16-17 New. #12485, eff 2-24-18

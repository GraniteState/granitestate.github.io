Ph 306.02 Reporting Changes

Effective 2016-01-29. Earlier expiration 2015-04-21.

Ph 306.02 Reporting Changes. The person to whom a permit to operate a pharmacy in New Hampshire has been issued shall, within 15 days of that person's discovery of a change in any of the data contained in the application for an original or renewal permit, report that change to the board in writing. An original new permit application, form Ph B-1 revised September 2015 shall be completed and filed in addition to the written notice when the name, location, ownership, licensed area or pharmacist in charge of the pharmacy are changed.

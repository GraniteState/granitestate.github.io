Ph 705.04 Authorization for Destruction of Non-Inventory Controlled Substances

Effective 2026-10-13. Expires 2036-10-13.

Ph 705.04 Authorization for Destruction of Non-Inventory Controlled Substances. (a) Subject to (b) through (d), below, any person legally possessing non-inventory controlled substances who wishes to dispose of the substances shall request authorization from the board to destroy the substances in accordance with Ph 705.05. (b) A consultant pharmacist shall be deemed to be an agent of the board for purposes of destroying non-inventory controlled substances at the institution for which the individual serves as consultant pharmacist without requesting prior authorization under Ph 705.05. (c) A pharmacist at an institution having an on-site pharmacy other than, or in addition to, an automated pharmacy system shall be deemed to be an agent of the board for purposes of destroying non-inventory controlled substances at the institution for which the individual works as a pharmacist without requesting prior authorization under Ph 705.05. (d) An individual designated as the board’s agent and so authorized by (b) or (c), above, to destroy non-inventory controlled substances shall comply with Ph 705.06 relative to having a witness and making a record of the destruction, and may designate another responsible individual to conduct the actual destruction. Source. #14648, eff 10-13-26, EXPIRES: 10-13-36

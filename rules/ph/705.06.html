Ph 705.06 Requirements for Destruction of Non-Inventory Controlled Substances

Effective 2026-10-13. Expires 2036-10-13.

Ph 705.06 Requirements for Destruction of Non-Inventory Controlled Substances. (a) Once authorized: (1) The destruction shall be carried out by the individual who is authorized to do so or that individual’s designated agent; (2) The destruction shall be witnessed by a healthcare professional or individual listed in Ph 705.05(d); (3) A record of the non-inventory controlled substances destroyed shall be made on a “Record of Authorized Destruction of Controlled Substances” dated 7/2026 , hereinafter “Record”, more particularly described in (e), below; and (4) The individual who completed the destruction and the witness shall each legibly print their name and then sign and date the form attesting that the controlled substance has been identified and rendered non-retrievable. (b) Each signature provided pursuant to (a)(4), above, shall constitute the individual’s attestation that each of the identified controlled substances was rendered non-retrievable. (c) The original “Record” shall be: (1) Retained on the premises where the destruction occurred for not less than 2 years; and (2) Made available for review during an inspection. (d) The individual making the destruction may retain a copy of the “Record”. (e) The following information shall be recorded on the “Record”: (1) The date of the destruction; (2) The name and address of the facility at which the destruction is made; (3) The name of the individual who signed the request for authorization, and the individual’s license number if that individual is a healthcare professional; (4) The following information for each controlled substance destroyed: a. The name and dosage form of the drug; b. The quantity of the drug destroyed; c. The last name of the individual with whom the drug is associated; and d. The prescription number of the drug, if any and if available; and (5) The method of destruction and the time the destruction was made. (f) If the same method of destruction is not used for all of the substances being destroyed, a separate “Record” shall be completed for each method of destruction. (g) If more than 20 lines are needed because there are more than 20 combinations of drug name, dosage form, individual name, and prescription number, one or more separate “Records” shall be made for the additional lines needed.

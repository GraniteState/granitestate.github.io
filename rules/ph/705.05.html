Ph 705.05 Request for Destruction of Non-Inventory Controlled Substances

Effective 2026-10-13. Expires 2036-10-13.

Ph 705.05 Request for Destruction of Non-Inventory Controlled Substances. (a) If a request to destroy non-inventory controlled substances is required by Ph 705.04(a), the request shall be made in writing by completing and submitting the “Request for Authorization to Destroy Non-Inventory Controlled Substances”, revised 7/2026, that is signed by an individual listed in (d), below, then submitted to the office of professional licensure and certification (OPLC) at the address listed in Plc 103.03. (b) The written “Request for Authorization to Destroy Non-Inventory Controlled Substances” shall: (1) Identify the name and title of the individual requesting authorization; (2) Affirm that destruction of the substances will not impair law enforcement efforts or endanger the health or safety of any person; and (3) Acknowledge that Ph 705.06 requires the destruction to be witnessed by a healthcare provider or other individual listed in (d), below and requires a record to be made. (c) The destruction process shall not proceed until the board’s authorization is received by the person who made the request. (d) The request for authorization shall be signed by: (1) Permit holders or their designated agent(s); (2) Practitioners or their designated agent(s); (3) Institution administrators or their designated agent(s); (4) Agents of the superior court; (5) County attorneys or designated agent(s); (6) Director, New Hampshire state police, or designated agent(s); (7) Chiefs of local police departments or designated agent(s); or (8) Director, New Hampshire division of public health services or designated agent(s).

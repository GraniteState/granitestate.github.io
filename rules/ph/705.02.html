Ph 705.02 Part-Specific Definitions

Effective 2026-10-13. Expires 2036-10-13.

Ph 705.02 Part-Specific Definitions. For purposes of this part, the following definitions shall apply: (a) “Agent”, for purposes of RSA 318-B:17-a, means the individual authorized by the board under the provisions in this part to destroy controlled substances that are outdated, deteriorated, excessive, or otherwise unwanted or confiscated, whether authorized directly under Ph 705.04(b) or (c) or in response to a request filed pursuant to Ph 705.05; (b) “Consultant pharmacist” means a licensed pharmacist under contract to oversee medication management at an institution that does not operate a full-service, on-site pharmacy; (c) “Controlled substance” means any substance that is subject to control under RSA 318-B or the federal Controlled Substances Act, Title 21 U.S.C. § 801-903, or both; (d) “Destroy” when applied to a controlled substance means to render the controlled substance non-retrievable by any method authorized by the DEA; (e) “Institution” means a facility in New Hampshire that provides inpatient health care on a short-term or long-term basis or provides outpatient health care, or both. The term includes hospitals, nursing homes, extended care facilities, and hospice houses. The term also includes places whose primary purpose is not health care, such as educational institutions, correctional facilities, and military installations, if a pharmacy or an automated pharmacy system, or both, is on-site. The term does not include residential care facilities or assisted living facilities licensed pursuant to RSA 151; (f) “Inventory controlled substances” means controlled substances that were received legally in the normal course of healthcare, veterinary, or pharmaceutical operations that not do not fall within the definition of “non-inventory controlled substances”; (g) “Healthcare professional” means an individual holding a license issued under RSA 318, RSA 326-B, RSA 328-D, RSA 329, or RSA 332-B; and (h) “Non-inventory controlled substances” means controlled substances that were brought to an institution by or on behalf of the individual for whom they were prescribed and have expired or been abandoned by that individual whether as a result of discharge or death, or that were seized in the course of law enforcement activities other than pharmaceutical drug take-back programs established under RSA 318-E, or that cannot be returned to a reverse distributor due to damage to the container or packaging or any other reason. The term includes any outdated, deteriorated, excessive, or otherwise unwanted or confiscated controlled substances as included under RSA 318-B:17-a. Source. #14648, eff 10-13-26, EXPIRES: 10-13-36

Auc 103.01 Composition of the Board

Effective 2026-02-08.

Auc 103.01 Composition of the Board. (a) The board consists of members who meet the eligibility requirements of RSA 311-B:2. (b) The board shall elect a chair at its first regular meeting of the calendar year. Source. #6657, eff 1-1-98; ss by #12155, eff 4-5-17; ss by #14500, eff 2-8-26

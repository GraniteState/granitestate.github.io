Auc 103.02 Office Location, Mailing Address, and Telephone

Effective 2026-02-08.

Auc 103.02 Office Location, Mailing Address, and Telephone. (a) The board’s office is within the office of professional licensure and certification (OPLC), at mailing address N.H. Board of Auctioneers, c/o OPLC, 7 Eagle Square, Concord, N.H. 03301. (b) Correspondence, filings, and other communications intended for the board shall be addressed to the board at the location stated in (a) above. (c) The board’s telephone number is (603) 271-2152. Source. #6657, eff 1-1-98; ss by #12155, eff 4-5-17; ss by #14500, eff 2-8-26 (formerly Auc 103.03)

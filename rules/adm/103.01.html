Adm 103.01 Requests for Information

Effective 1999-08-31.

Adm 103.01 Requests for Information. Consistent with RSA 91-A, information relating to the department is available for public inspection during normal working hours. Information that is available for public inspection may be copied at a cost of $.20 per page to be paid by the person requesting the information.

Mid 403.06 Recertification of Midwives Ineligible for Recertification Under Mid 403.04

Effective 2016-11-10. Earlier expiration 2016-11-08.

Mid 403.06 Recertification of Midwives Ineligible for Recertification Under Mid 403.04. For midwives not eligible for recertification under Mid 403.04 because they do not meet the recency of practice requirements of Mid 403.04(b), the midwifery council shall reinstate a certificate if the applicant for recertification: (a) Has been provisionally certified pursuant to Mid 403.07; and (b) Within 78 weeks of having been provisionally certified has met the supervised midwifery practice requirements set forth in Mid 403.08 and Mid 403.09. Source. #9089, eff 2-20-08, EXPIRED: 2-20-16 New. #11095, INTERIM, eff 5-12-16, EXPIRED: 11-8-16 New. #12040, eff 11-10-16

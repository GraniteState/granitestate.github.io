Mid 406.05 Teaching Midwifery

Effective 2016-11-10. Earlier expiration 2016-11-08.

Mid 406.05 Teaching Midwifery. Documentation of midwifery teaching shall include: (a) The name of the course(s) taught; and (b) A letter of confirmation signed by an official of the institution sponsoring the courses.

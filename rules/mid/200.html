Mid 200 PROCEDURAL RULES

CHAPTER Mid 200 PROCEDURAL RULES REVISION NOTE: Document #14497, effective 2-8-26, repealed Part Mid 201 through Part Mid 217 in Chapter Mid 200, titled “Procedural Rules”. This action was taken pursuant to 2023, 212:23, effective October 3, 2023, which repealed RSA 326-D:4, I(b) and (c), RSA 326-D:5, III, and RSA 326-D:8, I and III. Document #14497 also adopted a new Part Mid 201 titled “Applicability and Waiver of Substantive Rules”, containing Mid 201.01 titled “Applicability of Plc 200.” Document #14497 replaces all prior filings affecting the rules in the former Chapter Mid 200. The prior filings included the following documents. Italics indicate the rules were subject to expiration only pursuant to RSA 541-A:17, II: #8841, eff 3-13-07, EXPIRED: 3-13-15 (Mid 203, Mid 213, and Mid 217) #8842, eff 3-13-07 (Mid 201, Mid 202, Mid 204-Mid 212, and Mid 214-Mid 216) #11094, INTERIM, eff 5-12-16, EXPIRED: 11-8-16 (Mid 203, Mid 213, and Mid 217) #12039, eff 11-10-16 (Mid 203, Mid 213, and Mid 217) #12939-A, eff 12-10-19 (Mid 203) As practice and procedure rules, the rules in Document #14497 will not expire except pursuant to RSA 541-A:17, II.

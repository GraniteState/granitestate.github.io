Mid 407.07 Compliance with Laws

Effective 2016-11-10. Earlier expiration 2016-11-08.

Mid 407.07 Compliance with Laws. A midwife shall comply with applicable New Hampshire laws, including but not limited to: (a) The requirements of RSA 332-I relating to medical records and patient information; (b) Rules of the bureau of vital records regarding the reporting of births; (c) Rules of the bureau of vital records regarding the reporting of deaths; (d) The reporting requirements of RSA 132:6, II and any rules relating to prevention of eye inflammation which have been adopted by the commissioner of the department of health and human services; and (e) The requirements of RSA 132:10-a and RSA 132:10-aa relating to newborn screening tests. Source. #8840, eff 3-13-07, EXPIRED: 3-13-15 New. #11095, INTERIM, eff 5-12-16, EXPIRED: 11-8-16 New. #12040, eff 11-10-16 APPENDIX

Liq 302.08 Depletion of Delisted Products

Effective 2024-07-03.

Liq 302.08 Depletion of Delisted Products. Upon delisting of a product for any reason provided in these rules, the liquor and wine representative or manufacturer shall maintain any depletion allowance through the sale of the remaining inventory in state liquor stores to support the delist pricing the commission places on the product to deplete any remaining inventory. Source. (See Revision Note #1 and Revision Note #2 at chapter heading for Liq 300) #14018, eff 7-3-24

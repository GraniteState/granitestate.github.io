Liq 501.15 Transfers of Location

Effective 2022-10-13. Earlier expiration 2004-11-28.

Liq 501.15 Transfers of Location. (a) All petitions pursuant to RSA 178:25 for transfer of a club license to a location owned or controlled by the licensee that are received in the commission's offices at least 5 business days prior to the event and meet the requirements of RSA 178:25 and any other statutory requirements that apply shall be granted. (b) Transfers shall not exceed 3 consecutive days and 6 total days for any organization during their license year. Source.

Liq 205.08 Limitations

Effective 2024-05-31.

Liq 205.08 Limitations. (a) Prosecutions shall be commenced within the following time periods: (1) Violations, 2 years; (2) Aggravated violations that endanger the public or result in serious bodily injury, 2 years; and (3) Aggravated violations involving a fatality, 3 years provided that the violation was not discovered, and could not reasonably be discovered, by the division within 2 years. (b) Time shall begin to run on the day after a violation occurs or, in the case of a violation comprised of a continuous course of conduct, on the day after that conduct or the licensee’s complicity thereinterminates. (c) A prosecution shall be commenced on the day when a recommendation of commission action is served on the licensee. Source. (See Revision Note #1 and Revision Note #2 at chapter heading for Liq 200) #13992, eff 5-31-24

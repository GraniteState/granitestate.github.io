Liq 505.04 Records to be Maintained

Effective 2022-10-13. Earlier expiration 2004-11-28.

Liq 505.04 Records to be Maintained. (a) One-day licensees shall maintain records for one year showing the amount of money derived from the sale of beverage or liquor or both, the expense incurred in making such sales and the distribution of profits realized from said sales. (b) Said records shall be made available for audit on request by the commission, its examiners, or investigators at all times subsequent to the issuance of a license. Source.

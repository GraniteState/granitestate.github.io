CHAPTER Liq 400 RULES GOVERNING LICENSEE OPERATIONS Statutory Authority: 176:14 REVISION NOTE Document #5043, effective 1-11-91, made extensive changes to the wording and format of Chapter Liq 400. Document #5043 supersedes all prior filings for the sections in this chapter. The filings prior to Document #5043 for former Liq 400 include the following documents: #727, eff 12-18-75 #2137, eff 9-30-82 #1251, eff 9-27-78 #2594, eff 1-20-84 #1532, eff 2-13-80 #2742, eff 6-14-84 #1582, eff 5-25-80 #2931, eff 12-26-84 #1833, eff 10-13-81 #4041, eff 4-22-86 #4548, eff 12-19-88 All of Chapter Liq 400 except for the rules in former Sections Liq 402.10, 402.13, and 404.13 expired on 12-26-90. The rules in former Sections Liq 402.10, 402.13 and 404.13 were repealed by Document #5043. Many rules in Chapter Liq 400 filed as part of Document #5043 are amended and renumbered version of former rules in this chapter.

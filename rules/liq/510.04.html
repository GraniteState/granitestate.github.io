Liq 510.04 Age Verification

Effective 2022-10-13.

Liq 510.04 Age Verification. (a) Alcohol consultant licensees shall verify that all individuals attending an educational event are 21 years of age or over. (b) Attendees shall provide one of the acceptable forms of identifications listed in RSA 179:8 to show that such person is 21 years of age or over. Source. #10742, eff 12-10-14; ss by #13461, eff 10-13-22

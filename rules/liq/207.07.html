Liq 207.07 Evidence

Effective 2024-05-31.

Liq 207.07 Evidence. (a) The presiding officer shall consider evidence as provided in RSA 541-A:33, including but not limited to: (1) Police logs and reports; (2) Documented complaints made to: a. Local or state police; b. Other local officials; or c. State officials or the commission; (3) Orders, correspondence, or other documents from town officials to the licensee and any responses; (4) Police and court records concerning the licensee, licensed premise, or persons in charge; (5) Petitions or other statements by area residents or neighboring businesses; (6) Commission records of licensee conduct; and (7) Records demonstrating licensee non-compliance with: a. Zoning requirements; b. Health or safety requirements; or c. Other local or state ordinances. (b) The licensee may introduce exculpatory evidence for the presiding officer’s consideration. Source. (See Revision Note #1 and Revision Note #2 at chapter heading for Liq 200) #13992, eff 5-31-24

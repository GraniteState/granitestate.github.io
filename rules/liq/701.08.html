Liq 701.08 License Application Fees

Effective 2020-09-25.

Liq 701.08 License Application Fees. (a) Each new applicant for a one day, a beer festival, a liquor and wine festival, an alcohol consultant, or a liquor and wine representative license shall pay a processing and investigation fee of $25.00. (b) Each new applicant for any other type of license, except a special or supplemental license, shall pay a processing and investigation fee of $100.00. Source. (See Revision Notes #1 and #2 at chapter heading for Liq 700) #13114, eff 9-25-20

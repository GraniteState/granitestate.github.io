Liq 1106.03 Licensee Notification

Effective 2020-01-03.

Liq 1106.03 Licensee Notification. The direct shipping unit shall notify the licensee in writing whether or not the request for purchase was approved, and of any conditions that the commission attaches to the approval issued. A copy of the notice shall be sent to the direct shipper(s) named in the request.

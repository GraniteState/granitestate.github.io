Liq 101.02 Definitions

Effective 2023-04-25. Earlier expiration 1990-12-26.

Liq 101.02 Definitions. The words and phrases used in these rules shall mean and be construed as follows, except where a different meaning is clearly intended from the context: (a) “Commission” means the New Hampshire liquor commission; (b) “Chairman” means the executive director of the commission, also known as the liquor commissioner; (c) “Deputy commissioner” means the person appointed to exercise authority over the divisions of the commission subject to the supervision of the chairman; and (d) “New Hampshire liquor commission records” means governmental records as defined in RSA 91-A:1-a, III. The term also includes “commission records”.

Fors 401.04 Denial of Renewal

Effective 2024-11-24.

Fors 401.04 Denial of Renewal. Renewal shall be denied if the OPLC finds: (a) Noncompliance with the continuing education requirements of Fors 403.01; (b) Any unethical act for which discipline shall be imposed under Fors 500; (c) Any acts of misconduct as described RSA 310-A:112; or (d) Failure to furnish complete or accurate information on an initial or renewal license application.

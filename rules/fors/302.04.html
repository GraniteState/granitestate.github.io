Fors 302.04 References Required

Effective 2024-11-24.

Fors 302.04 References Required. Each applicant for licensure shall provide the OPLC with the names, addresses, e-mail addresses, and phone numbers of not fewer than 5 individuals, as references, not related to the applicant, of whom 3 or more shall be individuals having personal or professional knowledge of the applicant's forestry experience and 2 of these being foresters as defined by RSA 310-A:99, and one forester reference shall be an individual not connected with the applicant’s current place of employment. Source. #6876, eff 10-28-98; ss by #8710, eff 8-25-06; ss by #10544, eff 3-13-14; ss by #14085, eff 11-24-24

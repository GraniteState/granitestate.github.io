Fors 103.01 Duties and Responsibilities

Effective 1998-12-22. Earlier expiration 1998-02-05.

Fors 103.01 Duties and Responsibilities. The board shall administer the provisions of RSA 310-A:98-117 which include but are not limited to: (a) Establishing and maintaining a high standard of integrity, skills and practice in the profession of forestry; (b) Administering the application procedure for obtaining a license; (c) Evaluating qualifications of applicants; (d) Administering examination procedures; (e) Administering renewal procedures and continuing education requirements; (f) Establishing ethical and professional standards; and (g) Implement disciplinary actions. Source. #5222, eff 9-11-91; EXPIRED 9-11-97 New. #6597, INTERIM, eff 10-8-97, EXPIRED: 2-5-98 New. #6915, eff 12-22-98

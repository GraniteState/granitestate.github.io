Fors 402.04 Procedures for Assessing and Collecting Fines

Effective 2024-11-24. Earlier expiration 1998-02-05.

Fors 402.04 Procedures for Assessing and Collecting Fines. (a) Payment of a fine shall be included among the options available for settling disciplinary allegations, and shall be included among the types of disciplinary sanctions imposed after notice and hearing. (b) In cases where the board initially intends to limit disciplinary sanctions to an administrative fine, the board shall issue a notice in compliance with Plc 311.12(c). (c) Nonpayment of a fine by a licensee or respondent in contravention of an order, agreement, or promise to pay, shall be a separate ground for discipline by the board, or a basis for denying a subsequent license or renewal application, or a basis for judicial action seeking to collect the fine.

Fors 200 RULES OF PRACTICE AND PROCEDURE

CHAPTER Fors 200 RULES OF PRACTICE AND PROCEDURE REVISION NOTE: Document #13990, effective 7-28-24, repealed Part Fors 201 through Part Fors 218 in Chapter Fors 200, titled “Practice and Procedure”, and renamed the chapter as “Rules of Practice and Procedure”. Document #13990 also adopted a new Part Fors 201 titled “Applicability and Waiver of Substantive Rules”, containing Fors 201.01 titled “Rules of Practice and Procedure” and Fors 201.02 titled “Waiver of Administrative Rules.” Document #13990 replaces all prior filings affecting the rules in the former Chapter Fors 200. The prior filings included the following documents. Italics indicate the rules were subject to expiration only pursuant to RSA 541-A:17, II: #5222, eff 9-11-91, EXPIRED 9-11-97 #6597, INTERIM, eff 10-8-97, EXPIRED 2-5-98 #6876, eff 10-28-98 #8711-A, eff 8-25-06 #8711-B, eff 8-25-06 (Fors 217 only) #10543, eff 3-13-14 (Fors 217 only) #13582, eff 5-20-23 (Fors 218 only) As practice and procedure rules, the rules in Document #13990 will not expire except pursuant to RSA 541-A:17, II.

Fors 301.01 Application Process

Effective 2024-03-17. Earlier expiration 1998-02-05.

Fors 301.01 Application Process. (a) Persons wishing to become licensed as a forester in New Hampshire shall complete and submit the “Application for Licensure as a Forester” in compliance with Fors 301.02, and the application fee specified in Plc 1002.16. (b) An unsigned application not accompanied by the application fee, shall not be accepted, and shall be returned to the applicant. (c) The office of professional licensure and certification (“OPLC”) shall acknowledge receipt of a filed application within 30 days and shall notify the applicant of any deficiencies in the application and any additional documents required to complete the application. (d) The OPLC shall take action on an application within 60 days of the submission of a completed application, in accordance with the requirements of Fors 302 to Fors 304 as follows: (1) Grant the application for licensure pursuant to Fors 302 or Fors 304; (2) Review the application and request any further information needed to clarify the applicant’s qualifications; (3) Conditionally grant the application pending successful passage of the examination pursuant to Fors 303.01; or (4) Deny the application until the issues identified in the denial order are resolved. (e) If the application is denied, the applicant shall be provided an opportunity to request a hearing for reconsideration regarding the denial issues identified by the OPLC. The applicant shall request a hearing in writing within 30 days.

Env-WC 207.01 Explanation of Rules

Effective 2007-09-14.

Env-WC 207.01 Explanation of Rules. (a) As specified in RSA 541-A:11, VII, any interested person may, at any time before 30 days after final adoption of a rule by the council, request the council to issue an explanation of the rule that it has adopted. (b) The request shall be: (1) In writing; and (2) Filed in accordance with Env-WC 202.06(a). (c) As specified in RSA 541-A:11, VII, the explanation shall include: (1) A concise statement of the principal reasons for and against the adoption of the rule in its final form; and (2) An explanation of why the council overruled the arguments and considerations against the rule. (d) The explanation shall be issued within 30 days following the first council meeting after receipt of the request. Source. #8977-B, eff 9-14-07 APPENDIX

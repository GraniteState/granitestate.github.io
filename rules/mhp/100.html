Mhp 100 DEFINITIONS, ORGANIZATION AND PUBLIC INFORMATION

CHAPTER Mhp 100 DEFINITIONS, ORGANIZATION AND PUBLIC INFORMATION REVISION NOTE. Pursuant to 1992, 280:4, effective 1-1-93, the Board of Examiners of Psychologists became the Board of Examiners of Psychology and Mental Health Practice. Document #5675, effective 7-22-93, made extensive changes to the wording, format, and numbering of the rules of the former Board of Examiners of Psychology and Mental Health Practice (Board), which were readopted with amendments as Chapters Psy 100 through Psy 500 in Document #5675. Document #5675 superseded all prior filings for the rules of the Board. The prior filings for the rules of the Board, formerly the Board of Examiners of Psychologists, included the following documents: #1353, eff 5-14-79 #1976, eff 3-10-82 #2679, EMERGENCY, eff 3-21-84 #2731, eff 5-31-84 #4289, eff 7-6-87 #5358, eff 3-16-92 The Board of Mental Health Practice (rule title “Mhp”) was established pursuant to 1998, 234:1, effective 10-31-98, which abolished the former Board of Examiners of Psychology and Mental Health Practice. Pursuant to 1998, 234:18, effective 10-31-98, all existing effective rules of the (Board of Examiners of Psychology and Mental Health Practice rule title “Psy”) in Document #5675 adopted under the former RSA 330-A were transferred to the Board of Mental Health Practice and continued in effect until they expired 7-22-99. See the source notes for subsequent Documents affecting the rules of the Board of Mental Health Practice.

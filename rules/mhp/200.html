Mhp 200 PRACTICE AND PROCEDURE

CHAPTER Mhp 200 PRACTICE AND PROCEDURE REVISION NOTE: Document #14181, effective 2-20-25, repealed Part Mhp 201 through Part Mhp 214 in Chapter Mhp 200, titled “Practice and Procedures”. Document #14181 also adopted a new Part Mhp 201 titled “Rules of Practice and Procedure”, containing Mhp 201.01 titled “Rules of Practice and Procedure.” Document #14181 replaces all prior filings affecting the rules in the former Chapter Mhp 200. The prior filings included the following documents: #7624, eff 1-10-02, EXPIRED: 1-10-10 #9853-A, eff 1-25-11 #9853-B, eff 1-25-11 #10745, eff2-20-25 As practice and procedure rules, the rules in Document #14181 will not expire except pursuant to RSA 541-A:17, II.

Mirt 306.01 titled “Post-Graduate Temporary Licenses.”

Mirt 306.01 titled “Post-Graduate Temporary Licenses.” The prior filing affecting the deleted Mirt 305.02, Mirt 305.03, and Mirt 306.01 was Document #12998, effective 3-5-20. The amendments in Document #13329 also included the renumbering of Mirt 306.02 and Mirt 306.03 as, respectively, Mirt 305.02 under the amended heading “Basic Eligibility Requirements for Temporary Licenses” and Mirt 305.03 under the amended heading “Expiration of Temporary Licenses.” The readoption with amendments of Part Mirt 305 and Mirt 306 as a single Part Mirt 305 by Document #13329 necessitated the renumbering of the existing Part Mirt 307 titled “Fees” as Part Mirt 306, but Mirt 307 was not readopted by Document #13229. The former rule numbers of renumbered rules are indicated in the source notes of the repealed rules. Pursuant to 2022, 104:1 and 104:4, effective 7-26-22, the Board of Medical Imaging and Radiation Therapy was renamed and re-established as the Advisory Board of Medical Imaging and Radiation Therapy under RSA 328-J:7, I and RSA 328-J:3. The former rulemaking authority of the Board of Medical Imaging and Radiation Therapy was transferred to the Executive Director of the Office of Professional Licensure and Certification (OPLC) under RSA 328-J:7. The Executive Director of the OPLC subsequently readopted with amendments Mirt 303.05, Mirt 303.07, Mirt 303.08, and Mirt 303.09 in Document #13431, effective 8-11-22, on initial licensure eligibility requirements. The Executive Director adopted Chapter Plc 1300 governing medical imaging and radiation therapy as an interim rule in Document #13548, effective 2-1-23. The Executive Director subsequently adopted and filed Document #13586, effective 3-22-33, repealing Chapter Mirt 100 through Chapter Mirt 500 of the former Board of Medical Imaging and Radiation Therapy. The repeal of Chapter Mirt 100 through Chapter Mirt 500 meant that Document #13548 containing Chapter Plc 1300 replaced all prior filings affecting rules of the former Board of Medical Imaging and Radiation Therapy. The filings prior to Document #13586 which affected the former Chapter Mirt 300 included the following documents: #12597, effective 7-31-18 #12998, effective 3-5-20 #13329, effective 1-22-22 #13431, effective 8-11-22 The Executive Director subsequently readopted with amendment Chapter Plc 1300 as a regular rule in Document #13701, effective 8-1-23.

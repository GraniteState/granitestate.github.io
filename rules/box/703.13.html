Box 703.13 Weigh-ins

Effective 2024-08-27.

Box 703.13 Weigh-ins. Weigh-ins for both professional and amateur MMA fighters shall be held the night before competition at a location agreed upon by the commission and the promoter. Weigh-ins shall be scheduled with the commission at least one month prior to the event. Source. #10483, eff 12-5-13; ss by #14066 eff 8-27-24

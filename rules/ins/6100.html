Chapter Ins 6100 had formerly been numbered as Part Ins 1906 titled “Discontinuance and Replacement of Group Accident and Health Coverage” in Chapter Ins 1900 titled “Accident and Health Insurance.” Former Part Ins 1906 expired 6-12-14. The filing affecting the former Ins 1906 was Document #8646, effective 6-12-06. REVISION NOTE #2: Document #12560, effective 6-25-18, adopted Part Ins 6102 titled “Replacement of Ancillary Health Coverage”.

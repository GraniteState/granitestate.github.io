Ins 4703.07 Prohibited Policy Provisions, Exclusions, and Limitations

Effective 2024-05-29.

Ins 4703.07 Prohibited Policy Provisions, Exclusions, and Limitations. An inland marine travel insurance policy shall not contain provisions that: (a) Guarantee policy renewal; (b) Charge a penalty or administrative fee for trip cancellation by the covered individual; (c) Retain unearned premium payments when the trip is cancelled or terminated by the group policyholder or its affiliates; or (d) Reduce coverage based on the age of the insured. Source. #10681, eff 11-3-14; ss by #13989, eff 5-29-24

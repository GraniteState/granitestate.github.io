Ins 1501.09 Amendments to Form A

Effective 2023-07-21. Earlier expiration 2007-05-01.

Ins 1501.09 Amendments to Form A. The applicant shall promptly advise the commissioner of any changes in the information furnished on Form A arising subsequent to the date upon which the information was furnished but prior to the commissioner's disposition of the application.

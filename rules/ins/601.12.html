Ins 601.12 Other Security

Effective 2021-11-24. Earlier expiration 2003-09-07.

Ins 601.12 Other Security. A ceding insurer may take credit for unencumbered funds withheld by the ceding insurer in the United States subject to withdrawal solely by the ceding insurer and under its exclusive control. Source. (See Revision Note at chapter heading for Ins 600) #6090, eff 9-7-95, EXPIRED: 9-7-03 New. #8240, eff 1-3-05; ss by #8827, eff 3-1-07; ss by #10449, eff 11-1-13 (from Ins 601.11); ss by #13296, eff 11-24-21 (formerly Ins 601.11)

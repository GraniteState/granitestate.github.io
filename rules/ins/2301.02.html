Ins 2301.02 Definitions

Effective 2017-07-10. Earlier expiration 2008-08-01.

Ins 2301.02 Definitions. (a) “Administrator" means “third party administrator (TPA)" as defined in RSA 402-H:1, I. (b) "Affiliate" means “affiliate” as defined in RSA 402-H:1, II. The term includes “affiliated”. (c) "Commissioner" means the commissioner of insurance. (d) "Control" means "control" as defined in RSA 401-B:1, III. (e) "Employee Retirement Security Act of 1974" means "ERISA", Pub.L. 93-406, 88 Stat. 829, effective September 2, 1974. (f) "Insurer" means "insurer" as defined in RSA 402-H:1, VII. (g) "Person" means an individual or business entity. (h) "Underwrites" means “underwrites” as defined in RSA 402-H:1, XIII. The term includes underwriting.

Ins 301.07 Suitability of Recommendation or Sale

Effective 2023-07-21. Earlier expiration 2009-02-16.

Ins 301.07 Suitability of Recommendation or Sale. Reasonable inquiry shall be made by insurers and producers to determine the suitability of any recommendations or sales, and all replacement of life insurance policies shall comply with Ins 302.

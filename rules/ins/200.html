CHAPTER Ins 200 PRACTICES AND PROCEDURES Statutory Authority: RSA 400-A:15, I; RSA 541-A:30-a, I REVISION NOTE: Document #13073-A, effective 7-27-20, readopted with amendments Chapter Ins 200.

Ins 601.08 Credit for Reinsurance Required by Law

Effective 2021-11-24. Earlier expiration 2003-09-07.

Ins 601.08 Credit for Reinsurance Required by Law. Pursuant to RSA 405:47, V, the commissioner shall allow credit for reinsurance ceded by a domestic insurer to an assuming insurer not meeting the requirements of RSA 405:47, I, II, III, IV, IV-a, or IV-b, but only as to the insurance of risks located in jurisdictions where such reinsurance is required by the applicable law or regulation of that jurisdiction. As used in this section, "jurisdiction" means state, district or territory of the United States and any lawful national government. Source.

Ins 3902.06 Mandatory Disclosures

Effective 2026-02-07. Expires 2036-02-07.

Ins 3902.06 Mandatory Disclosures. The insurer shall disclose the following information to the insured at the time of the issuance of coverage: (a) The premium for the initial term of the insurance coverage; (b) The term of the insurance coverage; (c) The number of weekly payments payable to the insured under the policy, and any limitations on the amount of such payments; (d) A detailed description how any waiting period, exclusionary period, or elimination period affects benefits payable under the policy; and (e) The specific requirements that shall be met if the insured wishes to cancel the policy. Source. #10863, eff 8-1-15; ss by #14507, eff 2-7-26, EXPIRES: 2-7-36

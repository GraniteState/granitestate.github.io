Ins 3506.04 Minimum Reserves

Effective 2017-07-25.

Ins 3506.04 Minimum Reserves. (a) The minimum reserves during the secondary guarantee period are the greater of: (1) The basic reserves for the secondary guarantee plus the deficiency reserve, if any, for the secondary guarantees; or (2) The minimum reserves required by other rules or rules governing universal life plans. For sex-blended mortality tables, compute select mortality factors in the same proportion as the underlying mortality.

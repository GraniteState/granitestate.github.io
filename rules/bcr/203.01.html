Bcr 203.01 Failure to Comply with Rules

Effective 2012-07-13. Earlier expiration 2012-07-16.

Bcr 203.01 Failure to Comply with Rules. (a) Failure to comply with the rules of this chapter shall result in the board: (1) Refusing to accept or admit a noncompliant document for filing or refusing to consider a noncompliant oral petition or motion; or (2) Accepting or admitting, or denying or not admitting a noncompliant application, petition, motion or exhibit on the condition that conformity with specific procedural requirements be achieved by a specified date. (b) When a noncompliant pleading or other tendered information is not accepted or admitted by the board, or when conditions for the acceptance or admission of noncompliant information are not met, the board shall make a decision on the pending matter without considering the noncompliant information, unless the board notifies the parties that it has waived the rule in accordance with Bcr 203.02.

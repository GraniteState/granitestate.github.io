Bcr 215.10 Appeal Decisions

Effective 2012-07-13.

Bcr 215.10 Appeal Decisions. (a) All decisions rendered by the board shall be made on the basis of the record before the board. (b) Board members who hear an appeal, and who are not recused for good cause, shall not abstain from deciding the appeal. (c) The board may affirm or reverse the decision of the licensing board, or remand the case back to the licensing board for action consistent with the board’s decision. (d) The board shall set forth its decision in writing, with findings of fact and conclusions of law. Source. #8323, eff 4-12-05; ss by #10161, eff 7-13-12

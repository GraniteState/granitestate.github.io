Bcr 215.02 Definitions

Effective 2018-09-18.

Bcr 215.02 Definitions. For the purpose of this part, the following terms shall be defined as follows: (a) “Evidence” means all information, other than oral argument, submitted and considered by the board as part of the appeal record. (b) “Licensing board” means the electricians’ board, established under RSA 319-C:4, or the board of home inspectors, established under RSA 310-A:186. Source. #8323, eff 4-12-05; ss by #10161, eff 7-13-12; amd by #12620, eff 9-18-18

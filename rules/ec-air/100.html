Ec-Air 100 DEFINITIONS, ORGANIZATION AND PUBLIC INFORMATION

CHAPTER Ec-Air 100 DEFINITIONS, ORGANIZATION AND PUBLIC INFORMATION Statutory Authority: RSA 21-O:14, IV; RSA 541-A:16, I(a); and RSA 541‑A:30-a Revision Note: Document #13642, effective 5-23-23, readopted with amendments former Part Env-AC 201 and Part Env-AC 202 in Chapter Env-AC 200 titled “Procedural Rules” of the Air Resources Council and renumbered the rules as Chapter Ec-Air 100 on organizational rules titled “Definitions, Organization and Public Information.” The renumbering of rules with the subtitle “Env-AC” to “Ec-Air” was done pursuant to a rules redesignation plan for the Council’s rules approved by the Director of the Office of Legislative Services in 2021. There were no prior filings for the former Chapter Env-AC 100. The prior filings for the former Part Env-AC 201 and Part Env-Ac 202 included the following documents: #6586, eff 9-23-97 #8267-A, eff 1-25-05 As organizational rules Chapter Ec-Air 100 in Document #13642 will not expire except pursuant to RSA 541-A:17, II.

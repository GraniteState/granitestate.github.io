Pod 103.04 Office Hours, Office Location, Mailing Address, and Telephone

Effective 2025-09-19.

Pod 103.04 Office Hours, Office Location, Mailing Address, and Telephone. (a) The board's offices are located at: Board of Podiatry Office of Professional Licensure and Certification 7 Eagle Square Concord, New Hampshire 03301. (b) The board’s offices are open to the public Monday through Friday, excluding holidays, from 8:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m. (c) Correspondence shall be addressed to the board at the location stated in Pod 103.04(a). (d) The board's telephone number is (603) 271-2152. Source. #7816, eff 1-7-03; ss by #14372, eff 9-19-25

Pod 401.04 Late Filing

Effective 2026-02-08. Expires 2036-02-08.

Pod 401.04 Late Filing. (a) Pursuant to Plc 1001.13, there shall be no additional fee for late renewal applications. (b) A licensee who fails to renew their license not more than one year after such license has expired may complete the process for renewing a license specified in Plc 308.

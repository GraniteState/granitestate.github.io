Pod 501.01 Violations of APMA Ethical Guidelines

Effective 2026-07-01. Expires 2036-07-01.

Pod 501.01 Violations of APMA Ethical Guidelines. Violations of the “American Podiatric Medical Association, Inc. Code of Ethics”, March 2022, available as cited in Appendix II, shall constitute unprofessional conduct within the meaning of RSA 310:12, I(d).

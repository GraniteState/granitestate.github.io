Nur 303.04 Medication Nursing Assistant Examinations

Effective 2025-08-25. Expires 2035-08-25.

Nur 303.04 Medication Nursing Assistant Examinations. (a) Each person seeking licensure as a MNA and who graduated from a medication nursing assistant education program in New Hampshire or its equivalent shall take and pass a competency evaluation approved by the board and administered by an organization approved by the board. Approval of such examinations and organizations shall be determined by applying the criteria for each as set forth in Nur 800. (b) Only those who have completed a medication nursing assistant education program in New Hampshire or it’s equivalent shall take the examination.

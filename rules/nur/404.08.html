Nur 404.08 Obligations of Licensees as Delegatees

Effective 2014-04-17.

Nur 404.08 Obligations of Licensees as Delegatees. (a) A licensee to whom a specific task of client care has been delegated shall perform that task in accordance with RSA 326-B and these rules. (b) A delegatee who is a licensee shall wear a badge showing the delegatee’s name and title when performing the delegated task of client care. (c) A delegatee who is a licensee shall report to the board the fact of any delegation which the delegatee believes to be beyond his or her competency.

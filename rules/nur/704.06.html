Nur 704.06 Instructor Qualifications

Effective 2020-12-04. Expires 2021-06-02.

Nur 704.06 Instructor Qualifications. (a) The sponsoring institution shall submit for faculty approval for program instructors. The sponsoring institution shall complete and submit Form “Approval for Board Approved Education Programs,” 11-14-13; (b) The instructor shall: (1) Comply with Nur 704.04(b) (1) and Nur 704.04(b)(3); or (2) Comply with Nur 704.04(b)(2 and (3) and possess a current New Hampshire unencumbered licensed practical nurse license (LPN) or a multi-state LPN license issued by a compact state. Source. #8873, eff 4-24-07 (formerly Nur 706.06); ss by #9395, eff 2-25-09; ss by #10302, eff 3-22-13; ss by #10570, eff 4-17-14; ss by #13141, EMERGENCY RULE, eff 12-4-20, EXPIRED: 6-2-21

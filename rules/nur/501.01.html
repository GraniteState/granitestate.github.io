Nur 501.01 Obligation to Obey

Effective 2025-08-25. Expires 2035-08-25.

Nur 501.01 Obligation to Obey. (a) The ethical requirements set forth in this part shall bind all licensees and certificate holders and, as applicable, applicants for a license. (b) Violations of any of the rules in this part shall be included within the scope of the term "unprofessional conduct" as used in RSA 326-B:37, II(h) and shall result in disciplinary action by the board.

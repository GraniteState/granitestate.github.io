Nur 200 RULES OF PRACTICE AND PROCEDURE

CHAPTER Nur 200 RULES OF PRACTICE AND PROCEDURE Statutory Authority: RSA 326-B:12, RSA 326-B:13, RSA 326-B:14 REVISION NOTE: Document #13917, effective 3-29-24, repealed Part Nur 201 through Part Nur 214 in Chapter Nur 200, titled “Practice and Procedure”, and renamed the chapter as “Rules of Practice and Procedure”. Document #13917 also adopted a new Part Nur 201 titled “Practice and Procedure and Waiver of Rules”, containing Nur 201.01 titled “Rules of Practice and Procedure” and Nur 201.02 titled “Waiver of Administrative Rules.” Document #13917 replaces all prior filings affecting the rules in the former Chapter Nur 200. The prior filings included the following documents. Italics indicate the rules were subject to expiration only pursuant to RSA 541-A:17, II: #5887, eff 8-26-94 (See Revision Note #1 at chapter heading for Nur 100) #6778, eff 6-26-98 #7100, eff 9-20-99 (Nur 217.01 only) #8664, INTERIM, eff 6-24-06, EXPIRED: 12-21-06 #8778, eff 12-23-06 (Nur 201-Nur 210, Nur 211.01, Nur 212, and Nur 214 only) #8779, eff 12-23-06 (Nur 206.02, Nur 211.01-Nur 211.03, and Nur 213 only) #10297, eff 3-22-13 As practice and procedure rules, the rules in Document #13917 will not expire except pursuant to RSA 541-A:17, II.

Ac 503.01 Standards of Integrity and Objectivity

Effective 2025-04-05. Earlier expiration 2007-07-25.

Ac 503.01 Standards of Integrity and Objectivity. In the performance of any professional service, a licensee shall: (a) Maintain independence, objectivity, and integrity; (b) Be free of conflicts of interest; and (c) Not knowingly misrepresent facts or subordinate their judgment to others.

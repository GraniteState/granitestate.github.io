Ac 102.08 CPA firm

Effective 2012-01-01. Earlier expiration 2007-07-25.

Ac 102.08 “CPA firm” means “CPA firm” as defined by RSA 309-B:3, IV, namely, “a sole proprietorship, a corporation, a partnership including a limited liability partnership, a limited liability company including a professional limited liability company, or any other legally authorized association issued a license under RSA 309-B:8.” Source. #6901, eff 12-4-98; ss by #8810, INTERIM, eff 1-25-07, EXPIRED: 7-25-07 New. #9032, eff 11-21-07; ss by #10053, eff 1-1-12 (from Ac 101.07); renumbered by #13995 (formerly Ac 102.07)

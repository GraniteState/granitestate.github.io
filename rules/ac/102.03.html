Ac 102.03 Certified Public Accountant (CPA)

Effective 2012-01-01. Earlier expiration 2007-07-25.

Ac 102.03 “Certified Public Accountant (CPA)” means a person holding a current certificate issued under RSA 309-B:5, or a person holding an equivalent accounting license issued in any jurisdiction in the United States.

Rko 212.06 Waiver or Suspension of Rules by Presiding Officer

Effective 2024-05-20.

Rko 212.06 Waiver or Suspension of Rules by Presiding Officer. Upon the motion of any party or intervenor, or acting sua sponte, the presiding officer shall suspend or waive any requirement or limitation imposed by this chapter when the suspension or waiver: (a) Appears to be lawful; and (b) The presiding officer concludes that to do so would be more likely to promote the just, accurate, and efficient resolution of the pending dispute in accordance with the law than would adherence to the particular requirement or procedure sought to be waived. Source. #13921, eff 5-20-24 ART Rko 213 ADJUDICATIVE PROCEEDINGS

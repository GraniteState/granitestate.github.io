Rko 214.06 Requests for Rehearing or Reconsideration

Effective 2024-05-20.

Rko 214.06 Requests for Rehearing or Reconsideration. (a) No later than 30 days from the date appearing on a decision, a party may file with the ombudsman a request for rehearing or reconsideration. (b) Requests for rehearing or reconsideration shall: (1) Be in the form of a written motion addressed to the ombudsman; (2) Be delivered to the presiding officer, all persons who have filed an appearance in the case and any person who has filed an amicus brief; (3) Specify all grounds upon which it is claimed that the original decision is unlawful or unreasonable; and (4) Identify the issues to be addressed upon rehearing or reconsideration. Source. #13921, eff 5-20-24

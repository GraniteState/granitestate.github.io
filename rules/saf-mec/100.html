Saf-Mec 100 titled “Organizational Rules” was originally adopted by Document #10798, effective 3-8-15

Chapter Saf-Mec 100 titled “Organizational Rules” was originally adopted by Document #10798, effective 3-8-15. Pursuant to 2021, 91:82, effective 7-1-21, which amended RSA 153:27-a, the Mechanical Licensing Board was transferred from being a unit within the Division of Fire Safety of the Department of Safety to a unit within the Office of Professional Licensure and Certification (OPLC). The Board still had its own rulemaking authority under RSA 153:28, RSA 153:29, and RSA 153:29-a to adopt rules, but with the approval of the Commissioner of the Department of Safety. Pursuant to 2022, 314:21-23 and 25, effective 7-1-22, the Board still has its own rulemaking authority under RSA 153:28, RSA 153:29, and RSA 153:29-a to adopt rules but with the approval of the Executive Director of the OPLC. The existing rules of the Board were numbered with the rule prefix subtitle “Saf-Mec”, but all rules would eventually be renumbered with the rule prefix title “Mec” to indicate the transfer of the Board from the Department of Safety. Document #13483, effective 1-9-23, adopted various rules numbered with the rule prefix title “Mec” in Part Mec 102 titled “Definitions” of Chapter Mec 100. Document #13483 also repealed Saf-Mec 102.17 defining “supervision”, and readopted or readopted with amendment and renumbered various existing rules in Chapter Saf-Mec 100, including changing the prefix to “Mec”. The former prefix and, as applicable, the former numeral of the rule in Chapter Saf-Mec 100 are indicated in the source notes of the rules. The chapter heading, part headings, and all other existing rules in Chapter Saf-Mec 100 which were not in Document #13483 were subsequently renumbered with the rule prefix title “Mec” as editorial changes during the process pursuant to RSA 541-A:15, I of preparing the rules in Document #13483 for certification by the Board. The former rule numbers in Chapter Saf-Mec 100 are indicated in the source notes. As organizational rules the rules in Document #13483, and the rules in Document #10798 unaffected by Document #13483, will not expire except pursuant to RSA 541-A:17, II.

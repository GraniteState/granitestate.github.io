Saf-Mec 205.04 Prehearing Conference

Effective 2015-03-18.

Saf-Mec 205.04 Prehearing Conference. (a) A prehearing conference shall be scheduled on the request of any party or intervenor or on the initiative of the presiding officer if the presiding officer determines that to do so would facilitate the proceedings or encourage resolution of the dispute. (b) A prehearing conference shall address one or more of the following: (1) Offers of settlement; (2) Simplification of the issues; (3) By the consent of the parties, stipulations or admissions as to issues of fact or proof; (4) Limitations on the number of witnesses; (5) Changes to standard hearing procedures; (6) Consolidation of examination of witnesses; and (7) Any other matters that advance the efficiency of the proceedings. (c) All hearings before the board shall be open to the public. Source. #10798, eff 3-18-15

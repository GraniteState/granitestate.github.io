Bea 503.02 Role of the Program Advisory Committee

Effective 2024-08-08.

Bea 503.02 Role of the Program Advisory Committee. (a) The advisory committee established by the department pursuant to RSA 12-O:76 shall review and make recommendations on any proposed rules for the HC designation program and the municipal grant programs. (b) The advisory committee shall also review and provide feedback on program guidance, or amendments to rules before implementation of those changes. Source. #14043, eff 8-8-24

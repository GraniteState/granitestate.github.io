Bea 504.12 Reporting Requirements for Municipalities

Effective 2024-08-08.

Bea 504.12 Reporting Requirements for Municipalities. Municipalities that receive a New Hampshire HC designation shall report its housing-related activities in writing annually. Reports shall be submitted no later than 180 days after the initial HC designation award and then annually by July 1. The annual report shall include, but not be limited to: (a) Data regarding local land use board activities; (b) Data regarding the permitting, development, and approval for occupancy of new workforce housing and other housing necessary for the economic development of the state; and (c) A narrative describing the adoption, amendment, or repeal of land use regulations and ordinances, and how those changes support the goals of the HC designation program. Source. #14043, eff 8-8-24

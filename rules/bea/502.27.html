Bea 502.27 Land use regulations and ordinances

Effective 2024-08-08.

Bea 502.27 “Land use regulations and ordinances” means rules regulating how land might be used, including, but is not limited to, zoning ordinances adopted pursuant to RSA 674:16, innovative land use controls adopted pursuant to RSA 674:21, subdivision regulations adopted pursuant to RSA 674:35, and site plan regulations adopted pursuant to RSA 674:43. Source. #14043, eff 8-8-24

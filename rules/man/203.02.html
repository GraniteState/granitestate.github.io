Man 203.02 Procedure for Filing a Response

Effective 2013-04-13. Earlier expiration 2004-07-15.

Man 203.02 Procedure for Filing a Response. (a) Upon receipt of the Section I -Complaint and Section II- Response from the complainant, the respondent shall: (1) Complete the Section II - Response and file the same with the board within 14 days of receipt; and (2) On the same day of filing with the board, mail or deliver one copy of the Section I - Complaint including the completed Section II - Response to the complainant.

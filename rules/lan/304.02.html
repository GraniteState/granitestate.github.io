Lan 304.02 Application/Licensure Fees

Effective 2016-11-01.

Lan 304.02 Application/Licensure Fees. (a) The application fee for licensure as a land surveyor shall be $120.00. (b) The fee for the professional land surveyors exam and each re-exam shall be $75.00. (c) The fee for the fundamentals of land surveying application and each re-application shall be $75.00. (d) Re-exam fees shall be the same as initial exam fee indicated in Lan 304.02(b), (c) and (g). (e) The fee for the application for a certificate of authorization shall be $50.00 for a proprietorship, $75.00 for a corporation or partnerships. (f) The fee for verification of licensure shall be $25.00. (g) The fee for the New Hampshire legal aspects exam shall be $125.00. (h) The fee for a temporary permit shall be $300.00.

Lan 304.04 License Renewal Fee

Effective 2016-11-01.

Lan 304.04 License Renewal Fee. The biennial license fee shall be $150.00. If the renewal fee is not received by the date of expiration, there shall be a reinstatement fee of $30.00 per month assessed.

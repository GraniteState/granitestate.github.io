Spe 403.04 Reinstatement of Licenses Lapsed For Longer Than 70 Days But No More Than Two Years

Effective 2015-08-07.

Spe 403.04 Reinstatement of Licenses Lapsed For Longer Than 70 Days But No More Than Two Years. The board shall reinstate a license lapsed longer than 70 days but no more than 2 years if the reinstatement applicant submits to the board: (a) All documents as described in Spe 403.03; (b) A resume as described in Spe 403.07(f); (c) A criminal offender record report described in Spe 403.07(i); and (d) Letters of verification as described in Spe 403.07(g). Source. #9406, eff 3-7-09; ss by #9648, eff 1-29-10; ss by #9708, eff 5-8-10; ss by #9883-A, eff 3-5-11 (from Spe 403.03); ss by #10683, eff 10-4-14; ss by #10907, eff 8-7-15

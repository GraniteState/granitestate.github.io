Spe 403.01 Licenses Subject to Reinstatement

Effective 2016-09-17.

Spe 403.01 Licenses Subject to Reinstatement. The following licenses shall be subject to reinstatement by the board: (a) Licenses suspended for disciplinary reasons as part of a settlement pursuant to Ahp 214 or as part of an order of the board; and (b) Licenses lapsed pursuant to Spe 402.02(b). Source. #9406, eff 3-7-09; ss by #9883-A, eff 3-5-11; ss by #11178, eff 9-17-16

Spe 405.16 Teaching a Course Related to Speech-Language Pathology

Effective 2016-09-17.

Spe 405.16 Teaching a Course Related to Speech-Language Pathology. (a) The board shall recognize as maintenance of continuing competence teaching a college or university course: (1) Related to: a. Speech-language pathology theory or practice or both; b. Communication disorders; or c. Any conditions for which the services of a speech-language pathologist are often or usually recommended; and (2) Taught: a. On site at a college or university; b. On line; or c. By a combination of a. and b. (b) The board shall credit the licensee with 2 hours for each clock hour of teaching, to a maximum of 8 hours per renewal cycle, provided that, if the licensee teaches the same course more than once, the board shall credit the licensee for only a single course. (c) Documentation of teaching a course described in (a) above shall be: (1) A photocopy of the course description in the college or university catalog; or (2) A photocopy of the course syllabus. Source. #9385, eff 2-7-09; ss by #11178, eff 9-17-16

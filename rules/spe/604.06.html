Spe 604.06 Non-Completion of Maintenance of Continuing Competence Reported on the Renewal Form

Effective 2014-10-04.

Spe 604.06 Non-Completion of Maintenance of Continuing Competence Reported on the Renewal Form. (a) Renewal applicants who reported on their renewal application form that they anticipated completing continuing professional education between November 1 and December 31 of the renewal year and did not complete the professional education shall: (1) Report the cancellation or withdrawal to the Board no later than 15 days from the date of the cancellation or withdrawal or January 15 of the year following the renewal year whichever comes first; (2) Give a detailed written explanation of why the renewal applicant could not complete the continuing education as reported on the renewal form; and (3) If the opportunity to complete continuing professional education was prevented by cancellation, provide proof of the cancellation. Source. #10684, eff 10-4-14

Spe 403.02 Reinstatement of Licenses Suspended for Disciplinary Reasons

Effective 2016-09-17.

Spe 403.02 Reinstatement of Licenses Suspended for Disciplinary Reasons. (a) The board shall reinstate a license suspended for disciplinary reasons: (1) In accordance with the terms of the settlement agreement or the disciplinary order of the board, as applicable; and (2) The applicant for reinstatement submits: a. A completed, dated, signed and notarized reinstatement application form further described in Spe 403.05; b. Proof in accordance with Spe 406 of having completed 30 hours of continuing education within the immediate preceding 2 years; c. A written statement that he or she has not been employed or volunteered in New Hampshire as a speech-language pathologist since the date that his or her license ceased to be valid; d. Payment of the reinstatement fee set forth in Ahp 301.02(a); and e. Any documentation required in Spe 303.04 which was not previously provided to the Board. Source. #9406, eff 3-7-09; ss by #9708, eff 5-8-10; ss by #9883-A, eff 3-5-11; ss by #11178, eff 9-17-16

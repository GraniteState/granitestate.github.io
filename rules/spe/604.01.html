Spe 604.01 Procedure for Individuals Who Do Not Wish to Renew Their Certification as Speech-Language Assistants

Effective 2014-10-04.

Spe 604.01 Procedure for Individuals Who Do Not Wish to Renew Their Certification as Speech-Language Assistants. An individual who does not wish to renew his or her certification as a speech-language assistant shall so indicate by: (a) Submitting only the first page of the board-provided renewal form on or before December first of the renewal year; and (b) Completing that page only to the following extent: (1) Adding to or correcting as necessary the following to the preprinted home mailing address: (2) Placing on the lines provided for a person not wishing to renew: a. His or her signature; and b. The date of signing. Source. #9711, eff 5-8-10; ss by #10684, eff 10-4-14

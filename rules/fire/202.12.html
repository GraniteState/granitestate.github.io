Fire 202.12 Applicable Rules of Evidence

Effective 2017-06-28.

Fire 202.12 Applicable Rules of Evidence. (a) The following shall govern the introduction and admissibility of evidence at hearings: (1) Hearings shall not be bound by the rules of evidence; (2) All relevant and material evidence shall be admissible; (3) Evidence shall include but not be limited to: a. Depositions; b. Affidavits; c. Official documents; and d. Testimony of witnesses; (4) The presiding officer shall exclude any irrelevant, immaterial, or unduly cumulative or repetitious evidence; and (5) Applicable statutory and constitutional provisions and immunities requiring exclusion of evidence shall be recognized provided however, that nothing contained herein shall prohibit a party from waiving his or her privilege or immunity.

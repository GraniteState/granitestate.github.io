Fire 405.01 Reciprocity

Effective 2017-06-28. Earlier expiration 2017-08-01.

Fire 405.01 Reciprocity. (a) Any person who has been certified in any out-of-state jurisdiction may apply to the commission for certification following Fire 405.02. (b) The commission shall recognize out-of-state certifications that are accredited through a national or international accreditation organization as having the certification in question.

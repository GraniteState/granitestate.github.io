Fire 703.02 Lapse in Service

Effective 2025-01-10.

Fire 703.02 Lapse in Service. (a) The employment eligibility for a full-time career fire service personnel shall lapse if the employee terminates employment and is not employed as a fire service personnel within a period of 26 months after such termination. (b) If the person who has lapsed is re-employed as a full-time career fire service personnel after their employment eligibility has lapsed, the hiring authority shall follow the hiring requirements outlined in Fire 701.01 and Fire 701.02.

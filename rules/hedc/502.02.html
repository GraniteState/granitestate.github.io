Hedc 502.02 Eligibility

Effective 2025-03-12.

Hedc 502.02 Eligibility. (a) An applicant for a scholarship shall be an eligible student as defined in Hedc 501.02(a). (b) The scholarship shall only be used for the payment of board, room rent, and books and supplies, as established in RSA 193:19, at a New Hampshire institution of higher education. (c) The scholarship may be used for either graduate- or undergraduate- level higher education courses. Source. #10633, eff 7-2-14 (See Revision Note at chapter heading for Hedc 500); ss by #14213, eff 3-12-25 (formerly Hedc 501.04)

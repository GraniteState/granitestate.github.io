Hedc 102.02 Structure

Effective 2025-10-15.

Hedc 102.02 Structure. (a) The commission shall consist of members appointed pursuant to RSA 21-N:8-a, II(a). (b) The commission shall have a chair and vice-chair, each elected for a 2-year term, with one being a representative of a nonpublic institution. (c) The chair shall preside over the meetings of the commission. (d) The vice-chair shall assume the responsibilities of the chair in the chair’s absence. (e) If both the chair and the vice-chair are absent, the director shall serve as the chair pro-tempore. (f) The vice-chair shall assume the position of the chair upon the conclusion of the chair’s term, or upon vacancy of the position by the chair. Source. #14402, eff 10-15-25

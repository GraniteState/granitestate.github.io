Hedc 209.02 Failure to Attend Hearing

Effective 2025-10-15.

Hedc 209.02 Failure to Attend Hearing. If any party to whom notice has been given in accordance with Hedc 207.03 fails to attend a hearing, the presiding officer shall declare that party to be in default and shall either: (a) Dismiss the case, if the party with the burden of proof fails to appear; or (b) Hear the testimony and receive the evidence offered by a party, if that party has the burden of proof in the case. Source. #14403, eff 10-15-25

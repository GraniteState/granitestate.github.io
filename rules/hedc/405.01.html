Hedc 405.01 Mission and Purposes

Effective 2023-11-02.

Hedc 405.01 Mission and Purposes. (a) In its written plan, the applicant shall set forth a well-defined statement of the institution’s mission and purposes. (b) The applicant shall submit the following documents: (1) A copy of the vote of the board of the institution’s resolution adopting the statement of mission and purposes as submitted; (2) A certified copy of the bylaws or other governing instrument specifying the procedures for amendment of the statement of purpose; and (3) A certificate of registration filed with the New Hampshire secretary of state. Source. #10429, eff 10-5-13 (See Revision Note at chapter heading for Hedc 400); ss by #13802, eff 11-2-23

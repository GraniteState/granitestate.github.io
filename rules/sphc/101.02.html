Sphc 101.02 Scope

Effective 2025-02-12.

Sphc 101.02 Scope. Pursuant to RSA 326-F:2, I, these rules shall not apply to speech-language pathology students performing activities during their academic or supervised clinical practicum experience while under the supervision of a speech-language pathologist licensed in New Hampshire, and speech-language specialists certified under rules adopted by the board of education under the authority of RSA 21-N:9, II(s) unless such specialists are also licensed pursuant to RSA 326-F. Source. #14193, eff 2-12-25

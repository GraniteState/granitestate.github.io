Sphc 502.01 Scope

Effective 2025-07-10. Expires 2035-07-10.

Sphc 502.01 Scope. (a) The ethical standards in Sphc 503 shall apply to speech-language pathologists, whether or not they hold a CCC, and individuals certified as speech-language assistants. (b) The ethical standards in Sphc 504 shall apply to audiologists and registered hearing aid dealers. Source. #14311, eff 7-10-25, EXPIRES: 7-10-35

Bfc 103.02 Duties of the Board

Effective 2014-03-25.

Bfc 103.02 Duties of the Board. (a) The responsibilities of the board include: (1) Adopting jointly with the state fire marshal, and with the approval of the commissioner, pursuant to RSA 153:5, I, amendments to the state fire code described in RSA 153:1, VI-a; (2) Adopting rules pursuant to RSA 541-A relative to procedures of the board’s operation; (3) Reviewing and assessing the application of the state fire code and recommending legislation to modify the requirements of the state fire code; and (4) Acting in a continuing advisory capacity to the state fire marshal on any matters pertaining to his/her duties pursuant to RSA 153. Source. #10553-A, eff 3-25-14

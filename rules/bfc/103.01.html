Bfc 103.01 Composition of Board

Effective 2014-03-25.

Bfc 103.01 Composition of Board. (a) Pursuant to RSA 153:2, the board shall consist of 11 members, appointed by the governor, with the advice and consent of the council, for a term specified by law. (b) One member shall be designated chair by the governor. (c) The board shall elect a vice-chair at its annual meeting. (d) The chair shall preside over all meetings of the board. (e) In the absence of the chair at a meeting of the board, the vice chair shall preside. Source. #10553-A, eff 3-25-14

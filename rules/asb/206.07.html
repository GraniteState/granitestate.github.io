Asb 206.07 Public Participation

Effective 2017-05-25. Earlier expiration 2017-02-16.

Asb 206.07 Public Participation. (a) Each person who requests to speak on the issue or issues which are the subject of the public forum or subcommittee meeting shall provide the following information on a speakers list: (1) His or her full name; and (2) The names of each interest group he or she represents, if applicable. (b) Each person requesting to speak shall place his or her name on the speakers list before the last speaker on the list has finished speaking. (c) A public forum shall be continued, postponed or moved in accordance with RSA 541-A:11 when the capacity of the room does not accommodate the number of persons who wish to attend.

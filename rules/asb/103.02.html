Asb 103.02 Requests for Public Documents

Effective 2003-03-31.

Asb 103.02 Requests for Public Documents. (a) Public documents, pursuant to RSA 91-A, relative to the assessing standards board shall be available by contacting the department of revenue administration by the following means: (1) In person at the office located at Governor Hugh J. Gallen Office Park South, 109 Pleasant Street, Concord, NH 03302; (2) Via facsimile to (603) 271-1348; (3) By telephone at (603) 271-2687 or TDD access at 1-800-735-2964; (4) In writing to: Assessing Standards Board Clerk c/o NH Department of Revenue Administration PO Box 457 Concord, NH 03302-0457; or (5) From the department's web page at www.nh.gov/revenue, if the document is available in that format. (b) Public documents relative to the assessing standards board may be reviewed at the department, Governor Hugh J. Gallen Office Park South, 109 Pleasant Street, Concord, NH, 03302 between the hours of 8 a.m. to 4:00 p.m., Monday through Friday, except for state holidays. (c) Copies of public documents relative to the assessing standards board shall be charged at the actual cost of providing the copy. Source. #7861, eff 3-31-03

Phy 308.02 Temporary Licensure for Military Service Members and Spouses

Effective 2026-06-20. Expires 2036-06-20.

Phy 308.02 Temporary Licensure for Military Service Members and Spouses. Individuals on active military duty and military spouses may seek a temporary license under Plc 305 or Plc 306.

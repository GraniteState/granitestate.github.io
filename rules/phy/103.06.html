Phy 103.06 Other Organizational Rules

Effective 2025-09-23.

Phy 103.06 Other Organizational Rules. The board is part of the office of professional licensure and certification. Information such as the board’s office location, address, and telephone number may be found at Plc 103.03. Information regarding the public’s access to minutes and how to request information may be found at Plc 104.

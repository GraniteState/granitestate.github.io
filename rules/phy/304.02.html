Phy 304.02 Materials Required to Accompany the License Application Form

Effective 2026-06-20. Expires 2036-06-20.

Phy 304.02 Materials Required to Accompany the License Application Form. At the time that applicants for initial licensure submit their completed license application form, they shall submit the “Addendum to the Universal Application for Initial License - Physical Therapists and Physical Therapy Assistants” revised 4/2026, mark the box for the license type the applicant is applying for, either “Physical Therapist” or “Physical Therapy Assistant”, and attest that: (a) If the applicant is required by Phy 306.04 to take and pass a test of English language proficiency, the applicant’s examination scores have been sent directly to the office of professional licensure and certification (OPLC) by the Educational Testing Service; (b) The applicant’s examination scores have been sent directly to the OPLC by FSBPT; and (c) The passing scores on the jurisprudence assessment module have been sent directly to the OPLC by the FSBPT.

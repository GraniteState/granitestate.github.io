Cul-H 204.12 Pre-Hearing Conferences

Effective 2012-10-30. Expires 2018-07-01.

Cul-H 204.12 Pre-Hearing Conferences. As necessary, the presiding officer shall schedule on his or her own initiative or at the request of any involved party, a prehearing conference in accordance with RSA 541-A:31, V to consider: (a) Offers of settlement; (b) Simplification of the issues; (c) Stipulations or admissions as to issues of fact or proof by consent of the parties; (d) Limitations on the number of witnesses; (e) Changes to standard procedures desired during the hearing by consent of the parties; (f) Consolidation of examination of witnesses; or (g) Any other matters which aid in the disposition of the proceeding. Source. #10218, eff 10-30-12, EXPIRED: 7-1-18

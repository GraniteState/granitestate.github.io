Cul-H 206.01 Waiver of Rules

Effective 2012-10-30.

Cul-H 206.01 Waiver of Rules. (a) The commissioner shall waive the provisions of any rule, except where precluded by statute or by Cul-H 204.04, upon request by an interested party if the committee finds that: (1) The waiver serves the public interest; and (2) The waiver will not disrupt the orderly and efficient resolution of matters before the division. (b) In determining the public interest, the commissioner shall waive a rule if: (1) Compliance with the rule would be onerous or inapplicable given the circumstances of the affected person; or (2) The purpose of the rule would be satisfied by an alternative method proposed. (c) Any interested party seeking a waiver shall make a request in writing, except as provided in (d) below. (d) The commissioner shall accept for consideration any waiver request made orally during a hearing or pre-hearing conference. (e) A request for a waiver shall specify the basis for the waiver and the proposed alternative, if any. Source. #10219, eff 10-30-12 Appendix

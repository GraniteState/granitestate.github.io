Env-Sw 1104.08 Interaction with Department During Construction

Effective 2024-11-13. Expires 2034-11-13.

Env-Sw 1104.08 Interaction with Department During Construction. (a) During construction, the permittee shall: (1) Provide department personnel with access to the construction site for monitoring and inspection purposes, pursuant to Env-Sw 2000; (2) Provide department personnel with access to a construction trailer work station, or other appropriate on-site work station, for use in reviewing plans, specifications, and other construction-related records; (3) Allow department personnel to attend scheduled pre-construction meetings and other construction-related meetings with the project engineer or the contractor, or both; and (4) Address all issues of concern that the department identifies in writing identified during the inspection or monitoring of the facility construction activities. (b) If an issue of concern pursuant to (a)(4) above involves a determination by the department that facility construction is not proceeding in accordance with the approved design plans and specifications, the permittee shall halt construction until the issue is resolved. (c) Revocation or suspension of the construction approval shall be subject to the provisions in Env-Sw 306.

Env-Sw 300 SOLID WASTE PROGRAM: PERMITS

CHAPTER Env-Sw 300 SOLID WASTE PROGRAM: PERMITS Statutory Authority: RSA 149-M:7 REVISION NOTE #1: Document # 8462-B, effective 10-28-05, readopted with amendments and renumbered the permit rules on solid waste management, formerly in Part Env-Wm 301 through Part Env-Wm 316, under a new subtitle as Chapter Env-Sw 300. The redesignation from subtitle Env-Wm to subtitle Env-Sw was done pursuant to a rules reorganization plan for Department rules approved by the Director of the Office of Legislative Services on 9-7-05. Document # 8462-B replaces all prior filings for solid waste rules formerly in Chapter Env-Wm 300. The prior filings for the former rules in Env-Wm 300 on solid waste include the following documents: #5172, eff 7-1-91 #5296, eff 12-24-91 #5452, INTERIM, eff 12-24-92 #5886-A, eff 8-26-94 #6372, eff 11-16-96 #6535, INTERIM, eff 7-1-97 #6619-B, eff 10-29-97 #7225, eff 3-31-00 REVISION NOTE #2: Document #14124, effective 11-23-24, adopted, readopted, readopted with amendment, or repealed all of the rules in Chapter Env-Sw 300. These actions necessitated the renumbering of various existing rules as indicated in the source notes.

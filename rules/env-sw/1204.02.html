Env-Sw 1204.02 Authorized Waste

Effective 2024-02-06.

Env-Sw 1204.02 Authorized Waste. A permit-by-notification facility shall receive only those wastes specifically authorized in Env-Sw 407, Env-Sw 507, Env-Sw 607, or Env-Sw 707, as applicable to the type of facility being permitted. Source. #6619-B, eff 10-29-97; (See Revision Note at chapter heading for Env-Sw 1200) ss by #8459, eff 10-28-05 (formerly Env-Wm 2904.02); ss by #10595, eff 7-1-14; ss by #13865, eff 2-6-24

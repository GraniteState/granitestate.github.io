Effective 2014-07-01.

Env-Sw 201.01 Purpose. The purpose of the rules in this chapter is to supplement the provisions of RSA 541-A and Env-C 200 relative to procedures applicable to proceedings under RSA 149-M and the solid waste rules. Source. (see Revision Note at chapter heading for Env-Sw 200) #8461-B, eff 10-28-05; ss by #10594, eff 7-1-14

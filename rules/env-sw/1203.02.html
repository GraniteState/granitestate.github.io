Env-Sw 1203.02 Additional Location Restrictions for Landfills

Effective 2024-02-06.

Env-Sw 1203.02 Additional Location Restrictions for Landfills. (a) A permit-by-notification landfill shall not be sited less than 75 feet to surface water or wetlands. (b) A permit-by-notification landfill shall be located to provide at least 4 feet of vertical separation between the base of the disposal area and the seasonal high groundwater table. (c) A permit-by-notification landfill shall be located to provide at least 4 feet of vertical separation between the base of the disposal area and bedrock. (d) A permit-by-notification landfill shall not be sited less than 50 feet from the footprint of any landfill which is not yet capped. (e) A permit-by-notification landfill having an active life longer than 90 days shall be sited on publicly owned land only.

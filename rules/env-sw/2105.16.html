Env-Sw 2105.16 Project Recordkeeping and Reporting

Effective 2018-09-01.

Env-Sw 2105.16 Project Recordkeeping and Reporting. (a) Activities involving the excavation or other disturbance of asbestos at an asbestos disposal site shall be documented and records thereof shall be maintained by the owner as specified in Env-Sw 2104.08(c). (b) Waste shipment records shall be maintained in accordance with 40 CFR 61 and 49 CFR 171-172, as applicable. (c) Within 60 days after completion of a project that changes either the lateral limits of the disposal area, the depth of the disposal area, or any aspects of the capping system, including but not limited to the type or thickness of cover materials and structures and features required by Env-Sw 2104.03(f), the owner or, in the case of a utility/transportation project, the utility company or public authority, shall submit to the department as-built drawings that: (1) Show the new configuration of the disposal area and capping system, including the boundaries, the cover materials, and all other capping system design features; and (2) Bear the stamp of a professional engineer licensed in New Hampshire, when required pursuant to RSA 310-A:18, with certification either that the altered capping system complies with Env-Sw 2104.03 or that the capping system requirements in Env-Sw 2104.03 no longer apply to the site because all asbestos has been removed from the site. Source. #7647, eff 2-16-02 (See Revision Note at chapter heading for Env-Sw 2100) (formerly Env-Wm 3905.16); ss by #9645, eff 2-16-10; ss by #12605, eff 9-1-18

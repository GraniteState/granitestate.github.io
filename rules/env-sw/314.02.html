Env-Sw 314.02 Requirements and Limitations

Effective 2024-11-23. Expires 2034-11-23.

Env-Sw 314.02 Requirements and Limitations. (a) A standard permit facility shall comply with: (1) Env-Sw 1000; (2) Env-Sw 1100, if the facility has an active life longer than 90 days; (3) Env-Sw 400 through Env-Sw 800, as applicable based on the functional classification of the facility; (4) Env-Sw 900, as applicable based on the type of waste managed by the facility; and (5) Env-Sw 1400. (b) A standard permit shall be subject to the provisions in Env-Sw 315 for permit modification. Source.

Env-Sw 2303.01 Certificate of Compliance

Effective 2026-07-18. Expires 2036-07-18.

Env-Sw 2303.01 Certificate of Compliance. (a) Upon request by the department pursuant to (b) below, a manufacturer or supplier subject to this chapter shall provide a certificate of compliance, or a copy thereof, stating that the product is in compliance with RSA 149-M:64. (b) A certificate of compliance shall be requested by the department if it determines, based upon any of the following, that a certificate is necessary to confirm that the product does not contain intentionally added PFAS: (1) The department receives a public complaint indicating that the product might contain intentionally added PFAS; (2) The department receives information indicating that the product might contain intentionally added PFAS; or (3) Product testing of the product or similar products by the department or others indicates that the product might contain intentionally added PFAS. (c) Each certificate of compliance shall be sent electronically by email to PFASConsumerProducts@des.nh.gov and shall include the following: (1) Name and description of the consumer product; (2) Manufacturer or supplier name; (3) Manufacturer or supplier address; (4) Name, title, phone number, and email address of: a. An authorized official of the manufacturer or supplier; and b. An authorized individual who can answer questions regarding the composition of the consumer product; (5) Date the certificate of compliance is completed; and (6) A statement, signed by an authorized official, certifying that: a. The consumer product is not a PFAS-added consumer product; or b. For a consumer product for which an exemption has been granted under RSA 149-M:64, II, a statement identifying the applicable exemption. (d) In accordance with RSA 149-M:64, V(e), a manufacturer or supplier who receives a request for a certificate of compliance from the department shall within 30 days: (1) Provide a certificate of compliance as specified in paragraph (c) above; or (2) Notify persons who sell the PFAS-added consumer product that the sale of the product is prohibited in New Hampshire and provide a copy of the notice to the department with a list of the names, addresses, phone numbers, and email addresses of those notified. (e) If a manufacturer or supplier reformulates a product for which a certificate of compliance was requested, they shall provide a certificate of compliance pursuant to (c) above for the reformulated product prior to offering for sale or distribution. (f) If a manufacturer or supplier produces a new product to replace a product for which a certificate of compliance was requested, they shall provide a certificate of compliance pursuant to (c) above for the new product prior to offering for sale or distribution. (g) By signing a certificate of compliance, the party signing the certificate is certifying that: (1) The information provided is true, not misleading, and as complete as practicable, to the best knowledge and belief of the signer; and (2) The signer is aware that they might be subject to penalties for any false statements made in the certification, including those penalties specified in RSA 641:3 for unsworn falsification.

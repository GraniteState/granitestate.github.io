Env-Sw 2304.02 Schedule of Fines

Effective 2026-07-18. Expires 2036-07-18.

Env-Sw 2304.02 Schedule of Fines. (a) For selling, offering for sale, or distributing PFAS-added consumer products in violation of RSA 149-M:64, IV, the proposed fine shall be $2,000 for each violation. (b) For submitting false or misleading information relating to any certificate of compliance required pursuant to RSA 149-M:64, V and Env-Sw 2300, the proposed fine shall be $2,000 for each violation. (c) For failing to provide the department with a certificate of compliance within 30 days of receipt of a request, in violation of RSA 149-M:64, V(e)(1), the proposed fine shall be $1,750 per violation. (d) For failing to notify persons who sell the product containing chemicals regulated under RSA 149-M:64 that the sale of the product is prohibited, and provide the department with a copy of the notice and a list of the names, addresses, phone numbers, and email addresses of those notified, within 30 days of receipt of a request, in violation of RSA 149-M:64, V(e)(2), the proposed fine shall be $1,750 per violation. Source. #14658, eff 7-18-26, EXPIRES: 7-18-36

Env-Sw 1610.01 Issuance of Certificates

Effective 2023-05-01.

Env-Sw 1610.01 Issuance of Certificates. The department shall issue: (a) An assistant operator certificate to each individual who takes basic training but does not take or does not pass the examination; (b) A principal operator certificate to each individual who takes basic training and passes the examination; and (c) A renewal certificate to each operator who renews their certification in accordance with Env-Sw 1611. Source. #10600, eff 7-1-14; ss by #13623, eff 5-1-23

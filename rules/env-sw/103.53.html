Env-Sw 103.53 Motor vehicle crusher

Effective 2024-10-23.

Env-Sw 103.53 “Motor vehicle crusher” means “motor vehicle crusher” as defined in RSA 149-M:4, XI-a, namely “a mechanical device used to intentionally flatten and reduce the volume of end-of-life motor vehicles to facilitate the transportation of such vehicles to a scrap processing facility. The term includes both mobile and stationary devices. The term also includes loggers and balers.” Source. #14103, eff 10-23-24 (see Revision Note #2 at chapter heading for Env-Sw 100)

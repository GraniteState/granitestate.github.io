Env-Sw 1308.03 Adjustments Due to New Expenditures

Effective 2023-05-01.

Env-Sw 1308.03 Adjustments Due to New Expenditures. (a) A municipality seeking to be reimbursed for the expenditure of funds for facility closure not previously disclosed in a grant application shall: (1) File a supplemental "Application for Grant Reimbursement for Closure of an Unlined Municipal Landfill", NHDES-S-05-015 as specified in Env-Sw 1303, for the additional landfill closure expenditures; or (2) File a supplemental "Application for Grant Reimbursement for Closure of a Municipal Incinerator", NHDES-S-05-014, as specified in Env-Sw 1304, for the additional incinerator closure expenditures. (b) The application shall be processed in accordance with Env-Sw 1305. (c) The department shall adjust future financed grant award payments to reflect the expenditure of additional funds by a municipality for eligible costs relating to the closure project. (d) The department shall adjust grant awards previously paid in a lump sum, or financed grant awards for which all amortized grant payments have been made, by reimbursing the municipality in a lump sum any shortage of grant award money due.

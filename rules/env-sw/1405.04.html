Env-Sw 1405.04 Release of Financial Assurance Obligation

Effective 2024-09-03.

Env-Sw 1405.04 Release of Financial Assurance Obligation. An approved financial assurance plan, or any part thereof, shall remain in effect unless: (a) A facility meets the requirements in Env-Sw 1405.02(d); (b) The facility is no longer defined as a facility in accordance with Env-Sw 103 and has completed the general closure requirements specified in Env-Sw 1006.02, Env-Sw 1106, Env-Sw 1205, and other rules as applicable; or (c) The permittee obtains written approval from the department to replace a financial assurance mechanism specified in Env-Sw 1403.03(a), Env-Sw 1403.03(b), Env-Sw 1403.04(b), or Env-Sw 1403.04(c). Source. #14068, eff 9-3-24

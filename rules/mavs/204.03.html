Mavs 204.03 Contested Cases

Effective 2022-10-14.

Mavs 204.03 Contested Cases. In the event a complaint petition is considered a contested case which cannot be settled under Mavs 204.02, and a hearing is scheduled, all parties shall be notified of the: (a) Time, place, and nature of the hearing; (b) Statutory authority under which the hearing is to be held; (c) Particular sections of the statutes or rules involved; (d) Issues involved; (e) Right to have an attorney present to represent the party at the party’s expense; and (f) Right to have the department provide a certified shorthand court reporter at the party’s expense. Source. #13459-A, eff 10-14-22

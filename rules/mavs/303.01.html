Mavs 303.01 Responsibilities

Effective 2022-10-14.

Mavs 303.01 Responsibilities. (a) The adjutant general shall supervise the execution of the national guard enlistment incentive program. (b) The adjutant general shall: (1) Delegate program management to New Hampshire army national guard and New Hampshire air national guard; and (2) Appoint enlistment incentive program managers. (c) Enlistment incentive program manager(s) shall: (1) Ensure resources and systems shall be in place to administer program; (2) Certify enlistment incentive payments; and (3) Monitor program effectiveness. (d) The department shall process certified enlistment incentive payments. Source. #13459-B, eff 10-14-22

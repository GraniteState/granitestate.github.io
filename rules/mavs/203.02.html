Mavs 203.02 Presiding Officer

Effective 2022-10-14.

Mavs 203.02 Presiding Officer. (a) The adjutant general shall appoint as designee to act as the presiding officer, when necessary to aid in the efficiency and integrity of the hearing. (b) A presiding officer shall, at any stage of the hearing process, withdraw from a case if he or she: (1) Presently, or in the past, has had a personal or business relationship with a party, witness or attorney that could hinder him or her from arriving at an impartial decision on the issue(s); or (2) Cannot render an impartial decision for any other reason. Source. #13459-A, eff 10-14-22

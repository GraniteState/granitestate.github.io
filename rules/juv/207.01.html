Juv 207.01 Explanation after Adoption

Effective 2011-09-30. Earlier expiration 2011-10-13.

Juv 207.01 Explanation after Adoption. (a) Any person may request an explanation regarding adoption of the rules pursuant to RSA 541-A:11, VII by submitting a request to the board. (b) The request shall be considered at the next scheduled board meeting and the board shall issue a response within 45 days after reconsideration. Source.

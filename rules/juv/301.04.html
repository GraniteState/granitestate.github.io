Juv 301.04 Return of Juvenile Parolees to the Sununu Youth Services Center

Effective 2025-03-28. Earlier expiration 2019-09-30.

Juv 301.04 Return of Juvenile Parolees to the Sununu Youth Services Center. (a) Upon violation by a juvenile parolee of any of the juvenile's conditions of parole, the juvenile shall be returned to the Sununu youth services center (SYSC) at the request of any JPPO or law enforcement official. (b) The commissioner shall notify the board of the return to the SYSC of a juvenile parolee within 72 hours of the juvenile's return. (c) Upon the juvenile’s return to SYSC, the juvenile parolee shall receive written notice of the circumstances justifying the juvenile’s return, and of the juvenile parolee's right to a preliminary hearing, pursuant to Juv 202.02.

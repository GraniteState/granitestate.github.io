Juv 200 PRACTICE AND PROCEDURE

CHAPTER Juv 200 PRACTICE AND PROCEDURE REVISION NOTE: Document #7843-A and Document #7843-B, effective 3-6-03, contain rules in Juv 200 of the Juvenile Parole Board relative to practice and procedure. The prior filings of the Juvenile Parole Board containing rules relative to practice and procedure include the following documents: #2971, effective 1-28-85, containing Ju 100 through Ju 400 #4431, EMERGENCY, effective 6-2-88, containing Ju 100 through Ju 600 EXPIRED 8-31-88 #2971, effective again 8-31-88, pursuant to 1988, 173:5, and EXPIRED 1-28-91

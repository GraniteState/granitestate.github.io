Juv 103.01 Juvenile Parole Board

Effective 2003-03-06.

Juv 103.01 Juvenile Parole Board. (a) The board is comprised of 5 members appointed by the governor and approved by the executive council for staggered terms of 5 years or until their successors are appointed. (b) The board shall meet to hold hearings at least 12 times per year or more often as necessary to conform with statutory and due process requirements. (c) The quorum shall be 3 members. (d) The board shall be administratively attached to DHHS pursuant to RSA 170‑H:13. (e) The chairman shall be designated by the governor pursuant to RSA 170‑H:5. (f) The chairman shall: (1) Designate one other member to serve as chairman in his/her absence; (2) Individually swear in all parties and witnesses who testify at board hearings; (3) Preside at all proceedings of the board; (4) Order and direct the course of all proceedings of the board; (5) Be responsible for the creation and maintenance of the record of all hearings; and (6) Prepare written decisions as required by RSA 541-A:35.

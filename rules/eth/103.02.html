Eth 103.02 Organization

Effective 2018-09-06.

Eth 103.02 Organization. (a) As required by RSA 21-G:29, III, membership of the committee includes: (1) The governor’s nominees, of whom one is a Democrat, one is a Republican, and one has no political party affiliation; (2) The secretary of state’s nominees, of whom one is a Democrat and one is a Republican; and (3) The state treasurer’s nominees, of whom one is a Democrat and one is a Republican. (b) The governor designates one of his/her appointees to act as chair. (c) The committee members, by majority vote, elect a vice-chair and secretary from the remaining members. Source. #12617-A, eff 9-6-18

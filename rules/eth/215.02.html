Eth 215.02 Contents of Explanation

Effective 2018-09-06.

Eth 215.02 Contents of Explanation. The committee shall, within 90 days of receiving a request in accordance with Eth 215.01, provide a written response which: (a) Concisely states the meaning of the rule adopted; (b) Concisely states the principal reasons for and against the adoption of the rule in its final form; and (c) States, if the committee did so, why the committee overruled any arguments and considerations presented against the rule. Source. #12617-A, eff 9-6-18

Odb 207.01 Request for Rule Explanation

Effective 2019-12-15.

Odb 207.01 Request for Rule Explanation. (a) Any person may request, at any time before 30 days after the final adoption of the rule, that the board issue an explanation of a rule by filing an original and one copy of the request. (b) The request shall include: (1) The chapter, part or section, or paragraph, by alphanumeric code, of the rule(s), statutory provision(s), or order(s) which is the subject of the request; and (2) Any other information as the person filing the petition deems pertinent and relevant, including attachments, illustrations, and other written documents. Source. #8490-B, eff 11-22-05; ss by #10481, eff 12-3-13; ss by #12941, eff 12-15-19

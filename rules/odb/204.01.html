Effective 2019-12-15.

Odb 204.01 Applicability. With the exception of rulemaking hearings under Odb 205, this part shall apply to the conduct of hearings held by the board to provide information and receive public comment in any matter that is not a contested case as defined by RSA 541-A:1, IV. Source. #8490-B, eff 11-22-05; ss by #10481, eff 12-3-13; ss by #12941, eff 12-15-19

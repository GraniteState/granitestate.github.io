Lsa 403.08 Noncompliance

Effective 2024-03-10.

Lsa 403.08 Noncompliance. Failure of a licensee to submit documentation of continuing education required by Lsa 403.03, when requested to do so per Lsa 403.05(d), shall result in the issuance of a notice of hearing to the licensee to determine if there has been a violation of the board’s rules and laws. Source. #8861, eff 3-28-07; ss by #10679, eff 9-30-14; ss by #13850, eff 3-10-24

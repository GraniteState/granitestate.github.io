Nat 104.02 Inspection and Copies of Records

Effective 2025-09-30.

Nat 104.02 Inspection and Copies of Records. (a) Pursuant to RSA 91-A:4 members of the public may inspect and have copies of those records of the board which are public records and not exempt from disclosure under RSA 91-A:5 or other applicable law. (b) The executive director of the office of professional licensure and certification shall be the custodian of the board's records and shall respond to requests to examine and have copies of public records. (c) Minutes of board proceedings in public session shall be public records pursuant to RSA 91-A:2, II and shall be available for inspection during the board's ordinary office hours. (d) Minutes of board proceedings in nonpublic session shall be public records and made available within 72 hours of the meeting, unless, pursuant to RSA 91-A:3, III, the board votes to keep such records confidential. (e) Persons desiring copies of board records shall submit a request pursuant to Plc 104.01 that contains the information specified in Plc 104.02. (f) If records are requested which contain both public information and information exempt from disclosure pursuant to RSA 91-A or other law, the executive director shall redact the information exempt from disclosure and provide the remaining information.

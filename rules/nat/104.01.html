Nat 104.01 Office Location and Mailing Address, Office Hours, Telephone Number, and TTY/TDD Access

Effective 2025-09-30. Earlier expiration 2016-07-01.

Nat 104.01 Office Location and Mailing Address, Office Hours, Telephone Number, and TTY/TDD Access. (a) The board's office is located at: New Hampshire Naturopathic Board of Examiners Office of Professional Licensure and Certification 7 Eagle Square Concord, N.H. 03301 (b) The office shall be open to the public weekdays, excluding holidays, from 8:00 A.M. to 4:00 P.M. (c) The board's telephone number at the office of professional licensure and certification is (603) 271-2152. (d) Access to the board for in-state TTY/TDD users is through Relay New Hampshire by dialing 1-800-735-2964. (e) Persons wishing to make submissions to, or requests of, the board may mail a letter to the board at the address stated in paragraph (a), call the number stated in paragraph (c), or communicate by TTY/TDD using the number stated in paragraph (d). Source. #6379, eff 11-26-96; ss by #8299, eff 3-9-05; paras (c) and (e) EXPIRED: 7-1-16 pursuant to RSA 541-A:17, II and 2015, 276:45 and 276:97; ss by #12337, eff 7-22-17; ss by #14392, eff 9-30-25

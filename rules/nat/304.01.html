Nat 304.01 Licensure on the Basis of Licensure in another State, District of Columbia, or U.S

Effective 2017-05-17. Earlier expiration 2013-06-18.

Nat 304.01 Licensure on the Basis of Licensure in another State, District of Columbia, or U.S. Territory. Persons currently licensed as a doctor of naturopathic medicine or as a naturopathic physician in any other state, U.S. Territory, or the District of Columbia shall be eligible to be licensed by reciprocity if: (a) They meet the requirements of Nat 303 with the exception of the requirement to pass the NPLEX examinations; and (b) Their jurisdiction of licensure required at the time of their licensure that they pass examinations substantially equivalent, under the standards set forth in Nat 304.02, to the NPLEX examinations. Source. #6380, eff 11-26-96, EXPIRED: 11-26-04 New. #8374, eff 6-18-05, EXPIRED: 6-18-13 New. #12176, eff 5-17-17

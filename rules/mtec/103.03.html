Mtec 103.03 Copies of Records

Effective 2016-05-05.

Mtec 103.03 Copies of Records. Persons desiring copies of board records shall: (a) Identify as particularly as possible the information being sought; and (b) If seeking a copy of a board record subject to disclosure, agree to pay the actual cost of providing such copy, which shall be: (1) The amount of $.25 per page for a paper copy in the size of 8 ½ inches by 11 inches; or (2) The actual cost of the media and cost of reproduction for a record in any other format, which cost shall be disclosed prior to the creation of such copy. Source. #11091-A, eff 5-5-16

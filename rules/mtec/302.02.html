Mtec 302.02 Incomplete Form

Effective 2016-05-05.

Mtec 302.02 Incomplete Form. (a) All forms shall be reviewed by the board or its designee upon filing. (b) The submitting party shall be notified of any apparent errors or omissions and allowed to resubmit the form to the board within 60 days of the date notice is sent to the applicant. Source. #11091-B, eff 5-5-16

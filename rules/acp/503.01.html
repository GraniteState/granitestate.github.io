Acp 503.01 Obligation to Obey

Effective 2023-03-07. Earlier expiration 2022-06-27.

Acp 503.01 Obligation to Obey. (a) The ethical standards set forth in this part shall bind all licensees, and violation of any such standard shall be a basis for imposing disciplinary sanctions. (b) Conduct proscribed by these ethical standards shall be grounds for establishing that a person is in violation of RSA 328-G:9, II(e), with regard to character and shall be a basis for denying a license application or renewal or for issuing a restricted license.

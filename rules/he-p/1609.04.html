He-P 1609.04 Abatement Alternatives

Effective 2026-08-26. Expires 2036-08-26.

He-P 1609.04 Abatement Alternatives. (a) When an order of lead hazard reduction has been issued pursuant to He-P 1605.01, the owner(s) or owner’s agent may notify the department of their intent to use abatement alternatives electronically through the lead online portal at https://nh-dhhs.my.site.com/lead/s/ or via email to nhleadprogram@dhhs.nh.gov. (b) The notification in (a) above shall include: (1) The address of the dwelling; (2) The applicable dwelling units to which the notification applies to if it does not apply to the entire dwelling; (3) Whether the dwelling or applicable dwelling unit(s): a. Is vacant and will remain vacant until the order has been satisfied; b. Has been converted to non-residential use, with an explanation of the use and supporting documentation; c. Has been destroyed, with supporting documentation demonstrating the destruction; or d. Will be brought into compliance with the order by using interim controls in accordance with He-P 1610; and (4) The date the dwelling or applicable dwelling unit(s) became vacant, was converted to non-residential use, or was destroyed. (c) The following abatement alternatives shall be allowed: (1) Documenting that the dwelling is vacant and will remain vacant until the order has been satisfied; (2) The use of interim controls in accordance with He-P 1610; (3) Destruction of the dwelling or child care facility; (4) The property no longer houses a child care facility, child occupied facility, or residential housing ; or (5) Other abatement alternatives approved by the commissioner in accordance with (e) below. (d) When the dwelling or child care facility has been completely destroyed, in accordance with (c)(3) above, and the owner would like the order discharged, the owner shall submit to the department: (1) A signed statement from a representative of the city or town certifying that the property, including all outbuildings, have been completely destroyed; and (2) A signed statement from a representative of the city or town certifying there are no bare soils on the property or laboratory data collected and analyzed in accordance with He-P 1608.04(c) indicating that the bare soils are less than 400 ppm in children’s play areas or 1,200 ppm average in the rest of the yard. (e) If the owner wishes to use alternative abatement methods not provided by He-P 1600, the owner shall: (1) Submit a written variance request to the commissioner in accordance with He-P 1605.03 describing in detail what alternative methods they would like to use and describe how the proposed procedures will provide the same level of protection as those provided by abatement or interim controls; and (2) Receive written approval from the commissioner prior to using alternative abatement methods. (f) Upon receipt of a notification of abatement alternatives the department shall review the information for completeness and accuracy. If the information is complete and accurate and meets all the requirements of this section the department shall approve the request. (g) If the notification is found to be incomplete, inaccurate, or does not meet the requirements of this section the department shall notify the owner and enforce the order of lead hazard reduction.

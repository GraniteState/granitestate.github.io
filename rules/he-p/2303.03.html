He-P 2303.03 Requirements for Food Allergy Awareness

Effective 2022-08-05.

He-P 2303.03 Requirements for Food Allergy Awareness. (a) A food service establishment that offers unpackaged foods intended for immediate consumption by the consumer shall post a notice to customers of the customer’s obligation to inform the server of his or her food allergies. (b) This notice shall be displayed by brochures, deli case or menu advisories, label statements, table tents, place cards, or other effective written means and state “Before placing your order, please inform your server if a person in your party has a food allergy”. Source. #13428, eff 8-5-22

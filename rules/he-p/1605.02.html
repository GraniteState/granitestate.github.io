He-P 1605.02 Extension Requests

Effective 2026-08-26. Expires 2036-08-26.

He-P 1605.02 Extension Requests. (a) Any owner who has received an order of lead hazard reduction pursuant to RSA 130-A:7, II, may request a 30 day extension by sending to the department via the lead on-line portal at https://nh-dhhs.my.site.com/lead/s/ or email to nhleadprogram@dhhs.nh.gov, at least 5 business days before the due date in the order, the following information: (1) The address of the property; (2) The current occupancy status of the property including whether any children or pregnant women reside there; (3) An update on where in the order process the property is at and what is expected to be completed in the next 30 days including any copies of documents not already received showing evidence of forward progress as outlined in (b)(4) below; (4) The date in-place management was last completed; and (5) Any circumstances beyond your control affecting your ability to comply with the order under the current due date. (b) Upon submission and review of the extension request, the commissioner shall extend the time period for lead hazard reduction in accordance with RSA 130-A:7, II(c), by 30 days upon making the determination that the owner or owner’s agent has: (1) Provided the department with an extension request at least 5 business days prior to the expiration of the order; (2) Good cause, as defined in He-P 1602.01(af), for not completing lead hazard reduction work within the time frame ordered; (3) No outstanding administrative fines or court sanctions issued pursuant to RSA 130-A or He-P 1600; (4) Provided the department with evidence of forward progress toward bringing the property into compliance such as: a. A risk assessment conducted by a licensed lead risk assessor; b. A work scope and occupant protection plan developed by a licensed lead risk assessor or lead abatement supervisor; c. A notification of work indicating abatement work start and end dates; or d. Proof of enrollment in a United States Department of Housing and Urban Development (HUD) grant program with timeline for completion of a risk assessment and abatement; and (5) Provided the department with proof that in-place management is current in any occupied dwelling or dwelling unit(s). (c) Extensions of time for lead hazard reduction in accordance with this section shall be acknowledged by the department in writing and set forth the department’s findings and conditions. (d) If, upon completion of the time period stated in the order, or an extended time period, the owner has not complied with the order, the department shall impose an administrative fine in accordance with He-P 1606.01 or seek injunctive relief in accordance with RSA 130-A:17. (e) Property owners shall not be eligible for extensions when: (1) The owner is in violation of or noncompliant with one or more provisions of RSA 130-A or He-P 1600; (2) The request for extension does not meet the criteria in (b) above; or (3) The order for abatement is the result of the owner’s failure to maintain a current certificate of lead safe interim controls in accordance with He-P 1610.06. (f) If an extension request does not meet the criteria above, the department shall enforce the order of lead hazard reduction and notify the owner: (1) In writing and set forth the department’s reasons for the property not qualifying for an extension based on this section; and (2) Be sent to the owner by USPS mail or email. (g) If, upon completion of the extension of time, the owner or owner’s agent requires additional time to comply with the order, a new request shall be submitted and reviewed in accordance with this section.

He-P 3308.02 Farmers

Effective 2025-02-26.

He-P 3308.02 Farmers. (a) Administrative appeal procedures shall comply with 7 CFR 248.16 and the administrative appeal process as described in RSA 541-A and He-C 200. (b) If an appeal is requested, agency action shall not be finalized until the hearing officer reaches a decision or the expiration of the current FMNP season, whichever occurs first. (c) All notices of enforcement action from the department to the farmer and listed in (i) below shall be in writing and contain the option to appeal. (d) The farmer shall have 10 days from receipt of the notice of an enforcement action to submit an appeal, in writing, to the department. (e) The adverse action shall become final after the 10 days in (d) above, if the farmer fails to request an appeal. (f) In accordance with 7 CFR 248.16 (c)(4), farmers shall have at least one opportunity to reschedule a previously scheduled hearing. In doing so, the farmer shall waive the right to a decision pursuant to the timeline in (g) below. (g) Within 60 days of receiving the farmer’s request for an administrative appeal, the department shall provide the farmer with a written notification of the appeal decision. (h) The department shall not be responsible for losses incurred by the farmer as a result of adverse action taken by the department. (i) A full administrative appeal shall be provided for the following, if an appeal is requested: (1) Imposing a sanction as described in He-P 3307.02; (2) Termination of an agreement for noncompliance or cause; (3) Denial of authorization based on failure to meet federal, or department established vendor selection criteria as described in He-P 3305.01; or (4) Any disqualification of a farmer by the department. (j) Expiration of an authorized agreement and the validity or appropriateness of the department’s vendor selection criteria listed in He-P 3305.01, shall not be subject to appeal in accordance with 7 CFR 248.16(a).

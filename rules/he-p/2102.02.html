He-P 2102.02 Standards of Quality for Bottled Water

Effective 2021-10-23. Earlier expiration 2012-04-19.

He-P 2102.02 Standards of Quality for Bottled Water. (a) All sources of water for bottled, bulk, or vended water, under the regulatory authority of DES shall meet the quality standards required by DES in accordance with RSA 485:3. (b) All finished bottled water products, regardless of state or country of origin, shall meet the FDA standards of quality for bottled water in 21 CFR 165.110(b) and the maximum contaminant level (MCL) requirements of Env-Dw 702 through 705. (c) For standards that are included in both 21 CFR 165.110 and in Env-Dw 702-705, the level set in 21 CFR 165.110 (b) shall be used.

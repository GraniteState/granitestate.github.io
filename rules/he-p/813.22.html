He-P 813.22 Voluntary Closure of AFCR

Effective 2019-12-06. Earlier expiration 2019-09-16.

He-P 813.22 Voluntary Closure of AFCR. When an AFCR no longer intends to operate, an oversight agency shall notify the department in writing of the following: (a) The name of the AFCR; (b) The certificate number of the AFCR; (c) The address of the AFCR; (d) The date the AFCR closed or will close; and (e) The location that the resident(s) have moved to, including the name of the home and certificate number of the home, if available. Source. #8595, eff 4-1-06; ss by #9899-A, eff 3-29-11; ss by #12740, INTERIM, eff 3-20-19, EXPIRED: 9-16-19 New. #12934, eff 12-6-19

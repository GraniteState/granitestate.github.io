He-P 802.37 Psychiatric Hospital

Effective 2024-09-25. Expires 2034-09-25.

He-P 802.37 Psychiatric Hospital. (a) A psychiatric hospital, as defined in He-P 802.03(ci), shall meet the criteria set forth in He-P 802, including He-P 802.28. (b) Each psychiatric hospital shall have a full time administrator who meets the qualifications for an administrator as defined in He-P 802.17(b) and shall be responsible to the governing body for the daily management and operation of the hospital which, in addition to those responsibilities defined in He-P 802.17(b), shall include ensuring development and implementation of hospital policies and procedures on: (1) Voluntary and involuntary emergency admission; and (2) Seclusion and restraints. (c) Psychiatric hospitals shall be exempt from He-P 802.17(e) and (f) and He-P 802.32. Source. #13166, eff 1-28-21 (formerly He-P 802.33); ss by #14081, eff 9-25-24, EXPIRES: 9-25-34 (formerly He-P 802.36)

He-P 1612.04 Post Course Completion Proficiency Examinations

Effective 2026-08-26. Expires 2036-08-26.

He-P 1612.04 Post Course Completion Proficiency Examinations. (a) With the exception of lead abatement workers, after successful completion of the training course all lead professionals seeking licensure or certification under this part shall successfully complete a proficiency examination specific to the discipline of lead professional being applied for. (b) The examination required in (a) above shall cover the requirements of RSA 130-A, He-P 1600, 40 CFR 745, and the standards set forth in the HUD “Guidelines for the Evaluation and Control of Lead-Based Paint Hazards in Housing”, (2012 Edition) available as noted in Appendix A, and meet the following requirements: (1) The examination shall include a minimum of 50 multiple choice questions; and (2) The examination shall be administered by the department, another state, or a third party. (c) If the examination in (a) above are not completed successfully within 6 months of successfully completing the applicable lead educational program, the applicant shall retake and successfully complete the applicable lead educational program. (d) An individual shall receive a score of at least 70% on the examination they take. (e) Failure to attain a score of at least 70% shall result in the following: (1) After each examination failure, the applicant shall take a different version of the examination; (2) After the third failure of the examination in (a) above, the applicant shall be required to: a. Retake and successfully complete the entire initial educational program; and b. Successfully complete the failed examination; and (3) After the third failure of the New Hampshire examination in (a) above, the applicant shall be required to successfully complete the New Hampshire rules course described in He-P 1611.03(i). (f) The examination listed in (a) above shall not be taken more than 3 times within a 6-month period. (g) If an applicant provides proof of attaining a score of 70% or greater on an examination approved by the EPA, from an EPA authorized state, or Tribal Nation, covering the content listed in (a) above, the commissioner shall not require that the examination listed in (a) be retaken.

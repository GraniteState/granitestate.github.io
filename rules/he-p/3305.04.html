He-P 3305.04 Farmer Training

Effective 2025-02-26.

He-P 3305.04 Farmer Training. The department shall provide training to farmers pursuant to 7 CFR 248.10(d) by implementing the following: (a) The department shall provide farmers with initial face-to-face training for all farmers who have never previously participated in the program prior to their commencing participation in the FMNP; (b) At least one representative of the farmer shall participate in FMNP training annually as described in (d) below; (c) Annual training by the department shall communicate changes in pertinent regulations, rules, and procedures and keep farmers informed of problems and suggestions to improve eligible food availability, such as: (1) Eligible foods; (2) Proper FMNP benefit redemptions procedures; (3) Equitable treatment of FMNP recipients, including the availability of produce to FMNP recipients that is of the same quality and cost as that sold to other customers; (4) Civil rights compliance and guidelines; and (5) Other training topics that relate to compliance with the requirements of the FMNP; (d) Annual training shall be provided by the department in a variety of formats such as: (1) Face-to-face training; (2) Live webinar; (3) Self-paced learning module; (4) Newsletter; or (5) Other formats as developed by the department; (e) At least one alternative date on which to attend face-to-face training shall be provided to farmers; (f) The department shall be available for further farmer training and education that is: (1) Requested by the farmer; or (2) Deemed necessary as a result of farmer monitoring activities; (g) The farmer shall notify the department and request additional training at any time during the participation period if, in the opinion of the farmer, farmer staff does not adequately understand FMNP procedures; and (h) Mandatory training shall be deemed necessary by the department as a result of repeated farmer violations or as a result of farmer monitoring activities. Source. #14200, eff 2-26-25

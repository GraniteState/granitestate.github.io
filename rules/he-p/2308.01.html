He-P 2308.01 Application Requirements

Effective 2019-08-20. Earlier expiration 2019-08-20.

He-P 2308.01 Application Requirements. Food service establishments applying for a license as a bed and breakfast facility shall comply with all of the application requirements of He-P 2304.01, except that they shall not be required to submit a plan review application as required under He-P 2304.01(a)(5) and wastewater items as required under He-P 2304.01(a)(4) and He-P 2304.07.

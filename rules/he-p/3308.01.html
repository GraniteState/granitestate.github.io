He-P 3308.01 Recipients

Effective 2025-02-26.

He-P 3308.01 Recipients. (a) Administrative appeal procedures shall comply with 7 CFR 248.16 and the administrative appeal process as described in RSA 541-A and He-C 200. (b) If a recipient appeals the termination of benefits, the recipient shall continue to receive FMNP benefits until the hearing officer reaches a decision or the expiration of the current FMNP season, whichever occurs first. (c) Written notification of the adverse action, the cause(s) for the action, and the effective date of the action shall be mailed to the most recent address provided by the recipient. The notice shall contain the option to appeal the decision. (d) Recipients shall have 10 days from receipt of the notice of an enforcement action to request an administrative appeal in writing. (e) The adverse action shall become final after the 10 days in (d) above, if the recipient fails to request an appeal. (f) Advanced notice of the time and place of the hearing shall be provided to all parties involved to give them sufficient time to prepare for the hearing. (g) In accordance with 7 CFR 248.16(c)(4), recipients shall have at least one opportunity to reschedule a previously scheduled hearing. In doing so, the recipient shall waive the right to a decision pursuant to the timeline in (h) below. (h) Written notification of the decision in the appeal shall be provided to the recipient within 60 days from the date of receipt of the request for a hearing. Source. #14200, eff 2-26-25

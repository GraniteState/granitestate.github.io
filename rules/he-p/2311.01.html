He-P 2311.01 Application Requirements

Effective 2019-08-20. Earlier expiration 1997-08-23.

He-P 2311.01 Application Requirements. (a) Non-exempt homestead food operations shall apply for a Class H food service license. (b) Non-exempt homestead food operations applying for a Class H license shall comply with all of the application requirements of He-P 2304.01 except that: (1) They shall not be required to submit the plan review application required under He-P 2304.01(a)(5) and wastewater items required under He-P 2304.01(a)(4) and He-P 2304.07; (2) They shall submit process review documentation in accordance with He-P 2311.03, as applicable; and (3) They shall also submit the following: a. A copy of one finished product label which meets the requirements of He-P 2311.04 below; and b. A list of all products to be manufactured.

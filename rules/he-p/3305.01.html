He-P 3305.01 Selection of Farmers

Effective 2025-02-26.

He-P 3305.01 Selection of Farmers. (a) The following procedures shall apply to the selection of farmers for FMNP: (1) Farmers eligible to be authorized shall complete and submit the application form, “New Hampshire FMNP Farmer Application” (February 2025), to the department, certifying the following: “I understand that this is only a request to participate in the New Hampshire FMNP and does not constitute a contract. All information on this application is true, accurate, and correct to the best of my knowledge. I understand that any false information on this application or attached supporting documentation may lead to rejection of this application”; and (2) The department shall review each new applicant farmer for participation in the FMNP as follows: a. Department employees who hold dual employment with any FMNP-authorized or applicant farmer, regardless of capacity, shall not be involved in the review of farmer applications for authorization; b. If the department receives an incomplete application, the department shall notify the applicant in writing within 30 days of receipt of any errors or omissions, request any additional information that the agency is permitted by law to require, and notify the applicant of the name, official title, address, and telephone number of an agency official or employee who may be contacted regarding the application; c. The applicant shall have up to 30 days from the date of the department’s request to respond to the request for additional information; d. The department shall assess the applicant farmer’s qualifications using the selection criteria in accordance with (b)(1)-(12) below; and e. The assessment shall include a review of the list of fresh fruits, vegetables, and cut herbs offered by the farmer in order to determine whether or not the applicant farmer meets the eligibility criteria in accordance with 7 CFR 248. (b) The department shall deny authorization of an applicant farmer, or terminate an authorized farmer, if any of the following criteria are true at any time: (1) The farm’s location is not within NH borders; (2) The applicant farmer or authorized farmer does not provide information regarding eligible foods sold at the farmers’ market; (3) The department determines that the applicant farmer or authorized farmer is not able to meet application program requirements or existing selection criteria as evidenced by: a. Sanction points obtained under He-P 3307.02; b. Failure to meet monitoring requirements in accordance with He-P 3306; c. Failure to attend face-to-face training upon initial application as set forth in 7 CFR 248.10(d); and d. Failure to attend training as required by the department in accordance with He-P 3305.04; (4) The authorized farmer has failed to provide eligible foods at the current price or less than the current price charged to other customers; (5) A conflict of interest exists in that a farm employee is dually employed by the WIC program; (6) The farmer does not grow at least one eligible fruit, vegetable, or cut herb that is fresh, locally grown, and unprepared during the FMNP season in accordance with 7 CFR 248.8; (7) T he applicant farmer or authorized farmer does not accept, at a minimum, cash for food purchases from the general public as well as the FMNP recipient; (8) The applicant farmer or authorized farmer does not maintain a smart phone or tablet with the ability to scan a quick response (QR) code or bar code with an internet or data connection to accept and process the FMNP electronic benefit; (9) The applicant farmer or authorized farmer does not attend the market(s) on the dates, and times identified on the application; (10) The applicant farmer or authorized famer has prior, pending, or under appeal sanctions; (11) The department determines that the farm was sold or had a change in ownership to circumvent a FMNP sanction in accordance with 7 CFR 248; (12) The applicant farmer provides false or misleading information on the farmer application or other required program documentation; or (13) The applicant farmer was asked to provide additional information to the department and failed to do so within 30 days. (c) A farmer that has been denied authorization initially or terminated during the course of a current authorization period shall not reapply for authorization until: (1) The FMNP season following the date of the farmer’s receipt of the department’s notice; or (2) The FMNP season following the date of an appeal decision upholding the department’s decision to deny authorization or terminate. Source. #14200, eff 2-26-25

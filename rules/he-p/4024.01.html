He-P 4024.01 Purpose

Effective 2024-06-12.

He-P 4024.01 Purpose. (a) This part provides information for the licensee on removing the waste safely from a facility or site containing radioactive material, a radiation-producing machine, or radioactivity-inducing machine, pursuant to He-P 4030 through 4036, He-P 4039, He-P 4040, He-P 4044, and He-P 4047. (b) The purpose of this part is to conform with 10 CFR 20.1401 – 20.1406, 10 CFR 30.35, 10 CFR 30.36, 10 CFR 40.36, 10 CFR 40.42, 10 CFR 70.25, and 10 CFR 70.38. Source. #8393, eff 7-23-05; ss by #10492, eff 12-24-13; ss by #13997, eff 6-12-24

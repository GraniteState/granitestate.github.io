He-P 1610.01 Interim Control Standards

Effective 2026-08-26. Expires 2036-08-26.

He-P 1610.01 Interim Control Standards. (a) This part shall apply to any persons engaged in lead hazard reduction as defined in He-P 1602.01(az) and who has chosen to utilize interim controls as a means of temporarily reducing lead exposure hazards on a dwelling, dwelling unit, or child care facility. (b) All interim control activities shall be conducted in accordance with RSA 130-A and He-P 1600. (c) When a property is under an order of lead hazard reduction, the owner shall notify the department of their intent to use interim controls as an alternative to abatement electronically through the lead on-line portal at https://nh-dhhs.my.site.com/lead/s/ or via email to nhleadprogram@dhhs.nh.gov. (d) In addition to notifying the department the owner shall submit: (1) A copy of the work scope as defined in He-P 1602.01(ch); and (2) A copy of the occupant protection plan as described in He-P 1608.05. (e) Upon receipt the department shall review the notification and accompanying documentation for completeness, accuracy, and to make sure it meets the requirements of this section. If the information is complete and accurate and meets the requirements of this section, the department shall approve the request. (f) If the notification is found to be incomplete, inaccurate, or does not meet the requirements of this section the department shall notify the owner and enforce the order of lead hazard reduction. (g) The period of time that a property owner is allowed to use interim controls on properties under an order of lead hazard reduction shall not exceed 5 years from the date of notification. After 5 years the property shall be abated and have a valid certificate of lead safe abatement issued by a licensed lead risk assessor. (h) The commissioner shall enforce the order of lead hazard reduction and require the owner to abate all lead exposure hazards when it is found that: (1) The owner has failed to maintain a current certificate of lead safe interim controls; or (2) When a compliance inspection conducted in accordance with He-P 1605.04 reveals that the property with a certificate of lead hazard control interim controls no longer meets the requirements of He-P 1600.

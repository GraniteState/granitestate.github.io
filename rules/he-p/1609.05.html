He-P 1609.05 Removal of the Dwelling or Dwelling Unit(s) from the Rental Market

Effective 2026-08-26. Expires 2036-08-26.

He-P 1609.05 Removal of the Dwelling or Dwelling Unit(s) from the Rental Market. (a) When an order of lead hazard reduction has been issued pursuant to He-P 1605.01, the owner or owner’s agent may bring the property into compliance with the order of lead hazard reduction by removal of the dwelling or dwelling unit(s) from the rental market, pursuant to RSA 130-A:8-a, III. (b) To document that the dwelling or dwelling unit(s) are removed from the residential rental market, or that the dwelling or the dwelling unit is owner-occupied, the owner shall electronically, through the lead on-line portal at https://nh-dhhs.my.site.com/lead/s/ or via email to nhleadprogram@dhhs.nh.gov, notify the department of the dwelling or dwelling unit(s) removal from the residential rental market. (c) The notification in (b) above shall include: (1) The address of the dwelling and all applicable dwelling units; (2) The type of property, such as a single family or multifamily; (3) All occupant names, owner included, and their relationship to the owner and which dwelling or dwelling unit they occupy; and (4) The date the dwelling or dwelling unit(s) was removed from the rental market. (d) For any dwelling or dwelling unit for which the owner does not reside, in addition to (c) above, the owner shall submit: (1) An attestation that they have completed in-place management for all non-owner-occupied areas in which a child has access to in accordance with He-P 1608.16; and (2) Signed written certification statements from each adult occupant certifying their family relation to the owner, that they have been made aware of the lead exposure hazards found at the property, and do not pay rent in the form of money, goods, or other services in exchange for occupying the dwelling or dwelling unit. (e) Upon receipt, the department shall review the notification for completeness and accuracy. If the information is complete and accurate and meets the requirements of this section the department shall approve the request. (f) If the notification is found to be incomplete, inaccurate, or does not meet the requirements of this section the department shall notify the owner and enforce the order of lead hazard reduction.

He-P 3302.01 Definitions

Effective 2025-02-26.

He-P 3302.01 Definitions. (a) “Change in ownership” means any time a controlling interest in a sole proprietorship, joint venture, partnership, corporation, limited liability company, or any other kind of entity is transferred to or merged with another sole proprietor, joint venture, partnership, corporation, limited liability company, or any other kind of entity. (b) “Corrective action plan” means a plan developed and written by the farmer, which specifies the actions that will be taken to correct identified deficiencies. (c) “Department” means the New Hampshire department of health and human services. (d) “Eligible foods” means fresh, nutritious, unprepared, locally grown fruits, vegetables, and herbs for human consumption that are not processed or prepared beyond their natural state except for usual harvesting and cleaning processes. (e) “Face-to-face training” means an education session(s) held at a physical location with farmers physically present. This term includes “in person training”. (f) “Farmer” means an individual authorized to sell produce at participating farmers' markets or roadside stands. This term includes “producer”. (g) “Farmer designee” means a farm co-owner, manager, or supervisor authorized to attend department training in order to train other employees on the requirements of the farmers’ market nutrition program. (h) “Farmers' market” means one or more local farmers who assemble at a defined and authorized location for the purpose of selling their produce directly to consumers. (i) “Farmers’ market nutrition program season” means the time during which FMNP benefits can be redeemed. (j) “Food and Nutrition Service (FNS) of the U.S. Department of Agriculture” means the federal agency that authorizes state nutrition programs. (k) “Good cause” means any circumstance beyond a person’s control that prevents that person from complying with these rules, including: (1) A death in the person’s immediate family; (2) Personal injury or serious illness of the person or an immediate family member; or (3) Another compelling reason or justification. (l) “Household” means the same as that of “family” defined in 7 CFR 246.2. (m) “Local agency” means any nonprofit entity or local government agency which issues FMNP benefits, and provides nutrition education or information on operational aspects of the FMNP to recipients. (n) “Nutrition education” means individual or group education sessions and the provision of information and educational materials designed to improve health status, achieve positive change in dietary habits, and emphasize relationships between nutrition and health, all in keeping with the individual's personal, cultural, and socioeconomic preferences. (o) “Recipient” means a WIC participant or person chosen by the department to receive FMNP benefits. (p) “Roadside stand” means a location at which an individual farmer sells their produce directly to consumers. (q) “Special Supplemental Nutrition Program for Women, Infants and Children (WIC)” means the federal program authorized by section 17 of the Child Nutrition Act of 1966, as amended (42 U.S.C. 1771 et. seq.) that provides federal grants to states for supplemental foods, health care referrals, and nutrition education for low-income pregnant, breastfeeding, and non-breastfeeding postpartum women, and to infants and children up to age 5 who are found to be at nutritional risk. (r) “WIC Farmers' Market Nutrition Program” means the program authorized by section 17(m) of the Child Nutrition Act of 1966 (CNA), 42 U.S.C. 1786(m), as amended, to allow WIC benefits at state farmers’ markets.

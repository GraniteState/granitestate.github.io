He-P 2709.01 Variances

Effective 2021-03-02.

He-P 2709.01 Variances. (a) Applicants or license holders seeking variances from specific rules shall contact the department by telephone at (603) 271-4589 or by mail, to obtain a VR Form “Milk Variance Request Form” (February 2021). (b) Applicants or license holders shall complete and submit to the department VR Form “Milk Variance Request Form” (February 2021) by mailing to: Department of Health and Human Services Division of Public Health Services Food Protection Section 29 Hazen Dr. Concord, NH 03301 (c) By completing and submitting a VR Form “Milk Variance Request Form” (February 2021), the applicant or licensee holder acknowledges that: (1) The requested variance pertains to the rule mentioned in the variance request only, and shall not be deemed permission to waiver from any other requirements outlined in the He-P 2700; and (2) If approved, this variance shall be considered null and void at such time one or more of the following events should occur: a. Change of ownership; b. Renovation; or c. Failure to comply with said variance. (d) The commissioner shall approve a request for variance if: (1) The commissioner concludes that authorizing deviation from strict compliance with the rule item from which a variance is sought does not contradict the intent of the rule; and (2) The alternative proposed by the applicant or license holder ensures that the objective or intent of the rule item from which a variance is sought will be accomplished. (e) If a variance is approved, the license holder’s subsequent compliance with the alternatives approved in the variance shall be considered equivalent to complying with the rule item from which a variance was sought. (f) A variance shall be approved until the expiration of the current license or as specified by the department. (g) No request for a variance concerning the rules of other state agencies which are referred to in this chapter shall be approved. Source. #13175, eff 3-2-21 Appendix

He-P 1611.04 Examinations, Assessments, and Course Completion Certificates

Effective 2026-08-26. Expires 2036-08-26.

He-P 1611.04 Examinations, Assessments, and Course Completion Certificates. (a) At the conclusion of each program, each student shall complete an examination and if applicable, a hands-on skills assessment, or in the alternative, a proficiency test that tests the student’s knowledge of all required content areas. (b) Each student completing an examination as required by (a) above shall receive a passing score of 70% or greater to pass any course, or successfully complete a proficiency test developed and assessed according to the program manager’s criteria. (c) Hands-on skills assessments in (a) above shall: (1) Incorporate actual field conditions and current work practices; (2) Evaluate the ability of the student to correctly perform all work practices and procedures that were covered in the hands-on skills portion of the course; and (3) Be graded on a pass/fail basis and in accordance with the hands-on skills assessment developed by the program manager. (d) Each student who successfully completes the course, including class attendance and passage of the examination, and if applicable, a hands-on skills assessment or a proficiency test shall receive a course completion certificate. (e) The certificate shall include: (1) The name, address, telephone number, and certification number of the provider of the program; (2) The discipline of lead professional for which the completed educational program is certified; (3) If it was taught electronically or in-person; (4) The language in which the course was taught, if other than English; (5) The number of course hours in person versus e-learning; (6) The dates of the course; (7) The name of the student; and (8) A unique certificate number. (f) Course completion certificates shall be valid for one year.

He-P 2103.02 Equipment

Effective 2018-11-27. Earlier expiration 2012-04-19.

He-P 2103.02 Equipment. (a) All plant equipment and utensils shall be suitable for their intended use and in sound condition. (b) Self contained units for washing, sanitizing, and filling multiservice containers shall be located in a properly designed and constructed facility, in accordance with He-P 2103.01. (c) All product contact surfaces, including containers and closures, shall be constructed of nontoxic, nonabsorbent and corrosion resistant materials. (d) Storage tanks shall be of the type that can be closed to exclude all foreign matter, and have properly protected vents.

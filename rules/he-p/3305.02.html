He-P 3305.02 Written Agreement

Effective 2025-02-26.

He-P 3305.02 Written Agreement. (a) Written agreements shall include the requirements of 7 CFR 248.10(b) and the following: (1) The farmer shall provide at least one of the eligible foods pursuant to He-P 3302.01(a) and (b); (2) The farmer shall attend the markets on the dates as identified in the application; and (3) The farmer shall: a. Provide eligible foods at the current price or less than the current price charged to customers; b. Accept cash for food purchases from the general public as well as the FMNP recipient; c. Maintain a smart phone or tablet with the ability to scan a QR code or bar code; d. Use an active email account capable of receiving the FMNP agreement and program information; e. Use a smart device for the purpose of processing benefits; f. Provide the department information on inventory records for eligible foods and sales for the authorized FMNP season; g. Display the FMNP authorized poster at the farmers’ market; h. Label and price the eligible food available for sale at the farmers’ market; i. Comply with the following: 1. Title VI of the Civil Rights Act of 1964 (42 U.S.C 2000d et seq.); 2. Title IX of the Education Amendments of 1972 (20 U.S.C. 1681 et seq.) 3. Section 504 of the Rehabilitation Act of 1973 (29 U.S.C 794); 4. Age Discrimination Act of 1975 (42 U.S.C. 6101 et seq.); 5. Department of Agriculture regulations on non-discrimination (7 CFR parts 15, 15a, and 15b); and 6. FNS directives and guidelines to the effect that no person shall, on the grounds of race, color, national origin, age, sex, or handicap, be excluded from participation in, be denied the benefits of, or otherwise be subjected to discrimination under any program or activity for which the department received federal assistance from FNS; j. Provide FMNP customers the same courtesies provide to non-FMNP customers; k. Attend training on FMNP procedures as scheduled by the department ; l. Ensure that cashiers and all other staff are fully trained and understand the FMNP requirements; m. Ensure that only FMNP eligible foods are sold to recipients; n. Provide FMNP eligible goods at prices that are fair and competitive in comparison with farmers in their peer group; o. Be monitored by the department for compliance with FMNP program rules; p. Provide ready access to FMNP redemption information at the request of the department; q. Not charge the department for non-eligible foods; r. Not charge the department for foods not received by recipient; s. Not provide cash, change, non-eligible foods, or other items in lieu of eligible foods to recipients; t. Notify the department in writing within 15 days of the farmer ceasing operations or a change in ownership, name of farm, or location, thereby permitting the department to terminate the vendor agreement in accordance with He-P 3305.01(b)(11); u. Maintain a copy of the current NH FMNP approved goods at each cash register or check out line; and v. Not offer incentive items solely to FMNP recipients and not to other customers in an effort to obtain FMNP business. (b) Pursuant to changes in federal or state statutes, rules, or other requirements, the department shall revise all current agreements, and the amended agreements shall include the original expiration date without prejudice.

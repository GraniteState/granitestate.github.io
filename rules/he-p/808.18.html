He-P 808.18 Quality Assessment

Effective 2023-08-02. Earlier expiration 1999-12-20.

He-P 808.18 Quality Assessment. Licensed laboratories, including mobile laboratory vans, shall develop and implement a quality assessment program that reviews policies and services and maximizes quality by preventing or correcting identified problems.

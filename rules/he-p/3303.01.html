He-P 3303.01 Recipient Eligibility

Effective 2025-02-26.

He-P 3303.01 Recipient Eligibility. (a) An individual who is currently receiving WIC benefits or who is on the waiting list to receive WIC benefits pursuant to He-P 3202, excluding infants younger than 6 months of age, shall be eligible to receive FMNP benefits. (b) Recipient benefits shall be provided to recipients based upon approved categories as specified in He-P 3202.03. (c) The department shall limit recipient certifications, as necessary, by imposing additional eligibility requirements in accordance with 7 CFR 248.6(b), due to funding restrictions that impact the state of New Hampshire’s ability to fund all eligible FMNP recipients. (d) In accordance with 7 CFR 248.8(b), the value of the federal FMNP benefit received by each recipient shall not be less than $10 per year and not more than $30 per year. Source. #14200, eff 2-26-25

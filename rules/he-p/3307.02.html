He-P 3307.02 Farmer Sanctions

Effective 2025-02-26.

He-P 3307.02 Farmer Sanctions. (a) Farmer sanctions shall be as follows: (1) Failure to allow monitoring; (2) Failure to clearly identify pricing of fruits, vegetables, and cut herbs; (3) Failure to identify fruits, vegetables, and cut herbs grown out of state that are not eligible for FMNP benefits; (4) Selling fruits, vegetables, and cut herbs to a FMNP recipient that are not grown in New Hampshire; (5) Selling anything that is not an eligible food to a FMNP recipient; (6) Failure to follow proper redemption procedures; (7) Asking an FMNP recipient for identification or to share their PIN to complete a FMNP transaction; (8) Failure to attend farmers’ markets, on the dates identified on the FMNP application without good cause; (9) Failure to remain open during reported business hours at farmers’ markets without good cause; (10) Charging sales tax on FMNP approved foods; (11) Contacting recipients in an attempt to have them return to fix a farmer error or to seek reimbursement for a FMNP food cost not paid by the department; (12) Failure to keep all FMNP recipients’ information confidential; or (13) Failure to treat FMNP recipients like any other customers. (b) The department shall: (1) Issue sanctions in accordance with violations listed in He-P 3307.02(a) above; and (2) Notify the farmer in writing of the imposition of sanctions and the specific violation(s) for which they were assigned. (c) When a farmer has been assessed their first sanction, the department shall issue a: (1) Written notice of the sanction; and (2) Requirement to receive additional training related to the sanction. (d) When a farmer has been assessed for a second sanction within the same season as the first sanction, the department shall issue a: (1) Written notice of the sanction; and (2) Requirement to provide a corrective action plan within 30 days to be approved by the department to include the steps that the farmer will be taking in order to correct the sanction and how they will conduct business moving forward to achieve compliance with the farmer agreement. (e) The farmer’s failure to provide a corrective action plan pursuant to (d) above shall result in disqualification for the remainder of the current season. (f) When a farmer has been assessed a third identical sanction within the same season, the farmer shall be disqualified for: (1) The remainder of the current season; and (2) The following season. (g) A farmer committing fraud or abuse of FMNP shall be liable to prosecution under applicable federal, state, or local laws. Farmers who have willfully misapplied, stolen, or fraudulently obtained program funds shall automatically: (1) Receive a third sanction; and (2) Be disqualified for: a. The remainder of the current season; and b. The following season. Source. #14200, eff 2-26-25

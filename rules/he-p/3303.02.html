He-P 3303.02 Eligible Foods

Effective 2025-02-26.

He-P 3303.02 Eligible Foods. (a) Eligible foods shall comply with 7 CFR 248.8(a). (b) Eligible foods shall: (1) Be grown within the state of New Hampshire; and (2) Include fresh, unprepared, locally grown fruits, vegetables, and cut herbs. (c) In accordance with 7 CFR 248.8(a), the following foods shall be considered ineligible: (1) Honey: (2) Maple syrup: (3) Cider; (4) Nuts and seeds; (5) Eggs; (6) Cheese; (7) Meats; (8) Seafood; (9) Dried herbs; (10) Herb plants; (11) Pickled vegetables; (12) Those foods grown outside the state of New Hampshire, the United States and its territories; and (13) Other foods identified as not eligible by the FNS. Source. #14200, eff 2-26-25

He-P 803.01 Purpose

Effective 2019-08-28. Earlier expiration 2019-07-29.

He-P 803.01 Purpose. The purpose of this part is to set forth the classification of and licensing requirements for nursing home facilities pursuant to RSA 151:2, I(e)(2) and as described in RSA 151:9, VII(a)(3).

He-P 1611.02 Certification of Lead Educational Programs

Effective 2026-08-26. Expires 2036-08-26.

He-P 1611.02 Certification of Lead Educational Programs. (a) Any person or entity advertising to provide or providing lead educational programs for individuals to be licensed or certified by the state of New Hampshire as lead testing technicians, lead abatement workers, lead abatement supervisors, lead inspectors, or lead risk assessors shall be certified by the department in accordance with this section. (b) The department shall advertise or provide lead educational programs for individuals to be licensed or certified by the state of New Hampshire as lead testing technicians, lead abatement workers, lead abatement supervisors, lead inspectors, or lead risk assessors in accordance with He-P 1611.01(b). (c) The department shall certify the following programs: (1) Lead testing technician, initial or refresher; (2) Lead abatement worker, initial or refresher; (3) Lead abatement supervisor, initial or refresher; (4) Lead inspector, initial or refresher; (5) Lead risk assessor, initial or refresher; and (6) State of New Hampshire rules course which shall focus on the specific New Hampshire requirements of RSA 130-A and He-P 1600 for those seeking licensure, certification, and reciprocity. (d) Fees shall be as follows: (1) For initial applicants for certification of a lead educational program the application fee shall be $300; and (2) For renewal applicants for certification of a lead educational program the application fee shall be $300. (e) The fees in (d) above shall not apply to educational programs for certification and licensure offered by the department. (f) Any added disciplines or revision of course materials beyond those required by He-P 1611.01(d)(7)b. shall be considered an initial application and follow the fee structure in (d)(1) above. (g) Any application fees received by the department shall be processed in accordance with RSA 6:11-a. (h) The department shall process all applications under this section in accordance with RSA 541-A:29. (i) Each applicant for certification as a lead educational program provider shall submit the following to the department through the lead online portal at https://nh-dhhs.my.site.com/lead/s/ or via email to leadlicensing@dhhs.nh.gov, at least 120 days prior to the anticipated start date of the program: (1) A completed “Lead Education Training Provider Application” (August 2026) indicating the program(s) for which certification is being requested certifying the following: “I certify, on my own behalf or on behalf of the individual or entity for whom I am authorized to sign, that I have read, understand, and agree to comply with the New Hampshire Lead Poisoning Prevention Rules (He-P 1600) and the Lead Poisoning Prevention Statute (RSA 130-A). I further certify that all information contained herein, including any supplements attached, is true and correct to the best of my knowledge and belief. The parties agree that this Lead Education Training Provider Application may be executed electronically with electronic signatures, and the electronic signatures shall be valid and binding on both parties. Neither party will contest the enforceability of this Lead Education Training Provider Application on the basis that it was executed electronically.”; (2) The applicable certification fee(s) pursuant to (d) above, paid electronically through the lead online portal at https://nh-dhhs.my.site.com/lead/s/ or by check or money order, in the exact amount made payable to the “Treasurer, State of New Hampshire”; and (3) The following for each program for which the provider is requesting certification: a. A copy of the course agenda, which includes all content required by He-P 1611.03; b. A copy of all student materials, test blueprint, handouts, and instructor curriculum; c. A list of all audiovisual aids; d. All other course materials not listed above; e. For each program manager, principal instructor(s), guest instructor(s), or hands-on skills(s) instructor used by the program: 1. Names and NH license or certification number or photocopies of applicable lead licenses, certificates, or any other documents which have been issued by another state, jurisdiction, Tribal Nation, or the EPA; 2. Photocopies of official academic transcripts issued by the relevant educational institution; 3. Résumés, qualifications, or records of work experience that show that the program manager, principal instructor(s), and guest instructor(s) meets the requirements of He-P 1611.01(d) and (e); and 4. A list identifying which individual will be instructing which course and what content areas and or hands-on skills practice that each individual will be involved with; f. For each program teaching a course in a language other than English, a signed statement that they had compared the course to the English language version and found the translation to be accurate; g. Each instructor teaching the course in a language other than English shall be certified by the department in accordance with He-P 1611.01(e) and be fluent in the language in which they are teaching; h. A description of the hands-on training that shall be provided, including the outline for instruction, the number of students to be accommodated, and the number of instructors; i. A detailed description of the facilities and equipment available for both lecture and hands-on skills practice and assessment; j. A copy of the quality control plan as required in He-P 1611.01(d)(8); k. A description of the format for all course examinations, which shall include: 1. The proportion of written exam questions devoted to each major course topic; and 2. A detailed description of the procedures to be used in the evaluation of hands-on skills for all skills required under He-P 1611.04(d); l. An example of the numbered course completion certificates, as described in He-P 1611.04(e)-(f), to be issued to students who successfully complete the course; and m. Photocopies of applicable lead training provider licenses, certifications, or any other documents which have been issued by another state, jurisdiction, tribal nation or the EPA. (j) In addition to He-P 1611.02(i) above, a request for certification for a state of New Hampshire rules course program shall include documentation that the educational provider is currently certified or is currently requesting certification for at least one initial lead educational program. (k) The following conditions shall apply: (1) The certification(s) shall expire one year from the date of issuance, unless it has been suspended or revoked by the department pursuant to He-P 1606.02; (2) The certified program shall be required to comply with all standards set forth in He-P 1611; and (3) The program manager shall provide any of the above documentation in the paragraphs above, upon request by the department. (l) In order to renew a program certification, each certificate holder shall submit to the department the following within at least 120 days prior to the expiration date of the current certificate: (1) A completed “Lead Education Training Provider Application” (August 2026) indicating the program(s) for which certification is being requested, which has been legibly completed and signed by the applicant; (2) The applicable certification fee(s) pursuant to He-P 1611.02(d) above, paid electronically through the lead on-line portal at https://nh-dhhs.my.site.com/lead/s/ or by check or money order to 29 Hazen Drive, Concord, NH 03301, Attn: Lead Poisoning Prevention Program, in the exact amount made payable to the “Treasurer, State of New Hampshire”; and (3) Any new information required by (i)(3) above that has not been submitted to the department in accordance with He-P 1611.01(f). (m) The denial, suspension, or revocation of a certification issued under this part shall be in accordance with He-P 1606.02.

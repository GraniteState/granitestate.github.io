He-P 4021.17 Reports of Individual Monitoring

Effective 2024-10-01.

He-P 4021.17 Reports of Individual Monitoring. (a) This section applies to each person licensed or registered by DHHS/RHS to: (1) Possess or use sources of radiation for purposes of industrial radiography pursuant to He-P 4030 and He-P 4034; or (2) Possess or use at any time, for processing or manufacturing for distribution pursuant to He-P 4030, He-P 4032, He-P 4033, or He-P 4035, radioactive material in quantities exceeding any one of the quantities indicated in Table 4021.1: Table 4021.1 Radionuclide | Activity Ci | GBq Cesium-137 | 1 | 37 Cobalt-60 | 1 | 37 Gold-198 | 100 | 3,700 Iodine-131 | 1 | 37 Iridium-192 | 10 | 370 Krypton-85 | 1,000 | 37,000 Promethium-147 | 10 | 370 Technetium-99m | 1,000 | 37,000 (b) Each licensee or registrant in a category listed in (a) above, or used radionuclides not on this list in quantities sufficient to cause comparable radiation levels, shall submit an annual report of the results of individual monitoring carried out by the licensee or registrant for each individual for whom monitoring was required by He-P 4022.02 during that year, and that may include additional data for individuals for whom monitoring was provided but not required. (c) The licensee or registrant shall use DHHS/RHS Form Z or equivalent as specified in He-P 4021.07(e) containing all the information required by DHHS/RHS Form Z for filing the annual report described in (b) above. (d) The licensee or registrant shall file the report required by (b) above, covering the preceding year, on or before April 30 of each year, and the licensee or registrant shall submit the report to DHHS/RHS.

He-P 822.01 Purpose

Effective 2019-01-03. Earlier expiration 2018-09-20.

He-P 822.01 Purpose. The purpose of this part is to set forth the licensing requirements for all home care service provider agencies (HCSPA) pursuant to RSA 151:9-a, XI.

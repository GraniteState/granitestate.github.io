He-P 811.20 Patient Records

Effective 2025-02-28. Earlier expiration 2020-01-16.

He-P 811.20 Patient Records. (a) The licensee shall maintain a legible, current, and accurate record for each patient based on services provided at the ESRDDC which includes, at a minimum: (1) The written confirmation required by He-P 811.17(a)(2); (2) The identification data required by He-P 811.17(a)(3); (3) Consent forms as required by He-P 811.17(a)(4); (4) Consent for release of information as applicable required by He-P 811.17(a)(5); (5) The record of the medical history and health examination required by He-P 811.17(a)(6); (6) All orders from a licensed practitioner, including the date and signature of the licensed practitioner; (7) Results of any laboratory tests; (8) All consultation reports; (9) All assessments for the last 12 months; (10) All care plans for the last 12 months; (11) All written notes required by He-P 811.17(b)(10); (12) The medication record required by He-P 811.18(w) and (z); (13) Discharge or transfer documentation as required by He-P 811.17(e); (14) Documentation of nurse delegation of medications as required by the nurse practice act, as applicable; (15) The name and telephone number of the patient’s licensed practitioner; (16) The patient’s health insurance information; (17) Copies of any executed legal orders and directives, such as guardianship orders issued under RSA 464-A, a durable power of attorney for healthcare under RSA 137-J, or a living will; (18) Documentation of reportable incidents; and (19) Documentation of any accident or injuries to the patient occurring while in the care of the ESRDDC and requiring medical care by a practitioner. (b) Patient records shall be: (1) Safeguarded against loss, damage, or unauthorized use; and (2) Retained for 7 years from the date of the patient’s last contact with the licensee, unless, before that date, the patient has requested that the file be transferred to another health care provider, except that when the patient is a minor, records shall be retained for 7 years or until the person reaches the age of 19, whichever is longer. (c) Patient records and patient information shall be kept confidential and only provided in compliance with all applicable federal and state statutes and administrative rules. (d) The licensee shall arrange for storage of and access to patient records as required by (b)(2) above in the event the ESRDDC ceases operation. (e) The licensee shall develop and implement a written policy and procedure document that specifies the method by which release of information from a patient’s record shall occur. (f) Any licensee that maintains electronic records shall develop written policies and procedures designed to protect the privacy of patients and personnel that, at a minimum, include: (1) Procedures for backing up files to prevent loss of data; (2) Safeguards for maintaining the confidentiality of information pertaining to patients and staff; and (3) Systems to prevent tampering with information pertaining to patients and staff. (g) The ESRDDC shall, upon request, provide a patient, guardian, personal representative, or agent with a copy of the patient’s record pursuant to the provisions of RSA 151:21, X.

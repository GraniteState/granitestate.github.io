He-P 3304.01 Local Agency Requirements

Effective 2025-02-26.

He-P 3304.01 Local Agency Requirements. A local health or human service agency shall: (a) Identify FMNP recipients and provide them with FMNP benefits, nutrition education, and related benefits under state-approved agreements between the local WIC agency and the department in accordance with 7 CFR 248.9; and (b) Adhere to monitoring of FMNP policies and procedures once every 2 years in accordance with 7 CFR 248.10(e)(4). Source. #14200, eff 2-26-25

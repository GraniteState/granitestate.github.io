He-P 1612.02 Licensing or Certification Requirements

Effective 2026-08-26. Expires 2036-08-26.

He-P 1612.02 Licensing or Certification Requirements. (a) All individuals and business entities performing lead hazard reduction activities, inspections, or training shall create an account in the lead on-line portal at https://nh-dhhs.my.site.com/lead/s/ or obtain the applicable application from the department. (b) All individuals and business entities performing lead hazard reduction activities, inspections, or training shall be licensed or certified, as applicable, in accordance with RSA 130-A and this part. (c) The following categories of lead professionals and business entities shall be licensed by the department: (1) Lead risk assessor; (2) Lead inspector; and (3) Lead abatement contractor. (d) The following categories of lead professionals shall be certified by the department: (1) Lead abatement supervisor; (2) Lead abatement worker; and (3) Lead testing technician. (e) Pursuant to RSA 130-A:12, an owner-contractor owning more than 4 or more dwelling units shall be required to be licensed, and shall comply with all applicable licensing requirements, including the successful completion of a lead abatement supervisor educational program, successful completion of the required proficiency examinations, and submission of an application to the department. (f) To be licensed as a lead risk assessor, an individual shall be 18 years of age or older and meet the following requirements: (1) Successfully complete a lead risk assessor educational program certified in accordance with He-P 1611, another state, Tribal Nation, or by the EPA with prior approval from the department; (2) Successfully complete the proficiency examinations described in He-P 1612.04(a)(1); and (3) Have one of the following combinations of work and education requirements: a. A bachelor’s degree in any discipline and one year of experience by the individual in a related field of lead, asbestos, environmental remediation work, or construction; or b. An associate’s degree in any discipline and a minimum of 2 years of experience by the individual in a related field of lead, asbestos, environmental remediation work, or construction; c. A high school diploma or equivalent and a minimum of 3 years of experience by the individual in a related field of lead, asbestos, environmental remediation work, or construction; or d. Certification or licensure, as an industrial hygienist, civil, environmental, or structural engineer, architect, or environmental health practitioner. (g) The department shall approve or deny the individuals application for licensure in accordance with He-P 1606.02. (h) To be licensed as a lead inspector, an individual shall be 18 years of age or older and meet the following requirements: (1) Successfully complete a lead inspector educational program certified in accordance with He-P 1611, another state, Tribal Nation, or by the EPA with prior approval from the department; and (2) Successfully complete the proficiency examinations described in He-P 1612.04(a)(1). (i) To be licensed as a lead abatement contractor or to renew an existing lead abatement contractor license, an individual or business entity shall submit to the department a completed “Lead Abatement Contractor Business License Application” (August 2026) and certifying the following: “I certify, on my own behalf or on behalf of the individual or entity for whom I am authorized to sign that I have read, understand, and agree to comply with the New Hampshire Lead Poisoning Prevention Rules (He-P 1600) and the Lead Poisoning Prevention Statute (RSA 130-A). I understand I shall employ only individuals, to include subcontractors, certified or licensed in accordance with He-P 1612, including lead-certified or licensed employees or lead-certified or licensed subcontractor personnel to conduct lead-based paint activities, and the business entity and all its employees and lead-certified subcontractors shall follow the work practice standards of He-P 1600. I understand that the lead abatement contractor license carries no permissions to actively engage in lead-based paint or lead hazard reduction activities. I further certify that all information contained herein, including any supplements attached, is true and correct to the best of my knowledge and belief. The parties agree that this Lead Abatement Contractor Business License Application may be executed electronically with electronic signatures, and the electronic signatures shall be valid and binding on both parties. Neither party will contest the enforceability of this Lead Abatement Contractor Business License Application on the basis that it was executed electronically.” (j) Along with the “Lead Abatement Contractor Business License Application” (August 2026), the applicant shall demonstrate that they meet all applicable standards and requirements, including the following: (1) Evidence that the applicant’s business entity directly employs or employs as a subcontractor, a New Hampshire lead abatement supervisor, lead testing technician, lead inspector, or lead risk assessor licensed or certified in accordance with He-P 1612; (2) A list of all lead associated citations and notices of violation received in the United States including the name of the issuing agency or department and the final disposition of such citation or notice; (3) A list of states in which the applicant holds a license, certification, accreditation, or any other approval for lead abatement activity and description of the licensed activity; (4) The name, email address, and phone number of the person responsible for the day-to-day management or operations of the business entity and who is responsible for all enforcement actions; (5) A list of all individuals or business entities with any ownership interest in the applicant, and all individuals who have authority over the day-to-day management or operation of the business and who are responsible for all enforcement actions; (6) A list of all other business entities engaged in lead abatement or lead hazard control activities in which any individual identified in (4) holds an ownership interest or exercises managerial or operational control; (7) A list of all names and acronyms by which the applicant’s business entity is known or under which it does or has done business; (8) A statement certifying that all of the information provided in support of the application is true and complete from the person responsible for the day-to-day management or operations of the business entity and responsible for all enforcement actions; (9) A list of all individuals in the business entity’s employ that perform lead abatement activities including first and last names and license numbers; (10) A statement attesting that the business entity shall employ only individuals certified or licensed in accordance with He-P 1612, including lead-certified or licensed employees or lead-certified or licensed subcontractor personnel to conduct lead-based paint abatement, inspection, or risk assessment activities, and that the business entity and its employees and lead-certified subcontractors shall follow the work practice standards of He-P 1600 from the person responsible for the day-to-day management or operations of the business entity and responsible for all enforcement actions; and (11) Provide evidence of being registered to do business in the state of New Hampshire, as required by the secretary of state and provide evidence of being in good standing with the secretary of state. (k) To be certified as an owner-contractor, an individual shall be 18 years of age or older and meet the following requirements: (1) Successful completion of a lead abatement supervisor educational program certified in accordance with He-P 1611, another state, Tribal Nation, or by the EPA with prior approval from the department; and (2) Successful completion of the proficiency examinations described in He-P 1612.04(a)(1). (l) To be certified as lead abatement supervisor, an individual shall be 18 years of age or older and meet the following requirements: (1) Successful completion of a lead abatement supervisor educational program certified in accordance with He-P 1611, another state, Tribal Nation, or by the EPA with prior approval from the department; (2) Successful completion of the proficiency examinations described in He-P 1612.04(a)(1); and (3) The individual shall have the following experience requirements: a. At least 12 months of work experience as a certified lead abatement worker; or b. Have at least 2 years of work experience in asbestos, lead, environmental remediation, or in the building trades. (m) To be certified as a lead abatement worker, an individual shall be 18 years of age or older and successfully complete a lead abatement worker educational program certified in accordance with He-P 1611. (n) To be certified as a lead testing technician, an individual shall be 18 years of age or older and meet the following requirements: (1) Successful completion of a lead testing technician education program certified in accordance with He-P 1611; and (2) Successful completion of the proficiency examination described in He-P 1612.04(a)(1). (o) Any individual who has a current New Hampshire license as a lead inspector or risk assessor on the effective date of this section and who continuously maintains that license shall not be required to meet the requirements in (e)(4)and (f)(3)-(4) above. (p) A refresher lead educational program, certified in accordance with He-P 1611 and specific to the discipline of lead professional being applied for, shall be successfully completed every 3 years. (q) All individuals licensed or certified under this part shall notify the department, in writing, within 30 days of any changes to any of the information contained on the most current application submitted to the department.

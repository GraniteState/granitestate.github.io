He-P 2708.03 Permit Issuance and Duration

Effective 2021-03-02. Earlier expiration 2019-03-27.

He-P 2708.03 Permit Issuance and Duration. (a) If the application for a milk route permit meets the requirements of He-P 2708.01, a permit shall be issued. (b) All permits shall expire on August 1 each year. Source. #12631, INTERIM, eff 9-28-18, EXPIRES: 3-27-19; ss by #12743, eff 3-20-19; ss by #13175, eff 3-2-21

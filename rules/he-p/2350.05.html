He-P 2350.05 Fees

Effective 2009-11-05. Expires 2017-11-05.

He-P 2350.05 Fees. (a) The fee for a cold storage license shall be $350. (b) All fees paid shall be: (1) Nontransferable; and (2) Nonrefundable. (c) Any instrument of payment returned to the state shall be processed in accordance with RSA 6:11-a. Source. #8659, eff 6-14-06; ss by #9589, eff 11-5-09, EXPIRED: 11-5-17

He-P 3306.01 Types of Farmer Monitoring

Effective 2025-02-26.

He-P 3306.01 Types of Farmer Monitoring. (a) Food benefits presented for payment from a farmer participating in the FMNP shall be audited by the department for the following: (1) The food benefit redemption costs in relation to the farmer’s posted prices; (2) The foods received by the FMNP recipient; (3) The farmer’s adherence to state and federal regulations in accordance with 7 CFR 248.10(e), and 7 CFR 248.17(c); and (4) Other such factors as determined by individual circumstances including risk factors as described in (d) below. (b) Department staff shall annually monitor 10 percent of the authorized farmers and take corrective action as needed in accordance with 7 CFR 248.10(e). (c) Farmers that meet one or more of the risk factors identified in (d) below shall be reviewed during the FMNP season for: (1) Continued authorization; or (2) Sanctioning in accordance with He-P 3307. (d) Risk factors shall include one or more of the following: (1) Recurring written or verbal complaints by recipient(s) or local agency representatives; (2) Sanctions received during the previous FMNP season; (3) High volume of FMNP redemptions by a farmer or farmers’ market in comparison to other farms and farmers’ markets across the state; (4) Farmers in their first year of FMNP operation; (5) A recurring incidence of non-compliance with program rules and federal regulations; and (6) Other concerns brought to the department for review such as but not limited to, recurring written or verbal complaints by market managers or community members and transactions being completed after business hours reported on the application. Source. #14200, eff 2-26-25

He-P 2702.08 Effect of Denial of Application, Suspension, or Revocation

Effective 2021-03-02. Earlier expiration 2008-12-28.

He-P 2702.08 Effect of Denial of Application, Suspension, or Revocation. (a) Any applicant who has been denied a producer permit shall not offer milk for sale to any milk plant or cooperative. (b) If a producer permit has been suspended, the milk producer shall cease operation immediately, in accordance with RSA 184:90-a. (c) If a producer permit has been revoked, the milk producer shall not offer milk for sale to any milk plant or cooperative.

He-P 802.02 Scope

Effective 2024-09-25. Expires 2034-09-25.

He-P 802.02 Scope. This part shall apply to any individual, agency, partnership, corporation, government entity, association, or other legal entity operating a hospital, except: (a) All facilities listed in RSA 151:2, II(a)-(i); and (b) All entities which are owned or operated by the state of New Hampshire, pursuant to RSA 151:2, II(i).

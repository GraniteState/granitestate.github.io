He-P 1608.06 Notification of Work

Effective 2026-08-26. Expires 2036-08-26.

He-P 1608.06 Notification of Work. (a) The lead abatement contractor or person granted a variance in accordance with He-P 1605.03, shall notify the department electronically through the lead on-line portal at https://nh-dhhs.my.site.com/lead/s/ or via email to nhleadprogram@dhhs.nh.gov of the intended schedule of work at least 5 days before lead abatement work begins. (b) A complete notification of work shall be submitted to the department and the owner or owner’s agent at least 5 days prior to the abatement project beginning and include: (1) The date when lead-based paint activities will start; (2) The date when lead-based paint activities will end, approximation using best professional judgement; (3) The lead abatement contractor business name and license number; (4) Contact information for the lead abatement contractor’s business including name, phone number, and email for the primary contact person; (5) The property address for abatement work including apartment or unit number(s), if applicable; and (6) The name and license number for the lead abatement supervisor for the project. (c) If not received by the department prior, a notification of work shall be accompanied by: (1) A copy of the work scope, detailing actions to be taken to abate or manage by interim controls all lead-based substances; and (2) A copy of the occupant protection plan prepared in accordance with He-P 1608.05. (d) Notification of changes to the start or end date(s) documented on the notification of work shall be made by the lead abatement contractor, lead abatement supervisor, or person granted a variance by notifying the department through the lead on-line portal at https://nh-dhhs.my.site.com/lead/s/, via email to nhleadprogram@dhhs.nh.gov, or by contacting the department by telephone at least 48 hours before the new start or end date(s). (e) The owner or owner’s agent of a leased or rented dwelling or dwelling unit shall provide written notification to all occupants affected by the lead hazard reduction work at least 5 days in advance of the proposed lead hazard reduction work, which includes: (1) Scheduled dates and work hours for the lead hazard reduction work; (2) Identification of work site(s); (3) Information on the alternative entrance or exit to be used during the work; and (4) A copy of the occupant protection plan.

He-P 3307.01 Recipients

Effective 2025-02-26.

He-P 3307.01 Recipients. (a) Recipient sanctions shall be as follows: (1) The actions of a recipient, a parent, or caretaker of an infant or child recipient, a proxy, or an FMNP benefit holder shall be binding on the recipient in the determination of violations and imposition of sanctions; (2) Sanctions shall only be imposed on those recipients for whom the evidence supports the sanction and not to all participating family members, even though the violations might have been committed with food benefits issued to one member; and (3) When more than one violation is involved, the sanction shall be based on the highest category violation. (b) Category I violations shall include actions related to misuse of program benefits or disruptive recipient behavior such as: (1) Failure to follow proper redemption procedures such as attempting to purchase or purchasing non-eligible FMNP foods; and (2) Behavior within a farmers’ market that is disruptive but not abusive or threatening. (c) Category I violations shall be subject to the following sanctions: (1) Written warning for the first occurrence of violation within the FMNP season, unless a violation of Category II pursuant to (d)(1) below for abusive behavior has been issued within the same FMNP season, then (e)(2) below shall be implemented; or (2) Disqualification of the remaining FMNP season for the second occurrence of any violation within the same FMNP season as the warning letter in (c)(1). (d) Category II violations shall include actions related to more serious misuse of program benefits or abusive recipient behavior such as: (1) Abusive or threatening behavior that includes, for example, swearing, hitting, or threatening at a farmers’ market, that is directed towards WIC staff, the farmer, or other FMNP recipients; (2) Misrepresentation of eligibility for FMNP benefits; (3) Exchange of FMNP benefits for cash or credit from farmers; and (4) Sale, attempted sale, or exchange of FMNP eligible foods for cash, goods, or services. (e) Category II violations shall be subject to the following sanctions: (1) Written warning for the first actual or attempted occurrence of violation; or (2) Disqualification for the remainder of the current FMNP season and the following FMNP season for the second occurrence of any violation within the same FMNP season as the warning letter (e)(1). (f) The department shall collect reimbursement for improperly obtained items as follows: (1) In accordance with 7 CFR 248.20, the department shall demand repayment, in writing, from the recipient or parent or caretaker of an infant or child recipient equal to the full value of these benefits; (2) The department shall issue a second demand for repayment if full restitution is not made, or a repayment schedule is not agreed upon within 30 days of receipt of the demand for repayment as described in (f)(1) above; and (3) If no written or oral response has been received from the recipient, parent, or caretaker of an infant or child recipient after the second demand for repayment as described in (f)(2) above, the department shall refer the matter to the New Hampshire department of justice for enforcement.

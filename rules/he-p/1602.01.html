He-P 1602.01 Definitions

Effective 2026-08-26. Expires 2036-08-26.

He-P 1602.01 Definitions. (a) “Abatement” means measure(s) designed to permanently eliminate lead-based paint hazards, or in the case of dust-lead hazards to reduce to below the action levels, as defined in 40 CFR Part 745.223 including: (1) Activities resulting in the permanent elimination of lead-based paint hazards, or in the case of dust-lead hazards to reduce to below the action levels, including all preparation, cleanup, disposal, and post-abatement testing activities associated with such measures conducted by individuals certified or licensed in accordance with He-P 1612; (2) Activities resulting in the permanent elimination of lead-based paint hazards, or in the case of dust-lead hazards to reduce to below the action levels, that are conducted in response to an investigation, an order of lead hazard reduction or other enforcement action undertaken by the commissioner pursuant to RSA 130-A:5 or RSA 130-A:7, or by a local health department pursuant to RSA 130-A:11, II; or (3) Any other measures or set of measures conducted in, or to, a residential dwelling, dwelling unit, or child-care facility designed to permanently eliminate lead-based paint hazards, or in the case of dust-lead hazards to reduce to below the action levels. (b) “Abrasive blasting” means the procedure of removing paint from a surface by using a mechanical force to apply an abrasive material, including sand, grit, or other abrasive material, to a painted surface. (c) “Action levels” means the values that indicate the amount of lead in dust on a surface following completion of an abatement activity. (d) “Administrative inspection warrant” means “inspection warrant” as defined in RSA 595-B:1. (e) “Bare soil”, as used in the definition of “lead exposure hazard” in RSA 130-A:1, XVI(d), means soil or sand that is accessible to children or pets and is not covered with grass, sod, other vegetation, asphalt, concrete, decking, or other substantive covering, and includes soil or sand in a sandbox or play area. (f) “Blood lead level” means a blood lead measurement obtained by a diagnostic blood lead test conducted by a laboratory certified pursuant to 42 CFR Part 493 and, if applicable, licensed pursuant to He-P 808. (g) “Business entity” means a partnership, firm, association, corporation, sole proprietorship, or other business concern. (h) “Certificate of lead safe” means a certificate issued by a licensed lead risk assessor following a risk assessment or post abatement inspection when no lead exposure paint and soil hazards are identified or remain and lead dust levels are below the action level at the time of the inspection or assessment. (i) “Certification” means the process of being certified. (j) “Certified” means that an individual has a current valid certificate from the commissioner and has complied with all certification requirements set forth in this chapter. (k) “Child” or “children” means “child” or “children” as defined in RSA 130-A:1, I. (l) “Child care facility” means “child care facility” as defined in RSA 130-A:1, II. (m) “Clinical laboratory improvement amendments” (CLIA) means the requirements outlined at 42 CFR Part 493 which set forth the conditions that all laboratories need to meet to be certified to perform testing on human specimens. (n) “Commissioner” means “commissioner” as defined by RSA 130-A:1, III, or their designee. (o) “Common areas” means a portion of a building that is generally accessed by occupants and users including areas on the interior of the dwelling or exterior of the property such as hallways, stairways, laundry areas, attics, recreational rooms, yards, parking areas, play areas, and the building’s exterior, garages, and boundary fences. (p) “Component” means specific design or structural elements or fixtures in the interior or on the exterior of the dwelling, dwelling unit, or child care facility that are distinguished from each other by form, function, or location onto which paint, stain, varnish, shellac, or other similar coating has been applied and which have a common substrate. (q) “Containment” means the physical measures taken to ensure that dust and debris created or released during lead hazard reduction are not spread, blown, or tracked from inside to outside the worksite. (r) “Damage” means failure of a paint film or the underlying substrate that results in the paint becoming detached or is at risk of becoming detached from the substrate, including: (1) Peeling, chipping, flaking, or chalking paint; (2) Plaster which is crumbling; (3) Paint that can be removed with a fingernail; or (4) Other paint film or underlying substrate failure. (s) “Days” means calendar days unless otherwise specified. (t) “Department” means “department” as defined by RSA 130-A:1, IV. (u) “Deteriorated” means any condition of reduced structural or surface integrity. (v) “Dust-lead hazard” means surface dust in a residential dwelling or child-occupied facility that contains a mass-per-area concentration of any reportable level of lead based on wipe samples analyzed by a United States Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) National Lead Laboratory Accreditation Program (NLLAP) recognized laboratory. (w) “Dwelling” means “dwelling” as defined in RSA 130-A:1, V. (x) “Dwelling unit” means “dwelling unit” as defined in RSA 130-A:1, VI. (y) “Electronic” means the submission of work products, applications, payments, forms, or notifications using the department's lead on-line portal, successor platform, or email. (z) “Encapsulation” means “encapsulation” as defined in RSA 130-A:1, VII. (aa) “Enclosure” means the use of rigid durable construction materials that are fastened to the substrate in order to act as a barrier between lead-based paint and the living space and does not include encapsulants as defined in (z) above. (ab) “Environmental inspection” means an on-site inspection, conducted by the department, of the dwelling, dwelling unit, or child care facility for the purpose of determining if lead exposure hazards exist for a child. (ac) “Environmental investigation” means the collection of documentation and evidence, used to determine potential sources of lead exposure for a child in accordance with RSA 130-A:5. (ad) “Environmental lead testing laboratory” means a laboratory recognized by the EPA NLLAP to accurately analyze paint chips, dust, or soil samples for lead. (ae) “Friction surface” means a surface, such as a door, floor, stair, or window, which is subject to abrasion, damage, or deterioration with normal repeated use. (af) “Good cause” means any circumstances beyond a person’s control, that prevents that person from complying with an order of lead hazard reduction or taking some required action, including: (1) A death in the person’s immediate family; (2) Personal injury or serious illness of the person or an immediate family member; or (3) Another compelling reason or justification. (ag) “Hazardous waste” means “hazardous waste” as defined in RSA 147-A:2, VII. (ah) “Health authority” means “health authority” as defined in RSA 130-A:1, VIII. (ai) “HEPA vacuum” means a vacuum cleaner equipped with a high-efficiency particulate air (HEPA) filter capable of capturing particles of 0.3 microns or greater from a body of air, with 99.97 percent efficiency, and is designed so that all air drawn into the machine is expelled through the HEPA filter. (aj) “Impact surface” means a point of contact such as a door, floor, stair, or window where the component is subject to repeated impact through normal use, sufficient to cause damage or deterioration of lead-based paint. (ak) “In-house training” means training offered by the department and certified by the department pursuant to RSA 130-A:2, I(h) for the certification and licensure of lead abatement supervisors, lead inspectors, lead risk assessors, lead testing technicians, and lead abatement workers. (al) “In-place management” means “in-place management” as defined in RSA 130-A:1, XII. (am) “Interim controls” means “interim controls” as defined in RSA 130-A:1, IX. (an) “Laboratory” means any site that tests blood lead levels on individuals residing in New Hampshire. (ao) “Lead abatement contractor” means “lead abatement contractor” as defined in RSA 130-A:1, XIV. (ap) “Lead abatement supervisor” means a lead abatement worker that supervises other lead abatement workers and is certified in accordance with He-P 1612 to perform responsibilities as outlined in He-P 1612.01(e). (aq) “Lead abatement worker” means “lead abatement worker” as defined in RSA 130-A:1, X. (ar) “Lead-based paint” means paint or other surface coating that contains lead equal to or in excess of 1.0 milligram per square centimeter or 0.5 percent by weight or as established by the EPA, in which case the federal standard shall prevail. (as) “Lead-based paint inspection” means a surface-by-surface investigation to determine the presence of lead-based paint and the provision of a report explaining the results of the investigation. (at) “Lead-based substance” means “lead-base substance” as defined in RSA 130-A:1, XI. (au) “Lead-based substance inspection” means a surface-by-surface investigation to determine the presence of lead-based paint, collection and analysis of environmental samples including dust and soil samples, and the provision of a report explaining the results of the investigation. (av) “Lead testing technician” means “lead clearance testing technician” as defined in RSA 130-A:1, XVI-c. (aw) “Lead-containing waste material” means any waste, debris, dust, or material intended for disposal, including items such as disposable equipment and clothing that contains lead that was generated by lead hazard reduction work regulated under this chapter. (ax) “Lead education program” means any persons or entities offering or providing lead educational programs to individuals or entities to be licensed or certified as lead inspectors, lead risk assessors, lead abatement supervisors, lead testing technicians, or lead abatement workers by the state of New Hampshire in accordance with RSA 130-A and He-P 1611. (ay) “Lead exposure hazard” means “lead exposure hazard” as defined in RSA 130-A:1, XVI. (az) “Lead hazard reduction” means measures used to control lead exposure hazards through abatement, interim controls, or a combination of the 2 measures. (ba) “Lead inspection” means an examination to determine the presence of a lead-based substance, which can be used to evaluate an existing or potential lead exposure hazard, or to determine compliance with RSA 130-A or He-P 1600, and includes: (1) Post abatement inspections, both preliminary and final; (2) Compliance inspections; (3) Environmental inspections, as part of investigations; (4) Lead-based paint inspections; (5) Lead-based substances inspections; (6) Inspections as part of a risk assessment; (7) Visual, dust, and soil inspections; and (8) Any other examination to determine the presence of a lead-based substance, which can be used to evaluate an existing or potential lead exposure hazard, or to determine compliance with RSA 130-A or He-P 1600. (bb) “Lead inspection survey report” means a report, prepared by the department, documenting an environmental inspection of a dwelling, dwelling unit, or child-care facility where a lead exposure hazard exists for a child. (bc) “Lead inspector” means “lead inspector” as defined in RSA 130-A:1, XV. (bd) “Lead investigation” means a request for information on an environmental inspection performed by the department to determine potential sources of lead exposure for a child. (be) “Lead professional” means an individual who performs lead-based paint activities for remuneration, including individuals licensed or certified in accordance with He-P 1612 such as a lead abatement worker, a lead abatement supervisor, a lead inspector, a lead testing technician, or a lead risk assessor. (bf) “Lead risk assessor” means “lead risk assessor” as defined in RSA 130-A:1, XVI-b. (bg) “Lead safe” means a designation made after a risk assessment or a post abatement inspection has been completed and the dwelling, dwelling unit, or child care facility does not have paint and soil lead exposure hazards and lead dust levels are below the action level at the time of the assessment or inspection. (bh) “Letter of recommendation” means a letter issued to an owner explaining steps they are recommended to take to identify and eliminate lead exposure hazards. (bi) “Licensed” means that a person has a currently valid license from the commissioner and has complied with all licensing requirements set forth in this chapter. (bj) “Licensure” means the process of obtaining a valid license. (bk) “Mini-containment area” means the construction of a temporary walled area to isolate a specific space requiring lead hazard reduction work in order to ensure that no dust or debris leaves the space while work is being performed. (bl) “Multi-family dwelling” means a dwelling consisting of 2 or more dwelling units on a single lot including: (1) Apartment buildings; (2) Townhouses; (3) Condominiums; (4) Shared housing; (5) Rooming units; (6) Rooming houses; and (7) Other dwellings consisting of 2 or more dwelling units on a single lot. (bm) “Newly-constructed rental units” means “newly-constructed rental units” as defined in RSA 130-A:5-d, I, namely, “rental units being converted from a use other than residential rental housing”. (bn) “Occupant” means “occupant” as defined in RSA 130-A:1, XVII. (bo) “Occupant protection plan” means the measures and management procedures that will be taken during the lead hazard reduction activities to protect the building occupants. (bp) “Occupied” means a dwelling or dwelling unit where a person resides or a property being used as a child care facility. (bq) “Order of lead hazard reduction” means an order issued by the commissioner requiring all lead exposure hazards be reduced or eliminated from a given dwelling, dwelling unit, or child care facility. (br) “Owner” means “owner” as defined in RSA 130-A:1, XVIII. (bs) “Owner’s agent” means a person who has charge, care, or control of a dwelling or dwelling unit and has authority to fund capital or major property rehabilitation on behalf of the owner. This term includes “owner’s designee”. (bt) “Owner-contractor” means an individual who: (1) Meets the lead abatement supervisor education and licensing requirements for an initial or renewed license but is not required to have 2 years’ work experience; (2) Complies with the functions and responsibilities of a licensed lead abatement contractor and lead abatement supervisor; and (3) Performs lead hazard reduction work only on dwellings and dwelling units owned by the individual. (bu) “Owner occupied” means a dwelling or dwelling unit that is in use and occupied by the owner as defined in RSA 130-A:1, XVIII. (bv) “Participating” or “participate” “in the management of a dwelling, dwelling unit, or child care facility” means “participating” or “participate” “in the management of a dwelling, dwelling unit, or child care facility” as defined in RSA 130-A:1, XIX. (bw) “Permanently”, as used in the definition of “lead base substance abatement” in RSA 130-A:1, XIII, means that the duration of the method of abatement can reasonably be depended upon to last for a minimum of 20 years. (bx) “Person” means “person” as defined in RSA 130-A:1, XX. (by) “Play area” means an area of bare soil that is used by children as indicated by the following: (1) The presence of play equipment such as sandboxes, swing sets, and sliding boards; (2) Toys or other children’s possessions; (3) Observations of play patterns or wear; (4) Information provided by parents, residents, care givers, or owners; or (5) Any other evidence given or observed indicating a child uses the area for play. (bz) “Post abatement inspection” means a visual assessment, collection, and analysis of environmental samples following lead hazard reduction activities to determine that the lead hazard reduction activities are complete and no lead exposure paint and soil hazards remain in the dwelling, dwelling unit, or childcare facility and dust-lead hazards are below the action levels. (ca) “Rental” means any dwelling or dwelling unit that is not occupied by the owner listed on the deed. (cb) “Reportable level” means the lowest analyte concentration or amount that does not contain a “less than” qualifier and that is reported with confidence for a specific method by a laboratory recognized by EPA under TSCA section 405(b). (cc) “Risk assessment” means an on-site inspection conducted by a New Hampshire licensed lead risk assessor, to determine the existence, nature, severity, and location of lead exposure hazards, and the provision of a written report explaining the results of the inspection and options for reducing lead exposure hazards. (cd) “Rooming house” means a dwelling with multiple rooms rented out individually in which tenants share bathroom and kitchen facilities. (ce) “Rooming unit” means any room or group of rooms forming a single habitable unit intended for living and sleeping, but not for cooking or eating. (cf) “Substrate” means the material underneath paint and includes materials such as brick, concrete, drywall, metal, plasters, and wood. (cg) “Window well” means, for a typical double-hung window, the portion of the exterior window sill between the interior window sill or stool and the frame of the storm window, or when there is no storm window, the area that receives both the upper and lower window sashes when they are both lowered. The term includes “window trough”. (ch) “Work scope” means a written document detailing all lead hazard reduction activities including identification and location of each component as well as abatement or interim control methods planned for use for each component. (ci) “Work site” means an interior or exterior area where lead hazard reduction activity takes place. There might be more than one work site within a residential dwelling, dwelling unit, child care facility, or within individual rooms. When used, a mini containment system might constitute the work site. (cj) “X-ray fluorescence analyzers” (XRF) means a handheld non-destructive analytical device used to determine the elemental composition of materials by measuring the fluorescent or secondary X-rays emitted from a sample when excited by a primary X-ray source.

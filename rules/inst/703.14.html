Inst 703.14 Utility Connection and Testing

Effective 2025-02-25.

Inst 703.14 Utility Connection and Testing. (a) The installer shall consult with the authority having jurisdiction before connecting the manufactured house to utilities. (b) Individuals familiar with local and state codes shall be permitted to make utility site connections and conduct tests. (c) All utility, or exterior utility installations shall conform with the state fire code, Saf-C 6000 and the state building code pursuant to RSA 155-A.

Inst 103.06 Duties of the Board

Effective 2025-01-17.

Inst 103.06 Duties of the Board. (a) The statutory responsibilities of the board created by RSA 205-D:2, V and RSA 205-D:3 shall include but are not limited to: (1) Adopting rules and regulations to administer RSA 205-D in accordance with RSA 541-A; (2) Establishing licensing qualifications and standards for manufactured housing installers; (3) Setting standards for installation including but not limited to, supports, anchoring, and underpinning of manufactured houses installed in this state; (4) Establishing requirements for inspections; (5) Undertaking disciplinary proceedings in accordance with Plc 200; (6) Establishing requirements on forms as necessary; and (7) Developing and providing a program to train and educate installers or referring to a board approved program developed and taught by a private agency. (8) Developing and providing alternative dispute resolution. Source. #8492, eff 12-24-05; amd by #10336, eff 5-15-13; ss by #14111, eff 1-17-25

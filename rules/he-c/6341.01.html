He-C 6341.01 Purpose

Effective 2018-11-30. Earlier expiration 2018-11-20.

He-C 6341.01 Purpose. The purpose of this part is to identify the qualifications and compliance requirements to become a provider of communication access services for the division for children, youth and families (DCYF) to meet the communication access needs of individuals who are deaf, have hearing loss, are blind, have vision loss, are deaf-blind, have speech disabilities, or have limited English proficiency (LEP) when the criteria for the department wide contract for communication access services requiring the presence of DHHS staff are not met. Source. (See Revision Note at part heading for He-C 6341) #9392, eff 3-1-09, EXPIRED: 3-1-17 New. #12535, INTERIM, eff 5-24-18, EXPIRED: 11-20-18 New. #12684, eff 11-30-18

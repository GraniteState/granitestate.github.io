He-C 6910.01 Purpose

Effective 2024-04-30.

He-C 6910.01 Purpose. The purpose of this part is for the department of health and human services (DHHS), through the division of economic stability (DES), to: (a) Establish the eligibility criteria for DHHS’s 12-month employment-related child care scholarship program, which enables families to prepare for, secure, or maintain employment, and to support healthy child development for those families who meet, and continue to meet, the eligibility requirements of He-C 6910; and (b) Establish the payment amounts for the employment-related child care scholarship.

He-C 505.02 Definitions

Effective 2000-11-21. Expires 2008-11-12.

He-C 505.02 Definitions. (a) “Applicant” means any person, including any current or prospective state employee, who is selected for any position in DHHS through any application process, including hiring, promotion, transfer, contract, or demotion. (b) “Central registry” means the central registry of founded reports of child abuse and neglect as established by and defined under RSA 169-C:35. (c) “Child” means a person 18 years of age or younger. (d) “Criminal records” means information relative to conviction for felonies, misdemeanors, or violations maintained by the New Hampshire division of state police as provided for under RSA 106-B:14. (e) “Director” means the administrator of human resources for DHHS or his or her designee. Source. #7407, eff 11-21-00, EXPIRED: 11-12-08

He-C 6448.24 Applications by Employees of Child-Placing Agency to Adopt or Obtain a Foster Family Home License

Effective 2019-10-24. Earlier expiration 2019-10-29.

He-C 6448.24 Applications by Employees of Child-Placing Agency to Adopt or Obtain a Foster Family Home License. Persons employed by a child-placing agency who wish to adopt or obtain a foster family home license shall: (a) Apply, pursuant to He-C 6446.03- He-C 6446.12, to a licensed child-placing agency other than the one where they are employed; and (b) Receive services from a child-placing agency other than where they are employed.

He-C 1001.04 Duties of the Commission

Effective 2022-06-21.

He-C 1001.04 Duties of the Commission. (a) The commission shall consult with and advise the commissioner on the administration and management of the trust fund, and approve the selection of eligible fund recipients under RSA 126-A:83, II(b). (b) The commissioner shall approve funding awards, revolving loan funds, and matching funds for qualifying projects from the trust fund under RSA 126-A:83, I, in a manner compliant with RSA 126-A:83-86. Source. #13396, eff 6-21-22

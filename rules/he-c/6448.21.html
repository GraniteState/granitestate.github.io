He-C 6448.21 Annual Monitoring and Reporting

Effective 2019-10-24. Earlier expiration 2019-10-29.

He-C 6448.21 Annual Monitoring and Reporting. (a) A completed form 2648 “Child-Placing Agency Annual Report” (October 2019) shall be submitted by a licensed child-placing agency to DCYF one year from the date of the initial issuance of the child-placing agency license and at the anniversary date for every year the child-placing agency continues to be licensed. (b) Form 2648 “Child-Placing Agency Annual Report” (October 2019) shall be available on https://www.dhhs.nh.gov/dcyf/adoption/index.htm for downloading.

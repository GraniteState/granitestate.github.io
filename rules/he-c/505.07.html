He-C 505.07 Confidentiality

Effective 2000-11-21. Expires 2008-11-12.

He-C 505.07 Confidentiality. (a) All applicant and employee information obtained under this part shall be subject to department of personnel rules regarding confidentiality pursuant to Per 1500, except as specified in (b) below. (b) In accordance with RSA 170-G:8-c, VII and RSA 106-B:14, prior to transfer of an employee’s personnel records to any other state department, any information obtained from the division of state police or central registry shall be destroyed.

He-C 6344.11 Notification of Changes for Private Providers

Effective 2017-06-10.

He-C 6344.11 Notification of Changes for Private Providers. The private provider shall: (a) Notify DCYF in writing within 10 business days of any change in the information contained in the application and changes to the items required by He-C 6344.07 (c)(1) - (3) above, and provide documentation of the change; and (b) Submit a copy of the renewed license, at the time of expiration of mandatory state licenses to DCYF within 10 days of receipt from the licensing authority. Source. (See Revision Note at part heading for He-C 6344) #9311, eff 11-5-08; ss by #12206, eff 6-10-17 (from He-C 6344.14)

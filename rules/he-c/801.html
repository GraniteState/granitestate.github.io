He-C 801 HEALTH PROFESSIONS SURVEY

PART He-C 801 HEALTH PROFESSIONS SURVEY REVISION NOTE: Document #12774, effective 5-7-19, adopted Part He-C 801, which incorporated by reference 6 Department of Health and Human Services forms in He-C 801.03(a) and one form in He-C 801.04(b) as follows: In He-C 801.03(a)(1), the “New Hampshire APRN Licensure Survey” (April 2019); In He-C 801.03(a)(2), the “New Hampshire Physician Assistant Licensure Survey” (April 2019); In He-C 801.03(a)(3), the “New Hampshire Physician Licensure Survey” (April 2019); In He-C 801.03(a)(4), “New Hampshire Mental Health Practitioner Licensure Survey” (April 2019); In He-C 801.03(a)(5), the “New Hampshire Alcohol and Drug Counselor Licensure Survey” (April 2019); In He-C 801.03(a)(6), the “New Hampshire Psychologist Licensure Survey” (April 2019); and In He-C 801.04(b), the “New Hampshire Health Professions Survey Opt-Out Form” (April 2019). The following documents after Document #12774 readopted with amendments the forms indicated pursuant to the expedited revisions to agency forms process in RSA 541-A:19-c. The documents contained only the updated forms, giving them a new effective date, while leaving the effective date of the rules in He-C 801 under Document #12774 as 5-7-19. The forms’ revision dates in the rules were subsequently changed editorially: Document #12950, effective 12-21-19, amended the “New Hampshire Health Professions Survey Opt-Out Form”, updating the revision date from April 2019 to December 2019; Document #13053, effective 6-11-20, amended the “New Hampshire Health Professions Survey Opt-Out Form”, updating the revision date from December 2019 to May 2020; Document #13060, effective 6-25-20, amended the “New Hampshire APRN Licensure Survey” and the “New Hampshire Mental Health Practitioner Licensure Survey”, updating the revision dates from April 2019 to June 2020; Document #13061, effective 6-25-20, amended the “New Hampshire Physician Licensure Survey”, the “New Hampshire Alcohol and Drug Counselor Licensure Survey”, and the “New Hampshire Psychologist Licensure Survey”, updating the revision dates from April 2019 to June 2020; and Document #13127, effective 10-30-20, amended the “New Hampshire Physician Assistant Licensure Survey”, updating the revision date from April 2019 to October 2020. Document #13172, effective 2-23-21, readopted with amendments He-C 801, including continued incorporation by reference without change of the forms in He-C 801.03(a) and amendment of the form incorporated by reference in He-C 801.04(b), the “New Hampshire Health Professions Survey Opt-Out Form”, updating the revision date of that form from May 2020 to February 2021. Document #13172 also incorporated by reference 2 new forms in He-C 801.03(a) as follows: In He-C 801.03(a)(7), the “New Hampshire Dentist Licensure Survey” (February 2021); and In He-C 801.03(a)(8), the “New Hampshire Dental Hygienist Licensure Survey” (February 2021). The following documents after Document #13172 readopted with amendments the forms indicated in He-C 801.03(a) and He-C 801.04(b) pursuant to the expedited revisions to agency forms process in RSA 541-A:19-c. The documents contained only the updated forms, giving them a new effective date, while leaving the effective date of the rules in He-C 801 under Document #13172 as 2-23-21. The forms’ revision dates in the rules were subsequently changed editorially: Document #13182, effective 3-24-21, amended the “New Hampshire APRN Licensure Survey”, the “New Hampshire Physician Licensure Survey”, the “New Hampshire Mental Health Practitioner Licensure Survey”, the “New Hampshire Alcohol and Drug Counselor Licensure Survey”, and the “New Hampshire Psychologist Licensure Survey”, updating the revision date of each of the 5 forms to March 2021; Document #13239, effective 7-20-21, amended the “New Hampshire APRN Licensure Survey”, the “New Hampshire Mental Health Practitioner Licensure Survey”, and the “New Hampshire Health Professions Survey Opt-Out Form”, updating the revision date of the 3 forms to July 2021; and Document #13325, effective 1-6-22, amended the “New Hampshire Physician Assistant Licensure Survey”, updating the revision date from October 2020 to October 2021. Document #13352, effective 3-19-22, amended the “New Hampshire Health Professions Survey Opt-Out Form”, the “New Hampshire Alcohol and Drug Counselor Licensure Survey”, the “New Hampshire Physician Licensure Survey”, and the “New Hampshire Psychologist Licensure Survey”, updating the revision dates of the 4 forms to March 2022. Document #13353, effective 3-19-22, amended the “New Hampshire Mental Health Practitioner Licensure Survey” and the “New Hampshire APRN Licensure Survey”, updating the revision dates of the 2 forms to March 2022. Document #13389, effective 5-27-22, amended the “New Hampshire Health Professions Survey Opt-Out Form”, updating the revision date of the form from March 2022 in Document #13352 to May 2022. Document #13447, effective 9-21-22, amended the “New Hampshire Physician Assistant Licensure Survey”, updating the revision date of the form from October 2021 in Document #13325 to September 2022. Document #13579, effective 3-18-23, amended the “New Hampshire Physician Licensure Survey”, updating the revision date of the form from March 2022 in Document #13352 to March 2023. Document #13590, effective 3-24-23, amended the “New Hampshire Dental Hygienist Licensure Survey”, updating the revision date of the form from March 2022 in Document #13352 to March 2023. Document #13593, effective 3-24-23, amended the “New Hampshire Alcohol and Drug Counselor Licensure Survey” and the “New Hampshire Psychologist Licensure Survey”, updating the revision date of the 2 forms from March 2022 in Document #13352 to March 2023. Document #13618, effective 4-26-23, amended the “New Hampshire APRN Licensure Survey” and the “New Hampshire Mental Health Practitioner Licensure Survey”, updating the revision dates of the 2 forms from March 2022 in Document #13353 to April 2023. Document #13729, effective 8-22-23, amended the “New Hampshire APRN Licensure Survey”, updating the revision date of the form from April 2023 in Document #13618 to August 2023. Document #13782, effective 10-21-23, amended the “New Hampshire Physician Assistant Licensure Survey”, updating the revision date of the form from September 2022 in Document #13447 to October 2023. The expedited revisions to forms process in RSA 541-A:19-c was amended by 2023, 191:14, effective 10-3-23, to require that the affected, amended rule be adopted and filed with the updated form. The source notes of the affected rules in He-C 801 will therefore indicate the filing of such rules in the expedited process subsequent to Document #13782.

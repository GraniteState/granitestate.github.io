He-C 505.01 Purpose

Effective 2000-11-21. Expires 2008-11-12.

He-C 505.01 Purpose. (a) The rules in this part describe the procedures to be followed by the department of health and human services (DHHS) in reviewing information on new, transferring or contract staff members of DHHS whose job descriptions would cause them to come into direct contact with children. The names of these individuals will be reviewed against the state central registry of founded child abuse and neglect reports as well as state police criminal records information. (b) These rules further the department’s commitment to support families and ensure the protection of children and the communities in which they live. Source. #7407, eff 11-21-00, EXPIRED: 11-12-08

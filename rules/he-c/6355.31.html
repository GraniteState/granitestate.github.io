He-C 6355.31 Administrative Appeals and Hearings

Effective 2025-03-29. Expires 2025-09-25.

He-C 6355.31 Administrative Appeals and Hearings. (a) Applicants or certified programs that plan to appeal a decision made by the department relative to the denial of an application or revocation of certification, shall file an appeal with the commissioner, within 10 business days of the date of the letter or other written notification of the action, pursuant to RSA 170-G:4-a, RSA 541-A and He-C 200. (b) The appeal shall be: (1) Made in writing; (2) Signed by a person authorized to submit the appeal; and (3) State the reasons for the appeal pursuant to RSA 170-G:4-a.

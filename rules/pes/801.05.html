Pes 801.05 Reuse of Pesticide Containers

Effective 2024-05-31. Earlier expiration 2014-06-16.

Pes 801.05 Reuse of Pesticide Containers. No pesticide container shall be re-used for any other purpose except as provided on the manufacturers label. Source. #5127, eff 4-29-91; ss by #6317, eff 8-13-96; ss by #7987, eff 1-3-04, EXPIRED: 1-3-12 New. #10489, INTERIM, eff 12-17-13, EXPIRED: 6-16-14 New. #10712, eff 11-5-14; ss by #13981, eff 5-31-24

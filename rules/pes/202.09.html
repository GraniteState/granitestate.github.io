Pes 202.09 Appearance Before the Board

Effective 2012-10-03.

Pes 202.09 Appearance Before the Board. (a) Subject to (d) below, all interested persons with standing who wish to participate in the appeal hearing or to receive notice of the proceedings, filings or decisions shall file a written appearance with the board. (b) The appearance shall: (1) Identify the name and address of the person to whom further communication shall be sent; (2) Specify the person’s interest in the matters addressed by the appeal; and (3) Specify whether the person intends to participate in the appeal hearing or whether the person only wants to receive notice of filings or decisions, and, if so, which filings and/or decisions. (c) An appearance may be filed at any time before or at the appeal hearing. (d) The division and the appellant shall be deemed to have filed an appearance. Source. #5590, eff 2-25-93; ss by #6258, eff 5-30-96; ss by #8058, eff 5-30-04; ss by #10198-A, eff 10-3-12

Pes 506.08 Modifying or Rescinding a Special Permit

Effective 2024-08-22.

Pes 506.08 Modifying or Rescinding a Special Permit. The division, after a hearing pursuant to RSA 541-A where a finding is made that conditions of a special permit have not been met, shall: (a) Change the conditions of the special permit if it is still possible for the applicant to comply and make the proposed application of pesticides; or (b) Rescind the special permit if there is no longer sufficient time to comply with the conditions and still make the proposed application.

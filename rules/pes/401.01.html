Pes 401.01 Renewals, Delinquent Renewals, and Retesting

Effective 2022-06-01. Expires 2032-06-01.

Pes 401.01 Renewals, Delinquent Renewals, and Retesting. (a) Any person holding a current license, permit, or certificate may renew such license, permit, or certificate for the next year without taking another examination provided the following is submitted to the division: (1) “Renewal of Pesticide Registration or Permit Form” with the information required by RSA 430:34; (2) Proof of financial responsibility pursuant to Pes 302.03; (3) Report of pesticides used for the preceding year required by Pes 901.03 or Pes 901.04; and (4) Registration fee established by RSA 430:33. (b) An examination shall be required for any additional categories or classifications requested by the applicant but not held by the applicant the previous year. (c) Every fifth year of renewal, holders of commercial applicator or commercial applicator not for hire registrations or licenses, and holders of private applicator restricted use permits shall provide proof of compliance with the recertification requirements set forth in Pes 402.01 and Pes 402.02. (d) If a license, permit, or certificate is not renewed by June 1 of each year then such licensee, permittee, or certificate holder shall be required to take another exam pursuant to Pes 302.02 and Pes 305.04.

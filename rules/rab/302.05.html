Rab 302.05 Limitation for Certified General Real Estate Appraiser Applicants

Effective 2024-07-16. Earlier expiration 2023-02-02.

Rab 302.05 Limitation for Certified General Real Estate Appraiser Applicants. Experience for certified general real estate appraiser applicants shall total 3000 hours and be earned as follows: (a) No more than 1500 hours of the experience requirement shall be obtained from appraisals on single family residential, residential 2 to 4 family, residential building lot, or any written narrative appraisal report of a single-family property; and (b) A minimum of 1500 hours shall be from nonresidential appraisal work.

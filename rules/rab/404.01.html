Rab 404.01 Change of Address

Effective 2024-05-18. Earlier expiration 2023-02-02.

Rab 404.01 Change of Address. All apprentices, appraisers, and applicants shall notify the board in writing of any change in home or business address within 10 days of such change pursuant to RSA 310-B:15, II.

RSA 261:9 Delivery of Certificate.

Certificate of Title

261:9 Delivery of Certificate. – The certificate of title shall be mailed or delivered to the first lienholder named in it or, if none, to the owner. In the event the lien has been satisfied by a dealer, the dealer becomes the owner and the state is authorized to mail the title to the dealer.

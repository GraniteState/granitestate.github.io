RSA 208:3-d Pistols Permitted.

Deer

208:3-d Pistols Permitted. – Pistols permitted under RSA 208:3, 3-a, 3-b, and 3-c shall include .327 Federal Magnum, and any caliber .357 Magnum and greater, provided that a pistol used for taking deer under this section shall be loaded with no more than 6 rounds of ammunition at any time.

Source

Source. 2001, 143:3. 2008, 7:1, eff. July 4, 2008. 2021, 6:1, eff. June 22, 2021; 12:3, eff. June 22, 2021. 2024, 134:1, eff. Jan. 1, 2025.

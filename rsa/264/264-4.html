RSA 264:4 Form of Security.

Motor Vehicles

264:4 Form of Security. – Such security, when ordered, shall be in such form and amount as the director may require, but in no case in excess of the amount of actual damages, as determined to the satisfaction of the director, or in excess of the amount of proof of financial responsibility required under this chapter. Proof of responsibility as prescribed in RSA 264:20 shall in all cases be deemed sufficient.

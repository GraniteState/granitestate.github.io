RSA 664:9-c Reports of Receipts and Expenditures Filed by Other Methods.

Reports

664:9-c Reports of Receipts and Expenditures Filed by Other Methods. – A political committee or a candidate may file such candidate's required reports as an email attachment sent to the address elections@sos.nh.us, provided that: I. The font size of the reports as printed is not less than an 8 point font. II. Email attachments are in portable document format archive (PDFA) or other acceptable format as determined by the secretary of state. III. The report is received by the secretary of state on or before the date and time that the report is due. IV. Reports filed June 24, 2026 and thereafter must be in a format that meets web content accessibility guidelines.

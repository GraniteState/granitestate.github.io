RSA 6:3-a Rulemaking.

State Treasurer

6:3-a Rulemaking. – The treasurer may adopt rules under RSA 541-A and after a public hearing pursuant to: I. Payments to the treasurer. II. Deposits and investments. III. Disbursements. IV. Custodial duties. V. Administration of RSA 471-C. VI. Notifications required under RSA 6-B:3. VII. Access to confidential records.

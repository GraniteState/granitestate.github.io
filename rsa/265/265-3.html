RSA 265:3 Obedience to Police Officers.

Obedience to and Effect of Traffic Laws

265:3 Obedience to Police Officers. – I. No person shall willfully fail or refuse to comply with any lawful order or direction of any police officer invested by law with authority to direct, control or regulate traffic. II. The fine for a violation of this section shall be $100.

RSA 198:40-d Annual Adjustment.

Adequate Education; Education Trust Fund

198:40-d Annual Adjustment. – Beginning July 1, 2026, and every year thereafter, the department of education shall adjust the following with an increase of 2 percent annually, rounded up to the nearest whole dollar: I. Per pupil costs in RSA 198:40-a, II; and II. Chartered public school additional grants under RSA 194-B:11, I(b)(1)(A) and (B).

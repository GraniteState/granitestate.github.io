RSA 21-J:1-a Boards Administratively Attached.

General Provisions

21-J:1-a Boards Administratively Attached. – The following boards shall be administratively attached to the department of revenue administration, under RSA 21-G:10: I. The current use board, established under RSA 79-A:3. II. The assessing standards board, established under RSA 21-J:14-a. III. [Repealed.]

RSA 420-G:14 Rulemaking Authority.

420-G:14 Rulemaking Authority. – I. (a) The commissioner may adopt rules, under RSA 541-A, relative to: (1) Uniform age rating levels that are consistent with 45 C.F.R. 147.102. (2) Special enrollment periods designed to allow employees to purchase individual coverage on the exchange during their employer's open enrollment period, even if the employer's open enrollment period does not coincide with the open enrollment period in the individual market. (3) Essential health benefits, in accordance with RSA 420-G:4-d, II and III. (b) The commissioner may adopt further rules, pursuant to RSA 541-A, necessary to the proper administration of this chapter. II. The commissioner, with the approval of the commissioner of the department of health and human services, shall adopt rules, under RSA 541-A, defining the content, format, and schedule for the filing of encrypted claims data and HEDIS information under RSA 420-G:11.

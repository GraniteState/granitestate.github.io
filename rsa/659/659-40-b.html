RSA 659:40-b Intimidation of Election Officers.

Prohibited Acts

659:40-b Intimidation of Election Officers. – I. No person shall use or threaten force, violence, or any tactic of coercion or intimidation to knowingly discourage, interfere with, or compel any election officer, as defined in RSA 652:14, and other appointed individuals assisting at the polling place, such as ballot clerks and greeters, from engaging in or completing duties related to an election. II. Whoever violates the provisions of this section or who conspires to violate the provisions of this section shall be guilty of a class B felony.

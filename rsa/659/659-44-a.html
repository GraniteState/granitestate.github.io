RSA 659:44-a Electioneering by Public Employees.

Prohibited Acts

659:44-a Electioneering by Public Employees. – I. No public employee, as defined in RSA 273-A:1, IX,shall electioneer while in the performance of his or her official duties. II. No public employee shall use government property or equipment, including, but not limited to, telephones, facsimile machines, vehicles, and computers, for electioneering. III. For the purposes of this section, "electioneer" is defined according to RSA 652:16-h. IV. (a) Any person who violates this section shall be guilty of a misdemeanor. (b) Any person who violates this provision and is not subject to the penalties imposed under subparagraph IV(a) shall be subject to a civil penalty not to exceed $1,000.

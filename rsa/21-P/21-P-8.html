RSA 21-P:8 Division of Motor Vehicles.

General Provisions

21-P:8 Division of Motor Vehicles. – There is established within the department the division of motor vehicles under the supervision of an unclassified director of motor vehicles who shall be responsible for the following functions, in accordance with applicable law: I. Motor vehicle and driver regulation, including, but not limited to, driver licensing, vehicle registration, financial responsibility compliance, and title issuance. II. Registration of commercial and private boats, as provided in RSA 270-E:3. III. Administration of the motor vehicle laws under RSA 260, except for road toll administration under RSA 260:30 through RSA 260:65-a, which shall be administered by the division of administration. IV. [Repealed.]

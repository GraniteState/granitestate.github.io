RSA 231:8 Petition.

Laying Out Highways

231:8 Petition. – Selectmen of a town, upon petition, may lay out any new class IV highway not financed in whole or in part with federal aid highway funds, and class V or VI highway or alter any such existing highway within their town for which there shall be occasion.

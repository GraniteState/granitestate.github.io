RSA 259:121 Utility Dealer.

259:121 Utility Dealer. – "Utility dealer" shall mean: I. Any person engaged in the manufacture, purchase, sale, or exchange at wholesale or retail of travel or camp trailers, utility trailers, boat trailers, semi-trailers, tractors, farm equipment, construction equipment, chassis with special equipment, modular buildings, manufactured housing, or any combination thereof. II. Any person engaged in the lease or rental of storage trailers.

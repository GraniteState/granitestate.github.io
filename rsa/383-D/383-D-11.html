RSA 383-D:11-1101 Examination.

ARTICLE 11 Examinations

383-D:11-1101 Examination. – (a) The commissioner shall examine a family trust company every 36 months, or more often when necessary in his or her judgment. (b) RSA 383-C:14-1401 and RSA 383-C:14-1402 shall not apply to a family trust company.

RSA 260:19 Destruction of Original Records.

Powers and Duties

260:19 Destruction of Original Records. – Whenever such photographs, microphotographs or reproductions on film shall be placed in conveniently accessible files and provisions made for preserving, examining and using the same, the director shall have the power to authorize the destruction of the original records, papers or documents.

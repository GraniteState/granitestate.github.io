RSA 214:8 Form; Fees.

Hunting, Fishing, and Trapping

214:8 Form; Fees. – I. The executive director shall prepare licenses, game tags, permits, and such other forms as determined necessary to comply with all licensing requirements of title XVIII. Such license, when issued to the licensee, shall bear the date of issuance, and shall contain the name, age, color of eyes, and residence of the licensee and such other information, including affidavits, as may be requested by the executive director. The license, to be valid, shall be countersigned by the licensee on the face of the license in the space provided. II. The executive director shall set fees for the various license types described in RSA 214:9 pursuant to RSA 206:10, I.

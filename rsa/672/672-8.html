RSA 672:8 Local Legislative Body.

Words and Phrases Defined

672:8 Local Legislative Body. – I. "Local legislative body" means one of the following basic forms of government utilized by a municipality: (a) Council, whether city or town; (b) Mayor-council; (c) Mayor-board of aldermen; (d) Village district or precinct; (e) Town meeting; or (f) County convention. II. In non-charter towns, village districts, and counties in which there are located unincorporated places that have adopted the authority provided in RSA 674:18-a for the approval of amendments to zoning ordinances or bylaws and the local zoning map, the term "local legislative body" shall mean local governing body as defined in RSA 672:6 solely for the purpose of adopting amendments to a zoning ordinance or local zoning map.

RSA 669:27 Forms.

By Official Ballot

669:27 Forms. – Prior to each such election, the clerk shall prepare in such quantities as he may deem necessary the following forms: I. Official absentee voting ballots, with the words "absentee ballot" printed on them, similar in form to the official ballot to be used at said election, and similarly endorsed and printed on paper of the same color as that used for official ballots; II. Blank forms of application for absentee ballots worded similar in form to those required by RSA 657:4. III. Envelopes of sufficient size to contain the ballots specified in paragraph I, on which shall be printed an affidavit similar in form to that required by RSA 657:7 or 657:8, as applicable.

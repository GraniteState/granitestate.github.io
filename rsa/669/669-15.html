RSA 669:15 Officers Who Shall be Elected; Election by Ballot.

Officers Elected

669:15 Officers Who Shall be Elected; Election by Ballot. – The town officers specified in this section shall be elected at a town election by the voters of the town, and the election of such officers shall be by ballot as specified by the RSA section indicated: I. Selectmen (RSA 41:8 through 8-e). II. Moderator (RSA 40:1). III. Supervisors of the checklist (RSA 41:46-a). IV. Town clerk (RSA 41:16 through 16-b). V. Town treasurer (RSA 41:26 through 26-b), unless provision has been made for appointment pursuant to RSA 41:26-e. VI. Highway agents, unless provision has been made for their appointment (RSA 231:62 through 62-b).

Source

Source. 1979, 410:1. 1983, 164:3. 2007, 35:7, eff. July 13, 2007.

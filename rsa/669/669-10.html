RSA 669:10 Term of Office.

General Provisions

669:10 Term of Office. – I. Except as otherwise provided, the term of office of any officer elected under this chapter shall begin upon his election and qualification for office and shall end upon the election and qualification of his successor. II. No person shall assume a town office until after the time period for requesting a recount is over. If a recount is requested for a town office, no person shall assume that office until after the recount is completed.

Source

Source. 1979, 410:1. 1989, 116:1, eff. July 7, 1989.

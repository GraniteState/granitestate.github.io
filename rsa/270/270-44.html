RSA 270:44 Size of Rafts; Separation of Rafts and Single Boats.

Rafting of Boats

270:44 Size of Rafts; Separation of Rafts and Single Boats. – Except as otherwise provided in this subdivision, no person shall, in a prohibited location or at a prohibited time: I. Form or allow the boat which he is operating or in charge of to be a member of a raft consisting of 3 or more boats; II. Form or allow the boat which he is operating or in charge of to be a member of a raft if any part of such raft is: (a) Less than 50 feet from any other raft; or (b) Less than 50 feet from any occupied single boat which is stationary upon the waters of the same lake or pond. III. Anchor a single boat and cause it to remain stationary upon the waters of a lake or pond other than momentarily if any part of such boat is: (a) Less than 50 feet away from any raft; or (b) Less than 25 feet away from any other single boat which is stationary upon the waters of such lake or pond.

Source

Source. 1983, 314:2, eff. June 18, 1983.

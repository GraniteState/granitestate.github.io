RSA 147-B:11 Penalty; Fine.

147-B:11 Penalty; Fine. – I. Any generator shall be guilty of a misdemeanor who: (a) Does not pay the fee required in RSA 147-B:8; or (b) Knowingly gives or causes to be given any false information in reports, records, or documents required by the department under RSA 147-B. II. Each day of violation of RSA 147-B:11, I, shall constitute a separate offense. Notwithstanding RSA 651:2, each violation of RSA 147-B:11, I, shall be punishable by a fine not to exceed $100 a day. III. Fines paid for violations of RSA 147-B:11, I, shall be deposited into the fund, notwithstanding RSA 618:2.

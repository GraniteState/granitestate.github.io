RSA 243:5 Record of Articles of Agreement.

Formation of a Railroad Corporation

243:5 Record of Articles of Agreement. – When the full amount of the capital stock has been subscribed in good faith by responsible parties, the directors shall cause the articles of agreement to be recorded in the office of the secretary of state, and a true copy thereof, attested by a majority of the directors, to be filed in the office of the clerk of each city and town through or into which the route of the proposed railroad will extend.

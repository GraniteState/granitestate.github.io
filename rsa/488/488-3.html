RSA 488:3 Registration Required.

488:3 Registration Required. – I. No person shall withdraw or discharge a cumulative amount of more than 20,000 gallons of water per day, averaged over any 7-day period, or more than 600,000 gallons of water over any 30-day period, at a single real property or place of business without registering the withdrawal or discharge with the department. Transfers of such volume of water shall also be registered. Registration shall be in addition to any required permits. II. No registration shall be transferred to another person without written notification to the commissioner.

Source

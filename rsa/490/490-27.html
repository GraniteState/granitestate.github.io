RSA 490:27 Probate Court Fees.

Court Fees, Fines, Forms, and Personnel

490:27 Probate Court Fees. – I. Probate court fees as established by the supreme court under RSA 490:26-a shall be paid to the clerk of the applicable circuit court established in RSA 490-F for the benefit of the state. II. The sum of $5 shall be added to each entry fee collected in the probate courts and shall be deposited in the mediation and arbitration fund established under RSA 490-E:4.

RSA 167:63 Definitions.

Uncompensated Care and Medicaid Fund

167:63 Definitions. – As used in this subdivision: I. "Commissioner" means the commissioner of health and human services. II. "Department" means New Hampshire department of health and human services. III. [Repealed.] IV. "Hospitals" mean general acute care hospitals licensed under RSA 151 that provide inpatient and outpatient hospital services, but shall not include government facilities or special hospitals for rehabilitation. V. "Medicaid" means the New Hampshire medicaid assistance program as described in RSA 161 and RSA 167, and in Title XIX of the Social Security Act as amended.
